## What this shop does
- Car audio: car stereo installation, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, speaker replacement, amplifier installation, subwoofer installation and upgrades, DSP tuning
- Window tint: 3M and Solar films, hand-cut on a glass board, never cut on the vehicle. Side and rear glass; no roof tint.
- Vehicle security: car alarm installation, Viper alarms, Compustar remote start, GPS trackers, dash cameras
- Harley bagger audio: AMS Pods lower-fairing speaker pods for Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King, amplifier installation and tuning on the bike

## What this shop does not do
- No roof tint, no vehicle wraps, no paint protection film, no auto glass repair
- No DJ or pro-audio products; car audio only
- No national or 48-state shipping claims; orders are picked up in store or delivered by arrangement
- No mobile installation
