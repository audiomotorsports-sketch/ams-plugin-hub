# Tesla Tint LA

> Tesla window tint by Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, Carson, CA 90745. Solar Ceramic and 3M IR Ceramic film, hand-cut on a glass board, applied to the side and rear glass by appointment. Tesla vehicles only; other cars are tinted at LA Car Tint.

## Process facts
- Film is hand-cut on a glass board, never on the car, then applied from inside.
- The one-piece rear glass is done as a single sheet.
- Cameras, sensors and the key-card reader are left clear; ceramic film does not interfere with signals.
- The glass roof is factory dark and is not filmed. No roof tint.
- California front side windows: 70% VLT.
