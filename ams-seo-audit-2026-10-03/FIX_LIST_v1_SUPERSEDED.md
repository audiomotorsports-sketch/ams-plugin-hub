## Cross-site issues

**Duplicate content and cannibalization**
- The hub mounts about 775 copies of lacarbeats, lacartint, lacaralarm, lacarstereo, amspods and teslatintla content (item 36). It also mounts shop mirrors (item 37). Either these domains or the hub copies need to be the canonical home. Right now two URLs can answer the same query.
- Checked directly: shop.audiomotorsports.com sells the same products as lacarstereo.com (e.g. Kicker 46CSC654), and its homepage canonical points to the hub. Decide which domain owns "car stereo Carson".
- teslatintla.com states a Tesla-versus-everything-else split with lacartint.com. That overlap could only be judged from teslatintla's own copy. Audit lacartint's Tesla and ceramic pages once reachable.
- Payment-provider copy (Snap, Koalafi, EasyPay, Synchrony) and the "one bay in Carson" phrasing are shared across sites. lacarstereo's main copy is not duplicated verbatim.
- The hub links to lacarbeats (389 pages), lacartint (370), lacaralarm (340), teslatintla (86), amspods (44) and the shop (all 1,057). lacarstereo.com is not linked anywhere on the hub.

**One local entity, six domains**
- All audited sites share one phone number, address, hours, Google Maps profile link and the "Google 4.8 from 654" rating. Google and AI engines will treat them as one business. That helps local trust but makes separate domains compete.
- Recommendation: let the hub be the single entity (one @id, one GBP), and make the brand domains reference it. Keep each brand domain focused on one service query set.

**Local SEO: Carson / LA / OC / South Bay**
- The hub, lacarstereo (123 cities) and teslatintla (124 cities) all use templated city pages for a one-bay Carson shop. Coverage is wide but thin, and the copy admits there is no shop in most of those cities. Concentrate on real local pages for Carson, South Bay (Torrance, Gardena, Hawthorne, Long Beach, Redondo, Lakewood) and a small number of OC and LA gateway cities. Consolidate the rest (items 40, 41).
- Schema radius and areaServed disagree with what the pages claim (item 17). Pick one service radius per brand and apply it everywhere.
- Not checked (needs Zack's access): the GBP listing, review counts, citations and NAP on third-party sites.
