### Can you do GPS tracker installation on a fleet of work trucks?

Yes. Each truck gets a hidden tracker on constant power with a clean ground, so it survives washing and vibration and does not drain the battery when parked. Book the trucks in a block and we work through them one at a time, same install on each.

### What does a dash camera installation involve on a work van?

The camera is hardwired to a fuse tap with a voltage cutoff, the cable runs under the headliner and down the pillar, and parked mode is set to wake on impact. Front and rear units are available. Nothing hangs from the windshield and the driver's view stays clear.

### Does a Harley fairing amplifier need a separate battery?

No. A properly sized fairing amp runs from the bike's charging system with a fused power lead, and we check the charging output on your bike before choosing the amp. The pods and fairing speakers are matched to that power so the system is loud without stressing the electrical.
