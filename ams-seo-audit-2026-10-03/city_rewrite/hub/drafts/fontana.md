**GPS tracker installation.** Hidden and hardwired, reporting location and movement to your phone, for a vehicle that is financed or shared.

**Ceramic window tint.** Solar Ceramic film hand-cut on the glass, four doors and rear window, for cabins that bake in the sun.

## Fontana alarm and remote start questions

### Is a Fontana to Carson drive worth it for car alarm installation?

Yes, when the alarm is wired so it actually protects the vehicle: module hidden, connections soldered, starter interrupt tied in so the engine will not crank. That is done once and lasts. The drive is about 61.7 miles; book both the alarm and anything else you want so one trip covers it.

### Can I get the alarm, remote start and window tint all done in one visit from Fontana?

Yes, and for a drive this long we encourage it. Viper alarm installation, Compustar remote start installation and ceramic window tint run in the same bay on the same day. Tell us everything you want when you schedule so the parts and film are ready.

### Does remote start installation work on a car with a manual transmission?

On many manual vehicles, yes, using a safe-start procedure that confirms the car is in neutral before the system will start it. It requires a specific setup during installation. Send us the year, make and model and we will tell you whether your vehicle is supported.

### How does GPS tracker installation help if the car is stolen?

The tracker reports the vehicle's location and movement to your phone, and it is hidden where a quick search will not find it. Combined with a Viper alarm and starter interrupt, you have a deterrent, a way to stop the engine from cranking and a way to locate the car.

### Should I drop the car off or wait while the work is done?

For a trip from Fontana, most customers drop the car in the morning and spend the day nearby, since alarm, remote start and tint together take several hours. You are welcome to wait at the shop. Either way, we text when it is ready so you can time the drive back.
