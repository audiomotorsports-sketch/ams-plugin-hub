## West Hollywood alarm and tracker questions

### What makes a Viper alarm installation hard to defeat?

Placement and wiring. We hide the control module away from the obvious spots, solder and tape every connection instead of using crimp taps, and wire the starter interrupt so pulling a fuse does not disable it. The shock sensor is tuned so a window tap chirps and a hit sets off the siren.

### Should a West Hollywood car have a GPS tracker as well as an alarm?

If it parks on the street, yes. The alarm deters and the tracker recovers. A hidden tracker on its own circuit reports location to your phone and alerts you when the car moves. Together they cover both the break-in and the tow-away, which are the two things that happen to street-parked cars.
