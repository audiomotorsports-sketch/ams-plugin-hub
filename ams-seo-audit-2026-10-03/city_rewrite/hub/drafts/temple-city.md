## Bagger audio questions from Temple City

### Is Temple City to Carson a reasonable ride for bagger audio installation?

Yes. The ride is about 22.7 miles, the pods and amplifier are installed in a day, and you can wait or leave the bike. Bagger wiring that is sealed, fused and routed away from heat the first time does not come back for a dead channel, which is why riders make the trip.

### What is included in an AMS pods install?

The pods themselves, mounted in the fairing lowers with speakers aimed at the rider, an amplifier fused at the battery and grounded to the frame, and wiring routed and sealed so it survives vibration and rain. You keep the factory head unit controls.

### Can you replace the fairing speakers on a Harley without an amplifier?

You can, and better fairing speakers alone are an improvement at low speed. Above 50 miles per hour, wind noise overwhelms the factory radio's power, so amplifier installation is what makes the upgrade audible on the freeway. We will tell you which you actually need.

### Do Temple City drivers also come for car window tint?

Yes. Window tint is one of the shop's main services, with Solar and Solar Ceramic film hand-cut on the glass and installed on the doors and rear window. Bring the car on its own appointment, or both the car and the bike if we have bay time that day.

### How is GPS tracker installation done on a touring bike?

The tracker wires to the battery, hides under the bodywork and reports location and movement to your phone, drawing very little current while parked. It is a sensible add-on for a bike that is parked on the street or travels far from home. We can install it during the audio appointment.
