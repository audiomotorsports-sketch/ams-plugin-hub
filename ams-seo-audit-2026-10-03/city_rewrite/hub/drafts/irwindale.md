### Is Irwindale to Carson worth it for Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation?

Yes. The result depends on the dash kit fitting flush, the interface keeping your wheel buttons and camera, and the wiring behind the dash being tidy, which is routine here. The drive is about 33.5 miles and the install is usually done the same day, so you make one trip.

### Can CarPlay be added to a work truck with a basic factory radio?

Yes, and basic radios are the easiest to replace. A double-DIN touchscreen with the right dash kit and harness goes in, and a backup camera can be added to the same screen if the truck does not have one. Send the year, make and model and we confirm the parts.

### Will Android Auto work wirelessly on the head units you install?

On most current units, yes. Wireless Android Auto and wireless CarPlay connect as you start the vehicle; a wired connection also charges the phone. We will suggest a unit based on how you drive and whether you want the backup camera on the same display.

### Can subwoofer installation be done during the CarPlay appointment?

Yes. While the head unit is wired, we run fused power from the battery, mount the amplifier and secure the enclosure so nothing rattles. The Dual Kicker Comp box is the usual starting point. Tell us both jobs when booking so we block enough bay time.

### Do Irwindale drivers get window tint on the same day?

Yes. Window tint and head unit installation run together in the same bay, so you drop the car once and pick up one finished vehicle. Solar or Solar Ceramic film is hand-cut on the glass for the doors and rear window. Mention both when scheduling.
