### Can I keep my factory Harley head unit with the pods?

Usually, yes. The factory radio feeds an amplifier that drives the pods and new fairing speakers, so you keep the controls you already know. If you want a different head unit we can do that as well, and we will tell you what fits your year when you book.

Yes. Car amplifier installation means power wire fused at the battery, a solid ground, signal cables run away from power and gains set with a meter so the amp never clips. It pairs with subwoofer installation or speaker replacement, and the trunk stays tidy.
