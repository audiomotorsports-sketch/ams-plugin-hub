## Questions from Bell

### Should I tint my Tesla in Bell or drive to your shop in Carson?

Drive the 11.3 miles. Tesla tint is a specialty job because of the one-piece rear window, and we do it as a single sheet of ceramic film in a clean bay. A seam down the back glass or a bubble near the camera is what you get from a rushed job, and it is not fixable without redoing it.

### Does tint affect Tesla Autopilot or Sentry cameras?

No. The film stays off the camera housings and the windshield sensor area, and ceramic film has no metal to interfere with signals. Autopilot, Sentry Mode, the phone key and cellular data all behave the same after tint. The cabin just runs cooler and the glass looks cleaner.

### How do Solar and Solar Ceramic film differ on a Tesla?

Solar film is a dyed film that darkens the glass and cuts glare, $249 installed as of October 2026. Solar Ceramic uses ceramic particles to block infrared heat at the same shade, $329 installed. On a Tesla with a glass roof, ceramic is the one that makes the car noticeably cooler.

### Can I get a car alarm on a Tesla?

Yes. A Viper 3108V alarm adds a loud siren and a shock sensor to back up the factory system, and it is $269 installed as of October 2026. It is also a popular add-on for gas cars parked overnight on the street. We wire it to the vehicle, not to a plug-in harness.

### How long before I can roll the windows down after tint?

Keep the windows up for three to five days so the film can cure to the glass. You may see a slight haze or small water pockets during that time, and they clear on their own. Washing the outside is fine right away; clean the inside glass gently after the first week.
