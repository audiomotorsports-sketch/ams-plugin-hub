### Does tinting a Tesla interfere with the cameras or sensors?

No. The film goes on the door glass and rear window, not over the Autopilot cameras or the windshield camera housing. Ceramic film is non-metallic, so it does not affect the key card, phone key or cellular signal. Tesla owners from South Gate see no change in how the car drives or connects.

### What shade is legal on the front windows in California?

California requires the front side windows to let in at least 70 percent of light, while the rear side windows and back glass can be darker. We will walk you through the options at the shop and show you film samples on real glass so you can pick a shade that looks right and keeps you legal.

### Why not have a mobile tinter do the Tesla in my South Gate driveway?

Dust. Film laid in an open driveway traps grit under it, and on a Tesla's big rear glass every speck shows. Our bay is enclosed, the glass is cleaned and the film is cut on the car. Done once in a clean bay beats doing it twice.

### How long does a Tesla window tint take and do I have to wait?

Plan on a few hours for the full car. You can wait in the shop or drop it off and come back later the same day. The film needs a few days to fully cure, so leave the windows up during that time. Book ahead so the bay is reserved for your car.
