### Will ceramic window tint block the Tesla phone key?

No. Ceramic film has no metal in it, so the Bluetooth signal the phone key uses, along with cellular and GPS, passes through without trouble. Supercharging and the cameras are unaffected. It is also why ceramic, not metallic, film goes on every Tesla and on any car packed with electronics.
