## Pomona car audio questions

### Is Pomona to Carson a sensible drive for subwoofer installation?

Yes. A subwoofer system is mostly labor: fused power from the battery, a clean ground, the enclosure secured and the gains set correctly. Done right once, it never rattles or blows a fuse. The drive is about 41.6 miles and the install is finished the same day you drop the car.

### What does amplifier installation include?

A fused power cable from the battery, a ground to bare metal, a remote turn-on from the head unit, signal cables routed away from power, the amplifier mounted on a solid surface and the gains set to match the head unit. No alternator whine, no drain while parked.

### Can you do speaker replacement in a truck with a factory premium system?

Yes, with care. Factory premium systems often use a separate amplifier and unusual speaker impedances, so we match the new speakers and, if needed, add an interface so the factory amp keeps working. Send the year, make and model before you drive out so we have the right parts.

### Will Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation keep my steering wheel buttons?

In most vehicles, yes. An interface module translates the factory button signals to the new head unit, so volume, track and voice buttons keep working. The backup camera is kept the same way. We confirm compatibility by year, make and model before booking.

### Can Pomona drivers get window tint while the stereo is installed?

Yes. Window tint and audio work run in the same bay at the same time, so you make one drive and pick up one finished car. Solar or Solar Ceramic film gets hand-cut right on the glass, doors and rear window alike. Mention both jobs when you schedule.
