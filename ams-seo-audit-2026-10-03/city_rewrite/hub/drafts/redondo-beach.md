## Redondo Beach riders and drivers ask

### What are AMS pods and will they fit my Harley bagger?

AMS pods are lower-fairing speaker pods built by Audio MotorSports for Harley baggers such as the Street Glide, Road Glide and Electra Glide. They mount in the lower fairing area and run off an amplifier, so you get real volume at speed. Pods start at $649 a pair as of October 2026.

### Is it worth riding from Redondo Beach to Carson for motorcycle audio?

Yes. Redondo Beach is 7.2 miles from the shop, and bagger audio is a specialty here rather than a sideline. Pods, amps and fairing speakers are installed in the bay with the wiring routed and protected, then tuned on the bike. You ride home with it done right the first time.

### Can I hear a bagger system before I buy?

Come by during business hours and we will show you a pod setup and an amplified fairing so you can hear the difference against stock speakers. Call (213) 429-1092 first so someone is free to walk you through it. No pressure to book that day.

### Will window tint hold up on a car that lives by the ocean?

Yes. Both the Solar and the Solar Ceramic film we install are made for salt air and constant sun. Ceramic in particular rejects heat without a metal layer, so it does not corrode or interfere with radio reception. Keep the windows up for a few days after install while it cures.

### Do you add Apple CarPlay to cars that never had a touchscreen?

We do. A CarPlay and Android Auto head unit replaces the factory radio with a screen that fits the dash, and we keep the backup camera, steering-wheel buttons and factory amp working where the car has them. Bring the year, make and model and we will tell you which units fit.
