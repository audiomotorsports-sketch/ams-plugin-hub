### Why drive a Tesla from Signal Hill to Carson for window tint?

### Does ceramic tint interfere with the Tesla phone key or cameras?

No. Ceramic film is non-metallic, so Bluetooth phone keys, Autopilot cameras, FM and cellular all work exactly as before. We also keep film clear of the camera housings and the sensor areas on the windshield. The only thing that changes is how much heat gets into the cabin.

### Can you tint the glass roof on a Model Y?

No. The panoramic roof comes dark from the factory and already blocks some UV, so we leave it as it is. Where owners actually feel the heat on summer afternoons is through the side and rear glass, and a ceramic layer there cuts it noticeably. We quote the job at the shop once we see the car.

### How long does a full car tint take in your shop?

Plan on two to four hours depending on the vehicle, longer for a Tesla rear window or a car with old film that has to be stripped first. The film needs a few days to fully cure, so leave the windows up on the drive back to Signal Hill.

### Do you install car alarms on Teslas or only on gas cars?

Both. Many Tesla owners add a Viper 3108V alarm for the siren and shock sensor the factory system does not have. On gas and hybrid vehicles we also do Compustar remote start. The alarm is $269 installed as of October 2026, and we quote remote start by vehicle.
