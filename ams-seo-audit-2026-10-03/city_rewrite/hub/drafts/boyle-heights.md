## Bagger audio questions from Boyle Heights

### What makes bagger audio installation different from a car stereo job?

Everything on a bike is exposed to weather and vibration, so the wiring has to be sealed, routed away from heat and secured so it cannot chafe. Speakers must be heard over wind at freeway speed, which means real amplifier power and enclosures aimed at the rider. That is what AMS pods are built for.

### Is a Road Glide from Boyle Heights worth riding to Carson for AMS pods?

Yes. AMS pods are our own design for Harley touring bikes and we install them every week, so the fit and the wiring are routine for us. The ride is about 14.3 miles. A bike wired right once does not come back for a rattle or a dead channel later.

### Can you add an amplifier to the factory Harley radio?

Yes. Amplifier installation on the stock head unit is a common upgrade: the amp takes the factory signal, powers the pods or upgraded fairing speakers, and is fused at the battery with a solid ground. You keep the factory controls and gain the volume you were missing.

### Do you also tint cars for Boyle Heights drivers?

Yes. Car window tint is one of our core services: Solar or Solar Ceramic film, hand-cut on the glass, applied to the four doors and rear window. Many riders bring the car on a separate day, or both the car and the bike on one appointment if we have the bay time.

### How does GPS tracker installation work on a motorcycle?

The tracker is wired to the bike's battery and hidden under bodywork where it is not obvious. It reports location and movement to your phone and draws very little current while parked. It is a reasonable add-on for a bike that is parked on the street or in a shared lot.
