## Cudahy window tint questions

### How long should I wait before rolling down tinted windows?

Leave the windows up for a few days after window tinting so the film cures against the glass. You may see a slight haze or small water pockets in the first days; that moisture evaporates on its own. After that, clean the inside with a soft cloth and an ammonia-free cleaner and the film will last.

### Is ceramic window tint worth the extra money for a Cudahy car?

If the car sits in the sun during the day, yes. Solar Ceramic rejects far more heat than standard film at the same darkness, so the steering wheel and seats are cooler when you get in. As of October 2026 it is $329 installed versus $249 for Solar, a difference you feel every summer.
