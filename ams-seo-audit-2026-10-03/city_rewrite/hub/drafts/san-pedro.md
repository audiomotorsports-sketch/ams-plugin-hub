## Questions from San Pedro Tesla owners

### Is Tesla window tint worth the drive from San Pedro?

Yes. A Tesla cabin is mostly glass, and ceramic film on the side and rear windows cuts the heat without a dark, mirrored look; the roof is factory dark. The trip from San Pedro to the Carson shop is about 6.5 miles, so you spend more time charging than driving. Most owners are in and out the same day.

### Can you tint the rear window of a Model 3 in one piece?

We can. The Model 3 and Model Y back glass is tinted as a single sheet, heat-shrunk to the curve on the outside and then applied inside. No split line, no seam, and the third brake light and camera stay clear. It takes longer than a normal rear window, which is why we schedule it.

### Does window tinting interfere with Tesla cameras or Autopilot?

No. The camera housings on the pillars and the windshield area are left uncovered, and we do not tint the windshield. Side and rear cameras see through tint without trouble. We have done enough Teslas to know exactly where the cutouts go, so the car works the same way it did when you arrived.

### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic film?

Solar is a quality dyed film that darkens the glass and cuts glare. Solar Ceramic uses a ceramic layer that rejects much more infrared heat at the same shade, so the cabin stays cooler. As of October 2026 Solar is $249 and Solar Ceramic is $329 installed, four doors and the rear glass.

### Can I add dash camera installation and tint during the same San Pedro visit?

Yes. Dash cam wiring goes in first, routed along the headliner and A-pillar to the fuse box, and the tint follows. Book both and we set aside the bay. If you would rather not wait, drop the car at 9:30 AM and we will call when it is ready.
