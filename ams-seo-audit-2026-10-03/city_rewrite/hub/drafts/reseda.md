## Reseda bagger questions

### Do AMS pods fit a Road Glide with aftermarket lowers?

Usually, but it depends on the lowers. The pods are designed around factory Harley lower fairings; some aftermarket lowers work and some do not. Text a photo of the lowers and your year and model to (213) 429-1092 before you ride from Reseda and we will tell you straight.

### Can you install pods and a new amplifier in the same visit?

Yes, and it is the best way to do it, because the amp has to be matched to the pods, fairing and lid speakers together. We mount the amp dry, run weatherproof wiring, and set gains and crossover on the bike. Plan on a few hours at the shop in Carson.

### Is Reseda too far for a bagger audio appointment?

No. It is about 30.1 miles to 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, roughly 60 to 72 minutes outside rush hour (approx), and riders come from much farther for AMS pods. The appointment is set so the bike goes straight into the bay. Pods start at $649 a pair as of October 2026.

### Will bagger audio installation survive rain and vibration?

That is the point of doing it at a shop. Connections are sealed, wiring is routed away from heat and moving parts and secured so it cannot chafe, and the amp is mounted where water does not collect. A bike system that is wired like a car system fails on the first wet ride.
