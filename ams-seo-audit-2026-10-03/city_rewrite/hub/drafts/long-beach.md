**Remote start installation.** Compustar installation with a bypass module matched to your car, so the engine starts from the fob and the cabin is cool before you get in. Many Long Beach customers add a DroneMobile module so the phone does the same job from anywhere.

**GPS tracker installation.** A hidden tracker wired into the harness, out of sight, that shows where the car is from your phone and alerts you if it moves. Useful for a second car, a work truck, or a kid's first car.

**Car window tint for Long Beach commutes.** Solar or Solar Ceramic film on four doors and the back glass, hand-cut on the glass. Ceramic rejects more heat, which matters when the car sits in a lot all day.

**Car stereo installation and CarPlay.** Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation, speaker replacement, subwoofer installation and amplifier installation, by the same people who handle the alarm wiring.
