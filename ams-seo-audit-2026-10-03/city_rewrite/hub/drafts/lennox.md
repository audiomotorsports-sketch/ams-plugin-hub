## CarPlay questions from Lennox

### Can you add CarPlay to my car without losing the backup camera?

Yes. We use a vehicle-specific harness and interface that keeps the factory camera, steering-wheel controls and in many cases the factory amplifier. The new receiver shows the camera when you shift into reverse, same as before. We check the exact parts list for your year, make and model before you leave Lennox.

### Is wireless CarPlay better than wired?

Wireless is more convenient; you get in and it connects. Wired charges the phone while it is plugged in and never drops. Most Lennox commuters pick a receiver that does both, so the choice is yours each trip. We will show you both on the shop demo board before you decide.
