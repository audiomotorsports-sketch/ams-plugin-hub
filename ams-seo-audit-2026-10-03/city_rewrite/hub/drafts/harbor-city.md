## What Harbor City drivers bring us

**Car stereo installation.** A head unit installation here means a vehicle-specific harness, steering-wheel controls and the factory camera kept working, and wiring tucked behind the dash so nothing rattles or cuts out six months later. The part is cheap online; the clean install is why people drive in.

**Subwoofer installation and amplifier installation.** Most Harbor City audio jobs end with a sub and an amp. We run proper gauge power wire from the battery with a fuse, ground it to bare metal, and set gains by ear and meter so the amp never clips.

**Car window tint and ceramic window tint.** Solar or Solar Ceramic film on four doors and the back glass, no windshield, hand-cut on the glass and laid without bubbles or a light gap at the edge.

**Viper alarm installation and remote start installation.** The alarm brain goes where a thief will not find it and the wiring is soldered so it cannot be pulled apart at the fuse box. Compustar installation is done by the same crew that does the audio.

**Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation.** If your car predates CarPlay, a replacement receiver gives you maps, calls and music on a real screen, fitted with a dash kit so it looks factory.

## Harbor City questions we hear

### How long does car stereo installation take for a Harbor City customer?

A straightforward head unit installation is usually done the same morning or afternoon you drop it off. Add speaker replacement, a subwoofer and an amplifier and plan on most of a day. Because Harbor City is so close to the Carson shop, many customers drop off, run errands, and pick up after work.

### Can I get subwoofer installation without replacing my factory stereo?

Yes. We add a line-output converter or a DSP that takes the factory signal and feeds a new amplifier and subwoofer. Your dash stays stock, the screen and buttons keep working, and you get real bass. This is one of the most common car audio jobs we do for Carson and Harbor City.

### Is Audio MotorSports in Harbor City or in Carson?

The shop is in Carson at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, one bay that handles car stereo installation, car window tint, alarm installation, remote start, dash camera installation and GPS tracker installation. There is no Harbor City storefront, and there does not need to be: it is about 2.8 miles door to door.

### Do you do window tinting the same day as a stereo install?

Often, yes. Schedule both when you book and we plan the bay around it. Tint goes on after the audio wiring is finished so nothing disturbs the film while it cures. If the combined job runs long, you can drop off in the morning and collect the car at closing.

### What should I bring to my Harbor City appointment?

Bring the car, the keys, and any factory stereo code if your vehicle uses one. For remote start installation or an alarm, bring all of your key fobs. If you already own speakers or a head unit, bring them and we will tell you honestly whether they are worth installing.
