### How do AMS pods change the sound on a Street Glide?

Fairing speakers sit above you and get swallowed by wind at speed. Pods in the lower fairings put speakers at knee level, closer to the rider, and fill in the midbass the fairing cannot. With an amplifier tuned on the bike, the mix stays clear at freeway speed instead of turning into noise.
