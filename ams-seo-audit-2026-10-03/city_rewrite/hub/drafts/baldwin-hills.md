### Which head unit do you recommend for CarPlay and Android Auto?

It depends on the dash opening and whether you want wireless connection, a larger floating screen or a factory-look fit. ### Can I keep my factory steering-wheel buttons after the install?

Yes. A steering-wheel control interface translates the factory buttons to the new head unit, so volume, track and voice buttons keep working. We include it on every CarPlay and Android Auto install where the car has buttons. The backup camera stays connected too.

### Will Android Auto work on the same unit as Apple CarPlay?

Yes. Every unit we install supports both, so a household with an iPhone and an Android phone can share the car. The unit switches to whichever phone connects. Wireless connection works for both on most current head units, and wired connection is always available as a backup.
