Most vehicles from roughly 2014 onward with a factory color screen can take a plug-in CarPlay and Android Auto module that uses the original display and keeps the factory radio menus. Yes. The factory camera is retained through an interface harness built for your vehicle, and steering-wheel buttons come across through a control module.
