### Can I start the car from my phone?

Yes. A phone control module adds lock, unlock, start and location to an app, with no range limit as long as the car has cell coverage. It is quoted with the remote start when you book and can be added to the same install.

### Will an aftermarket alarm or remote start void my warranty?

In most cases, no.
