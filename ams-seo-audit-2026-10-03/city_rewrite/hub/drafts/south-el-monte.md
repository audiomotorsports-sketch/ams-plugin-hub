## South El Monte Tesla tint questions

### Why does Tesla window tint cost more than tint on a regular car?

The rear glass. A Tesla back window is one large curved piece that has to be heat-shrunk on the outside and applied inside as a single sheet without a seam. That takes more film, more time and more skill than a sedan rear window. Ask for a Tesla tint quote by model when you call.

### Does ceramic window tint block the Tesla phone key or the cameras?

No. Ceramic film has no metal in it, so Bluetooth, cellular and GPS signals pass through. The camera housings on the pillars and the rear are left uncovered, and we never tint the windshield. Autopilot and the cameras work exactly as they did when you drove in from South El Monte.

### Can I get a dash camera installation on a Model Y?

Yes. Some owners want a dedicated dash cam with its own storage and parking mode in addition to the built-in cameras. We hardwire it to the fuse box and hide the cable in the headliner and pillar trim so nothing dangles. For a non-Tesla from South El Monte, it is a routine install.

### What should I do after Tesla window tint to let it cure?

Keep the windows up for a few days so the film bonds to the glass. A light haze or small water spots in the first week are normal and clear on their own. Clean the inside with a soft cloth and an ammonia-free cleaner once it has cured, and the tint will last for years.
