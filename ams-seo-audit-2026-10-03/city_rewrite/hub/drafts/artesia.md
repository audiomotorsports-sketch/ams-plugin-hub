## Artesia questions

### Does tint interfere with a Tesla's cameras or glass roof?

No. Window tint goes on the door glass and rear window, not over the camera housings, and the ceramic film we use is metal-free so it does not affect cell, GPS or key-card signals. The glass roof can be filmed too if you want the heat down, and that is quoted at the shop.

### How long will my Tesla be at the shop if I drive in from Artesia?

Plan on a few hours for Tesla window tint. Most Artesia owners drop the car at 9:30 and are back by early afternoon, or wait at the shop. Customers have driven in from San Diego for the same job, so 10.8 miles is a quick trip by comparison.

### Can you do ceramic tint on a car that already has old purple film?

Yes. We strip the failed film, clean the adhesive off the glass and the defroster lines, and lay new Solar Ceramic. Removal is quoted at the shop because it depends on how the old film comes off; the new ceramic window tint itself is $329 installed as of October 2026.

### What does a car alarm installation cost for an Artesia driver?

A Viper 3108V alarm is $269 installed as of October 2026, with a two-way remote, shock sensor and starter interrupt. Compustar remote start is from $499 as of October 2026. Add GPS tracking to either and you can see the car on your phone.

### Does a dash camera installation hide the wiring?

Yes. The camera is hardwired to a fuse tap so parking mode works with the car off, and the cable runs along the headliner and A-pillar so nothing dangles. Front and rear units are both available and quoted at the shop by vehicle.
