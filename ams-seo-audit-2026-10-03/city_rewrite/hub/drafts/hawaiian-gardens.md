### Is Tesla tint any different from tinting a regular car?

Yes, in three ways. The rear glass is a single large curve that the film has to be shrunk to fit, the side cameras and sensors must stay uncovered, and the glass roof is an optional extra panel. We handle all three on the car rather than relying on a pre-cut pattern.

### Will tint interfere with the Tesla cameras or Autopilot?

No. The cameras sit behind their own clear housings and we keep film off them entirely. The front windshield gets no film beyond the legal top strip, so the forward cameras are untouched. Side and rear tint has no effect on the sensors or the backup view.

### How long should I plan for a Tesla tint appointment from Hawaiian Gardens?

Plan on a few hours for four doors plus the rear glass; add time if you want the roof panel done as well. Most Hawaiian Gardens owners drop the car in the morning and get a call when it is finished. Waiting at the shop is fine too.

### Does a subwoofer installation work with a factory premium stereo?

Yes. A line-output converter picks the signal off the factory speaker wires and feeds an amplifier and sub, so the factory head unit stays and the bass gets added underneath it. A Dual Kicker Comp box at $249 box only as of October 2026 is the usual starting point.

### Do you do remote start installation on older trucks?

We do, and older trucks are often the easiest. A Compustar 9910 remote start starts from $499 as of October 2026, and a standard key ignition needs less bypass hardware than a push-button car. Bring the year, make and model and we confirm fit before you drive down.
