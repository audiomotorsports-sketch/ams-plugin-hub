## Questions from Del Aire riders and drivers

### What are AMS pods and why use them on a Harley bagger?

AMS pods are speaker enclosures we build for Harley touring bikes. They fit the fairing lowers, hold real speakers rather than small factory drivers, and are shaped so the sound points at the rider. With an amplifier behind them you can hear music clearly above wind noise at freeway speed.

### Is it worth riding from Del Aire to Carson for bagger audio?

Yes, since bagger audio is a specialty and we build the pods ourselves. The ride from Del Aire is about 8.6 miles. Riders come from over 100 miles for this work, so a short run down to Carson for a same-day install is a small ask for the result.

### Can you tint my car the same day you do the bike?

We can, if you bring both and book both. The car gets Solar or Solar Ceramic window tint on the doors and rear glass while the bike is being wired. Mention both when scheduling and we will set aside enough bay time for the pair.

### Can you do subwoofer installation in a truck without losing the back seat?

Yes. Under-seat and behind-seat enclosures are built for most pickups, and a compact amplifier mounts out of the way. You keep the seat and the storage while gaining real low end. Bring the truck and we will show you which enclosure fits.

### Will dash camera installation still work with window tint?

Yes. We leave a clear area where the camera lens meets the windshield when needed, and most dash cams sit behind the mirror where front-window film is not applied anyway. If you want both done, we install the camera and the tint in one appointment.
