## Audio questions from Beverly Hills

### Can you upgrade the sound without replacing the factory screen?

Yes, and that is the usual approach on newer vehicles. Speaker replacement and amplifier installation take the signal from the factory head unit, so the screen, climate controls and steering wheel functions stay exactly as they are. You get clearer, louder sound with no change to the dash.

### Is a Beverly Hills to Carson trip reasonable for a speaker replacement?

Yes. The drive is about 18.5 miles, the job is usually done within the day, and you can wait or drop the car. Speakers installed on proper rings with sealed connections do not rattle or fail early, which is the reason to bring it to a shop that does this every day.

### Will amplifier installation cause alternator whine or battery drain?

Not when it is done correctly. We fuse the power wire at the battery, ground the amplifier to bare metal and set the gains to match the head unit, which eliminates whine. The amp switches off with the ignition, so there is no drain while the car is parked.

### Do you tint Teslas and other electric cars from Beverly Hills?

Yes. Tesla window tint is a regular job here: ceramic film shrunk to the single-piece rear glass and trimmed on the frameless doors. Other EVs are tinted the same careful way. Ceramic film has no metal, so none of the car's connectivity or sensors are affected.

### Can dash camera installation be hidden on a luxury interior?

Yes. The camera sits behind the mirror, the cable runs inside the headliner and down the pillar to a fuse tap, and nothing is visible from the seat. Parking mode records while the car is parked without draining the battery. It can be done during a tint or audio appointment.
