### Is it really worth driving from Florence-Firestone to Carson for a car stereo?

At 10 miles, yes. You get a shop that does car audio every day, with the wiring run correctly, the amp gains set with a meter and the subs tuned in the bay. A cheap install that cuts out or rattles costs more in the long run than the drive down.

### Can you make my factory stereo louder without replacing the head unit?

Yes. We add an amplifier that takes the factory signal, then upgrade the speakers and add a subwoofer. The dash stays stock, the steering-wheel controls keep working, and the system gets real volume and bass. It is the standard fix for newer cars with integrated screens.

### How long does a full car stereo install take?

A head unit swap runs two to three hours. Adding an amplifier, subwoofer box and new door speakers can take most of a day because of the wiring and tuning. Florence-Firestone customers usually drop off in the morning and we call when the car is ready.

### Will a loud system drain my battery or hurt the alternator?

Not if it is wired right. We size the power wire and fuse to the amplifier, add a proper ground and, for larger systems, recommend the big-three wiring upgrade. A system that dims your headlights on every bass note is a wiring problem, not a reason to turn it down.
