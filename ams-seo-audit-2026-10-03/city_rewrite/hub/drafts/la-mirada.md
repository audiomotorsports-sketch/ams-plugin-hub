## La Mirada window tinting questions

### Why hand-cut the tint on the glass instead of using a plotter?

Cutting on the glass means the film follows the exact curve and edge of your window, including the small differences between cars of the same model. The result is an edge that sits tight to the gasket with no light gap and no lifting corner. It takes longer, and it is why La Mirada drivers come to Carson.

### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic tint for a La Mirada car?
