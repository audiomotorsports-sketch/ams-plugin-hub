## What Exposition Park drivers bring us

**Tesla window tint.** The rear glass on a Model 3 and Model Y is one large curved piece; we heat-shrink the film to it and lay it as a single sheet, hand-cut on the glass, so there is no seam across your mirror view. The glass roof can be filmed to cut heat.

**Ceramic window tint.** For any car that parks in the open all day. Solar Ceramic blocks heat without a dark shade, and the edges are finished to the gasket.

**Car stereo installation.** Head unit installation with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto on a factory harness, speaker replacement, and subwoofer installation that keeps the trunk usable.

**Car alarm installation and remote start installation.** Viper alarm installation with the module hidden and the wiring wrapped; Compustar installation for remote start.

**Dash camera installation and GPS tracker installation.** Hardwired, parking mode on, cables hidden.

### How far is Exposition Park from Audio MotorSports and how long is a tint job?

### What does ceramic window tint do for a car parked outside all day?

Solar Ceramic film, $329 installed as of October 2026, rejects most of the infrared heat coming through the glass, so the cabin is cooler when you get in and the seats and dash fade slower. It does this without going darker than the legal limit on the front doors.

### Can you do a car stereo installation on a car with a factory touchscreen?

Yes. If the screen already runs CarPlay we leave it and do amplifier installation and speaker replacement behind it for better sound. If the screen is older, a head unit installation with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto replaces it with a factory-fit dash kit and no drilled dash.

### Do you handle car alarm installation for a car that parks on the street?

Yes. A Viper 3108V alarm, $269 installed as of October 2026, is wired so it cannot be bypassed from the fuse box and the module is hidden. Add a tilt sensor for wheels and a GPS tracker installation if you want to find the car from your phone.
