### Can you improve the sound in a Brentwood luxury car without changing the dash?

Yes. Speaker replacement, an amplifier and a subwoofer can all be added behind the factory screen using a signal converter, so navigation, the backup camera and the interior stay exactly as they are. This is the most common request from Brentwood owners.

### What does speaker replacement involve?

Door panels come off, the factory speakers come out, and new speakers go on adapters made for the opening with plug-in harnesses, so nothing is cut. The panel goes back with the clips intact. Done right, there is no rattle and no wind noise afterward.

### Is a subwoofer upgrade worth it in a car with a factory premium system?

Often, yes. Factory premium systems are usually weakest in the low end, and a properly installed sub with its own amplifier fills that in without touching the rest. We tune it to blend with the factory speakers rather than overpower them.

### Why have a shop do amplifier installation instead of a mobile installer?

Because the wiring is what matters: a fused power lead from the battery, a short ground to bare metal, signal routed away from power to avoid noise, and gains set with a meter. In a bay with the right tools it is done once and stays done.
