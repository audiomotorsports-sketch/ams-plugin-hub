## Paramount drivers ask

### How long does car window tinting take at the Carson shop?

A sedan or small SUV takes about two to three hours for the side glass and rear window. Paramount customers often drop the car at opening and pick it up after lunch, or wait in the shop. Leave the windows up for a few days after the install so the film cures flat.

### Is ceramic window tint worth the extra cost over Solar?

If heat is the problem, yes. Solar Ceramic window tint, $329 installed as of October 2026, rejects far more infrared heat than the $249 Solar film at the same shade, so the cabin stays cooler and the AC works less. If glare and privacy are all you care about, Solar does the job.

### Can a Paramount work truck get tint, a dash cam and a tracker in one visit?

Yes, and we recommend it. Book one appointment for window tint, dash camera installation and GPS tracker installation and the truck is off the road once instead of three times. Tell us the year, make and model when you book so parts are on hand.

### What alarm do you recommend for a car parked on the street?

The Viper 3108V, $269 installed as of October 2026. It has a shock sensor, siren, starter interrupt and a two-way remote that lets you know when the alarm trips. Add a GPS tracker if you want to locate the car from your phone.

### Do you do subwoofer installation in a truck with a factory stereo?

Yes. A powered subwoofer or a loaded box with an amplifier can be fed from the factory head unit, so you keep the stock dash and add real bass. For a regular-cab truck we size the enclosure to the space behind or under the seat.
