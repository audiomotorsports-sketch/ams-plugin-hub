### Does ceramic tint interfere with the phone key or Supercharging?

No. Ceramic film carries no metal, so Bluetooth from your phone key, cellular, GPS and the cameras all pass through as before. Supercharging is unaffected because the film is on the glass, not near the charge port. That is exactly why we put ceramic, not metallic film, on every Tesla.
