## What Venice riders and drivers bring us

**Bagger audio installation and AMS pods.** Lower fairing pods mounted solid, wiring run inside the fairing away from weather, and an amplifier set up so the music holds at freeway speed. Fairing speaker replacement and saddlebag lid speakers are done the same way.

**Car window tint.** Solar or Solar Ceramic film hand-cut on the glass and tucked to the gasket on the four doors and rear window.

**Car stereo installation and head unit installation.** The receiver sits in the correct dash kit on a plug-in harness, trim sits flush, and your steering wheel buttons keep working.

**Viper alarm installation and remote start installation.** Alarms and Compustar remote start wired into the harness so they cannot be defeated at the fuse box.

**Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation.** A touchscreen that runs your phone, camera retained.

### What does a bagger audio installation with AMS pods include?

The pods themselves, from 649 dollars a pair as of October 2026, mounted to the lower fairings. Wiring is routed inside the fairings and fused at the battery. An amplifier is matched to the pods and fairing speakers and the gains are set on the bike. Amp and labor are quoted on your model.

### Can I ride from Venice and wait while the pods are installed?

Yes. Venice is about 15.9 miles from the shop, and a pod install is a few hours. You can wait in the shop or drop the bike and pick it up later. Book ahead by phone or text so the bay is reserved for the bike.

### Do you only work on Harley-Davidson motorcycle audio or other bikes too?

Most of the bike work is Harley baggers because the fairings give us room for real speakers. If you ride something else with a fairing, text a photo and the model to (213) 429-1092 and Nick will say whether there is a clean way to do it.

### Will road salt or rain from the beach ruin a bike stereo install?

Not if the wiring is done for weather. Connections are heat-shrunk and routed inside the fairing, the amp is a marine-rated unit placed out of spray, and the pods seal to the lowers. Venice riders park near the water all the time and the systems hold up.

### Do you tint cars too, and is the price the same for Venice drivers?

Yes. Solar window tint is 249 dollars and Solar Ceramic is 329 dollars installed as of October 2026, four doors and rear glass, same price no matter where you drive in from. Many riders bring the bike for pods and the car for tint on separate days.
