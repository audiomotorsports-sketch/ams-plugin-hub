No. Ceramic film contains no metal, so the Bluetooth signal your phone key uses, along with cellular and GPS, passes through without trouble. Supercharging and the cameras are unaffected. It is a big part of why ceramic, not metallic film, goes on every Tesla and on any car full of electronics.
