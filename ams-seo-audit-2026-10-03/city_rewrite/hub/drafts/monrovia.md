### Does ceramic tint affect Supercharging, the phone key or cameras?

No. Ceramic film is non-metallic, so the phone key, key card, cellular and GPS all work as before. Film goes on the door glass and rear window only, never over the camera housings, so Autopilot and the cabin camera are not touched.
