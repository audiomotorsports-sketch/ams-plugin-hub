### Does a subwoofer need a new head unit?

No. A line output converter takes signal from the factory speaker wires and feeds the amplifier, so the dash stays stock and the bass comes up anyway. If you later want Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation, the amp is already in place and only the deck changes.
