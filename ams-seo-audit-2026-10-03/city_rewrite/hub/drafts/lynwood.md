## Lynwood rider and driver questions

### What are AMS pods and do they fit my Harley?

AMS pods are speaker enclosures that mount in the lower fairings of a Harley bagger, built and sold by Audio MotorSports. They fit most Street Glide, Road Glide, Ultra and Road King touring models with lowers. Tell us the year and model when you call from Lynwood and we will confirm fitment and speaker options.

### How loud can bagger audio installation get without distorting at speed?

Loud enough to hear clearly over wind and pipes at freeway speed, if the amplifier is matched to the speakers and the gains are set right. We tune every bagger on the bike, not on a bench. Fairing speakers, lid speakers and pods together give you sound from the front and the sides.
