### Why do AMS pods sound better than louder fairing speakers alone?

Fairing speakers point up and forward, and at speed the wind carries the sound away. AMS pods sit in the lower fairings at knee level, aimed at the rider, so the music stays full where you actually hear it. With an amplifier the fairing speakers and pods work together.

### Will the pods and amplifier survive rain and washing?

Yes. The speakers are marine-rated, the connections are weatherproofed and the amplifier is mounted where water does not collect. A bagger audio installation here is built for a bike that gets ridden year-round, not just on dry days.
