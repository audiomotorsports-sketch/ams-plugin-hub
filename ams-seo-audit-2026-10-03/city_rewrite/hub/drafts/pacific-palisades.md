## Pacific Palisades questions

### Can you upgrade the sound in a Range Rover or Mercedes without changing the dash?

Yes. The factory screen and controls stay. Amplifier installation with a DSP takes the factory signal, corrects it, and drives new speakers and a subwoofer. From the driver's seat the car looks untouched; it just sounds the way it should have from the factory.

### How far is the drive from Pacific Palisades and how long is a full audio install?

About 21.4 miles, or 43 to 51 minutes (approx). A speaker replacement with amplifier installation is a full day; a subwoofer installation alone is a few hours. Drop the car at 9:30 and we text when it is tuned and ready.

### What does the tuning step involve after a car stereo installation?

Gains, crossovers and time alignment are set in the bay with the doors shut and a reference track playing. The goal is a system that is clear at low volume and does not distort at high volume. Every car stereo installation here ends with that step before the keys go back.

### Does ceramic window tint affect the sound or the car's electronics?

No. Ceramic window tint is metal-free, so it does not interfere with radio, cell, GPS or key signals, and it has nothing to do with the audio. It does keep the cabin cooler, which matters for a dark interior. Solar Ceramic is $329 installed as of October 2026.

### Can I add a Viper alarm installation to the same appointment?

Yes. A Viper 3108V alarm is $269 installed as of October 2026, wired so it cannot be bypassed from the fuse box, with the module hidden. GPS tracker installation and Compustar remote start installation, from $499 as of October 2026, can be added to the same day.
