### Will amplifier installation drain my battery or cause electrical problems?

Not when it is installed right. We size the power wire, fuse it at the battery, and ground it to bare metal so the amp draws cleanly. A properly set gain keeps the system from clipping. If the car has a weak battery or alternator we will tell you before adding load.
