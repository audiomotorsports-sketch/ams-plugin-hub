## Westchester Tesla tint questions

### Do you tint the glass roof on a Model Y?

We can tint the glass roof if you want it, though most Westchester owners choose ceramic film on the doors and rear glass first, because that is where the heat and glare hit the driver. The roof already has factory tinting. Ask when you book and we will walk you through both options on your car.

### How dark can I legally go on a Tesla in California?

California requires the front door windows to let in at least 70 percent of light, so we use a light ceramic film up front that still cuts heat. Rear doors and rear glass can go darker. We will show you the shades side by side at the shop and pick one that looks right and passes.

### Will ceramic window tint affect my phone key or Supercharging?

No. Ceramic film is metal-free, so it does not block the Bluetooth signal your phone key uses or any cellular or GPS signal. Charging is unaffected. That is one reason we recommend ceramic over older metallic films for Teslas and for any car with a lot of electronics.

### Can I get dash camera installation on my Tesla too?

Yes, although many Tesla owners rely on the built-in cameras. If you want a dedicated dash cam with its own storage and parking mode, we hardwire it cleanly. For other cars from Westchester, a front-and-rear dash cam is a straightforward install we quote at the shop.
