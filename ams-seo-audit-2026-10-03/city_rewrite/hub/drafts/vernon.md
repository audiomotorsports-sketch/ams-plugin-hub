## Alarm questions from Vernon

### Why drive a work truck from Vernon to Carson for Viper alarm installation?

Because an alarm is only as good as its wiring. We hide the module, solder every connection and tie in the starter interrupt so the truck will not crank even if the siren is cut. The drive from Vernon is about 12.1 miles and the install is usually done the same day.

### Can one GPS tracker installation cover a truck that several employees drive?

Yes. The tracker reports where the vehicle is and when it moves, regardless of who is driving. It is hardwired, hidden and draws almost nothing while parked. For a small fleet, we can schedule several trucks across one or two days so the business is not without them all at once.

### Does remote start installation work on a diesel pickup?

Yes, with the right bypass and a glow plug delay set in the Compustar unit, so the engine waits for the plugs before cranking. The truck runs for a set time and shuts off if no one gets in. Text us the truck year, make and model and we will confirm the bypass module.

### How does car alarm installation protect against a thief at the fuse box?

The alarm module and its power feed are placed away from the fuse box and the wiring is soldered into the factory harness rather than plugged into an accessible spot. A starter interrupt stops the engine from cranking. Pulling a fuse does not disable it.

### Can you tint the cab and add a dash camera in the same appointment?

Yes. Solar Ceramic window tint on the doors and rear glass keeps the cab cooler, and dash camera installation runs in parallel. One drop-off, one pickup. Mention both when you book so we block enough bay time and have the film ready.
