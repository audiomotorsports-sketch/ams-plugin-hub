## CarPlay questions from Newport Beach

### Will a Newport Beach driver notice the difference in a professional CarPlay install?

Yes, every day. The screen sits flush in a kit made for the vehicle, the steering wheel buttons and backup camera keep working, and nothing behind the dash is spliced loosely. A unit installed that way works for years. The drive from Newport Beach is about 24.1 miles.

### Can you install CarPlay in a car that has a premium factory sound system?

Usually, yes. Vehicles with a factory amplifier need an interface that keeps the amplifier and its speaker channels working with the new head unit. We check your year, make and model before booking and tell you exactly which functions carry over.

### Does Android Auto installation cost more than CarPlay?

No. The same head unit supports both Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, so the price is the same and a household with both kinds of phones shares one screen. The quote depends on the vehicle, the dash kit and the interface, not on which phone you carry.

### Is Tesla window tint available during a trip from Newport Beach?

Yes. Tesla window tint is a regular service: ceramic film shrunk by hand to the one-piece rear glass and trimmed on the frameless doors so it clears the seal. Book it on its own or with a dash camera installation, and plan to leave the car for most of the day.

### Can dash camera installation be combined with a CarPlay appointment?

Yes. While the head unit is being wired, we hardwire the dash camera to a fuse tap with parking mode and hide the cable along the headliner and pillar. It adds a little time to the same visit and saves a second drive from Newport Beach.
