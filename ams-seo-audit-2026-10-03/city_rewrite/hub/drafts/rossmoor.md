## Rossmoor riders ask

### What are AMS pods and why do Harley riders want them?

AMS pods are lower-fairing speaker pods built by Audio MotorSports for Harley baggers. They put a second pair of speakers at knee level, pointed at the rider, so the music stays full at freeway speed instead of blowing away behind the fairing. From $649 a pair as of October 2026.

### Can I ride from Rossmoor for a bagger audio installation and ride home the same day?

Yes, if you book ahead so the pods and amplifier are ready. A pod-and-amp install on a bagger is usually a single day; ride in at 9:30 and the bike is normally done by late afternoon. Rossmoor is close enough that waiting at the shop is realistic.

### Do I need a new amplifier for AMS pods?

Most riders do. Factory Harley head units do not have the power to drive four speakers at road speed, so we add a compact amp sized for the pods and the fairing speakers. If you already run an aftermarket amp, we check its channels and power before adding anything.

### Will you tint my car as well as work on the bike?

Yes. Solar window tint is $249 installed and Solar Ceramic is $329 installed as of October 2026. Plenty of Rossmoor households book the bagger one day and the car the next, or bring both in and set up a drop-off for one of them.

### Is a remote start installation worth it for a car in Rossmoor?

For a car that sits outside, yes. A Compustar 9910 remote start, from $499 as of October 2026, lets you cool the cabin from the driveway or from your phone. Pair it with a Viper alarm at $269 installed and GPS tracking for a complete security and convenience package.
