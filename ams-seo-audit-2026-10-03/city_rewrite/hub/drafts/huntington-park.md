### Why does my amp cut out when I turn it up?

Usually the amp is going into protect because the gain is set too high, the ground is poor, or the power wire is too thin. Bring it to Carson and we will check the wiring, reset the gains and fix the ground so it holds at volume. Amplifier installation done right does not cut out.

### Can I keep my factory screen and still add a sub and speaker replacement?

Yes. A line-output converter or a DSP takes the signal from the factory head unit and feeds a new amplifier and subwoofer. Your dash looks stock and everything on the screen keeps working. This is the most common car audio upgrade we do for newer cars from Huntington Park.
