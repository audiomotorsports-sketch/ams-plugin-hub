## West Los Angeles questions

### What is involved in a bagger audio installation with AMS pods?

The lower fairings come off, the pods are fitted and the speakers wired through the fairing to a compact amplifier mounted on the bike. The amp is tied into the Harley's harness with weatherproof connections, then the whole system is tuned at idle and at revs. Riders go home the same day.

### Can AMS pods be added to a bike that already has an aftermarket fairing system?

Usually. We check the existing amplifier for spare channels and power; if it has them, the pods are wired in and the system retuned. If not, a second amp or a larger one is added. Bring the bike by or text photos of the current setup for a straight answer.
