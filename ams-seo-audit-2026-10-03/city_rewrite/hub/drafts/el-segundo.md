### What are AMS pods and why put them on a Harley bagger?

AMS pods are lower fairing speaker enclosures Audio MotorSports builds and installs, from 649 dollars a pair as of October 2026. They put a speaker low and close to the rider, so you hear music at highway speed instead of losing it to wind. They bolt onto Harley lower fairings and run off a proper amplifier.
