## What Montclair drivers bring us

**Subwoofer installation and amplifier installation.** Power wire fused at the battery, ground sanded to bare metal, the box strapped so it cannot slide, and the gains set in the car so the amp is loud without clipping.

### Why drive from Montclair instead of having a stereo put in closer to home?

Because a stereo is only as good as its wiring. A receiver on a plug-in harness with the camera and wheel buttons kept, an amp on a proper fused lead and a clean ground, and a tune done in the car is what you are driving for. Done once, done right, and never redone.

### Will a new head unit keep my backup camera and steering wheel buttons?

Yes. The interface module for your make carries the camera, the wheel controls and the factory chimes over to the new unit, and we confirm each one works before you leave. Send the year, make and model and we tell you which units fit and what they keep.

### What does a subwoofer installation actually include?

The amplifier, the fused power run from the battery, a ground on bare metal, signal from the head unit or a line-out converter on the factory radio, the box secured in the trunk, and gain and crossover set in the car. The Dual Kicker Comp box is $249 box only as of October 2026; the install is quoted on the vehicle.

### Can Montclair drivers get the stereo and window tint done in one day?

Yes, and it is the smart way to use a trip that long. Book both and the bay is yours for the day: the audio goes in first, the Solar or Solar Ceramic film last, so nobody touches the glass while it cures, and you drive home with both finished.

### Do you tune the system before I drive home?

Every time. Gains, crossovers and balance are set with the car running and the doors closed, then played at the volume you actually use. If a factory amp or a dead speaker is holding the system back, you hear about it before any more money is spent.
