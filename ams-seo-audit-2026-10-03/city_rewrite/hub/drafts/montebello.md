### Why have a Viper alarm installed at a shop instead of doing it at home?

Because the install is the security. An alarm wired into easy-to-reach spots can be pulled in a minute; one with the module hidden and the wiring soldered into the factory loom cannot. Montebello drivers get the Viper 3108V, $269 installed as of October 2026, done once and done right.

### Can a Montebello driver get an alarm and remote start in one appointment?

Yes. Viper alarm installation and Compustar remote start installation share the same wiring job, so doing both at once saves a second trip of 15.3 miles. Plan on most of a day, drop the car at 9:30, and we text when it is ready. Remote start is from $499 as of October 2026.

### Does a GPS tracker installation drain my battery?

No. The tracker draws very little current and is wired to a circuit chosen for your vehicle, so it stays connected without flattening the battery on a car that sits for a week. GPS tracker installation is quoted at the shop by vehicle and service plan.

### Will remote start work with my factory key fob?

Yes. Compustar installation keeps your factory fob for lock and unlock and adds a long-range remote or a phone app for starting. On push-button cars the bypass module handles the start without a spare key, and the factory immobilizer stays active.

### Is car window tint done the same day as an alarm?

Yes, when you book both. The tint goes on while the alarm is wired, so the car leaves with Solar Ceramic on the glass and the Viper armed. Solar is $249 installed and Solar Ceramic is $329 installed as of October 2026; Tesla window tint is quoted at the shop.
