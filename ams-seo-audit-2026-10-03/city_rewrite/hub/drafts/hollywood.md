### What is the legal tint limit for front windows in Los Angeles County?

The rule is statewide: front side windows must pass at least 70 percent of visible light, and the windshield can only carry film above the AS-1 line. Rear doors and the back glass have no limit. We stock shades that meet the front rule and still cut glare on the drive home.

No. Ceramic film blocks heat through the ceramic layer rather than the color, so you can pick a lighter shade and still get the cooling. Many Hollywood drivers choose a light front and a darker rear for privacy, and Solar Ceramic is $329 installed as of October 2026.
