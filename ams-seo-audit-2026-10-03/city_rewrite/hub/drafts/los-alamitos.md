### What does speaker replacement change in a factory system?

Factory speakers are usually paper cones on thin magnets. Replacing them with proper coaxial or component speakers on adapter rings gives clearer vocals and tighter bass on the same head unit. If you want more volume without distortion, amplifier installation is the next step after the speakers.

### Can you do subwoofer installation in a small hatchback?

Yes. A shallow-mount subwoofer in a compact enclosure or a Dual Kicker Comp box fits most hatchbacks while leaving room for groceries. The amplifier mounts under a seat or against the enclosure. We size the setup to your cargo space, not the other way around.

### Will Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation keep my backup camera working?

In most vehicles, yes. The new head unit has a camera input and we use a retention harness or interface module so the factory camera still displays when you shift into reverse. Send us the year, make and model and we confirm it before you drive over.
