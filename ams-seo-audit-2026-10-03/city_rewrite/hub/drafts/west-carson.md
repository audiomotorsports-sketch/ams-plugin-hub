## What West Carson drivers bring us

**Car window tint and ceramic window tint.** Film is hand-cut on the glass, not pulled from a pre-cut roll, so the edges sit to the gasket and nothing peels at the top of the door.

**Car stereo installation.** Head unit installation on Hondas, Toyotas and work trucks, wired through a proper harness so the factory camera and steering wheel buttons keep working and no dash is drilled.

**Subwoofer installation and amplifier installation.** Power run from the battery with a fused cable, ground sanded clean, enclosure strapped down so nothing rattles over bumps.

**Viper alarm installation and remote start installation.** The alarm module is hidden and the wiring is soldered, so it cannot be defeated by pulling a fuse under the hood.

**Dash camera installation and GPS tracker installation.** Hardwired, cable tucked in the headliner, nothing dangling from the mirror.

### How long does car window tint take for a sedan or small SUV?

Plan on a few hours for Solar or Solar Ceramic on four doors plus the rear window. The film is cut to the glass, cleaned, laid and squeegeed, then the car sits while it sets. Done once, done right, then you wait on site or come back for it later that day.

### Can you add Apple CarPlay to my older car without changing the dash look?

Usually, yes. A CarPlay and Android Auto head unit with a matching dash kit keeps the factory appearance while giving you maps, calls and music on the screen. Bring the year, make and model and we will tell you which unit keeps your camera and wheel buttons working.

### Is remote start installation worth it for a short commute from West Carson?

It is if your car sits in the sun. A Compustar remote start lets you cool the cabin from the house before you walk out, which matters more in a parked-outside car than in a garaged one. Pair it with an alarm and the same fob does both.

### Do you do Tesla window tint and Harley bagger audio too?

Yes to both. Tesla window tint is one of our regular jobs, including the large rear glass. Harley bagger audio uses our own AMS pods, built for the fairing and lower vents on touring bikes. Both are booked the same way as any other appointment: call or text first.
