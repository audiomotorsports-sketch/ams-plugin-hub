## Alarm and remote start questions from Midway City

### Can a thief bypass a Viper alarm by pulling a fuse?

Not the way we install it. The alarm has its own fused power lead at the battery side and a starter interrupt, so pulling an interior fuse does not silence it or let the engine start. Wires are hidden and tied into the factory loom rather than left hanging under the dash.

### Does Compustar remote start installation void my factory warranty?

We cannot speak for your dealer's policy, but the install uses a bypass module designed for your key system and does not cut the factory harness in a way that cannot be undone. Bring your questions when you book and we will walk through exactly what gets connected on your car.

### Can I add GPS tracker installation to the same appointment as the alarm?

Yes, and it is the most common pairing. The tracker is hardwired and hidden in a spot that is not the diagnostic port, with location and movement alerts on your phone. Booking both at once saves a second trip from Midway City and the install time overlaps.

### Will the shock sensor trigger from wind or a passing truck?

Not when it is adjusted on your vehicle. We set the sensitivity in the bay so a hit on the glass or a door sets it off while road vibration and wind do not. If it ever starts false-alarming, bring it back and we will readjust it at no charge for the adjustment.
