## Questions we get from Hermosa Beach

### What is the best speaker replacement for a Jeep Wrangler that runs with the top off?

Start with the speakers and an amplifier, not the head unit. Factory Jeep speakers give up first in open air, so a marine-rated speaker set and a compact amp make the biggest difference. Add a subwoofer in the rear if you want low end, and keep the Jeep's factory screen if it already has CarPlay.

### Can I get a car stereo installation done the same day if I come from Hermosa Beach?

For a speaker-and-amp job or a head unit swap, yes, when you book ahead so the parts are pulled. Drop the vehicle at 9:30 and it is usually ready by afternoon. Larger subwoofer and amplifier builds may take a full day, and we will tell you when you book.

### Does Audio MotorSports tune the system after the install?

Yes. Every car stereo installation gets gain, crossover and level settings adjusted in the bay with the doors shut, so it sounds right at normal volume and does not clip at high volume. A system that is installed and not tuned is half a job.

### How much is window tint for a car coming from Hermosa Beach?

Solar window tint is $249 installed and Solar Ceramic window tint is $329 installed as of October 2026, for the side glass plus the rear window on a standard car. Tesla window tint and oversized SUVs are quoted at the shop. Combine tint with an audio install and the car makes one trip.

### Will a Viper alarm installation work on a Jeep with a soft top?

Yes. We set the Viper 3108V, $269 installed as of October 2026, with the shock sensor tuned for a soft top so wind does not trigger it, and add a tilt sensor if wheels and tires are the worry. GPS tracking can be added to the same install.
