## Downey tint questions

### Why pick ceramic over regular tint for a Downey commute?

Ceramic film rejects a much larger share of infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler on a car parked outside all day and the air conditioning works less. Regular Solar film handles glare and privacy well but does less for heat. For a daily commuter, ceramic is the better long-term choice.

### How far is Carson from Downey and is the drive worth it for tint?

The shop is about 10.9 miles from Downey, roughly twenty-two to twenty-six minutes when traffic is normal. It is worth it because the film is cut to your glass by hand and installed by the same crew every time. Drop the car off or wait; most tint jobs finish the same day.

### Can tinted windows be removed later if I sell the car?

Yes. Old or bubbling film can be stripped and the glass cleaned, and new film applied if you want. If you are leasing, choosing a legal shade on the front doors now avoids a removal at turn-in. We can advise on shade when you book.

### Do you do remote start installation on trucks from Downey?

Yes, trucks are a big part of our remote start work. The Compustar 9910 starts the engine from the fob so the cab cools down, and it can be paired with a Viper alarm on one remote. Give us the year, make and model and we confirm the bypass module needed.

### Can you do subwoofer installation without replacing my factory stereo?

Yes. A line output converter takes the signal from the factory head unit and feeds an amplifier and subwoofer, so the dash stays original. Most Downey customers start with the Dual Kicker Comp box. We quote the amplifier and wiring at the shop after seeing the vehicle.
