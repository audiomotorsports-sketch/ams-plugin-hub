### Can you alarm the bed and cargo doors on a Garden Grove work truck?

Yes. Standard alarms only watch the cab doors, so we add pin switches or sensors on the cargo doors, tailgate and bed cover, wired back to the alarm so any of them sets it off. Toolboxes with their own lids can be covered too if you tell us what is mounted.

### Do you install GPS trackers on more than one truck at a time?

We do, and it is easier to schedule a fleet in a row than one at a time. Each tracker is hardwired the same way, named in the app, and tested before the truck leaves. Tell us how many vehicles and their models when you call and we will block the bay time.

### What stops a thief from pulling the fuse to silence the alarm?

The siren has its own battery backup so pulling power does not quiet it, and the starter kill relay is placed away from the fuse panel, so even a silent truck will not start. An alarm that lives on the fuse box is one most people can defeat; we build ours to not live there.

### Will a remote start work on a diesel or a fleet-spec truck?

Many diesel and fleet trucks are supported with the right bypass and a glow-plug delay set in the remote start. We confirm your exact year, engine and ignition type when you book. Compustar 9910 remote start installs start from $499 as of October 2026 on supported vehicles.

### How far is Garden Grove from the shop and is the trip worth it for a work vehicle?

About 18.9 miles, under an hour most of the day. It is worth it because a work vehicle that gets stolen takes your week with it, and a tracker plus a properly hidden starter kill is the most reliable way to keep it from leaving. Book early and the truck is back before the next job.
