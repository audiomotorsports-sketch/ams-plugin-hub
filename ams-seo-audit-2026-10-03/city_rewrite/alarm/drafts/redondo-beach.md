## Redondo Beach questions

### What kind of alarm makes sense for a car parked on the street in Redondo Beach?

A two-stage shock sensor alarm with a hidden siren and an ignition kill is the sensible baseline. The sensor is tuned on the car so street noise does not trigger it, and the ignition interrupt means the car does not start even if a thief gets inside.

### Can you install a car alarm without it going off every time a truck passes?

Yes. Sensitivity is set on the vehicle, not left at the factory default, and we test it by hand before you leave. A properly tuned Viper alarm ignores vibration from traffic and reacts to a strike on glass or a lift on the body.

### How long does the drive from Redondo Beach to Carson take?

Roughly 14 to 17 minutes outside rush hour for the 7.2 miles. Book first so a bay is ready, then drop the car or stay while the alarm is installed. Most basic alarm jobs are finished the same day.

### Is a GPS tracker worth adding to an alarm?

For a street-parked car, yes. The alarm makes noise and stops the ignition; the tracker tells you where the car is if the thief tows it. We hide the tracker on its own fused constant feed so it keeps reporting after the battery is reconnected.
