### Does the Viper alarm work on a newer car with a factory security system?

Yes. The factory system handles the key and immobilizer; the Viper adds a proper siren, sensors that react to impact and tilt, and a separate starter kill. The two are integrated so the factory fob still works as usual. On data-bus vehicles we add the right interface module.

### Can you install the alarm without drilling or cutting factory trim?
