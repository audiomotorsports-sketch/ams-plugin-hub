### Why should a Los Angeles driver hardwire a dash camera rather than plug it in?

Street parking is where cars get hit in Los Angeles, and a plug-in camera is off the moment the ignition is. Hardwiring gives you parking mode that wakes on impact, a clean windshield with no cord, and a camera that cannot be unplugged by accident when someone borrows the car.

### Will a hardwired dash camera drain my battery overnight?

Not when it is installed with a hardwire kit that has a voltage cutoff. We set the threshold so the camera shuts itself off before the battery drops below a safe starting level. If you park for a week at a time, tell us and we will pick a setting and a camera that suit that pattern.

### Can you install a rear camera too, or only a front one?

Both. The rear unit mounts high on the back glass and its cable runs forward under the headliner to the front camera, which is a longer job on SUVs and hatchbacks because of the tailgate loom. Two channels mean a rear-end hit is on video as clearly as a front one.

### How long does a car alarm installation take in your Carson shop?

A Viper 3108V alarm installation at $269 installed as of October 2026 is normally a half-day job, longer if the vehicle needs a data interface or you add sensors. Book a morning slot and it is usually ready by afternoon. We do not rush the wiring to hit a clock, because the wiring is the product.

### Is it realistic to drive from Los Angeles to Carson for security work?

Yes, and plenty of people do. The reason is simple: the install decides whether the system can be bypassed, not the box it came in. A half-hour drive to a shop that does this every day beats a quick job that has to be pulled out and redone later.
