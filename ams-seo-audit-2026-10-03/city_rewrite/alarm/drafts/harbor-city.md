# GPS Tracker and Car Alarm Installation for Harbor City Drivers

LA Car Alarm is the security side of Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, about 2.8 miles from Harbor City. We install GPS trackers, car alarms, remote start and dash cameras in our own bay, with the wiring hidden and the factory trim put back the way it came. Harbor City is one of the shortest trips any of our customers make.

## What Harbor City drivers bring us

**GPS tracker installation.** A tracker only helps if a thief cannot find it in thirty seconds. We tuck the module behind factory panels, power it from a circuit that stays live with the key out, and test the locate function on your phone before the car leaves.

**Car alarm installation.** A Viper or Compustar alarm is wired so the siren and immobilizer cannot be killed by pulling a fuse under the dash. Shock sensitivity gets set on your car in our lot, not left at a bench default.

**Remote start installation.** Compustar remote start on a push-button or key vehicle, integrated with the factory security so the doors stay secured while it idles. No cut harnesses; everything plugs in or is soldered and taped.

**Dash camera installation.** Front and rear cameras hardwired to a fused circuit, cable run inside the headliner and A-pillar, parking mode set so it records bumps while you are away.

We also do window tint, car stereo and CarPlay installation, and Harley bagger audio under the same roof if you want to combine trips.

### Which GPS tracker do you install for a Harbor City daily driver?

We quote the tracker on your vehicle and how you plan to use it, whether that is simple locate-and-recover or a subscription with alerts on your phone. The install is the same standard either way: hidden placement, constant power, antenna positioned so it reports reliably, and a live test before you pay.

### How long does a car alarm installation take at the Carson shop?

Most Viper or Compustar alarm installs are finished in a few hours, which is why Harbor City customers often wait at the shop. Vehicles with complicated factory electronics or an added immobilizer can take longer, so Nick will give you a realistic window when you book with the year, make and model.

### Can you add remote start to a car that already has an alarm?

Usually yes. If the existing alarm is a Compustar or Viper unit in good shape, remote start can often be added to it. If it is an older or unknown system, we will tell you honestly whether it is better to replace it than to build on top of it.

### Will a dash camera drain my battery while parked overnight?

Not when it is hardwired properly. We use a fused kit with a low-voltage cutoff so parking mode shuts down before the battery gets too low to start the car. The camera still captures hits and door dings in the lot, but it stops recording when it should.

### Why drive to Carson instead of ordering a tracker online and installing it myself?

A plug-in tracker in the OBD port is the first thing a thief pulls. Having it installed at a shop that does this every day means it is hidden, wired to constant power and verified on your phone. The difference shows up the one night it matters.
