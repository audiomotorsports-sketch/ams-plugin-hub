### Does remote start installation work on a diesel pickup?

### How does the alarm stop a thief who knows where the fuse box is?

The module is not there. It is deep behind the dash with its own fused feed, the siren has protected power, and the starter interrupt is in the ignition circuit. A thief who pulls every fuse still cannot crank the engine. That is the difference between a kit and a shop install.
