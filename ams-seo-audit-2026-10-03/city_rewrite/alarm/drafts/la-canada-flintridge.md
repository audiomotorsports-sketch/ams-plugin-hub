### What does a work truck alarm cover that a regular car alarm does not?

Every opening that holds tools. A basic alarm watches the cab doors and hood. We add triggers on the cargo doors, the tailgate, a bed cover or camper shell, and a ladder rack lock if you have one, so a thief lifting the shell sets the siren off the same as a cab door would.

### Can La Canada Flintridge fleet owners book several trucks on one day?

Yes, and it is the efficient way to do it. Give us the count, the models and what each truck needs when you call and we will block the bay. Each tracker is named in the app and tested before the truck leaves, so the whole fleet is live by evening.

### Will the alarm siren be loud enough on a job site?

Yes. The siren is placed high and away from exhaust heat so it carries, and it has its own backup battery so cutting the truck's power does not silence it. The starter kill relay is placed separately, so a quiet truck is still a truck that will not start.

### Does remote start work on a diesel work truck?

Many diesel trucks are supported with the right bypass and a programmed glow-plug wait before crank. Manual transmissions are discussed case by case.
