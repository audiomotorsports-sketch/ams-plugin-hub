### How do you protect the toolbox on a Maywood contractor truck?

A pin switch or sensor on the toolbox lid ties into the Viper alarm, so lifting it sounds the siren. The same system covers the cab doors, the hood and a camper shell, and a tilt sensor can be added for wheels and trailers.

### Can a thief just pull a fuse and quiet the truck alarm?

Not on an install done here. The module is hidden, the siren is backed up, and the starter interrupt sits on a circuit that is not obvious, so pulling the first fuse does nothing. That labor is the difference between a deterrent and a decoration.

### Will a tracker drain the battery on a truck that sits over the weekend?

A properly wired tracker draws very little at rest and sleeps between check-ins. We use the right fuse rating and confirm the draw before the truck leaves, so Monday morning starts like any other.

### How long is my work truck tied up for an alarm and tracker?

Most of a working day for both, since each goes behind the dash and panels. Many Maywood owners book a Saturday so the truck is ready for Monday. An alarm by itself is a few hours.
