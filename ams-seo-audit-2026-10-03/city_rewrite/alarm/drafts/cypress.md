### Does a hardwired dash camera installation keep recording when I park in Cypress?

Yes. Parking mode is the whole point of hardwiring. The camera wakes on impact or motion, records the event, and the cutoff module shuts it down before the battery gets too low to start the car. A plug-in cigarette-lighter camera cannot do that.

### Will the rear camera cable be visible inside the car?

No. It runs inside the headliner, down the pillar trim and through the hatch grommet to the rear glass. When we are done, the only visible parts are the two camera bodies. That is what separates an installation from a camera left dangling.
