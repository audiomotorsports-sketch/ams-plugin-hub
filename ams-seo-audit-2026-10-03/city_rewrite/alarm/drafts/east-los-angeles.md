### Which East Los Angeles cars can take Compustar remote start installation?

Most gas vehicles from the last fifteen years, push-button or key, and many hybrids and manuals with the right safety sequence. The bypass module is specific to the make and year, so text us the vehicle before you come and we will tell you plainly whether it is supported.

### Does the car stay secured while it is running on remote start?

Yes. The doors stay shut, the steering stays immobilized, and if anyone presses the brake without the key present the engine shuts off. Remote start is a comfort feature that does not loosen the security; with a Viper alarm added, the siren is still armed while it idles.

### Why solder the alarm wiring instead of using clip-on taps?

Because taps loosen, corrode and cause the ghost problems that get alarms blamed for everything. A soldered, taped joint holds for the life of the car. It takes longer, and that is part of what the drive to Carson is for. Google 4.8 (654 reviews) and Yelp 4.7 (777 reviews), as of October 2026.
