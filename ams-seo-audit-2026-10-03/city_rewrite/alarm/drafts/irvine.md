### What is the best alarm setup for a car parked in an Irvine apartment lot?

A Viper alarm with a two-stage shock sensor and a tilt sensor, so a window strike or a jack under the corner both trigger it, plus a hidden starter kill. For a car that is a known target, add a hardwired GPS tracker. The alarm stops the quick attempt; the tracker handles the one that succeeds.

### Will a tilt sensor stop my wheels from being stolen?

It will make the attempt loud. A tilt sensor triggers the siren when the car is lifted even slightly, which is the moment a jack goes under it. Combined with a siren that keeps sounding on its own battery, most wheel thieves move on to a quieter car.

### Can the shock sensor tell a bump from a break-in?

Yes, when it is adjusted on your car rather than left at the default. A two-stage sensor gives a warning chirp on a light knock and a full alarm on a hard hit. We set both levels in the lot with you, then show you how to adjust if your parking spot changes.
