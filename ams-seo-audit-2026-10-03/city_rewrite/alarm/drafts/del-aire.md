### Can a thief defeat the alarm by pulling a fuse?

Not the way we install it. The module is hidden away from the fuse panel, the siren has its own protected feed, and the starter interrupt means the engine will not crank even if the siren is somehow silenced. An alarm that is only plugged into the fuse box is the kind we end up redoing.

Yes. Compustar makes bypass modules for most push-to-start vehicles, and the install keeps your factory fob working exactly as before. The car runs for a set time, stays secured with the doors shut, and shuts down if someone tries to drive it without the key present.
