### How long does Compustar remote start installation take for a Lennox customer?

Plan on a half day. The bypass has to be programmed, the connections soldered, and the system tested from the fob and from the app before you leave. Vehicles with push-button start or a data-bus interface can take a little longer. Book a morning appointment and it is normally ready that afternoon.

### Do I need two fobs after remote start, or can it use my factory key?

Many Compustar systems can be set up to start the car from your factory fob by pressing lock three times, which means no extra remote on your keychain. If you want longer range or phone control, we add a Compustar remote or the app module instead. We will go over the options for your vehicle.

### Will the remote start turn on the air conditioning or the heat?

It brings on whatever the climate control was set to when you shut the car off, so leave the AC running when you park and the cabin will be cooling by the time you reach it. On cars with heated seats or defrost tied to the climate system, those come on too.

### Is it a long trip from Lennox for a $269 Viper alarm?

No, it is one of the shortest drives on this site. The Viper 3108V alarm is $269 installed as of October 2026, and that labor is where the protection comes from. A short drive to have it placed and wired properly beats a bargain install that leaves the module sitting on the fuse box.

### Can you add remote start to a car that already has an aftermarket alarm?

Often, yes. If the existing alarm is a brand that accepts a remote start add-on, we can integrate it; if it is a bargain unit with poor wiring, we will tell you and suggest replacing it. Either way, we look at what is under the dash before quoting, not after.
