### What does a Lynwood driver gain from hardwiring a dash camera?

Parking mode, a clean cabin and a safe battery. On the lighter socket the camera sleeps when the car does. Hardwired through a fused tap it records the hit while you are inside the store, and the cutoff shuts it down before the battery gets low.

### Do you hide the cable completely?

Yes. The front cable runs inside the headliner and down the A-pillar trim to the fuse box; the rear cable follows the headliner to the back glass. Nothing hangs across the dash or the windshield.

### Can the camera and a Viper alarm be installed the same day?

They can, and doing both at once saves a second visit. The alarm wiring and the camera cable go in while the trim is off, and the Viper 3108V is $269 installed, as of October 2026, on top of the camera.

### What if my car sits for days at a time between drives?

We set a conservative cutoff voltage and explain the parking-mode options, so a car that sits all week still starts. Timed parking mode or a lower cutoff is the right call for a vehicle that is rarely driven.

A front camera is fast; front and rear takes a little longer. Most Lynwood customers wait at the shop, or drop the car in the morning and pick it up the same day. The app is paired with you before you leave.
