### If my car is stolen from La Habra Heights, how does the tracker help get it back?

The tracker reports its position on a map in your phone app and sends an alert the moment the car moves outside the window you set. You give that live location to the police rather than guessing. Recovery depends on a tracker that is still powered and still hidden, which is why we hardwire it and do not put it on the OBD port.

### Will a hidden GPS tracker still work if the car is on a flatbed?

Yes. The tracker runs on the car's constant power and has its own backup, so it does not care whether the engine is running. A car being towed reports its location the same way a car being driven does. Cellular coverage is the only limit, and that is rarely a problem in the area.

### Can thieves use a signal jammer to beat the tracker?

A jammer can block reports while it is switched on, but most are only used during the drive, and the tracker stores and sends its history once the signal returns. Pairing the tracker with a starter kill means the car usually has to be towed, which is slower and more visible.

For a car parked in a garage, the Viper 3108V at $269 installed as of October 2026 is a solid defense by itself. For a car that lives in a driveway or on the street, or one that is a known target model, we recommend adding the tracker, because an alarm deters and a tracker recovers.
