### Does salt air affect remote start or alarm wiring?

Coastal air can corrode poor connections, so we solder or use sealed connectors where appropriate and keep modules inside the cabin. Clean, protected wiring lasts longer. We explain what we do when you pick up the car. Dry, protected connections are the difference you will notice in a few years.

### Can I park on a Venice street with a Viper alarm?

Yes, and that is a common reason to install one. The siren and shock sensor discourage break-ins. We tune the sensitivity so a passing bus does not trigger it. Pricing is $269 installed as of October 2026. Visible alarm lights add a little extra deterrence.

### How does a dash camera help with street scrapes?

Parking mode records the impact and the plate when it is in view, which helps with claims. We hardwire it with a battery cutoff so the car still starts. You review clips on an app or card. A clear clip usually saves a lot of arguing.

### Will remote start work on a manual transmission?

Some manual cars are supported with a specific safety sequence, but not all. Send the year, make and model and we will tell you honestly before you drive over. We do not install it where it would be unsafe. Safety always comes before convenience on these cars.
