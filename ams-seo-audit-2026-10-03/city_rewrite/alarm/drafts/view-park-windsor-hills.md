### Can security be installed on a restored classic without leaving marks?

Yes, and that is the standard we hold for a car kept to that level. Modules tuck behind existing panels, wiring follows factory looms, and no holes are drilled in the dash or firewall. If you sell the car, everything comes out and the interior looks exactly as it did before.

### Will an immobilizer throw fault codes on a newer Lexus or Mercedes?

Not when it is wired through the proper interfaces, which is how we do it. The car's own electronics never see anything unusual, the dealer scan stays clean, and the key still works as before. The only difference is that a cloned signal cannot start the engine.

### Is a siren enough on a quiet View Park hillside?

On its own, no. Long driveways, mature planting and no passing cars mean nobody hears it. We pair the siren with a phone-link alert and, on a weekend car, a GPS tracker, so you know the moment something happens and where the car went afterward.

### What does a tilt sensor protect against?

Wheels and jacking. A tilt sensor fires when the car's angle changes, which is what happens when someone lifts a corner to take the wheels or tows it onto a flatbed. A door-trigger alarm never sees either event, which is why the sensor matters on a valuable car.
