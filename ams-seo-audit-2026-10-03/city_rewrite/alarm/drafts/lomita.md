# Harley Bagger Amplifier Installation and Tuning for Lomita Riders

Lomita riders bring their Street Glides, Road Glides and Road Kings to AMS Pods at Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, Carson, roughly 3.7 miles away, for amplifier installation that is tuned on the bike itself. We pair the amp with AMS lower-fairing speaker pods and fairing speakers, fuse the power at the battery, and set the gains with the motor running.

## What Lomita riders bring us

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** An amp on a bagger has to survive heat, vibration and weather, so placement matters as much as wattage. We mount it where it can breathe, run fused power from the battery, and dial in gains and crossovers with the fairing buttoned up.

**AMS lower-fairing pods.** The lower pods are what give a bagger real midbass at the rider's knees. We install them square to the fairing, seal the speaker in, and route the wire inside the fairing so the finished bike looks factory.

**Replacing stock fairing speakers.** Wind beats the factory speakers to death. We swap in speakers built for an open-air bike and re-seal the fairing so there is no buzz at idle or at speed.

**Head unit installation.** If the stock radio stays, we make sure it feeds the amp a clean signal. If it goes, we fit a unit that works with the bar controls and is readable in sunlight.

### Why does amp tuning on the bike matter more than the amp itself?

Because the fairing, the pods and the rider's helmet all change what you hear. We set gains and crossover points with the engine running and the fairing closed, so the lower pods and fairing speakers blend at highway speed rather than sounding right only when the bike is parked.

### How far is the ride from Lomita to your Carson shop?

About 3.7 miles, which most riders cover in roughly eight minutes outside rush hour. Audio MotorSports is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson. You can drop the bike at opening and have it back the same day in most cases, or wait at the shop.

### Can a Lomita rider get pods and an amp done in one visit?

Usually, yes. Pods, amp and fairing speakers are one job when they go in together, and the wiring is cleaner that way because every run is planned at once. Book ahead so the lift is reserved for your bike, and we will tell you how long to expect.

### Where do you mount the amplifier on a Street Glide or Road Glide?

In a spot that keeps it out of direct exhaust heat and still lets it cool, with short, fused power from the battery and a solid ground. The exact location depends on the model and what bags or racks are on the bike, so we decide it with the bike in front of us.

### Will the amp drain the battery when the bike is parked?

Not when the remote turn-on is wired properly, which it is here. The amp powers up with the ignition and shuts down with it. We also check the charging system can carry the load, so you are not trading sound for a bike that will not start on a cold morning.
