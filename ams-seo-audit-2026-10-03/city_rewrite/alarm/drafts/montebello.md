### Which alarm sensors matter most for a Montebello car parked on the street?

A shock sensor tuned on the car, a hood pin and a starter kill are the core. Add a tilt sensor if wheels or catalytic converters are a worry, and a glass-break sensor if the car sits under a streetlight where a window smash is the likely entry.

### How do you keep the alarm from going off every time a truck passes?

We set the sensor sensitivity on your vehicle with the doors shut and test it with a hand on the glass, then show you how to adjust it. A properly calibrated Viper ignores traffic rumble and reacts to an actual hit on the body.

### Should I add a GPS tracker to the alarm for a street-parked car?

If the car is a model thieves go after, yes. The alarm stops the casual attempt; the tracker gets the car back if someone tows it or flatbeds it. Together they are the most complete answer for a car without a garage.
