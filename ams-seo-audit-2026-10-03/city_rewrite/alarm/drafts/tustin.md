### Do alarms and trackers work together?

They complement each other. The alarm sounds and may stop the engine from cranking, while the tracker follows the car if it is towed or driven away. We install both in one visit when asked and set up the phone app together.

### How do I arm and disarm the Viper alarm?

With the remote, and in many cars with the factory key fob too, when it is wired that way. We show you each button, the valet switch and how to silence the siren. A short walk-through at pickup covers everything.

### What if my Tustin car already has a factory alarm?

A Viper adds extra protection, such as a stronger siren, shock sensor and a starter interrupt. We integrate it without cutting factory harnesses. Tell us the make and model and we will quote what makes sense. Both systems then work side by side without conflict.

### Does a GPS tracker need a subscription?

Most do, because the unit uses a cellular data connection. We explain the cost of the plan before you buy, and the unit and install are quoted at the shop. Ask about a payment plan if the total matters. Plans are usually small compared with the value of the car.
