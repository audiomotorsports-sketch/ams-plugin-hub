# Car Alarm Installation for San Pedro Drivers Who Park on the Street

When the car sleeps at the curb, the alarm has to do real work. LA Car Alarm at Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, is about 6.5 miles from San Pedro and installs Viper and Compustar alarms, GPS trackers, dash cameras and remote start for drivers who do not have a garage to hide behind.

## What San Pedro drivers bring us

**Car alarm installation for street parking.** The shock sensor is set for your street, so it fires on a window hit and not on a passing truck. The siren goes where it cannot be reached by hand, and the starter kill is wired so pulling a fuse does not free the car.

**GPS tracker installation.** For a street-parked car, a hidden tracker is the difference between a police report and a recovered vehicle. We hardwire it and check the locate on your phone before you leave.

**Dash camera installation.** Parking mode on a hardwired camera records the hit-and-run or the mirror that got clipped overnight. Cable hidden in the trim, fused circuit, low-voltage cutoff.

**Remote start installation.** Compustar installation that keeps the doors secured while the car runs, so remote start does not become a free ride for someone walking by.

Window tint, subwoofer installation and CarPlay head units are available at the same shop if you want to combine the visit.

### What alarm setup makes sense for a car parked on a San Pedro street?

A Viper alarm with a two-stage shock sensor, a hidden siren and a starter kill covers most street-parked cars. If the vehicle is a frequent theft target or you want to be able to find it, we add a GPS tracker. We talk through your parking situation before recommending anything.

### Will the alarm annoy my neighbors with false triggers?

Not if it is set up correctly. We calibrate the sensor on your car with the doors closed, then show you how to adjust it. A well-installed alarm stays quiet for wind and traffic and only sounds when the car is actually hit, tilted or opened.

### Can a thief disable the alarm from under the hood or the fuse box?

That is exactly what a careful install prevents. The siren is backed up, the module is hidden, and the starter interrupt is wired so cutting the obvious fuse or wire does not start the car. This is why the labor matters more than the box.

### How much is a Viper alarm installed at the Carson shop?

The Viper 3108V is $269 installed, as of October 2026. That covers the unit, the labor, the sensor calibration and a walk-through of the remote. Add-ons like a tracker, extra sensors or an immobilizer are quoted on your vehicle.

### Do you do remote start installs on San Pedro commuter cars with push-button start?

Yes. Compustar remote start works with most push-button vehicles using a bypass module matched to your car. The factory key stays the same, the doors stay secured while it idles, and the system shuts down if someone tries to drive away without the key.
