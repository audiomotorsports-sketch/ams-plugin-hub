### Can a dash camera record while my Signal Hill car is parked?

Yes, when it is wired for parking mode. We connect it to a constant fuse with a battery cutoff, and the camera wakes on bumps or motion. Power stays protected, and footage is saved to the card for you to review later.

### Which dash camera setup do you recommend for a daily driver?

A front and rear pair covers most situations, including a rear-end hit at a light. We choose a model based on your budget and glass shape, then hide the wires. Pricing is quoted at the shop and depends on the car.

### Will a hardwired camera drain my battery?

Not if the install includes a voltage cutoff, which we always add. The camera shuts down before the battery gets low. If your battery is old, we may suggest testing it first so you are not stranded in the morning.

### Do the camera and the alarm talk to each other?

They are separate devices, but we place them so each does its job. The alarm sounds and flashes; the camera records. Some cameras also trigger on impact. Ask us about combining it with a tracker for the full picture. Each one still works if the other fails.

### Why not just mount a camera myself in Signal Hill?

A stick-on cable with a cigarette-lighter plug works but dangles, blocks the socket and stops recording when the car is off. A proper hardwire job looks factory and keeps watching. That difference is why customers bring cars in. The result is safer for the dash and the battery as well.
