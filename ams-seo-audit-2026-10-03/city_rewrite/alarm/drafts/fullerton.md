### What alarm setup do you recommend for a car parked on a Fullerton street overnight?

A Viper alarm with a two-stage shock sensor, a tilt sensor if the wheels are worth stealing, and a starter kill, plus a hidden GPS tracker if the car is worth more than a weekend. The alarm scares off the casual attempt; the tracker and the kill handle the determined one.

### How do you set the shock sensor so it does not go off all night?

We adjust it on your vehicle with the doors shut and tap the glass and the quarter panel until the warning chirp and the full trigger land where they should. You get a quick lesson on the adjustment so if your parking spot changes, you can fine-tune it without a return trip.

### Does the alarm still work if someone cuts the battery cable?

A siren with its own backup battery keeps sounding even if main power is cut, and the starter kill relay stays open with no power at all, so the car is still immobile. That is the difference between an alarm that was installed and one that was simply plugged in.

### Is a GPS tracker visible in the car or on the OBD port?

Not the way we install it. Plug-in OBD trackers are the first thing a thief pulls. Ours are hardwired behind trim and powered from a circuit that is not obvious, so the car keeps reporting its location while it is being driven away. We will not describe the spot in writing.
