### How do I protect tools inside a work truck from Walnut Park break-ins?

Pair a Viper alarm with bed or tailgate triggers and a shock sensor, then add a hidden tracker for the worst case. The alarm scares off most attempts, and the tracker helps recover the truck if someone drives it away. Both are wired in at the shop in a single visit.

### Will a remote start installation drain my truck battery?

Not when it is wired correctly. A Compustar unit draws very little while idle, and the run time is set so the engine shuts off by itself. If the battery is already weak, we tell you before the install, not after a dead morning.

### Can the alarm cover a camper shell or toolbox?

Yes, a pickup with a shell or locking box can get an added trigger on that lid, wired back to the main alarm brain. Tell us what you have when you book and we will plan the wiring around it, so the cab and cargo area are covered together.

### Is a dash camera installation hard to hide in a pickup?

No. The camera mounts behind the mirror, the wire follows the pillar and headliner, and power comes from a fuse tap so nothing hangs from the cigarette lighter. A cab-facing lens can be added if the truck is shared by several drivers.
