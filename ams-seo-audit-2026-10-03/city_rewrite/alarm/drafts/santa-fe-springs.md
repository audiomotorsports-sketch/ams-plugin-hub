### Can you install trackers on several Santa Fe Springs work vehicles?

Yes. Book them in sequence and each one gets the same treatment: hidden placement, constant power and a locate test. Nick will plan the bay time so the fleet is not all out of service at once.

### Why not just use a plug-in tracker from the diagnostic port?

Because that port is the first place a thief looks, and unplugging it takes a second. A hardwired tracker behind trim keeps reporting after the ignition is off and after the car is gone, which is the whole point of having one.

### Will a tracker drain the battery on a van that sits over the weekend?

Wired correctly, a tracker sips almost nothing while parked and stays dormant between check-ins. We use the right fuse rating and check the draw before the vehicle leaves, so it starts on Monday morning. A van that sits parked from Friday night to Monday is well within what the system handles.

### Can the tracker and a Viper alarm be done in a single visit?

They can, and it is the better plan. Both go in while the panels are off, the alarm stops the attempt and the tracker handles the recovery. The Viper 3108V is $269 installed, as of October 2026, with tracker hardware quoted on the vehicle.

### How long will my vehicle be in the Carson bay for a tracker?
