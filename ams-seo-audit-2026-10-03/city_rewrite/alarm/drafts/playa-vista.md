### Why hardwire a dash camera for a Playa Vista garage instead of using the plug?

Parking mode. A camera on the cigarette plug sleeps when the car does, so it misses the door ding in the garage. Hardwired through a fused tap it records on impact or motion, and the cutoff keeps it from draining the battery.

### Will the wiring be visible on the windshield or the pillar?

No. The cable runs inside the headliner and down behind the A-pillar trim to the fuse box, and the rear camera cable follows the headliner to the back glass. The only thing you see is the camera behind the mirror.

### What does a front and rear dash camera install involve?

Mounting both cameras, routing both cables inside the trim, wiring the hardwire kit to a fused circuit, setting parking mode and the cutoff voltage, then checking the footage and pairing the app with you before you leave.
