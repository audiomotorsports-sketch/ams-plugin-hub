### Is a GPS tracker worth it for a car parked on the street in Hermosa Beach?

Yes. Street parking invites break-ins and theft, and a tracker lets you or the police find the car after it disappears. Combined with an alarm, you cover both the attempt and the recovery. Placement matters, which is why we hide units on a separate fuse.

### Can you hide a tracker on a motorcycle or scooter?

Often yes, though space is tight. We choose a compact tracker, secure it under the seat or behind a panel and run a fused feed from the battery. The bike keeps its factory look. Bring it in and we will find the safest spot.

### What happens to the tracker when the battery is disconnected?

Many trackers carry an internal backup battery that keeps reporting for a limited time. We choose a unit with that feature and place it beyond easy reach. Ask about the options at booking, and we explain how the subscription and the app behave.

### How long does a GPS install take for someone coming from Hermosa Beach?
