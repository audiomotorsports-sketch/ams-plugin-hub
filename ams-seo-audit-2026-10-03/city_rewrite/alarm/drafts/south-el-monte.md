### What exactly is a starter kill and how does it work?

It is a relay wired into the starter or ignition circuit that opens when the alarm is armed. The engine will not crank until you disarm it. Because the relay is hidden, a thief who gets inside cannot find and bypass it quickly.

### Can a starter kill strand me by accident?

A good install fails safe, meaning it releases when the system is disarmed or loses power. We test it repeatedly and show you the disarm steps. Learning the procedure matters, so we walk you through it before you leave. Spare remotes help, and we explain where to keep them.

### Is a South El Monte commute car a candidate for a Viper alarm?

### Does a flatbed theft defeat a starter kill?

A car lifted onto a truck does not need to start, so a kill relay alone will not stop it. That is why we suggest adding a GPS tracker. Together they cover both driving the car away and towing it.
