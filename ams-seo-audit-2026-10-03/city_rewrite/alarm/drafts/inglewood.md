### How does a starter kill work on a modern keyless car?

The interrupt sits on the starter or ignition signal and stays open until you disarm the system with a remote or a hidden step. A copied key or relay trick may open the door, but the engine will not crank. Some keyless models need a specific module.

### Where is the starter kill hidden on my car?

We do not publish that, and it is different on each vehicle. It is never beside the fuse box and not in the obvious bundle. You are told where it is for your own records, and nobody else needs to know.

### Can a starter kill cause problems if the battery is low?

The relay itself draws very little power. Should the battery die, the car will not start regardless, and a jump works as normal once the system is disarmed. We test behavior with a weak battery during the install so you know what to expect.
