## Rolling Hills Estates questions

### What goes into a work-truck security setup?

Usually three parts. An alarm with sensors extended to the bed and toolbox, a GPS tracker hidden on constant power so it reports even when the truck is off, and a dash camera with parking mode. We size the package to how the truck is used and quote it by vehicle.

### Can a tracker be hidden on a truck that different crew members drive?

Yes, and that is the normal case. The unit is placed where nobody on the crew would stumble into it, powered from a fused constant circuit, and the account stays with the owner. You see where every truck is from your phone without anyone needing to touch the hardware.

### Is Rolling Hills Estates close enough for a same-day alarm install?

### Will a remote start make my car easier to steal?
