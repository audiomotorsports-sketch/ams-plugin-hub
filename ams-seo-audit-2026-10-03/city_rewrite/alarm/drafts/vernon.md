# Fleet Security and Car Alarm Installation for Vernon

Vernon runs on trucks, and after the last shift there is almost nobody left to watch them. LA Car Alarm by Audio MotorSports, at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, installs GPS trackers, immobilizers and Viper alarms on fleet vehicles and on the personal cars of Vernon shift workers, 12.1 miles down Alameda from the industrial corridor. Fleet jobs are sequenced around your routes so the yard keeps moving.

## What Vernon operators bring us

**GPS tracker installation.** On a box truck, a tractor or a cargo van the tracker is hardwired on its own fused circuit, hidden where a driver swap will not expose it, and checked on the fleet phone before the unit leaves the bay. A refrigerated unit, its fuel and its load are three separate things worth stealing, and a tracker is how you find all three.

**Immobilizer and starter-kill installation.** In a city where a siren has no audience, the useful piece is the one that keeps the engine from turning over. The interrupt goes into the ignition circuit, not the fuse panel, so pulling fuses accomplishes nothing.

**Viper alarm installation.** For the personal cars parked along the industrial streets for ten or twelve hours, a Viper 3108V with the module buried deep behind the dash and wired so it cannot be defeated from under the hood.

**Trailer security.** Trailers leave on their own, so they are treated as their own job: a tracker on the trailer and a hitch trigger on the tractor where the hardware allows.

**Sealed connections.** Vehicles that cycle between cold storage and a hot yard push moisture through every joint, so each connection is sealed rather than simply crimped.

Need car window tint or a car stereo with Apple CarPlay on a driver's personal car? Same bay, same appointment.

Drivers who ride along can wait inside; we have seating, free Wi-Fi, a restroom and Netflix on the TV, and a mall with restaurants and coffee is a short walk off if the install runs long. The shop is LGBTQ-friendly and open to everyone, which our Google and Yelp profiles state plainly. We sit right off the 110 freeway with the 405 and the 710 both close, so a Vernon yard truck can take the 710 south and still get here in one straight run.

### How do you handle a fleet of Vernon trucks without taking them all off the road?

We book them one or two at a time around your route schedule, so a truck comes in on its slow day and goes back out the same afternoon. Every unit gets the same hidden tracker logic and the same test before release, and the fleet phone or dashboard is set up on the first vehicle.

### Does an alarm do anything in a city where nobody lives overnight?

The siren does very little, which is why we lead with the parts that do not need a listener. An immobilizer keeps the truck from driving away, a GPS tracker tells you where it went if it was towed, and a phone-link alert reaches you at home instead of an empty street.

### Can a GPS tracker be installed on a refrigerated trailer?

Yes. The trailer gets its own tracker with its own power arrangement, because it will not always be hooked to the same tractor. Tell us whether the reefer unit runs on its own fuel and whether the trailer stays coupled overnight, since both change how the unit is wired and powered.

### What does a Viper alarm installation cost for a shift worker's car?

The Viper 3108V is $269 installed, as of October 2026, on a standard car or pickup. That covers the siren, shock sensor, starter interrupt and two remotes, plus the labor to hide the module and test every trigger. Trackers and immobilizers for fleet vehicles are quoted per unit at the shop.

### Why drive a Vernon work truck to Carson rather than call a mobile installer?

Because the truck goes on a lift and the wiring is done where we can reach it properly, seal it and test it under load. Customers already come from over 100 miles away for that kind of install. A fleet that fails one truck at a time costs more than doing each once, done right.
