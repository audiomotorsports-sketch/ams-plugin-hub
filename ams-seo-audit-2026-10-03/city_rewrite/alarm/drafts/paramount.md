### Why would a Paramount driver pay for GPS tracker installation instead of a plug-in tracker?

Plug-in trackers live in the diagnostic port, which is the first thing a thief unplugs. A hardwired tracker sits behind trim on its own power feed, keeps reporting after the ignition is off, and cannot be found without taking the interior apart. The labor is the protection.
