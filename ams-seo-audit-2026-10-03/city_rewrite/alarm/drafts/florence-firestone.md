### Where do you hide a GPS tracker so it is not found and ripped out?

Nowhere we will describe on a web page, and never where a plug-in OBD tracker sits. The point of a hardwired unit is that it draws from a circuit that is not obvious, sits behind trim that takes tools to remove, and keeps reporting while the car is being driven away.

### Does a GPS tracker installed in Florence-Firestone need a monthly subscription?

### Can an alarm and a GPS tracker be installed in the same appointment?

Yes, and it is the best way to do it, because the two share wiring work and the car is already apart. The alarm handles the noise and the starter kill; the tracker handles the recovery if someone still manages to tow it. Plan on a full morning or afternoon for both.

### What is the difference between a starter kill and an immobilizer?

Your factory immobilizer checks for a chip in the key; a starter kill is an added relay that opens the starter circuit until the alarm is disarmed. Thieves who defeat the factory system with a programmer still have to find our relay, which is why the placement of it matters more than the brand.
