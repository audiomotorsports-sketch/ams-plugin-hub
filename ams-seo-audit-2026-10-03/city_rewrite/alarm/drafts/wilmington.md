## What Wilmington drivers bring us

- **GPS tracker installation.** The unit goes behind a trim panel on a fused, constant-power circuit, so it keeps reporting even after the ignition is off and a thief has pulled the obvious wires. You get the location on your phone, and recovery has somewhere to start.
- **Car alarm installation.** A Viper alarm is wired to the vehicle's own door, hood and ignition circuits with the siren and brain tucked away from the fuse box, so it cannot be silenced by yanking a fuse.
- **Dash camera installation.** Hardwired to a switched circuit with a parking-mode lead, cable run inside the headliner and A-pillar, no loose wire hanging across the windshield.

### Will a tracker still work if someone disconnects the battery?

### Can an alarm be added without cutting into the factory harness?

Yes. Connections are made at the right circuits with proper splices and heat shrink, then wrapped to look factory, so a future tech can trace the work. The brain and siren are mounted away from the fuse box so the alarm cannot be bypassed from there.
