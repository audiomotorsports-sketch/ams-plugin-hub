### Can a Rolling Hills work truck get a bed-cover and toolbox trigger?

Yes. We add pin switches or sensors to the toolbox lid, the bed cover and the tailgate so the alarm reacts to any of them opening. The module is hidden behind the dash. Tell us what you carry when you book and we plan the sensors around it.

### How do I protect a trailer parked at a Rolling Hills property?

A small tracker installed in the trailer itself follows the load even when the truck is gone. We choose a spot inside the frame or a cabinet, fuse it separately and set up the app with you. It pairs well with a hitch lock.

### Does parking-mode recording drain a truck battery?

A hardwired dash camera uses a low-voltage cutoff, so recording stops before the battery gets weak enough to matter. We wire it to a fuse tap that stays live and test the cutoff with you. Heavy daily use may still call for a battery check.
