### What stops a thief from pulling a fuse and silencing a truck alarm?

The install does. We hide the alarm module, route the starter interrupt away from the obvious circuits and back up the siren so cutting one wire does not quiet it. A box bought online and plugged in quickly gives none of that.

### Can you protect the toolbox and shell on a Rossmoor contractor truck?

Yes. Pin switches or sensors on the toolbox lid and the camper shell tie into the alarm so opening either one sets it off. The same system covers the cab doors and the hood, and a tilt sensor can be added for wheel and trailer theft.

### Do you install GPS trackers on trailers as well as the truck?

We do, on a case-by-case basis depending on the trailer wiring. A self-powered or trailer-wired tracker lets you find the trailer separately from the truck. Bring both, or send photos, and Nick will quote it.

### How long will my work truck be out of service for an alarm and tracker?

Plan on most of a day for an alarm and tracker together, since both go behind the dash and panels. Many Rossmoor owners book a Saturday so the truck is back for Monday. Simple alarm-only jobs are faster.
