### What protects the tools in a Santa Monica work van overnight?

Door sensors on every cargo and side door tied into a Viper alarm, with the siren hidden and a starter kill so the van cannot be driven to a quieter spot. A GPS tracker adds recovery if it is towed. Interior motion sensing is available for vans with glass.

### Can a thief defeat a van alarm by cutting power or pulling a fuse?

Not when the install is done properly. The module is hidden, the siren has backup, and the starter interrupt is on a circuit a thief will not find by opening the fuse box. That is the difference between a box plugged in and a job done right.

### Will remote start work on a diesel work truck?

Yes, with a setup that respects the glow-plug cycle and the transmission type. Compustar installation on diesels is quoted on the specific truck. The doors stay secured and the engine shuts down if anyone tries to drive without the key.

### How long is the van out of service for alarm and tracker together?

Most of a working day, since both need the dash and panels opened. Many Santa Monica owners book a Saturday and have the van back for Monday. An alarm by itself is a few hours.
