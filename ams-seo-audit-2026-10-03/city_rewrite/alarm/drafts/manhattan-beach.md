### Is it worth the drive from Manhattan Beach for a hardwired dash camera?

Yes, because the difference between a camera that works and one that does not is the wiring. A plug-in camera dies when the ignition goes off. A hardwired one with a fused tap and a voltage cutoff records the parking-lot hit at night, and the cable is invisible.

### Will the camera cable show along my windshield or pillar?

No. The cable runs inside the headliner, down the A-pillar behind the trim and to the fuse box, and the rear camera cable follows the headliner to the hatch. The only visible part is the camera itself behind the mirror.

### Can parking mode on a dash camera flatten the battery?

Not when the hardwire kit has a working low-voltage cutoff, which we set on your car. The camera shuts itself down before the battery drops too far to start the engine. On a car that sits for weeks, we talk through the settings with you.
