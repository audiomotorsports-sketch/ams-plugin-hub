### Will remote start interfere with my factory key or warranty behavior?

No. The system is integrated through a data interface, not spliced into the ignition switch, so the factory key, the push-button and the stock immobilizer all behave as before. The car simply gains a second way to start. Nothing is cut that would stop a dealer from servicing the vehicle normally.
