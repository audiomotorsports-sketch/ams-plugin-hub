## What Bell drivers bring us

**GPS tracker installation.** The tracker is placed where nobody checks, fused on its own circuit and tested with the app on your phone before you leave. It reports location with the ignition off, which is when a car actually goes missing.

**Viper alarm installation.** The 3108V gives you a siren, a shock sensor and a starter interrupt that keeps the engine from cranking. We bury the module behind the dash instead of near the fuse box.

**Compustar installation for remote start.** Start the car from inside and walk out to a running, secured vehicle. The bypass module is matched to your make, and the factory key still does everything it did before.

**Dash camera installation.** A hardwired camera records a break-in attempt even when the car is parked. The wire runs behind the trim so the install looks like it came from the factory.
