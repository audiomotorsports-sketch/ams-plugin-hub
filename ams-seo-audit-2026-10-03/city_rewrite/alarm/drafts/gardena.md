### Do Gardena drivers really need a hardwired dash camera instead of a plug-in one?

A plug-in camera dies when the car is off, which is exactly when a parking-lot hit happens. Hardwiring gives you parking mode with a voltage cutoff, a clean windshield with no cable in your line of sight, and a camera that is still recording six months later instead of unplugged in the glovebox.

### What is the difference between a Viper alarm and a Compustar system?

Both are solid security platforms and we install both. Viper is the common choice for a straightforward alarm with shock sensor and starter kill. Compustar is strong on remote start and phone control. Nick matches the brand to your vehicle rather than pushing one line on everyone.

### Can I get remote start on a manual transmission?

Some systems support manual transmissions with a specific safety sequence, but not every vehicle qualifies and it is not something we will shortcut. Tell us the year, make and model when you call and we will say plainly whether it can be done safely.

### Is it worth the trip from Gardena for a $269 alarm install?

Yes, and it is a short trip. The price includes the labor, which is the part that decides whether the alarm can be bypassed from the fuse box or not. A cheap install that leaves wires exposed under the dash is the kind of job we end up redoing.

### How do you keep the alarm from going off every time a truck passes on my street?

Shock sensitivity is adjusted on your car, in our lot, with the doors closed. Then we tell you how to fine-tune it if your parking situation changes. A properly set alarm reacts to a window break or a hit, not to a bus rolling by.
