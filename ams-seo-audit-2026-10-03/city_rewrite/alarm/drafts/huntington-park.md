### What does a GPS tracker actually do once it is installed in my car?

It reports the car's location to an app on your phone, keeps a history of trips, and sends an alert when the car moves outside a time or area you set. If the car is stolen, you hand that live position to the police. Some units also let you see speed and ignition events.

### Can a Huntington Park driver have a tracker installed while they wait?

Usually, yes. A tracker install is one of the shorter jobs, often an hour or two depending on how much trim has to come out to hide it well. Book a time, bring the car, and wait at the shop. If you are adding an alarm the same day, plan on leaving it longer.

### Is there a difference between a plug-in OBD tracker and a hardwired one?

A big one. A plug-in unit is found and yanked in seconds. A hardwired tracker is behind panels, on constant power, with its own backup, and keeps reporting while the car is being driven away. The install is the difference between a gadget and a recovery tool.

### How does the Viper alarm work together with the tracker?

The alarm makes noise and kills the starter, which turns a quick theft into a tow job. The tracker then reports where the tow went. The Viper 3108V alarm is $269 installed as of October 2026; the tracker is quoted for your car. Together they cover both the attempt and the aftermath.

### Will the tracker keep working if the car battery is disconnected?

For a while, yes. Hardwired trackers carry an internal backup battery that keeps them reporting after main power is cut, usually long enough to give a location trail. We also choose a power source that is not obvious to someone looking to cut a wire under the hood.
