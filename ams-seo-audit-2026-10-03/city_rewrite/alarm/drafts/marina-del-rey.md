### How do you secure a Marina del Rey contractor truck with a toolbox and shell?

Pin switches or sensors on the toolbox lid and the shell tie into the Viper alarm, so lifting either one sets off the siren. The cab doors and hood are covered too, and a tilt sensor can be added for wheels and trailers.

### What keeps a thief from cutting a wire and driving off in the truck?

The starter interrupt is on a circuit that is not obvious, the module is hidden and the siren is backed up. Cutting the first wire a thief finds does nothing. That is the labor you are paying for, not the box.

### Do you track trailers separately from the truck?

When the trailer wiring allows it, yes. A self-powered or trailer-wired tracker lets you find the trailer even when it is unhitched. Send photos of the trailer and Nick will quote it.
