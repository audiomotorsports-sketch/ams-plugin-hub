### What happens if a thief tows the car instead of starting it?

A starter kill does nothing against a flatbed, which is why we pair it with a GPS tracker and, where it fits, a tilt sensor that triggers the siren when the car is lifted. Recovery then comes down to the tracker reporting its position.

### Can you install a tracker on a keyless-entry car without upsetting the electronics?

Yes. The tracker is wired to power and ground only; it does not touch the data bus. On a push-button car the alarm or remote start uses an interface matched to the platform, so the factory key, locks and lights behave as they did.
