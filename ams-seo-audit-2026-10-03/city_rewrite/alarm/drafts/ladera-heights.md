### Can a dash camera be installed so it is nearly invisible from outside?

Yes. A small camera mounted behind the rear-view mirror with the cable run inside the headliner and down the A-pillar is hard to spot from the sidewalk. That matters, because a visible camera is a reason to break a window. The rear camera sits high in the back glass for the same reason.

### Does parking mode on a dash camera really capture a hit-and-run in a Ladera Heights driveway?

When it is hardwired, yes. Parking mode wakes the camera on impact or motion and saves the clip, including the seconds before the bump. A plug-in camera is dead when the ignition is off, so it misses exactly the event you bought it for.

### What memory card and settings do you recommend for a dash camera?

A high-endurance card rated for continuous rewriting, sized for at least a few hours of front and rear footage, and loop recording with impact clips protected from overwrite. We set the date, time, resolution and parking-mode sensitivity before you leave so the first clip you ever need is usable.

### How is the Viper alarm wired so it cannot be bypassed from the fuse box?

The control module and the starter kill relay are placed away from the fuse panel and behind trim, the siren has its own backup battery, and the wiring is soldered rather than crimped. Pulling a fuse neither silences the alarm nor restores the starter. That labor is included in the $269 installed price as of October 2026.

### Is a 13 mile drive from Ladera Heights worth it for a camera and an alarm?

Yes. The camera takes an hour or two and the alarm a half day, and both are done in one visit if you book it that way. Half an hour on the road to a shop that does this every day is a good trade for an install that does not have to be pulled out and redone.
