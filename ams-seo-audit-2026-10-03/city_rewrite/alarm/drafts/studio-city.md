### Is remote start practical for a Studio City hillside driveway?

Yes. A remote start brings the engine and climate to a good state before you walk out. Safety features keep the car from rolling, and the system will not start in gear. We test the setup with you before the keys are returned.

### Can a remote start and an alarm share one remote?

They can, which is the tidy way to do it. A Compustar remote and a Viper system can be integrated or paired so one fob handles both. We explain the options and quote the combined install after reviewing your vehicle.

### Will a hardwired dash camera record when the car is off?

When installed with parking mode, yes. We run the cable to a constant power fuse and add a low-voltage cutoff to protect the battery. The camera wakes on motion or impact and saves the clip. We set it up on site.

### How does a GPS tracker alert me?

A tracker sends a phone notification when the car moves, leaves an area or runs the ignition. You can open the app to see the live location. We show you the setup and alerts before you leave, and explain the data plan.
