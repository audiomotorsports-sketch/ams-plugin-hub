### How fast does a GPS tracker report a stolen car's location?

Within seconds of movement, and then continuously while it moves. You get an alert on your phone, open the app and see the car on a map. That live position is what you give the police. The whole point of hardwiring and hiding the unit is that it keeps reporting during the drive away.

### Can a thief find and remove the tracker from a La Puente car?

Not quickly. The unit is behind trim that needs tools to remove, wired to a circuit that is not the first one a thief checks, and small enough that it does not stand out among factory modules. We will not say where it goes in writing, and that discretion is part of the installation.

### Does a tracker replace the need for an alarm?

No. They do different jobs. The alarm and starter kill make the theft loud and slow, usually forcing a tow. The tracker tells you where the tow went. A car with both is far more likely to be recovered than a car with either alone. We recommend installing them in one visit.
