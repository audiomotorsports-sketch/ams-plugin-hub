### Is a 32-mile trip from Sierra Madre sensible for a tracker install?

A tracker only helps if it is hidden well and set up right, so a skilled install is worth the road time. Combine it with an alarm or camera and you finish everything at once. Many customers drive over an hour for that.

### Can a GPS tracker work after a thief cuts the battery?

Some models carry a small internal battery for a short time, and we pick one with that backup. A thief who disconnects the battery will not necessarily silence it. We also run the unit on a separate fuse that is difficult to find.

### What monthly cost comes with a tracker?

Most trackers need a data plan to send location, and we explain the plan before you commit. Prices for the unit and the install are quoted at the shop based on the vehicle. Ask Nick about a payment plan for larger jobs.

### Does a dash camera install need a special power wire?

For parking recording, yes. We run a hardwire kit to a fuse that stays live and add a cutoff so the battery is protected. The unit starts recording on impact. Cables stay hidden, so the cabin looks stock. The camera is tested in parking mode before you leave.

### Do you check for existing alarms before adding a new one?

Yes. If a car already has an aftermarket system, we find the old wiring first so two systems do not fight each other. We may remove or integrate it. Telling us at booking helps us estimate the time correctly. Nothing is cut until the old wiring is understood.
