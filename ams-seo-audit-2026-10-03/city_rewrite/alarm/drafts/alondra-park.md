### What should an Alondra Park contractor put on a work truck first?

Start with an alarm that has a starter kill and a hidden siren, then add a GPS tracker. The alarm makes the truck hard to take; the tracker gets it back when someone tries anyway. Bed and toolbox sensors come next if the tools are worth more than the truck.

### Can you protect the tools in the bed, not just the cab?

Yes. We can add sensors to the toolbox lids, the tonneau cover or the cargo doors on a van so the alarm triggers when they are opened. It is wired into the same Viper or Compustar system that protects the cab, so there is one remote and one siren.

### Does a GPS tracker need a monthly subscription?

Most trackers with live location on your phone use a service plan; some simpler units do not. Nick will tell you which option fits how you use the truck. Either way the install is hidden and hardwired so it cannot be pulled out of a port.

### How long will my van be in the shop for an alarm and tracker?

Plan on part of a day. A straightforward alarm install takes a few hours, and adding a tracker or cargo sensors adds time. If you cannot be without the van, book an early slot and wait at the shop, or arrange a drop-off and pick it up after work.

### Is the drive from Alondra Park worth it for a work vehicle?

It is a short drive, and the alternative is a rushed install that gets the siren cut or the fuse pulled on the first attempt. A shop that does this every day hides the components, protects the circuits and tests the whole system before handing back the keys.
