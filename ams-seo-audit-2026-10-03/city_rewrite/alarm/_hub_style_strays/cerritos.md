### What makes AMS pods different from the speakers Harley sells?

AMS pods are built here as a lower-fairing speaker pod sized for a six-and-a-half-inch driver and shaped to clear the engine guard and the rider's legs. They start from $649 a pair as of October 2026. Paired with a proper amplifier, they add the midbass that stock fairing speakers cannot produce.

### Can a bagger amplifier installation be done without cutting the fairing?

In most cases yes. The amplifier mounts inside the fairing or under the seat depending on the model, and the harness runs along the factory loom. We only cut when a specific bike leaves no other option, and we tell you that before the work starts, not after.

### Is it worth riding from Cerritos for a bagger audio installation?

Riders come from much farther, so yes. A bagger audio build is a full-day job that depends on the installer more than the parts, and the pods are made in this shop. Schedule it, ride down in the morning, and pick the bike up with the system tuned and tested.

### How do you set the gains on a car amplifier?

With a meter, not by turning it up until it sounds loud. Gains are matched to the head unit's output voltage so the amplifier reaches full power without clipping, which is what protects the speakers and the sub. It is the step most installs skip and the one that matters most.

### Will a head unit installation keep my Cerritos car's backup camera?

Yes. Nearly every current Pioneer and Kenwood deck has a camera input, and the factory camera is retained through an interface harness for your year, make and model. Steering-wheel controls come across the same way. We confirm the exact parts before anything comes out of the dash.
