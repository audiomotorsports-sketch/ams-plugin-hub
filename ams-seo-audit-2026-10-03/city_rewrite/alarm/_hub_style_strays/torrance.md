## What Torrance drivers bring us

**Subwoofer installation and amplifier installation.** The most common Torrance job is a loaded Kicker box in the trunk with a monoblock amplifier. We run four-gauge or eight-gauge power wire depending on the amp, fuse it at the battery, and set the gain with a meter rather than by ear.

**Car audio in factory-amp cars.** Camrys, Accords and most newer trucks have a factory amplifier that has to be summed correctly before an aftermarket amp will sound right. We use a line output converter or a DSP so the signal is clean at every volume.

### Will adding an amplifier drain my battery?

Not when it is installed correctly. We wire the amplifier to turn on with the ignition, fuse it at the battery and size the power wire for the current it draws. A properly wired amp pulls nothing when the car is off, and we check charging voltage before you leave.

### Can you add bass to a Torrance car with a factory amplifier?

Yes. Factory-amplified systems need the signal summed and flattened before it reaches an aftermarket amplifier, otherwise the bass rolls off as you turn it up. We use a line output converter or a small DSP to fix that, and the factory radio stays in place.

### Do you do subwoofer installation with customer-supplied subwoofers and amps?

Yes, bring them in and we will quote the labor at the shop. We check that the amplifier matches the subwoofer's impedance and power rating before wiring anything, and if the pairing will not work we will tell you before we start.
