**Head unit installation on older cars.** Single-DIN and double-DIN openings in 2000s sedans and trucks take a modern touchscreen with a trim ring that closes the gaps, and we add a microphone and a USB port where the factory never had one.

**Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation.** Phone projection goes in through the new receiver, or through an interface module in cars that already have a factory screen worth keeping.

**Speaker replacement.** Worn factory speakers are swapped for coaxials or components on adapter rings, with the door panel checked for rattles before it goes back on.

**Subwoofer upgrade.** A loaded box in the trunk with a compact amp wired on a fused run from the battery gives you the bottom end the stock system skipped.

### Will my factory backup camera still work after head unit installation?

Yes. The camera signal is retained through a vehicle-specific interface and shown on the new screen when you shift into reverse. If your car never had a camera, we can add one at the same time and quote it with the install.

### What is involved in a speaker replacement?

We remove the door panel, mount the new speaker on an adapter plate sized to the factory hole, connect it with a plug-in harness, and check the panel for buzzing before reassembly. Component sets add a tweeter at ear level and a crossover tucked behind the panel.

### Do you offer a loaded subwoofer box for the trunk?

Yes. The Dual Kicker Comp loaded box is $249 box only as of October 2026. The amplifier, wiring kit and the labor for the subwoofer installation are quoted at the shop by vehicle, and we set the gains on a meter before you drive off.
