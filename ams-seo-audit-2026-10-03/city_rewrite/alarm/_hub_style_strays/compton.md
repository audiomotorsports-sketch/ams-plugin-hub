# Bagger Audio Installation for Compton Riders: Wired Right Inside the Fairing

The difference between a Harley sound system that lasts and one that fails by summer is the wiring nobody sees. AMS Pods at Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, Carson, is about 5.4 miles from Compton, and we install AMS lower-fairing pods, amplifiers and speakers on Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King baggers with every connection soldered, protected and tucked inside the fairing.

## What Compton riders bring us

**Wiring inside the fairing.** Fairing wiring takes wind, heat and constant vibration. We run proper gauge wire, protect it where it passes through the fairing, and tie it off so nothing chafes against the forks or the inner fairing panel.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** The pods go on the lower fairings and fill the gap in sound below the bars. Installation includes sealing the speaker into the pod and routing the lead up inside the fairing, so the finished install is invisible from the outside.

**Amplifier installation.** Fused at the battery, grounded to clean metal, and set on the bike. That is how an amp survives a Harley.

**Speaker replacement.** When the stock fairing speakers distort, we fit speakers that can handle an open bike and re-seal the fairing so there is no rattle.

### What goes wrong with bagger audio wiring, and how do you prevent it?

Wires rubbing on the inner fairing, undersized power runs, and grounds on painted metal are the usual failures. We size the wire to the amp, protect every pass-through, ground to bare metal, and secure each run so it cannot move. The result is a system that still works after years of vibration.

### Do AMS pods fit a Road King without the full fairing?

The pods mount to the lower fairings, so a Road King needs lowers fitted to take them. Riders with lowers already on the bike are good to go. If you are not sure what your bike has, send a photo by text and we will tell you before you ride over.

### Can you install pods on a Compton rider's bike while I wait?

Depending on what else is being installed, often yes. Pods alone are quicker than pods plus an amp and fairing speakers. When you book, tell us the model and what you want done and we will give you an honest time so you can decide between waiting and dropping off.

### Will the new wiring interfere with the bike's own electronics?

Not when it is done this way. Power comes straight from the battery through a fuse, the remote turn-on is tapped at a proper switched source, and nothing is spliced into the bike's control harness. The factory systems keep working and the audio has its own clean supply.
