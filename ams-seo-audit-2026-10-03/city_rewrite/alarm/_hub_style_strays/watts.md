### Can you install a stereo in my Watts car so it still looks stock?

Yes, that is the point of a factory-look install. We use a vehicle-specific dash kit in matching trim, a plug-in harness and a steering-wheel interface, and we route the wiring behind panels. When the trim goes back on, the only visible change is the better screen.

### Will the new stereo keep my factory backup camera and USB port?

Yes. The backup camera is retained through the interface so it shows on the new screen when you shift into reverse. The factory USB can be kept or replaced with a port that supports CarPlay data, and we tell you which before starting.

### Where does the amplifier go in a factory-look install?

Under a seat, behind trunk side trim or on the back of the subwoofer enclosure, depending on the vehicle. Power wire runs through a factory grommet and along existing looms. Nothing is screwed through the carpet in the open, and the trunk stays usable.
