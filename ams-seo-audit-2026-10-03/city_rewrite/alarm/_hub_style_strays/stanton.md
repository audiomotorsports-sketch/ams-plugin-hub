### What does a Viper alarm installation actually involve?

The siren, shock sensor and control module are mounted where they are hard to reach, the door, hood and trunk triggers are tied into the factory wiring, and the starter interrupt is added so the engine will not crank while armed. It is $269 installed as of October 2026 for the 3108V.

### Can a thief just pull the fuse and shut the alarm off?

Not when it is installed properly. The module is hidden away from the fuse panel, the siren has its own feed, and the starter interrupt stays in place even if power is cut to the dash. That hidden wiring is the reason to have it done at a shop that does this every day.
