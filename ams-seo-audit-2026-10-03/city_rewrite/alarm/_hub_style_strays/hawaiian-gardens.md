### Will new speakers fit my doors without drilling?

Yes in almost every car. We use adapter plates made for your vehicle's speaker opening so the new speaker bolts to the factory holes. Tweeters mount in the factory sail panel or A-pillar position where one exists, and we confirm fit before the appointment.

### Do I need an amplifier for a speaker installation to sound good?

Not always. Good coaxials on a factory radio are a clear step up. An amplifier matters when you drive at highway volume or want component speakers to open up; a compact four-channel amp hides under a seat and is quoted at the shop.
