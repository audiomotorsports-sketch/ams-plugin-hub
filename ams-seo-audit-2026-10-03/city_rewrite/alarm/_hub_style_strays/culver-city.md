### Which speakers should a Culver City commuter choose, components or coaxials?

Coaxials are simpler and sound good on a factory radio. Components separate the tweeter so it can sit at ear level, which improves imaging, and they benefit from an amplifier. We recommend based on your car and how you listen, and everything is quoted at the shop.

### Will new speakers rattle my door panels?

Not when installed correctly. We mount each speaker on a solid adapter plate, use foam baffles where they help, and add deadening to the door skin when the panel needs it. Then the panel is checked for buzz at volume before you leave.
