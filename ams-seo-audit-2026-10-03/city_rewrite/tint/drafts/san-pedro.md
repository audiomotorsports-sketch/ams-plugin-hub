## Questions from San Pedro drivers

### Does car window tint actually block UV, or just darken the glass?

Both Solar and Solar Ceramic block nearly all UV light regardless of shade. Even the light legal film on the front doors protects skin and interior plastics. Darkness is a separate choice; a nearly clear ceramic film still gives full UV protection and strong heat rejection.

### What shade can I legally run on the front windows?

California keeps the front side windows light; the film has to let most of the visible light through. The back doors and rear window are unrestricted. We install the legal light shade up front, so you still get UV and some heat protection, and go darker behind the driver.

### Will Tesla window tint affect my cameras or glass roof?

No. The side cameras and sensors sit outside the tinted areas, and the roof glass is already dark from the factory. The one-piece rear window is the tricky part on a Model 3 or Model Y, and we shrink and fit it as a single panel rather than cutting it.

### How long do I wait before washing the car after window tinting?

Wait a few days before lowering the windows and about a week before any pressure washing near the edges. Exterior washing is fine sooner since the film is on the inside of the glass. Slight haze while it cures is normal and clears on its own.
