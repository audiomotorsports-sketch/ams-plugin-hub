## Redondo Beach window tint FAQ

### Does window tint protect a Redondo Beach car interior from sun damage?

Yes. Window tint filters out nearly all UV, the wavelength that fades fabric, dries leather and cracks dash plastic. A car that spends weekends at the beach gets the most benefit. The ceramic film adds heat rejection on top, but even the standard Solar film gives full UV protection.

### How long is the drive from Redondo Beach to LA Car Tint?

Around 7.2 miles, which works out to roughly a quarter hour outside rush hour. The shop is Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson. Call or text ahead and we will have your bay time held so you are not waiting on arrival.

### Can you tint a car with frameless windows?

Yes, and it is one of the jobs where hand-cutting matters most. Frameless glass has no channel to hide the film edge, so we cut to the true edge, finish it by hand and let it cure before the window is rolled down. The result is a clean line with nothing to catch.

### What shade of window tint is legal on the front doors?

California requires front side windows to stay mostly clear, so the darker shades go on the rear doors and back window only. We keep the fronts within the limit, explain the choice at the counter, and write the coverage on your ticket so there are no surprises at a traffic stop.

### How soon can I roll my windows down after tinting?

Give it a few days. The film needs time to cure to the glass, and rolling the window early can lift the top edge. Small haze or water pockets during that period are normal and clear on their own. We go over aftercare before you drive back to Redondo Beach.
