## South El Monte window tinting questions

### Can a South El Monte business get several trucks tinted together?

Yes. Give us the list with year, make and model and the shade you want, and we book the trucks back to back so the crew is not off the job for long. Each one is hand-cut on its own glass and the coverage is logged so the fleet matches. Fleet pricing is quoted at the shop.

### Will tint on the cab windows make the truck cooler inside?

Yes, and ceramic film makes the biggest difference. It rejects a large share of infrared heat through the door glass and rear window, so the cab reheats slower between stops. Both films also block nearly all UV, which keeps the dash from cracking and the seats from fading.

### How does the tint shop handle a sliding rear window?

Panel by panel. Each pane of the slider is tinted separately so the window still opens and closes cleanly, and the fixed side panes are done in place. The film is cut by hand on each piece of glass rather than from a pattern, so it fits the actual truck.

### Is it worth the drive from South El Monte to Carson for window tinting?

For a truck you depend on, yes. A tint job that bubbles or peels in a year has to be stripped and redone; one that is cut on the glass and applied indoors lasts. The drive is around forty-five minutes outside rush hour, and most vehicles are finished the same day.

### What tint shade is legal on the front of a work van?

The same light shade as any car: California requires the front side windows to stay mostly clear. We install to that limit and put it on the ticket. Rear doors and side cargo glass, if the van has any, can be a full privacy shade to hide the load.
