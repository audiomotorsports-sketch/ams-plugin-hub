## Montebello questions about ceramic tint

### How much heat does ceramic window tint block compared with regular tint?

Noticeably more. Standard dyed film mostly cuts glare and UV; ceramic film also rejects a large share of infrared, the part of sunlight you feel as heat. In Montebello that means a cooler cabin on arrival and faster relief from the air conditioning. The shade can stay light and legal.

### Is Carson too far from Montebello to bother for tint?

No. It is 15.3 miles, a bit over half an hour outside rush hour, and the car is usually done the same day. You are buying the installation, and ours is hand-cut on your glass in a clean bay. We have customers who come from Temecula for the same appointment.

### Can I tint only the rear windows to save money?

You can, but most people regret skipping the fronts because that is where the heat hits the driver. Our four-door packages cover the four doors and the rear glass. If you want a different combination, we will quote it at the shop and tell you honestly whether it is worth it.

### Will ceramic tint help my truck with the big rear window?

Yes. A truck's rear glass and side windows let in a lot of heat, and ceramic film on them cools the cab substantially. We form the film by hand on the rear window and handle sliders panel by panel so they still open. Trucks are quoted in the store.

### What does the installation involve at your shop?

We clean the glass, hand-cut the film on each window, heat-shrink it to match the curve, then apply it from inside and tuck the edges to the seals. The whole job happens indoors so dust stays out. You get aftercare instructions and the car back the same day in most cases.
