### Does window tint make a street-parked car in Exposition Park less of a target?

It helps. Dark rear film means nobody can see what is on the seat or the floor, which removes the easy reason to try a door. Pair it with car alarm installation or a dash camera from the same bay and the car is harder to bother in every way that counts.

### What shade will the front windows be on an Exposition Park car?

Light. California requires the front door glass to pass most of the light, so the fronts get a subtle film that cuts glare and UV without looking dark. The windshield stays clear. The rear doors and back glass are where you pick the shade.
