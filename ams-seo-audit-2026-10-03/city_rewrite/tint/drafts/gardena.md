## Questions Gardena drivers ask about tint

### Is it worth leaving Gardena for window tinting in Carson?

For a 10 minute drive, yes. The difference between tint that lasts and tint that bubbles is the installation, not the roll of film. Gardena is one of the closest cities to the shop; people come from Temecula for the same job, so the drive down to Carson is nothing.

### Will window tint reduce glare on my evening commute?

Side-window tint does reduce glare coming in at an angle, and it stops the bounce off the car beside you. The windshield itself stays clear under California rules apart from a narrow top strip, so the main relief comes from the doors and back glass, which is where we tint.

### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic film?

Solar is the standard film: good shade, good looks, holds its color. Solar Ceramic uses a ceramic layer that rejects much more heat at the same darkness, so a parked car warms up slower. Both are installed the same careful way; the ceramic is the pricier of the two.

### How long does a tint appointment take for a Gardena customer?

Plan on a few hours for a four-door car. We prep the glass, hand-cut and heat-shrink each piece, apply it and check the edges before you leave. Most Gardena customers either wait at the shop or run an errand nearby and return when we text that it is done.

### Can you tint an SUV with a large curved rear window?

Yes, and that back window is where a careful installer earns their keep. We shrink the film to the curve in a single sheet rather than cutting strips, so there are no seams across your rear view. SUVs and minivans are quoted in the store because glass sizes vary.
