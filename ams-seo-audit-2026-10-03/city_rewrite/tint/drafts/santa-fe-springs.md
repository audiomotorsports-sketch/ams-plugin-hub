### Will dark cargo glass on a van be a problem with the law?

No. California only restricts the front door windows and the windshield. Cargo and rear glass on a van can go as dark as you want for privacy over equipment. We put the light legal shade on the fronts and the dark shade behind, and mark every window before cutting.

### Does a Santa Fe Springs work truck get enough out of ceramic window tint to justify it?

For a truck that parks in an open lot all day, yes. Ceramic film rejects the infrared heat that a dyed film passes, so the cab is bearable at pickup and the dash does not cook. It also holds its color for years. Pricing for trucks is set at the shop after a look at the glass.

### What does the installation involve on a cargo van?

Each piece of glass is cleaned, the film is cut by hand to that panel, heat-formed to any curve, applied wet and worked to the seal. Nothing is drilled and no interior panels come off. A few days of curing with the windows up and the film is set for the life of the van.
