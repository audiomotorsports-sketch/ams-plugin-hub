### How much does ceramic tint help with Pasadena heat?

A lot. The ceramic layer rejects most infrared heat, so the glass stops radiating into the cabin while the car is parked. The interior still warms in direct sun, but from a much lower starting point, and the air conditioning catches up fast.

### Can I get the car done and be back in Pasadena the same afternoon?

Yes. Book a morning time, drive down, and a sedan or SUV is finished that day. Wait at the shop or leave the car and return in the afternoon; we give you the pickup time when you book. Morning slots leave the most room for the drive back.

### Does a light shade still block heat?

With ceramic film, yes. Heat rejection comes from the ceramic particles, not the darkness, so a light front-door film stays legal in California and still blocks most infrared energy. Choose the rear shade for looks and privacy. Many Pasadena drivers run a light ceramic front and a darker ceramic rear.

### Is window tint removable if I sell the car?

Yes. Film sits on the inside of the glass and can be removed cleanly. Most buyers see good tint as a plus, so it usually stays on. Rear windows with defroster lines need care during removal, which we handle. Mention it when you call and we will set the time aside.
