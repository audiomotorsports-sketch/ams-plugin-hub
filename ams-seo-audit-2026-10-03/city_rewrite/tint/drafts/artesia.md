## Artesia families ask about tinting the big vehicles

### How much does window tint cost for an SUV from Artesia?

SUVs are quoted at the shop because the glass count and size vary so much between a compact crossover and a full-size three-row. For reference, a standard sedan is $249 installed for Solar and $329 installed for Solar Ceramic as of October 2026. Bring the vehicle and Nick writes the number.

### Can the rear of a minivan be tinted darker than the front doors?

Yes, and it usually is. California limits only the front door windows and windshield; the sliding doors, quarter glass and liftgate can go as dark as you like for the kids and the cargo. We mark each window with its shade before cutting so the plan is clear.

### Does ceramic window tint keep the third row cooler?

Noticeably. The back of a three-row SUV has the most glass and the least air flow, and ceramic film blocks the infrared heat that pours through it. Both films stop UV; ceramic is the one that changes the temperature. It is the film we suggest for family vehicles that park outside.

### Is it a long wait to tint a three-row SUV?

Longer than a sedan, because there is more glass and each panel is cut and fitted by hand. Book a morning appointment and it is usually ready the same afternoon. Many Artesia customers drop the vehicle and pick it up later rather than wait; either works.

### Why drive to Carson rather than the nearest tint shop to Artesia?

Because the installation is the whole job. Film is hand-cut on your glass, shrunk to the curve, and run out to the seal, by a crew that does it every working day. A job done once and done right beats a cheap one that lifts at the corners a year later.
