### How much cooler will my car be after ceramic window tint?

Noticeably. Ceramic film rejects most infrared heat, so the glass stops radiating into the cabin while the car is parked. The interior still warms in full sun, but from a much lower starting point, and the air conditioning wins quickly.

### Which windows on my car can be tinted dark in California?

Rear doors and the back glass can be any shade. The front side windows must stay light enough to pass most visible light, and only a strip at the top of the windshield is allowed. We keep the fronts legal and you choose the rear.

### Does tint block UV even in a light shade?

Yes. UV rejection comes from the film construction, not its darkness, so a light front-door film blocks nearly all UV. That protects skin on long drives and keeps the dash and seats from fading. Both Solar and Solar Ceramic do this, so even the lightest legal front film is doing real work.
