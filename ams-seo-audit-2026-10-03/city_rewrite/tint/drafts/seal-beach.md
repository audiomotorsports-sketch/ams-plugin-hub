### Does window tint help with glare off the ocean on the drive home?

Yes. Even a moderate shade on the side glass cuts the reflected light that comes in low off the water, and a strip at the top of the windshield handles the sun visor zone. Ceramic film does this while also rejecting heat. Fronts stay light to meet the California rule.

### How far is Seal Beach from the LA Car Tint bay?

### Will salt air or beach sun shorten the life of ceramic window tint?

No. Film is applied to the inside of the glass, so salt and sand never touch it, and ceramic film does not fade or turn purple the way old dyed films did. Keep the windows up for a few days after installation while the film cures and it will last.

### Can you tint frameless windows on a convertible or coupe?

Yes. Frameless door glass is cut on the window itself so the film follows the top edge exactly, since there is no frame to hide a short cut. Small quarter windows are handled the same way. It takes more time than a sedan, which is why coupes are quoted on the car.
