## Playa del Rey questions

### Does window tint survive salt air in Playa del Rey?

Yes, when the edges are sealed properly. The film itself is unaffected by salt; what fails on a cheap job is a loose edge that lets moisture under. Each piece here is cut on the glass and pressed to the seal.

### What is the best tint for glare off the ocean?

Either film helps, because any shade on the side glass reduces reflected light. Solar Ceramic adds heat rejection as well, so if the car also sits in the sun it is the better value. Many Playa del Rey drivers choose ceramic for exactly that reason.

### Can a Playa del Rey driver drop the car and come back?

Yes. Book a time, drive down, and leave the keys. Most cars are finished the same day, and we will tell you the pickup time when you book. Waiting at the shop is fine too. Either way the car is ready before the shop closes at 6:00 PM.

### How do I know the film will be fitted properly?

Read the reviews before you drive: Google 4.8 (654 reviews) and Yelp 4.7 (777 reviews), as of October 2026. The film is hand-cut on your glass, not pre-cut, so it matches the exact shape of each window. That is the difference between a tint that lasts and one that lifts.

Yes. Film is applied to the inside of the glass and can be taken off cleanly. Rear windows with defroster lines need care, which is one more reason to have it done by people who do it daily. Mention old film when you book so the extra time is set aside.
