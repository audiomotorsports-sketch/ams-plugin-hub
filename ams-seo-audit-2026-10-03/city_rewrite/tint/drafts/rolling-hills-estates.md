## Rolling Hills Estates tint questions, answered plainly

### Why drive down from Rolling Hills Estates instead of using a mobile tinter?

Dust. Window tint needs a clean, still, indoor space to go on without specks trapped under the film, and a driveway is neither. Our bay in Carson is 6 miles away and the film is cut on your glass, not on a tailgate. Done once, done right, is cheaper than done twice.

### Can I get dark privacy tint on the back of my SUV?

Yes. California lets the rear doors and back glass go darker than the fronts, so privacy shades are normal on SUVs and vans. We put the legal lighter shade on the front doors, the darker film behind, and note the exact coverage on your ticket before the work starts.

### Does ceramic window tint change how the car looks?

Not in a way you would notice from outside. Ceramic window tint comes in the same shades as the standard film; the difference is the heat it stops. Pick the look you want, and the ceramic layer does its job quietly. It also holds color rather than fading toward purple.

### How is the film cut for a curved rear window?

We lay a sheet over the outside of the glass, heat-shrink it until it follows the curve with no fingers or creases, trim it to the exact edge, then move it inside and apply it. One piece, no seams, no pattern pulled from a database and trimmed short.

### Do you do Tesla window tint for Model X falcon doors?

We do, along with Model 3, Model Y and Model S. Tesla glass is large and curved, which is exactly the kind of work that rewards hand-cutting. Windshield strips are quoted in the shop after we see the car, and the fronts stay within California limits.
