## Inglewood tint questions

### Can you tint several work vans for an Inglewood business?

Yes. We schedule fleet vehicles one at a time or back to back, keep the shade consistent across all of them, and quote per vehicle since cargo vans have different glass than passenger vans. Bring the first one in and we will plan the rest around your schedule.

### Does tint help a rideshare driver who is in the car all day?

It helps more than most people expect. Tint on the side and rear glass cuts glare and keeps the seats from heating up between rides, and ceramic film goes further by blocking infrared heat. Passengers get a cooler, more private back seat, which they notice.

### What is the legal limit for front window tint in California?

The front door windows must stay light enough that most of the light passes through, so they can only take a very light film. The rear doors and back glass have no such restriction. We set the fronts to the legal level and go darker behind the driver.

### Is ceramic window tint worth the extra cost over regular film?

If the vehicle sits in the sun or is driven for hours at a time, yes. Regular film handles glare and privacy; ceramic adds real heat rejection so the cabin cools faster and the dash is not radiating at you. For a garaged weekend car, the standard film is fine.
