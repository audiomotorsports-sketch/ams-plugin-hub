## Questions from South Los Angeles

### Can you replace the purple, bubbling tint on my car from South Los Angeles?

Yes. Aged dyed film turns purple and lifts as the adhesive breaks down. We strip it, clean the leftover glue off the glass without damaging the rear defroster, then install new film cut to fit. Removal is quoted when we see the car, since some old film comes off in small pieces.

### Does window tint reduce headlight glare at night?

It does on the rear and side glass. Tint on the back window dims the headlights behind you in the mirror, and the rear side windows cut the flash from cars passing. The front doors stay light by law, so your own view ahead is not affected at night.

### How dark is too dark for the back windows?

Legally the rear doors and back glass can go as dark as you want in California. Practically, a very dark rear makes reversing into an unlit spot harder, so most drivers pick a shade that gives privacy in daylight while leaving the mirrors usable. We will show you the shades side by side.

### What is the benefit of cutting the film on a glass board instead of on the car?

Blades never touch your paint or seals, and the film can be heat-shrunk to the exact curve of each window before it goes on. The result is an edge that finishes right at the gasket with no light line and no lifted corner for air to get under later.

### How long should I wait to wash the car after window tint?

The outside of the car can be washed right away since the film is on the inside of the glass. Leave the windows up for a few days while the film bonds, and clean the inside of the glass with a soft cloth and a mild cleaner once it has dried, nothing with abrasives.
