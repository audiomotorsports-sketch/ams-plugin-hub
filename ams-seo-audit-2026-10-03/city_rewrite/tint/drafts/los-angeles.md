- **Tesla window tint.** The one-piece rear glass on a Model 3 or Model Y is formed as a single sheet. Factory roof glass is already dark.
- **Rideshare and delivery cars.** Legal front shades, cooler cabin, a nicer ride for the passenger in back.

## Los Angeles car window tint questions

### With so many tint shops in Los Angeles, why drive to Carson?

Because the film is the cheap part and the installation is what you are paying for. Ours is cut by hand on your actual glass, heat-formed and applied indoors, by people who tint all day. Done once, done right. Customers come from over 100 miles away for it; from Los Angeles it is about half an hour.

### What is the legal tint shade for front windows in Los Angeles?

The same as everywhere in California: front side windows stay mostly clear, the windshield takes only a top strip, and the rear doors and back glass can be dark. We install to that line and write the coverage on your ticket, so you have it if you are ever asked.

### Can a rideshare driver get window tint that passes inspection?

Yes. We put a light, legal shade on the front doors and a medium or dark shade behind, which keeps the car compliant and gives passengers a cooler, more private ride. Ceramic film on the fronts keeps the driver cooler without going dark. Many Los Angeles rideshare drivers do exactly this.

### Do you tint Teslas for Los Angeles owners?

Yes, all models. The rear glass on a Model 3 or Model Y is done as one sheet, no seam. Front shades stay within the California limit, and the factory-dark roof glass needs no film. Book a Tesla slot by text with the model and year.
