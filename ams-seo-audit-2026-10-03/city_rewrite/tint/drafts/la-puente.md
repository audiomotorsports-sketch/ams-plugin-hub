### Can you tint a lifted or modified truck from La Puente?

Yes. Ride height does not change the glass. Each window is measured and cut on the truck, and the rear slider is handled as separate pieces so it still opens. Aftermarket tint on a previous owner's windows can be removed first if needed; just mention it when you book.

### Which film is right for a truck that sits outside all day?

Solar Ceramic. It rejects most infrared heat without needing a dark shade, so the cab starts cooler and the dash does not bake. Solar is fine if privacy and glare are the main goals and heat is secondary. Either way the cab glass is cut by hand on the truck.

### Does window tint block UV on the driver's side?

Yes. Even the light film used on the front doors blocks nearly all UV, which protects the left arm and face on long commutes and keeps the interior from fading. The protection is there whether the shade is light or dark, because UV rejection comes from the film itself.

### What is the turnaround for a full vehicle?

Same day. A truck, SUV or sedan is finished the day it comes in. Give us the vehicle and the film when you book, and we will tell you when to expect it. If you want other work done in the same visit, say so when you call so the time is set aside.
