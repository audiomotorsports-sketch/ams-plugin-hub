### How much does the ceramic film help a van parked all day in Buena Park?

A great deal. Solar Ceramic rejects infrared heat, the part of sunlight that makes a cabin feel like an oven, so the van starts cooler and the air conditioning catches up quickly. Standard Solar still cuts glare and UV; ceramic is the upgrade for heat.

### What is the legal tint limit for front windows in California?

The front door windows must let most of the light through, so they get a light film, and the windshield is left untinted. Rear doors, quarter glass and the back window have no shade limit. We set the fronts legal and let you choose the rear.
