### What does ceramic window tint do that regular tint does not?

Ceramic film blocks infrared heat, which is the part of sunlight that makes the cabin hot. A dyed film at the same shade darkens the glass and cuts UV but lets most of that heat through. If your car sits outside, Solar Ceramic is the one that makes it easier to get into.

### Can you remove old bubbled tint before installing new film?

Yes. Old film that has turned purple or bubbled is stripped, the adhesive is cleaned off the glass and new film goes on. Removal is quoted at the shop because the time depends on how the old film comes off, especially on rear glass with defroster lines.

### Can I add car alarm installation to my window tinting appointment?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we will plan the day for both. Viper alarm installation and window tinting are done by the same crew in the same bay, and GPS tracker installation or dash camera installation can be added if you want the car protected and recorded.
