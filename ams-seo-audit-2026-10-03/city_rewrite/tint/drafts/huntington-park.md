## Huntington Park drivers ask

### Which tint should a Huntington Park driver pick if the car parks on the street all day?

Ceramic. A car on the street has no shade, and ceramic film blocks the infrared heat that builds up in a closed cabin, so it cools down faster and the interior ages more slowly. Standard film will still cut glare and add privacy, but the heat difference is noticeable.

### Does ceramic tint look different from regular tint?

Not from the outside. Both come in the same range of shades, so a ceramic car and a standard car can look identical. The difference is what you feel: less heat through the glass and a clearer view at the same darkness, since ceramic film tends to have less haze.

### How much heat does window tint actually block?

It depends on the film. Any tint blocks some heat because it blocks light, but ceramic is made to reject the infrared portion, which is what you feel on your skin. Owners typically describe the cabin as cooling faster and the steering wheel staying touchable after an afternoon in the sun.

### Can I get just the back windows done for now?

Yes. Some drivers start with the rear doors and back glass for privacy and add the fronts later. We cut and fit whichever windows you choose and match the shade when you return. The posted price assumes four doors plus the back glass, so a partial job is quoted on its own.

### Why drive to Carson instead of a shop around the corner?

Because the install is the part that lasts. Film cut on a glass board and fitted in a clean indoor bay stays flat for years; film trimmed on the car in a dusty lot tends to lift and speck. Doing it once, properly, is cheaper than doing it twice.
