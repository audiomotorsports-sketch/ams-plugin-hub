Yes, with notice. Fleet vehicles are booked back to back so your trucks rotate through the bay rather than all sitting at once. Each gets the same hand-cut film and edge-to-gasket fit. Vans and pickups are quoted per vehicle at the shop; call (213) 429-1092 with the count.

### Which window tint is best for a car that commutes from Norwalk every day?

Solar Ceramic, if the car parks outdoors or you spend an hour in afternoon sun on the freeway. It rejects infrared heat, not just light, so the cabin starts cooler. Solar is a fine choice when the car lives in a garage. Both block UV and both are hand-fitted.

### Will window tint block my FasTrak or phone signal?

Ceramic film will not, because it has no metal. That is the film we recommend for any car with a toll transponder on the windshield or antennas in the glass. The windshield itself stays clear except for an optional strip at the top, so the transponder sees out fine.

### How is a work van tinted so the cargo is hidden but the driver still sees out?

The rear and side cargo glass gets a dark shade for privacy, and the front door windows get the light film California allows so the driver keeps full visibility and stays legal. Film is cut by hand on each panel. The windshield is left clear apart from a top strip if requested.
