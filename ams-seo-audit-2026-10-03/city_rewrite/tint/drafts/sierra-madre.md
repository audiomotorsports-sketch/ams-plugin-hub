### How much of the heat does ceramic film actually stop?

Most of the infrared band, which is the part of sunlight that heats surfaces. The visible light is handled by the shade you choose. In practice the car starts much cooler after parking and the air conditioning catches up quickly.

### Can a Sierra Madre driver make it a single trip?

Yes. Book a time, bring the car, and it is tinted the same day. Wait in the shop or go get lunch and come back. If you want other work done at the same time, say so when you book.

### Does tint affect how well I can see through the rear glass?

A dark rear shade reduces what you see through the back window a little, especially at night. Many drivers choose a medium shade there and rely on mirrors. The front doors keep a light film, so forward vision is barely changed.

### Is the film safe for rear defroster lines?

Yes. Film goes on top of the defroster grid and does not affect it. Removing old film from a rear window is where defroster lines get damaged, which is why that job needs experienced hands. Your rear window will clear exactly as it did before the tint went on.
