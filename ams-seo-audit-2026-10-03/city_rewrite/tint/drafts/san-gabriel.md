### What does ceramic film do that standard film does not?

It rejects most infrared heat. Standard film darkens the glass and blocks UV; ceramic film does both and also stops the heat that warms seats and the dash. At the same shade the ceramic car is noticeably cooler. For a San Gabriel car parked outside, that is the difference worth paying for.

### Can I choose different shades for different windows?

Yes. The fronts must stay light under California law, and behind the driver you can go as dark as you want. A light ceramic front with a darker rear is a common combination. Tell us the look you are after and we will match the shades so the car reads as one piece from the outside.

### Does tint keep the interior from fading?

Yes. Both films block nearly all UV, which is what bleaches fabric and cracks vinyl. Ceramic film also lowers the surface temperature inside, which helps leather and plastics age more slowly. On a car that sits in San Gabriel sun all day, that is years of extra life for the interior.
