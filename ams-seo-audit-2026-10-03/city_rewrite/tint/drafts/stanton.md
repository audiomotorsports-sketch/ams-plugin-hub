### What should I tell you when I book from Stanton?

### Is Solar Ceramic noticeably cooler than regular Solar film?

Yes. Both block UV, but the ceramic layer rejects most infrared heat as well, which is the part of sunlight that warms seats and the dash. At the same darkness, the ceramic car is clearly more comfortable after sitting in the sun.

### Will tint interfere with my phone or radio signal?

No. Neither Solar nor Solar Ceramic contains metal, so phone, GPS and radio reception are unaffected. That is one reason ceramic film has replaced older metallic films. Toll transponders and keyless entry are unaffected as well, so nothing about the car changes except how it feels inside.

### How long does the film need before I use the windows?

A few days, so the moisture under the film can dry and the adhesive can cure. We will give you the exact wait when you pick up the car. After that, the windows are used normally. A little haze during that period is normal and clears on its own.
