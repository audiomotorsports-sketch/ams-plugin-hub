## Hermosa Beach Tesla and tint questions

### Do you tint the full rear glass on a Tesla Model 3 in one piece?

Yes. The Model 3 and Model Y rear window is one large curved pane, and we shrink and fit a single sheet of film to it rather than splitting it with a seam. That takes longer and more care, which is the reason Tesla owners from Hermosa Beach drive to Carson for it.

### Why come from Hermosa Beach to Carson for Tesla window tint?

Because the installation is what you are buying. Tesla glass is big, curved and expensive to replace, so the film needs to be hand-formed indoors by people who do it daily. We are 8.1 miles away, the job is usually finished the same day, and the result looks like it left the factory that way.

### Will tint interfere with Tesla cameras or sensors?

No. Window film goes on the side and rear glass, not over the camera housings, and it does not affect the car's cameras, radar or key detection. If you want the windshield strip done, we look at the car first and quote that at the shop. The roof glass is already dark.

### Which film is best for a car that sits at the beach all day?

Solar Ceramic. It rejects far more heat than the standard film at the same shade, so a car left in a beach lot warms up slower and cools down faster once the air conditioning runs. Both films block nearly all UV, which protects the seats and dash from fading.

### How dark can the front windows be on my Tesla?

California keeps the front side windows mostly clear, and we stay within that line on every car, Tesla or otherwise. The rear doors and the rear glass can be darker. We show you the options on sample glass and write the chosen coverage on the ticket before the work begins.
