## What Lawndale drivers ask about window tint

### What is the legal window tint for front windows in California, in plain words?

The two front door windows must let most of the light through, so only a very light film is allowed there. The windshield can only take a narrow strip at the top. The rear doors and back window have no shade limit. We fit the front shade that passes and show you the sample before cutting.

### Can you remove old window tint before installing new film?

### Does ceramic window tint look different from regular tint?

At the same shade, no. Ceramic looks the same from outside and stays clear from inside. The difference is heat: ceramic rejects far more infrared, so the cabin and the seats stay cooler on a parked car. Both films block UV, which is what fades the dash and upholstery over time.
