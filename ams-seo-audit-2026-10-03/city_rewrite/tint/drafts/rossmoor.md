## Rossmoor tint questions

### Is ceramic tint safe for a car with kids in the back seat?

Yes, and it is the film we suggest for family cars. Ceramic window tint blocks nearly all UV and much of the infrared heat, so the rear seats, buckles and armrests stay cooler on a hot day. There is nothing in the film that affects air quality or the child seat anchors.

### How dark can I go on the rear windows of my SUV in Rossmoor?

As dark as you like. California limits only the front side windows and the windshield; rear doors, quarter glass and the back window can be a full privacy shade. We show the options on sample glass, note your choice on the ticket and install exactly that.

### Why drive 11 miles from Rossmoor instead of a closer tint shop?

Because the installation decides whether the tint still looks right in five years. Our film is cut by hand on your glass, formed to the curve and applied in a dust-controlled bay, and the shop has done this every day for a long time. The drive is about twenty-five minutes.

### Can you tint a Tesla Model Y for a Rossmoor family?

Yes. The Model Y rear window is one large curved pane and we tint it in a single sheet with no seam. The front doors stay within the California limit, the rear doors can go darker, and the glass roof is quoted at the shop once we see the car in person.

### What should I do after the tint is installed?

Leave the windows up for a few days so the film cures to the glass. Light haze or small water spots are normal in that period and clear up on their own. After that, clean the inside of the glass with a soft cloth and an ammonia-free cleaner. We go over this before you leave.
