### How much heat does ceramic window tint block compared with regular film?

Considerably more. Standard film blocks UV and adds privacy; the ceramic layer in Solar Ceramic also rejects most infrared energy, which is the part that heats seats and dashboards. At the same shade, ceramic leaves the cabin noticeably cooler. On a hot Santa Ana afternoon that gap is easy to feel.

### Does tinting affect the window seals or the door electronics?

No. Film is applied to the inside of the glass and nothing is removed from the door except, on some cars, a trim piece that goes back on. Seals, regulators and sensors are untouched. Power windows, locks and speakers work exactly as they did before, and the door panels go back on the way they came off.

### How light do the front windows have to stay on a car from Santa Ana?

Front side windows must let most of the visible light through, so they get a light film. The windshield can only carry a strip across the top. Rear doors and back glass are unrestricted, so those can be as dark as you like.
