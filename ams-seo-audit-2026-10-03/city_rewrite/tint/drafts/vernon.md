## Vernon fleet tinting questions

### Can a Vernon company book window tinting for a whole fleet?

Yes. Send the vehicle list with year, make and model and the shade you want, and we schedule the units back to back so the fleet is not off the road for long. Every vehicle gets film hand-cut on its own glass, and the coverage is logged so the fleet looks uniform.

### Is it worth sending company vehicles from Vernon to Carson for tint?

At 12.1 miles, yes. A tint job on a fleet vehicle has to survive years of daily use, and that depends on the installation: film formed to the glass, edges tucked, applied indoors away from dust. Doing it once properly costs less than having a bubbled job stripped and redone.

### What shade can go on a commercial van?

The front doors follow the same California rule as any car and stay mostly clear. Rear and side cargo glass, if the van has it, can be a dark privacy shade so nobody can see the load. We will recommend a shade for the fronts that cuts heat without risking a ticket.

### Does window tint keep a delivery truck cab cooler?

Yes, especially the ceramic film. A driver opening and closing the door all day loses the air conditioning constantly, and ceramic window tint slows how fast the cab reheats between stops. Both of our films also block nearly all UV, which protects the dash and seats.

### Can you add GPS tracking or a dash camera during the tint visit?

Yes. GPS tracker installation and dash camera installation are done in the same shop, so a fleet vehicle can get all three in one appointment. Wiring is hidden and tied into the vehicle cleanly so nothing dangles under the dash. Mention it when you book and we will plan the time.
