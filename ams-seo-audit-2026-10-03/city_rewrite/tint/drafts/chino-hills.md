### Will ceramic tint look mirrored or dark from the outside?

No. Solar Ceramic comes in the same shades as Solar and has a neutral, non-reflective look. The heat rejection comes from the ceramic layer, not from darkness, so you can run a light legal shade on the fronts and still get the cooling benefit on every window.
