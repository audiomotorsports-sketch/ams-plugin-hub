## Lynwood drivers ask

### What shade of tint is legal on the front windows of a car from Lynwood?

California lets you put a light film on the front door windows as long as most of the light still gets through. In practice that means a very light shade that reduces glare and UV without looking dark. The rear doors and back window have no such limit, so those can be darker.

### Can you fix a tint job that was done too dark on the fronts?

Yes. We remove the old film, clean the adhesive off the glass and install a legal shade on the front doors. Removal takes extra time and is quoted once we see how the old film comes off, since some aged film separates in small pieces.

### Should I wait at the shop or drop the car off?

Either works. A four-door takes a couple of hours, which many Lynwood customers simply wait out in the lobby. If you have things to do, leave the car with us in the morning and we will call when it is done. Just book ahead so the bay is ready.

### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic?

Both are quality films installed the same way. Solar is a dyed film that cuts glare, adds privacy and blocks UV. Solar Ceramic adds infrared rejection, so the cabin stays cooler and the steering wheel is not hot to the touch. If the car parks in sun, ceramic is worth the difference.

### Does window tint come with a risk of bubbles later on?

Not when the glass is cleaned properly and the film is fitted indoors. Bubbles and peeling usually come from dust under the film or edges that were cut short. We prep each window, cut the film on a glass board and finish the edges at the gasket so it stays flat for years.
