## Manhattan Beach questions about ceramic tint

### Is ceramic window tint worth the extra cost for a Manhattan Beach car?

If the car is parked outside most of the day, yes. Ceramic film cuts the heat you feel through the glass far more than a standard film at the same shade, so the cabin and the leather stay cooler. For a garaged car driven mostly at night, the standard Solar film does the job.

### How far is the shop from Manhattan Beach?

About 9.3 miles, which is around twenty minutes outside rush hour. You are heading to Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, Carson. Some of our customers come from over 100 miles away for window tint, so Manhattan Beach is a neighbor by comparison.

### Does ceramic tint have to be dark to work?

No. That is the main advantage. Ceramic window tint blocks heat through its ceramic layer rather than through darkness, so a light, legal shade on the front doors still keeps the heat down. You choose the look; the heat rejection comes along regardless of how dark you go.

### Will my window tint bubble or turn purple over time?

Not with the films we install and not with film that is applied correctly. Purple fade comes from cheap dyed film; bubbles come from dirt or poor adhesion during installation. Our bay is indoors, the glass is cleaned properly, and the film is hand-fitted to the edge.

### Can I wait at the shop while my tint is installed?
