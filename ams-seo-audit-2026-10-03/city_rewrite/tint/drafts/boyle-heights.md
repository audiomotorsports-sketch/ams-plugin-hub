### Do you tint the glass roof on a Tesla?

We can, and we quote it in the store after looking at the car. Roof panels are large and the shade choice depends on how much light you want in the cabin. The side and rear glass are priced as a normal job; the roof is a separate line item.

### Does Tesla window tint affect the car's cameras or sensors?

No. Film goes on the side and rear glass only and does not touch the camera housings, radar or key sensors. The front side windows stay within the California limit, which also keeps the driver-monitoring and auto-dimming features working as designed.

### Can you tint an older car with flat glass and rubber seals?

Yes. Older cars are often easier on the curves and harder on the seals, which can be brittle. We cut the film by hand on the actual glass, work the edges carefully and avoid prying on old rubber. Bring it by and we will look before we quote.
