### Why do some tint jobs bubble or peel and how do you avoid it?

Bubbles come from dirt trapped under the film or a rushed application, and peeling comes from film cut short of the edge. Each window here is cleaned, the film is cut on that glass, applied wet and squeegeed to the gasket. Then the car cures a few days with windows up.

### Is ceramic window tint worth the extra money for privacy tint?

If heat matters, yes. Privacy is about shade, and both films give you that. Ceramic adds infrared rejection, so a dark car parked at the curb is far cooler inside at pickup. As of October 2026 it is $329 installed on a sedan versus $249 for Solar.

### Can you tint just the rear windows and leave the fronts alone?

Yes. Plenty of customers tint the rear doors and back glass only, either to keep the fronts factory or to add them later. It is quoted at the shop as a partial job. If you later want the fronts done to the legal shade, the film is matched to what is already on.
