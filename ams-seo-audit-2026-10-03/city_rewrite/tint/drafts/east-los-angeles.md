## East Los Angeles drivers ask

### Why did the tint on my car turn purple and bubble?

Cheap dyed film loses its dye under years of sun and shifts purple, and weak adhesive lets go at the edges and in the middle, which makes bubbles. Neither can be fixed in place. The film has to come off and be replaced with a quality film laid on clean glass.

### Does removing old tint damage the rear defroster?

Not when it is done carefully. The defroster lines sit on the inside of the rear glass under the film, so the old film is peeled slowly and the adhesive is softened and lifted rather than scraped hard. We check the lines before and after so you know they still work.

### Can an East Los Angeles driver get removal and new tint done the same day?

Usually yes. Removal adds time, especially if the old adhesive is stubborn, so we book a longer slot. Mention the old film when you call so the day is planned right. The car leaves with fresh film on clean glass, ready to drive.

### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic for a daily driver?

Both are hand-cut and installed the same way and come in the same shades. Solar handles privacy, glare and UV. Solar Ceramic adds infrared heat rejection, which is the difference between a warm cabin and a hot one after the car sits in the sun.
