## Questions from Commerce drivers

### Can a Commerce business book window tinting for several vehicles at once?

Yes. Tell us the vehicle count and models and we will schedule them in a block, over one day or a few, so the fleet keeps working. Every unit gets privacy film on the rear glass, a legal light shade on the front doors and the same hand-cut fit.

### Is there a legal limit on tint for commercial vans and trucks?

The front side windows must let most of the light through, and the windshield only takes a strip at the top. Rear doors, cargo windows and the back glass have no limit. We install to those rules on every vehicle, so a driver is not stopped over the glass.

### Should a fleet get Solar or Solar Ceramic window tint?

Solar for privacy and a uniform look at the lowest cost. Solar Ceramic when the vehicles park in the sun between jobs, because it blocks the infrared heat that makes a cab miserable. Many fleets choose Solar on cargo glass and ceramic on the driver's door.

### Do you do GPS tracker installation for Commerce fleets?

Yes. A GPS tracker is hidden and hard-wired on each vehicle during the same visit as the window tinting, so a missing truck can be located. Dash camera installation can be added, with the wiring tucked away so the cab stays clean.
