## Huntington Beach Tesla and truck tint questions

### Do you tint lifted trucks from Huntington Beach?

Yes. A lifted truck is tinted the same as any other truck; the glass does not care about the suspension. Door glass is cut by hand on the window, the rear slider is done panel by panel so it still opens, and the back glass is heat-formed in one piece. Trucks are quoted at the shop.

### Is the Tesla rear window tinted in one piece?

Yes. Model 3 and Model Y rear glass is one large curved pane, and we form a single sheet of film to it by hand rather than splitting it. The front doors stay within the California limit, the rear doors can go darker, and the roof glass is already dark from the factory.

### Is Carson too far from Huntington Beach for a tint job?

No. It is about 19.3 miles, roughly forty minutes outside rush hour, and the car is normally finished the same day. The point of the trip is an installation done by hand in a clean bay by a shop that does this daily. People have come from Temecula for the same reason.

### Which film is best for a car parked at the beach all day?

Solar Ceramic. It rejects far more heat than the standard film at the same shade, so the cabin stays cooler and the air conditioning recovers faster. Both films block nearly all UV, so the interior is protected either way; ceramic is about the heat.

### Can I tint the front windows on my truck darker because it sits higher?

No. California applies the same front-window rule to trucks as to cars, regardless of ride height. We keep the fronts within the limit, which still cuts glare and, with ceramic film, blocks heat. The rear doors, slider and back glass can be as dark as you like.
