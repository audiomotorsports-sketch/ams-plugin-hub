## What South Gate drivers want to know

### Can you tint a three-row SUV from South Gate, and how long does it take?

Yes. A three-row SUV has more glass and bigger curved panels than a sedan, so it takes longer, often most of a morning. Drop it off when we open and plan to pick it up in the afternoon. We measure and quote each SUV model individually because the glass count varies.

### Is there a shade that keeps the back seat cooler for kids?

A darker ceramic film on the rear doors and quarter glass is the usual answer. Ceramic blocks UV and the infrared heat that makes a child seat hot to the touch, and the darker shade keeps direct sun off faces. The fronts stay lighter to meet the California rule.

### Do you do pickup trucks with a sliding rear window?

We do. The slider is tinted as separate pieces so it still opens, and the small quarter panes on an extended or crew cab are cut individually. Trucks are quoted per model since the glass layout differs from one cab style to another.

### How do I know the tint will not peel on a big tailgate window?

Peeling almost always starts at an edge that was cut short or at dust trapped under the film. We shrink the film to the curve of the tailgate glass on a glass board, lay it indoors, and finish the edge at the gasket so there is no lip for air to get under.
