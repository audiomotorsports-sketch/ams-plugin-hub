## Bell window tinting FAQ

### Does window tinting really cut glare on the drive from Bell?

Yes. Tinted side and rear glass reduces the light coming in from low angles and stops reflections from neighboring cars and buildings. The windshield stays clear by law apart from a top strip, so the relief comes from the doors and back glass, which is exactly where the film goes.

### How long is the trip from Bell to the tint shop?

About 11.3 miles, roughly twenty-five minutes outside rush hour. Head for Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, Carson. Call or text before you leave and the bay will be ready. Customers have come from San Diego for the same job, so this is a short drive.

### Will tint make it harder to see out at night?

Not with a sensible shade. The front doors stay light under California law, and we help you choose a rear shade that looks good without turning the back window into a wall at night. Many commuters pick a medium shade behind and are happy with it in both directions.

### How is window tint installed at your shop?

The glass is cleaned, the film is laid over the outside and cut by hand to each window's exact shape, heat-shrunk to the curve, then moved inside and applied with the edges tucked under the seals. Everything happens in an indoor bay so dust stays out of the film.

### Can you tint a work truck for a Bell business?

Yes. The cab doors get the legal shade, sliding rear windows are done panel by panel so they still open, and any rear glass can go darker. If you have more than one truck, we can schedule them together. Truck and fleet pricing is quoted at the shop.
