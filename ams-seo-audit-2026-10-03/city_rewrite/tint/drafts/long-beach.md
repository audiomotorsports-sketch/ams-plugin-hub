## Questions from Long Beach drivers

### Is ceramic window tint worth it for a car parked near the water in Long Beach?

Yes, if heat and glare bother you. Ceramic film rejects a lot more infrared heat than a dyed film at the same shade, so a black car parked in the open comes back cooler. It also cuts the reflected glare off water and light pavement. Solar Ceramic is $329 installed as of October 2026.

### Do I need an appointment to get window tinting from Long Beach?

We ask for one. Audio MotorSports is a single-bay shop and tint jobs are scheduled so the glass is clean and the bay is yours when you arrive. Call (310) 513-8800 or text (213) 429-1092, pick a morning or midday slot, and the car is usually ready the same afternoon.

### What does a car window tint installation actually involve at LA Car Tint?

The glass is cleaned inside and out, the film is cut by hand on the window itself, shrunk to the curve with heat, then applied wet and squeegeed to the edge of the gasket. Door panels are not pulled and nothing is drilled. The film cures over a few days with the windows up.

### How dark can the rear windows go on a Long Beach commuter?

As dark as you want behind the driver. California limits the front door windows to a light shade and leaves the rear doors and back glass open, so a common setup is light fronts for legality and a deep rear for privacy. We show you the shades side by side before cutting.

### Will ceramic film block my phone, toll tag or keyless entry?

No. Ceramic window tint has no metal layer, so it does not interfere with phone reception, FasTrak transponders, GPS or key fobs. That is one of the reasons it has replaced older metalized films for newer cars with antennas built into the glass.
