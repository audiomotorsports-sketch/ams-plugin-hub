## Azusa drivers ask

### Can an Azusa driver make the Carson tint trip in one day without hassle?

Yes. Book a morning slot, drive down, and the vehicle is ready in a few hours. Wait at the shop or leave it and run errands. The round trip plus the install fits in a normal day, and the windows just need to stay up for a few days afterward while the film cures.

### Does the film hold up on a truck that sits in foothill sun all day?

It does when the install is right. The film is cut on the glass so it reaches the seal, heat-set so it bonds flat, and Solar Ceramic in particular keeps its color under heavy sun. Film that fails early is almost always a pattern-cut job with loose edges.

### Will ceramic tint cut the glare on an early morning commute from Azusa?

Yes. Any tint lowers the light coming through the side glass, which takes the sting out of a low sun and out of headlights in the mirror at night. Solar Ceramic adds infrared heat rejection on top. The fronts stay legal and light; the rear glass does the heavy lifting.

### What is included in a standard tint job and what is extra?

Standard covers the four door windows and the rear glass, with fronts at the legal shade. Sunroofs, Tesla roof panels, truck sliders, old film removal and oversized vehicles are extra and quoted at the shop after we see them. Tell us the vehicle at booking so the quote is right.
