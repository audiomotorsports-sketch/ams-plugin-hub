## Bradbury owners ask

### Will ceramic tint change the look of a luxury car from Bradbury?

Only if you want it to. Solar Ceramic is neutral and non-reflective, available from a very light shade to full privacy. The heat rejection comes from the ceramic layer rather than from darkness, so a barely-there tint on a light-colored sedan still keeps the cabin cooler.

### How does the shop keep dust out from under the film?

Clean glass, clean film, clean technique. Each window is scrubbed and scraped before the film is laid, the film is cut on the glass so it is handled less, and it is squeegeed from the center out. The occasional speck is addressed before the car leaves, not after.

### Is a lightly tinted front window still legal in California?

Yes. The front doors must pass most of the light, so a light film that cuts glare and ultraviolet is within the rule and still looks factory. The windshield is left alone. Behind the front doors you can go as dark as you like.
