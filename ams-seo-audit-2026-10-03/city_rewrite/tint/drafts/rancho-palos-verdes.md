## Rancho Palos Verdes owners ask about Tesla and ceramic tint

### How is Tesla window tint different from tinting a regular car?

The rear glass on most Teslas is one large, deeply curved piece, so the film has to be heat-shrunk to that shape and laid as a single panel without seams. There is also electronics and speakers near the glass that get protected during the wet application. It is a job for a shop that does it regularly.

### Is it worth driving from Rancho Palos Verdes to Carson for a Tesla tint?

Nine miles and about twenty minutes buys an installation done by hand on your own glass, with the big rear panel laid in one piece and the front doors kept legal. People drive in from over 100 miles for the same bay. Compared with redoing a cheap job, the trip is nothing.

### Can you tint the glass roof on a Model 3 or Model Y?

Ask when you book. Roof panels are handled as a store visit because the curve and size vary by model and year, and the glass has to be seen in person. Rear and side windows are priced after we see the car, since Tesla glass is not standard sedan glass.

### Will ceramic window tint interfere with the Tesla cameras or sensors?

No. Ceramic film carries no metal, so it does not block radio signals, GPS, phone reception or the key card. Cameras look through the glass areas that are left clear per the car design, and the windshield itself only gets a strip at the top if anything.

### What does Solar Ceramic cost compared with Solar on a standard car?

As of October 2026, Solar is $249 installed and Solar Ceramic is $329 installed for a four-door sedan with the rear glass. The extra eighty dollars buys much stronger heat rejection at the same shade. Tesla glass and large SUVs are quoted in the bay after the vehicle is measured.
