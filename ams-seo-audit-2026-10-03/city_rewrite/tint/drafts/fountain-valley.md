## Fountain Valley owners ask

### What is special about tinting the rear glass on a Tesla?

It is one tall, deeply curved piece. The film has to be heat-formed to that curve on the glass and then laid as a single panel, with no seam across the middle. A shop that splits it into two pieces leaves a line you will see every time you look in the mirror.

### Can a Fountain Valley minivan get all the rear glass tinted dark?

Yes. Rear sliding-door windows, quarter panes and the hatch glass can all run a privacy shade, since California only limits the front doors and windshield. Each pane is cut on the van so the sliders still drop and seal and the whole side reads one shade.

### What cuts the heat in a Tesla if the roof is glass?

The roof glass is already dark from the factory, so the heat that gets in mostly comes through the side and rear windows. Ceramic film on those panes blocks infrared and keeps the cabin cooler. We fit it by hand and quote after seeing the car.

### What happens to old bubbled tint before new film goes on?

It comes off completely. The old film is peeled, the adhesive is lifted off the glass, the rear defroster lines are protected, and the window is cleaned to bare before new film is cut and laid. Removal takes extra time and is quoted at the shop, so mention it when you book.
