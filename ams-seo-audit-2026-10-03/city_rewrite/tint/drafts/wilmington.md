### Is Wilmington close enough to get window tint done and go home the same afternoon?

### How dark can I legally tint the front windows on a car registered in Wilmington?

California treats front door glass differently from the rear. The front side windows have to stay light enough that most of the light still passes through, so we keep them at the legal level and put the darker shades on the rear doors and back glass. We will explain the options at the counter.

### What does ceramic window tint actually do that regular film does not?

Ceramic film is built to reject infrared heat, not just visible light. Two cars can look the same shade from outside, but the ceramic one stays noticeably cooler inside and the dash is not radiating heat at you. Regular dyed film cuts glare and adds privacy, but it does far less for heat.

### Do you cut the film on my windows or off the car?

We cut and shape the film on a glass board before it ever touches your car. That keeps blades away from your paint and seals, and it lets us shrink each panel to the curve of the window so the edges finish clean at the gasket instead of leaving a visible light line.

### Can you tint a Wilmington work truck with a rear slider?

We can. Rear sliders, quarter glass and tall cab windows are measured and quoted per vehicle, since the glass is different from a sedan. If the truck sits outside all day, ceramic film is the better choice for keeping the cab bearable when you climb back in after a shift.
