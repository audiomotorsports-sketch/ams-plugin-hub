Behind the driver, as dark as you want: the rear doors and back window have no shade limit in California. The two front door windows must stay light, and the windshield only takes a strip at the top. The usual privacy setup is a deep rear shade with legal light fronts.

### Does dark window tint make it hard to see out at night?

Not with a quality film. Solar and Solar Ceramic stay optically clear, so a dark rear shade looks dim from outside but you still see mirrors and traffic fine from the seat. Very dark fronts would be a problem, which is one reason California keeps them light.

### How long does a window tinting appointment take from Walnut Park?

### What makes a hand-cut tint install hold up longer?

Film cut on the glass follows the real shape of that window and reaches the seal, so there is no short edge to catch and lift when the window rolls down. Combined with a clean application and a few days of curing with windows up, the film stays put for years.
