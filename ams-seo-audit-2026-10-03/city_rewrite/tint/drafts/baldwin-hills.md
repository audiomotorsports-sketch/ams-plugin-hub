## Baldwin Hills window tinting questions

### What is the legal window tint limit in California, in plain words?

The front side windows have to let most of the light through, the windshield can only take a narrow strip across the top, and the rear doors and back window can be as dark as you want. We install exactly to that, show you the shades on sample glass, and put the coverage in writing.

### Can I still cut heat on the front windows if they have to stay light?

Yes, with ceramic window tint. The ceramic layer rejects infrared heat regardless of how dark the film is, so a light legal shade on the front doors still keeps the cabin cooler. This is the main reason Baldwin Hills drivers choose Solar Ceramic over the standard film.

### Is it worth driving from Baldwin Hills to Carson for tint?

### Will you remove old purple tint before installing new film?

Yes. Old, faded or bubbled film has to come off cleanly before new film goes on, including the adhesive left behind on the glass and around the rear defroster lines. Removal takes extra time and is quoted at the shop once we see how the old film is holding on.

### How long does window tinting take?

A few hours for a four-door car. The time goes into cleaning the glass, hand-cutting and heat-forming each piece, applying it and checking every edge. You can wait on site or drop the car and come back. Book ahead by phone or text so the bay is ready when you arrive.
