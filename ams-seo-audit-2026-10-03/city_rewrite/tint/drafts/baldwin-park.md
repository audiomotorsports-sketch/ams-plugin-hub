### Does tint stop the dashboard and seats from fading?

Mostly, yes. Quality window film blocks the large majority of ultraviolet light, which is what bleaches upholstery and dries out dash plastic. A truck or SUV you expect to keep for years holds its interior far better behind tinted glass.
