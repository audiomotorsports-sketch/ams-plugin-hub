### How soon after buying a new car can it be tinted?

Right away. New glass is the easiest surface to tint because there is no old film or adhesive to deal with. Bring the car in as soon as you have it; the only thing to plan around is keeping the windows up for a few days after the install while the film sets.

### How long does the film take to cure after a La Habra tint job?

The film is dry to the touch when you leave, but the slip solution under it needs several days to fully evaporate. During that time keep the windows up. Small hazy spots or moisture pockets in the first days are normal and clear on their own as it cures.

### Does hand-cutting on the glass matter on a new car?

Yes. Even a brand-new window has a unique edge shape, and cutting the film on the pane means it follows that shape exactly instead of a pattern that may fall short by a hairline. The result is film that reaches the seal on every side and never shows a bright strip.
