### How does ceramic film differ from the standard Solar film?

Both block UV and add privacy. The ceramic layer in Solar Ceramic rejects a much larger share of infrared heat at the same darkness, so the cabin stays cooler. If your car sits in the sun all day, ceramic is the one to pick.

### Can I keep a light shade on the front doors and still get heat rejection?

Yes. Heat rejection in ceramic film comes from the ceramic particles, not the darkness. A light ceramic film on the front windows stays legal in California and still cuts most of the infrared energy, while the rear glass can go as dark as you like.

### What happens to window tint when my lease ends?

Film goes on the inside of the glass and comes off cleanly when the car is returned, leaving no marks or holes. Many West Los Angeles drivers tint a lease anyway because three years of cooler seats and less glare is worth it.
