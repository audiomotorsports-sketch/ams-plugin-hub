## El Monte drivers ask

### Will tint on a pickup cab survive doors that get slammed all day?

Yes, when the edges are done right. The film is trimmed to sit under the window seal and heat-set to the glass, so there is no loose edge for the seal to catch and lift. Pattern-cut film that stops short of the edge is what peels on work trucks.

### Can an El Monte truck get the rear slider tinted and still open?

It can. The two fixed panes and the sliding panel are filmed as separate pieces, each trimmed so the slider clears its track. The job takes more care than a one-piece rear window, which is why trucks with sliders are quoted at the shop.

### Does ceramic film protect a truck's interior as well as cool it?

Both. Solar Ceramic stops infrared heat, which is the comfort part, and like all quality film it stops almost all ultraviolet, which is what cracks dashboards and fades seats. On a truck you plan to run for ten years, the interior protection alone earns its keep.
