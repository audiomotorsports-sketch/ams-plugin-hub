## Lennox tint questions

### Do you tint Teslas from Lennox, and what is different about them?

Yes, all models. The rear window on a Model 3 or Model Y is a single large curved piece, so the film has to be heat-shrunk carefully to sit flat without creases. The glass roof is already dark, so the film stays on the sides and back. Allow a little more time than a standard sedan.

### Does the glass roof on a Tesla need tint?

It does not. Tesla roof glass is dark from the factory, so the film goes on the side and rear windows, where most of the heat and glare get in. Because window shapes vary by model, we measure them on the car and quote in person rather than over the phone.

### Will ceramic tint help a car that heats up fast in a Lennox parking lot?

That is where ceramic shows its value. It blocks a large share of infrared heat, so the cabin does not climb as high while parked and cools down faster when you get in. On a Tesla that also means less energy spent on climate control during the drive home.

### How long does Tesla window tint take compared to a regular car?

Plan on a few hours for a Tesla, somewhat longer than a typical sedan, because of the size and curve of the rear glass and the extra care it needs. You can drop the car in the morning and pick it up in the afternoon, or wait if you prefer.

### Is the drive from Lennox worth it for a tint job?

For a car with this much glass, the install quality matters more than the film brand on the box. We cut each piece on a glass board, fit it indoors and finish the edges at the gasket. Twenty minutes each way is a small price for a job that does not need redoing.
