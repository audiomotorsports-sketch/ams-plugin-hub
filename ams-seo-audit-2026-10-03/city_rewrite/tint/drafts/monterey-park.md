### Is ceramic film clearer than regular tint?

At the same shade they look alike from outside. The difference is what the film does: ceramic rejects most infrared heat while standard film mainly darkens and blocks UV. Neither contains metal, so neither has a mirrored look. Visibility out of the car is the same with either.

### What happens if I want the fronts darker than California allows?

We keep the front side windows at the legal light shade and the windshield to a top strip. Anything darker risks a ticket and a removal order. The rear doors and back glass can be as dark as you like.

### How long does the car need to sit after tinting?

Drive it home right away. Just leave the windows up for a few days so the film cures and the moisture underneath dries out. We will tell you the exact wait at pickup. A little moisture haze in the first days is normal and clears by itself as the film settles.
