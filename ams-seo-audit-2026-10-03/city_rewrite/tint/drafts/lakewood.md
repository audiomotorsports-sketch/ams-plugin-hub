## Lakewood tint questions

### What window tint is legal on a car in Lakewood?

The same rules apply across California: the front side windows must stay mostly clear, the windshield only takes a narrow strip at the top, and the rear doors and back window may be as dark as you want. We install to those limits every time and put the coverage in writing.

### Is it worth driving from Lakewood to Carson for window tinting?

At 7.7 miles, the drive is shorter than most people's commute, and the result is film cut on your glass by a shop that does this every day. People drive in from Temecula for the same appointment. A tint job that stays bubble-free for years is worth fifteen minutes each way.

### Will darker rear tint cause problems with my mirrors at night?

Not with the shades we recommend. Dark rear glass is legal in California because the side mirrors handle the view behind. We will show you the shades on sample glass in daylight so you can judge the look, and many Lakewood drivers choose a medium rear shade for exactly this reason.

### How does hand-cut window tint differ from pre-cut kits?

A pre-cut kit is a computer pattern that has to be trimmed short so it fits every car in the database, which leaves a light gap at the edges. Hand-cutting on your actual glass means the film reaches the gasket on each window. It takes longer and it is why the finished job looks like factory glass.

### Do you tint pickup trucks with sliding rear windows?

Yes. The slider panels are tinted individually so the window still opens cleanly, and the fixed side panes are done in place. Truck cab glass is quoted in the shop because the layouts vary so much. Bring the truck by and we will price it on the spot.
