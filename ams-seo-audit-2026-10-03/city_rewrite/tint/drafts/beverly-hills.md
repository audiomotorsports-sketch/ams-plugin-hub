## Beverly Hills owners ask

### Will ceramic tint look out of place on a high-end car from Beverly Hills?

No. Solar Ceramic is neutral in color and does not have the mirrored look of older metallic films. A light shade on the fronts and a moderate shade on the rear reads as factory glass. The heat rejection comes from the ceramic layer, so you do not need to go dark to get the benefit.

### Do you tint the glass roof on a Tesla or a panoramic sunroof?

Fixed roof glass, yes. A ceramic film on the roof panel cuts the heat that comes straight down, which side-window tint cannot address. Sliding sunroofs are evaluated at the car. Roof work is quoted at the shop after we see the panel.

### How is the film kept free of dust and specks during the install?

The glass is scrubbed and scraped, the film is cut on the window so it is handled less, and it is laid and squeegeed in a controlled way. Every pane is inspected before the car leaves. For a car you look at closely, that inspection is the difference.

### Can a Beverly Hills owner drop the car and have it collected the same day?

Yes. The install takes a few hours, so a morning drop-off is usually ready by afternoon. Some owners wait at the shop instead. Appointments are made by calling (310) 513-8800 or texting (213) 429-1092; tell us the model so the right time is set aside.

### Why trust a Carson shop with a Beverly Hills car?
