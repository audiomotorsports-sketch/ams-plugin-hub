### Which film should a Pomona driver pick for heat?

Solar Ceramic. Its ceramic layer rejects most infrared energy at any shade, so the cabin starts cooler and the air conditioning catches up sooner. Solar is the right choice if you mainly want privacy and glare control at a lower price.

### Will window tint stop my seats and dash from fading?

It slows fading dramatically. Both films block nearly all UV, the main cause of faded fabric and cracked vinyl. Ceramic film also lowers the surface temperature, which helps leather and plastics last longer. For a car that sits outside in Pomona, that protection pays for itself over a few summers.
