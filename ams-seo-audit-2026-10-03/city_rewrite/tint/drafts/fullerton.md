## What Fullerton owners want to know

### What makes a window tint job look factory instead of aftermarket?

Three things: the film is trimmed to the exact glass edge so there is no visible border, the shade on the fronts matches California law so it does not scream for attention, and the surface is free of dust and contamination before the film goes down. Skip any one and the car tells on you.

### Should a Fullerton student on a budget get Solar or Solar Ceramic?

If the car lives outdoors and you keep it for years, Solar Ceramic pays back in comfort and color stability. If the budget is tight and the main goal is privacy and glare, Solar does the job well. Either way the install is identical and the fronts stay legal.

### Does the shop tint only the rear windows if that is all I need?

Yes. Rear doors and back glass alone is a common job, especially for parents and anyone hauling gear. Tell us at booking so the time and quote match. You can always come back and add the fronts at the legal shade later.

### How far is the drive from Fullerton, and can I wait at the shop?

About 19.7 miles, roughly forty-five minutes outside rush hour. You can wait at the shop while the car is tinted or leave it and pick up later in the day. Appointments are set by calling (213) 429-1092 or texting (213) 429-1092.

### Why does hand-cutting film on the car matter?

Because every window is a slightly different shape, even on the same model year. Cutting on the glass means the film follows that exact edge instead of a stored pattern, so the fit is tight at the seal and there is no trimmed-short hairline. It takes longer, which is why cheaper installs skip it.
