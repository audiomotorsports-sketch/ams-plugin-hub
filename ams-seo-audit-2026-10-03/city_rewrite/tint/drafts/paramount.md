## Paramount tint questions, answered plainly

### Can LA Car Tint handle a small fleet of work vans from Paramount?

Yes. We schedule fleet vehicles one after another so your crew is not without trucks for long, and each van gets the same hand-cut film and tight edge as a private car. Pricing for vans and trucks is quoted per vehicle at the shop. Call (213) 429-1092 to set it up.

### Which window tint holds up best on a truck that parks outdoors all day in Paramount?

Solar Ceramic. A ceramic film rejects far more heat than a dyed film at the same shade, so the seats and steering wheel are not scorching at the end of a shift. It also will not fade to purple over years of sun. On a sedan Solar Ceramic runs $329 installed as of October 2026.

### Does window tinting protect the interior from sun damage?

Yes. Both Solar and Solar Ceramic block nearly all UV, which is what cracks dashboards and fades seats over time. Ceramic adds strong infrared heat rejection on top of that. For a work vehicle you plan to keep for years, tint is cheap protection for the cabin.

### How is the film installed so it does not peel on a hard-working door?

Each window is cleaned, the film is cut by hand on that exact piece of glass, heat-shrunk to the curve, then applied wet and squeegeed to the gasket edge. Nothing is drilled and no door panel is removed. After a few days of curing with the windows up, the film is bonded to the glass.

### Can I get a cargo van rear and side glass tinted darker than the front?

Yes. The rear doors and back glass of any vehicle can go as dark as you like for privacy over cargo. The front door windows stay at the light shade California allows. We mark which windows get which film before starting, so there is no surprise.
