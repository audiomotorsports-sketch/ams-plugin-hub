## Hacienda Heights ceramic tint questions

### Does ceramic window tint make a real difference in Hacienda Heights heat?

Yes. Standard film cuts glare and UV; ceramic film also rejects much of the infrared, which is the heat you feel on your skin through the glass. For a car parked outside in an inland afternoon that means a cooler cabin and less strain on the air conditioning. Most inland customers pick ceramic.

### Why would I drive from Hacienda Heights to Carson for tint?

Because the installation is what makes ceramic film work. Ours is cut by hand on your glass, heat-formed and laid in a dust-controlled bay by people who do it every day. The trip is about forty-five minutes outside rush hour. Customers have driven from Temecula for the same appointment.

### Can I keep the front windows light and still block heat?

Yes. That is the advantage of ceramic window tint: it blocks heat through the ceramic layer, not through darkness. The front doors stay light and legal under California rules and still reject infrared. The rear doors and back glass can go darker if you want privacy.

### What does a tint appointment involve?

We clean the glass, cut the film by hand on each window, heat-shrink it to the curve, apply it from inside and tuck the edges to the seals. It all happens indoors. A four-door takes a few hours, and you leave with aftercare instructions and a few days of windows-up.
