### Can I get privacy tint without the mirrored look?

Yes. Neither Solar nor Solar Ceramic contains metal, so there is no mirror effect. A dark rear shade gives privacy from outside while the view out stays normal, and the fronts keep a legal light film. The finish is a clean, even charcoal rather than a reflective one.

### Does ceramic film work on a convertible with the top up?

Yes. The side glass is tinted the same as any car, and the rear window is done if it is glass. The film blocks heat and UV whenever the windows are up; with the top down you get the benefit on the side glass only.

### Will tint be a problem when my West Hollywood lease ends?

No. Film is applied inside the glass and removed cleanly at turn-in, leaving nothing behind. Many drivers tint a lease anyway because three years of cooler seats and privacy is worth it. If the leasing company asks for removal, that is a quick job we can also handle.
