### Does ceramic tint block heat without going dark?

Yes. The heat rejection in Solar Ceramic comes from the ceramic layer, not from the shade. A light ceramic film on the front doors stays legal and still cuts most infrared energy, while the rear glass can be darker if you want privacy.

### Will window tint make it harder to see at night?

Front windows keep a light film, so forward and side vision at night is barely changed. Dark film behind the driver reduces what you see through the rear glass a little, which is why some drivers choose a medium shade there.

### How long before I can roll the windows down after tinting?

Give the film a few days to cure before lowering the windows. The water left under the film during installation needs that time to dry out. We will tell you the exact wait when you pick the car up. Leaving them up for that short stretch is what keeps the edges sealed for good.
