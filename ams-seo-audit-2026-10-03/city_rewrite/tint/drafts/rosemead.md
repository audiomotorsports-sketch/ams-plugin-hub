### How does ceramic film keep a car cooler?

The ceramic layer absorbs and reflects infrared energy before it reaches the cabin, and it does that at any shade. The glass stops radiating heat inward while the car is parked, so the interior starts cooler and the air conditioning wins faster.

### Is a darker tint always better for heat?

No. Darkness controls visible light and privacy; heat rejection comes from the film's construction. A light Solar Ceramic film blocks more heat than a dark standard film. Choose the shade for looks and legality, and the film type for heat.

### Can tint be put on a car that already has old film?

Yes, but the old film has to come off first, and that is extra work, especially on a rear window with defroster lines. Mention it when you book so the time is set aside. Removing it carefully protects the defroster lines and the glass, which is why it is worth having done here.
