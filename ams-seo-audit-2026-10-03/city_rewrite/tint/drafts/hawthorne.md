### Which is better for a daily commuter, Solar or Solar Ceramic window tint?

### How dark will my Hawthorne car look from the outside after tinting?

That depends on the shade you choose for the rear, which can be as dark as you like. The front doors stay light to meet the California rule, so from outside the car reads as light fronts and dark rears, the look most people want. We hold samples against your glass before cutting.

### How soon can I roll the windows down after ceramic window tint is installed?

Wait a few days. The film is applied wet and the moisture under it needs time to cure out, so the windows stay up for about three days depending on the weather. Small hazy spots during that time are normal and clear on their own. After that, treat the glass as usual.
