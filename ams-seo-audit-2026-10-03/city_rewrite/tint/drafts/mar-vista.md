## Mar Vista questions

### Is Carson too far from Mar Vista for a tint job?

Not at all; it is about half an hour. Customers come from more than 100 miles for this work. The film is hand-cut and sealed, and the reviews are public: Google 4.8 (650+ reviews) and Yelp 4.7 (770+ reviews), as of October 2026.

### Does window tint help a car that is parked on the street in Mar Vista?

Yes, two ways. Dark rear film hides whatever is inside from passers-by, and ceramic film keeps the cabin from overheating when there is no shade. Both films also block nearly all UV, so the interior does not fade. It is a small cost for a car that lives outdoors.

### What is the difference in price between Solar and Solar Ceramic?

As of October 2026, Solar is $249 installed and Solar Ceramic is $329 installed for a standard car. The extra buys a ceramic layer that rejects most infrared heat. Larger vehicles and Teslas are quoted at the shop. If heat is your problem, the ceramic is worth it.

### How soon can I wash the car after tinting?

The outside of the car can be washed right away, since the film is inside the glass. Leave the inside of the windows alone for a few days while the film cures, and do not roll the windows down until we tell you.

### Can I bring the car in and wait for it?

Yes. Book a time, drive down, and wait at the shop while the film goes on; most cars are done the same day. If you would rather drop it and come back, that works too. Tell us which you prefer when you book so we can plan the day around it.
