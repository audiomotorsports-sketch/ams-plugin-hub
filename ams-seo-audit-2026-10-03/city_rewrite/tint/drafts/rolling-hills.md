## Common questions from Rolling Hills

### Will ceramic tint protect the leather in a car that sits outside in Rolling Hills?

That is the main reason to choose it. Ceramic film blocks almost all UV, which is what bleaches leather and dries out dash plastics, and it also cuts the infrared heat that cooks the cabin. The interior stays closer to the condition it was in when the car was new.

### Can I get window tint that is barely noticeable but still blocks UV?

Yes. A light ceramic shade changes the look of the glass very little while still rejecting UV and a good share of heat. It is a common choice for the front doors, where the law requires a light shade anyway, paired with something darker on the rear.

### How is the film cut so the edges look clean?

We cut and heat-shrink the film on a glass board to match each window, then transfer it to the car. Cutting off the car keeps blades away from your seals and lets us finish the edge right at the gasket, so there is no thin line of light along the top.

### Do you tint Teslas from Rolling Hills, and what about the roof?

We tint every Tesla model, including the single-piece rear glass that requires careful shrinking to lie flat. The glass roof is already dark from the factory, so the film goes on the side and rear windows, and we quote that in person.

### What should I do in the first week after the tint goes on?

Keep the windows up for a few days so the film can bond. You may notice slight haze or small moisture pockets while it dries; that is normal and clears on its own. Clean the inside of the glass with a soft cloth and a mild cleaner, nothing abrasive.
