## Willowbrook tint questions, answered plainly

### Can I get the rear windows dark enough that people cannot see in from a Willowbrook street?

Yes. The rear doors and back glass can go to a dark shade that hides the interior in normal daylight. You can still see out at night because tint works by blocking light coming in, not by blocking your view. The front doors stay lighter to meet the California rule.

### Why do the front windows have to be lighter than the rear?

California requires the front side windows to let most of the light through so other drivers and police can see the driver. That is why nearly every tinted car in the state has lighter fronts and darker rears. We set the fronts to the legal level and darken the back to your taste.

### Does a dark tint make it harder to drive at night?

A very dark rear can make backing up in an unlit driveway slower, so we talk it through before choosing a shade. Most Willowbrook customers land on a shade that gives privacy in daylight while keeping the mirrors usable at night. Ceramic film stays clearer at the same darkness than cheap dyed film.

### Is window tint worth the short trip from Willowbrook instead of a mobile installer?

The difference is the bay. Film laid in a driveway picks up dust and wind, and that shows up as specks and lifting edges within months. Here the car is inside, the film is cut on a glass board, and the edges are finished to the gasket, so it is done once.

### How soon can I roll the windows down after tint?

Leave them up for a few days. The film needs time to bond to the glass, and rolling a window down early can catch the edge on the seal and peel it. Light haze or small water pockets in the first week are normal and clear on their own as the film dries.
