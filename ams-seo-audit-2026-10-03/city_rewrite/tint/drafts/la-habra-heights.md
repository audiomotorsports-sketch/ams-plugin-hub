## Questions from La Habra Heights

### Does ceramic tint make a real difference on a large SUV from La Habra Heights?

The bigger the glass area, the bigger the difference. Solar Ceramic blocks infrared heat across every tinted window, so a full-size SUV parked in open sun stays noticeably cooler than one with dyed film. The climate system has less to fight and the rear rows get comfortable sooner.

### Can a pickup's sliding rear window be tinted without jamming it?

Yes. The fixed panes and the sliding panel are filmed separately, with the edges trimmed so the slider still clears its track. It is more careful work than a sedan rear window, which is why it is quoted at the shop after we see the truck.

### If one window was replaced, can the new glass be tinted to match?

It can. We look at the existing film under daylight, pick the closest shade, and tint the replaced pane only. The goal is that nobody can tell which window is new. Bring the vehicle in and we will match it at the counter.
