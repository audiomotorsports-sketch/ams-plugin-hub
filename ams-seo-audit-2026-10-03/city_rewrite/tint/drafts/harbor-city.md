### What does the window tint installation involve?

We clean the glass, hand-cut the film directly on your windows, heat-shrink it to match the curve, then apply it inside with the edges tucked to the gasket. Nothing is pre-cut from a pattern and trimmed short. The whole job happens indoors in one bay, usually in a single visit.

### Can I tint my front windows legally in California?

California keeps front side windows mostly clear, so the darker shades go on the rear doors and back glass. We will tell you plainly what passes at the counter, write the coverage on your ticket before the car goes in, and never put a shade on the fronts that will get you pulled over.

### Do you tint Tesla glass for Harbor City owners?

We do. Tesla window tint is a regular job here: Model 3, Model Y, Model S and Model X. The big rear window is done in one piece, roof glass is quoted in the store once we see the car, and the front side shades stay within what California allows. Ask Nick for a Tesla slot.
