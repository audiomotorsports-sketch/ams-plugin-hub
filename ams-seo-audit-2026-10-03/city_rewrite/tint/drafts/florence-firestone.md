## Florence-Firestone window tinting questions

### Can you tint several work vans from Florence-Firestone in one week?

Yes. Tell us how many vehicles and what coverage you want, and we will schedule them back to back so your crew loses as little road time as possible. Each van gets the same hand-cut installation and the same shade so the fleet looks uniform. Fleet pricing is quoted at the shop.

### Does window tint help a cargo van that has no rear windows?

It helps the cab, which is where the driver sits. Tinting the two front doors to the legal shade cuts glare and UV, and ceramic film on those windows drops the heat coming through. For vans with rear or side glass, those panels can go darker for privacy over the cargo.

### What does the tint installation involve on a pickup truck?

We clean the cab glass, cut the film by hand directly on each window, heat-shrink it to the shape, then apply it from inside with the edges tucked. Sliding rear windows are handled panel by panel so they still open. The truck is usually ready the same day.

### How far is Florence-Firestone from the tint shop in Carson?

About 10 miles, roughly twenty minutes outside rush hour. The shop is Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, Carson. Call ahead and the bay is held for you. We have had customers drive in from as far as Temecula, so ten miles is an easy run.

### Is darker tint on the back of a work truck legal?

Yes. California allows the rear doors and the back window to be darker; only the front side windows and windshield have to stay mostly clear. We install to that rule on every vehicle and put the coverage in writing, so a driver who gets stopped can show exactly what was done.
