## Westminster window tinting questions

### Can you tint the sliding doors on a minivan?

Yes. Sliding-door glass is tinted like any other window, cut by hand on the pane and tucked to the seal, and the door still slides freely afterward. The quarter windows and rear hatch are formed with heat so there are no creases. Minivans are quoted at the shop because glass counts differ.

### How dark can the back rows of my SUV be in Westminster?

As dark as you want. California only restricts the front side windows and the windshield; the second and third rows and the rear glass can be a full privacy shade. We show you samples in daylight, write the choice on the ticket, and install exactly that.

### Is driving from Westminster to Carson for tint worth it?

At about 35 minutes outside rush hour, yes. The installation is the whole point: film cut on your actual glass, formed by hand, applied in a dust-controlled bay by a shop that does it every day. We have had customers come from San Diego for the same job.

### Does ceramic window tint help on a van with a lot of glass?

Especially on a van. More glass means more heat, and ceramic film rejects a large share of the infrared on every window it covers. The back rows stay cooler for the kids, and the front doors can be light and legal while still blocking heat.

### How long will the van be at the shop?

Plan on a good part of the day for a minivan or large SUV, because there is more glass to cut and form than on a sedan. You can wait in the shop or drop the van and come back when we text. Book ahead so the bay is ready when you arrive.
