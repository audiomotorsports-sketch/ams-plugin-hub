## Playa Vista Tesla owners ask

### Why should a Playa Vista Tesla owner drive to Carson for window tint?

Because the Tesla rear glass is a single curved sheet and the film has to be shaped and laid as one piece on the car, with the electronics near the glass protected. That is a skill, not a product. A half-hour drive to a bay that does it every week is cheaper than having it redone.

### Does ceramic window tint help a Tesla battery range in summer?

A little, indirectly. Ceramic film cuts the infrared heat coming through the glass, so the cabin starts cooler and the climate system works less. The bigger benefit is comfort: a glass-roofed car parked in the sun is noticeably better with ceramic film on the side and rear glass.

### Does the Tesla glass roof get tinted at LA Car Tint?

It is already dark from the factory, so the film goes on the side and rear glass instead. Those panes differ by model and year and have to be seen on the car, so ask when you book and bring the vehicle. The one-piece rear is our usual Tesla job.

### How long does Tesla window tint take and can I wait for it?

Longer than a sedan because the rear panel is large and curved and is shaped on the car. Book a morning slot; most Teslas are ready the same afternoon. You are welcome to wait in the shop, or drop the car and come back before 6:00.
