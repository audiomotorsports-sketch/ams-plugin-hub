### Why does hand-cut window tinting matter for a Lomita daily driver?

Film cut by hand on your own glass follows the exact curve of each window and reaches the gasket, so there is no bright hairline of light at the edge. A pattern trimmed by machine is a database shape that often stops short. The difference shows every time you park in the sun.

### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic window tint?

Both block UV and reduce glare. Solar is a quality film that darkens the glass and cools the cabin. Solar Ceramic uses a ceramic layer that rejects noticeably more heat at the same shade, so it is the pick for a dark car that parks outside all day. Ceramic costs eighty dollars more installed as of October 2026.

### Can I tint my front door windows legally?

Yes, with a light film. California allows only a very light shade on the front doors and nothing on the windshield beyond a strip at the top, while the rear doors and back glass can go as dark as you like. We fit the front shade that passes and tell you plainly what will not.

### How long does ceramic window tint take to install on a sedan?

Plan on a couple of hours for a four-door sedan, side glass plus the rear window. SUVs and Tesla glass take longer. Book a morning slot if you want it back the same afternoon. The film needs a few days to cure, so leave the windows up until then.
