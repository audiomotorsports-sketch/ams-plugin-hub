### Does window tint fade or turn purple in constant sun?

Cheap dyed film can. Solar and Solar Ceramic are color-stable and sit on the inside of the glass, so sun, salt and spray never touch the film itself. Keep the windows up for a few days after installation while the film cures, and it keeps its shade for years.

### Can you tint a convertible with frameless windows?

Yes. The film is cut directly on the frameless door glass so the top edge follows the glass exactly, with no frame to hide a short cut. Small rear quarter windows are handled the same way. Convertibles take longer than a sedan, so they are quoted on the car.
