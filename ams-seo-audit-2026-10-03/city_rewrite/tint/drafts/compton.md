## Questions from Compton drivers

### How long does window tint take for a sedan from Compton?

Plan on a couple of hours for a four-door sedan, which is short enough to wait for. If you have errands, drop the car at the Carson shop in the morning and pick it up when we call. Trucks, SUVs and Teslas can take longer because the glass is bigger and more curved.

### Will window tint help with glare on the drive out of Compton in the late afternoon?

It will. Tint on the side and rear glass takes the sting out of low sun reflecting off cars around you, and it reduces the mirror glare from headlights at night. The front doors stay at the lighter legal shade, so forward visibility is unchanged.

### Does tint keep the inside of the car cooler, or just darker?

Both, depending on the film. Any tint cuts some heat because it blocks part of the light, but ceramic film is designed to stop infrared, which is the part you feel. The result is a cabin that cools faster and a steering wheel you can hold right away.

### What happens if a window has old, bubbling tint on it already?

We strip the old film and clean the adhesive off the glass before new film goes on. Removal is extra labor and is quoted once we see the condition of the windows, since purple, peeling film on a rear defroster takes more time than a clean window.

### Is the price the same for every car?

The posted price covers a standard four-door car: four side windows plus the back glass. Two-door coupes, trucks, SUVs, minivans and Teslas have different glass counts and shapes, so those are quoted at the counter. Windshield film is always a separate quote.
