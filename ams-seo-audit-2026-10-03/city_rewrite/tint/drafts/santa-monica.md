## Santa Monica ceramic tint questions

### Why would a Santa Monica driver go to Carson for ceramic window tint?

For the installation. Ceramic film only performs if it is applied properly: cut on the glass, heat-formed, laid in a clean bay with no dust under it. That is what we do all day. The drive is about forty minutes outside rush hour, and the job is usually finished the same day.

### Does window tint reduce glare from the ocean and wet pavement?

Yes on the side and rear glass. Tint cuts the light bouncing in from low angles, and ceramic film does it without needing a dark shade on the front doors. The windshield stays clear under California law apart from a top strip, so sunglasses still matter straight ahead.

### Will ceramic tint protect leather seats from sun damage?

Yes. Both of our films block nearly all UV, which is what dries and cracks leather and fades fabric. Ceramic adds heat rejection, so the seats do not bake either. For a car that spends its days parked near the beach, that combination pays for itself in interior condition alone.

### Can you tint a convertible without the film peeling?

Yes. Convertibles and cars with frameless doors have no channel to hide the film edge, so we cut to the true edge, finish it by hand and let it cure before the window moves. Done that way the edge stays put. We tell you how long to leave the windows up.
