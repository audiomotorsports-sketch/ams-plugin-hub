### Will privacy car window tint on the rear windows cause a problem with the law?

No. California only restricts how dark the front side windows and windshield can be. Rear doors and the back glass can be as dark as you want, so a privacy shade on the back half of the car is fully allowed and is the most common setup we install.

### Is ceramic window tint worth the extra money over Solar?

If the goal is a cooler car, yes. Solar Ceramic rejects far more heat than a dyed film at the same darkness, which you feel most on a vehicle that sits outside all day. If the goal is only privacy or a darker look, Solar at $249 installed, as of October 2026, is plenty.

### How soon after window tinting can I roll the windows down?

Give the film a few days before lowering the side windows so the edges fully bond to the glass. You can drive the car home right away, and small haze or water pockets are normal while the film cures and clear up on their own.
