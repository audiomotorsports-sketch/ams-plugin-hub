# Group-Ride Harley Bagger Audio for Signal Hill

When the whole club rolls out, the bike with the stock speakers disappears into the pipes. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, sets up Harley-Davidson bagger audio for Signal Hill riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King so the music carries in a pack. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, about 5.8 miles from Signal Hill, and the pods are made there.

## What Signal Hill riders bring us

**Audio that carries in a group.** Pack riding is about clean power. The amplifier is tuned with headroom so the music stays clear beside other bikes at speed rather than turning to mush when you chase the knob.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Built to order in Carson, chrome or gloss, crash-bar bracket and screws included. They fire at the rider from below the wind, which is where group riding needs the sound.

**Amplifier installation and tuning on the bike.** Fused at the battery with a tank pull on the Glides; saddlebag mount on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect on the Road King.

**Bag-lid bass and speaker replacement.** Cut lids with inverted speakers for the low end, and Hertz Neo units in the fairing when the stock ones give up.

Audio MotorSports also installs car stereos, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, ceramic window tint, car alarms, remote start and GPS trackers, so the car can be booked for the same week.

## Signal Hill riders ask

### How loud should a bagger be for riding with a club?

Clear at highway speed next to other bikes, which is about clean power rather than peak volume. A tuned amplifier and lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider get you there without clipping. A weak system turned to the stop just adds distortion to the noise.

### Can a Signal Hill rider stage the build over a few visits?

Easily, because the shop is close. Have the amplifier and speakers done one week, come back for the pods when they are built, and add bag-lid bass later if you want it. Each stage is quoted on the bike, and a payment plan is available if you ask Nick.

### Will a Road King keep up with the Glides in a group?

Yes. The pods do the midrange, the amplifier rides in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect, and cut bag lids with inverted speakers supply the bass. The Road King stops being the quiet one in the line.

### How long before the pods are ready?

Pods are built to order in Carson and take about three weeks. Order them when you book and the install date lands when they are done. The bracket, hardware and recommended Hertz Neo speakers are handled at the same time.

### What does the shop need from a Signal Hill rider before the first visit?

A text to (213) 429-1092 with the model year, which of the three baggers you ride, and a photo of the fairing and saddlebags if the lids are aftermarket. Nick writes a plan from that and the final quote is confirmed at the counter.
