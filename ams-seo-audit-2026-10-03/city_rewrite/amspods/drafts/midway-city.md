# Harley Bagger Audio for Midway City Riders Who Ride in a Pack

Ten bikes on PCH make a lot of noise, and a stock fairing radio loses the fight by the second light. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds bagger audio for Midway City riders who ride in groups: AMS lower-fairing pods, an amplifier with real headroom and a tune done on the bike. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, 17.0 miles from Midway City.

## What Midway City riders bring us

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation for the group ride.** Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King systems set up so the rider hears the music over the exhausts around them, without running the amp into distortion.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** The pods sit on the crash bar below the wind blast and aim at the rider, which matters most when the bike next to you is loud. Built in Carson in chrome or gloss, hardware included.

**Amplifier installation.** Clean power is what group-ride volume takes. The amp is fused at the battery, mounted in the fairing or a saddlebag, and tuned with the engine running.

**Speaker replacement.** Worn fairing speakers swapped for weatherproof units matched to the amp, so the whole system plays at the same level.

If the group includes a truck or two, Audio MotorSports does car stereo installation, subwoofers, window tint and remote start in the same bay.

## Group-ride questions from Midway City

### Can a group of Midway City riders book back to back?

Yes, within what one bay can do in a day. Call with the number of bikes and what each needs, and the appointments get staggered. Pods are built to order, so give us the models ahead of time and every pair is ready when the group rides down.

### How much volume does it take to hear music in a pack?

Less than people think, if the sound is placed right. Pods near your legs sit out of the main wind, so the midrange reaches you without being cranked. The amplifier supplies clean headroom, and the tune keeps the highs from turning harsh when you do turn it up.

### Will AMS pods fit a Road King in a group of Glides?

Yes. The pods mount on the crash bar, which the Road King shares with the Street Glide and Road Glide. The Road King has no fairing, so its amp goes in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect, and the pods carry most of the sound.

### Is a 17-mile ride from Midway City worth it for pods?

Riders come to this bay from much farther; Temecula is a regular trip. Seventeen miles is under an hour even with traffic, and the result is pods built and installed by the people who designed them, tuned on your bike before you leave.

### Do you do a pods-only install, or does it have to be a full system?

Pods-only is a common first step and a same-day job once the pair is built. Many riders run them on the stock radio for a while, then add an amplifier when they want more at freeway speed. We lay out both paths when you book.
