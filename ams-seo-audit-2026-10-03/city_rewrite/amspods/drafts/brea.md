## What Brea riders bring us

**Wiring inside the fairing.** Power cable comes off the battery through a fuse, runs under the tank and into the fairing without being pinched by the inner panel. Every splice is soldered, every connection to a speaker or pod gets a quick disconnect, and the harness is tied down so a year of freeway miles does not shake it apart.

**Amplifier installation.** The amp is positioned inside the fairing where airflow and clearance allow, then tuned with the engine running so the output matches the way a Street Glide or Road Glide is actually ridden.

**AMS lower-fairing pods.** Built in the shop in chrome or gloss, bracket and hardware included, mounted at an angle on the crash bar and wired into the same harness.

**Road King setups.** No fairing means a different plan: the pods carry the front and the amp lives in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect.

## Questions Brea riders ask

### Why does the fairing wiring matter so much on a Harley bagger?

Because the fairing is a vibrating plastic shell full of heat and wind. A splice that is crimped instead of soldered, or a power lead that rubs on the inner fairing, is what makes a stereo cut out six months later. Doing the harness properly the first time is the whole value of the install.

### Does a fairing amp install need a tank pull on a Street Glide?

Yes. The cleanest path for the power cable from the battery to the fairing runs under the tank, so the tank comes up during the install. That is also why the shop asks Brea riders to bring the bike in with the tank reasonably low and the bags empty.

### Is it worth riding from Brea instead of using a mobile installer?

For fairing work, yes. A proper install needs a bay, a lift, a soldering station and time to tune with the engine running. Riders who have had a parking-lot install redone here usually say the second trip was the one that should have happened first.

### Can the pods be added later without rewiring the fairing?

That is exactly why the harness is built with quick disconnects. If the fairing is wired now and AMS pods are added after the three-week build, the pod leads plug into the amp channels that were already planned for them.
