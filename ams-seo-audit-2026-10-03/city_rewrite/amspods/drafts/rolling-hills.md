### Does an Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation always need a new radio?

Not always. Many vehicles from the last ten years accept a plug-in CarPlay or Android Auto module that works through the factory screen and keeps every original function. Where the factory unit is too old for that, a replacement head unit with a dash kit and integration harness is the route.

### Is wireless CarPlay available or does it have to be a cable?

Both are options. Most current head units support wireless CarPlay and wireless Android Auto out of the box, and there are adapters that make a wired factory system wireless. Wired still charges your phone during the trip, so some Rolling Hills customers keep a cable anyway.

### Can you do car window tinting for a Rolling Hills car while I wait?

Yes. A full tint on four doors and the back glass is a few hours, and the shop is open while we work. Book a morning slot and you can drive back up the hill in the afternoon with the film already curing on the glass.

### Will a GPS tracker drain the battery on a car that sits for weeks?

A properly installed tracker draws very little current and goes to sleep when the car is parked, waking on movement or a ping from your phone. We wire it to a constant-power source with a clean ground so it stays hidden and stable even on a car that is driven rarely.
