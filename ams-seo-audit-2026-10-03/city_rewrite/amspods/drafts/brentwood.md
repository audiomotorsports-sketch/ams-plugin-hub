# AMS Pods Installed for Brentwood Harley Riders

Brentwood riders ride about 20.2 miles to AMS Pods at Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, for one specific product installed one specific way: AMS lower-fairing speaker pods on a Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King. The pods are made in the shop, the bracket is included, and the install is done on a lift by the people who build them.

## What Brentwood riders bring us

**Pods on the Glides.** On a Street Glide or Road Glide the pods become the lower half of a two-stage front system. They mount at an angle on the crash bar, plug into the fairing amplifier through quick disconnects, and the harness is routed under the tank and tied down so it never rubs.

**Pods on a Road King.** With no fairing, the pods are the front stage. The amplifier goes in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect, and the pods carry the music on their own.

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** Pods are only as good as the channels driving them. The amp is set with the engine running so the pods and the fairing are balanced at riding speed rather than in the driveway.

**Speaker replacement.** Riders adding pods often refresh the fairing speakers at the same time so the two stages match.

The car side of Audio MotorSports covers window tint, car stereo installation, CarPlay and Android Auto, car alarm installation, remote start, GPS trackers and dash cameras.

## Questions Brentwood riders ask

### What is included when I order AMS pods?

A pair of lower-fairing pods in chrome or gloss, the crash-bar bracket and the mounting screws. Speakers, the amplifier and the install are separate and quoted at the shop, so a Brentwood rider knows exactly what the pods price covers before anything else is added.

### How long is the wait for a pair of pods?

About three weeks, because each pair is built to order in-house. The usual plan is to text the year and model now, let the pair get built, and book the install for the day they are ready so the ride from Brentwood happens once.

### Do the pods come off easily if I need the crash bar removed?

Yes. The speaker leads use quick disconnects, so the pods and bar come off for service without cutting a wire. That is one reason the shop solders the disconnects in rather than using push-on connectors that corrode.

### Can pods go on a Road King that has no amplifier yet?

They can, but they will be running off the factory radio and will sound like it. The shop recommends a saddlebag amp with the pods on a Road King; both are installed and tuned in the same visit.
