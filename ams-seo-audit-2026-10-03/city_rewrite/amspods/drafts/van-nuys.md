## Van Nuys riders ask

### What is the smartest first step for a stock Road Glide from Van Nuys?

Replace the fairing speakers and add an amplifier on the first visit, and order the pods the same day so they are built by the time you return. That order means nothing is thrown away later, and the bike already sounds much better on the ride home.

### Can the factory Harley radio feed an aftermarket amplifier?

Sometimes. Some model years have enough clean output; others do not and are better replaced. The shop stocks Harley-fit head units from Diamond Audio, Sony and Soundstream, and Nick will tell you honestly whether yours should stay before anything is ordered.

### Is riding from Van Nuys to Carson worth it for a first upgrade?

Yes. A first upgrade done properly is the only one you pay for. Pods are made in the building, the installer works on baggers every week, and the plan is written for your bike. Done once, done right is cheaper than a quick install that has to come back apart.

### How is the Road King handled for a first audio upgrade?

With no fairing, the Road King starts with lower-fairing pods and an amplifier mounted in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect. Bag-lid bass can come later. The quick disconnect means the bag still lifts off in seconds.

### Can the upgrade be paid for in stages?

Yes. Staging is the normal way to do a first upgrade, and a payment plan is available if you ask Nick. Every stage is quoted on the bike before the work starts, so there are no surprises when you pick it up.
