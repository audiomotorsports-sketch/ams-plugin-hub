New bagger, stock radio, and the music gone by the time you reach the first curve on PCH. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, maps out a first audio upgrade for Pacific Palisades riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King, starting with AMS lower-fairing pods and growing into an amplified system when you want it. ## What Pacific Palisades riders bring us

**A first upgrade with a plan behind it.** Pods first, because they put speakers below the wind and change what you hear the most. Amplifier second. Head unit and bag-lid speakers only if you want them later. Each step is wired for the next.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Built here in Carson, chrome or gloss, with bracket and hardware. Loaded with Hertz Neo speakers that handle fog, salt air and sun.

**Amplifier installation.** When you are ready for freeway volume: a fairing amp on a Glide or a saddlebag amp with a quick disconnect on a Road King, fused at the battery and tuned on the bike.

**Speaker replacement.** Tired stock fairing speakers swapped for weatherproof ones, matched to the pods so the system plays evenly.

## First-timer questions from Pacific Palisades

### I know nothing about bagger audio; where do I start?

Start with a text: year, model, and what bothers you about the sound now. For most riders the answer is pods, because the complaint is music disappearing at speed. You do not need to pick an amplifier or a head unit on day one; the pods are wired so those can come later.

### Will pods alone make a difference on my stock Street Glide?

Yes. The stock radio can drive them, and the change at 50 mph is immediate because the sound is now coming from below the wind and aimed at you. The limit you hit next is the radio running out of clean power, which is when an amplifier makes sense.

### Can a Pacific Palisades rider see the pods before buying?

Ride down and look. Nick will show you the pods, the mount on the crash bar and how the wiring runs, and lay out a first step and a path for later. Nothing is ordered until you decide. If you would rather not make two trips, text photos of the bike and we talk it through.

### How do I keep the first upgrade from becoming a mistake later?

Choose parts that work together as the system grows. Pods that will take amplifier power, wiring run so an amp drops in, an amp chosen with a head unit or bag-lid speakers in mind. Planned once, nothing gets replaced; planned badly, you pay for the same work twice.

### How long is the bike at the shop for a first pods install?

A same-day appointment once the pods are built. Ride in from Pacific Palisades in the morning and most riders wait at the shop or walk while the pods go on and the system is checked. Add an amp the same day and it becomes a full-day stay, so say so when booking.
