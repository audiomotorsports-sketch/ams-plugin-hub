# Harley Amplifier Installation and Tuning for South Gate

An amplifier is what turns a bagger stereo from loud-ish into clear at seventy. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, installs and tunes amplifiers on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King for South Gate riders, and builds the lower-fairing pods that go with them. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, about 9.3 miles from South Gate.

## What South Gate riders bring us

**Amplifier installation on the bike.** Power comes off the battery with a fuse at the source, never tapped from a handy wire. On a Street Glide or Road Glide the amp mounts in the fairing with a tank pull for the power run; on a Road King it sits in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect.

**Tuning on the bike, not on a bench.** Gains, crossovers and levels are set with the engine running and then checked at speed, because the wind is part of the system.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Built to order in Carson, chrome or gloss, bracket and screws included. With a tuned amp behind them they carry the music at highway speed.

**Speaker replacement.** Stock speakers that cannot take amplifier power are swapped for Hertz Neo units that can.

Audio MotorSports also does car stereo installation, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, ceramic window tint, car alarm installation, remote start and GPS trackers, if the car needs work too.

## South Gate riders ask

### Does a bagger really need an amplifier, or just better speakers?

Better speakers on the factory radio help a little; the same speakers on a tuned amplifier are a different bike. The amp is what keeps the sound clean at highway speed instead of clipping. If you ride on the freeway at all, the amplifier is the upgrade that matters.

### What does amplifier tuning on the bike involve?

Setting the gain so the amp never clips, setting the crossover so the pods and fairing speakers each get the frequencies they handle well, and balancing the levels. Then the bike goes out for a test at speed and the settings are adjusted for real wind.

### Is it worth riding from South Gate for amplifier installation?

Yes, because a clean amp install is about the wiring more than the box. A fused power lead from the battery, a solid ground, signal cables kept away from ignition wiring and soldered splices are what make it reliable for years. Twenty minutes from South Gate buys that.

### Where does the amp go on a Road King from South Gate?

In a saddlebag, mounted on a carpeted board, with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still lifts off in seconds. The lower-fairing pods do the midrange and, if you cut the lids, the bags do the bass.

### Can I wait at the shop while the amplifier goes in?

For an amp-and-pods install, usually yes. A full system with a head unit, tank pull and bag-lid work can take the bike for longer, and Nick will tell you which it is when you book at (310) 513-8800.
