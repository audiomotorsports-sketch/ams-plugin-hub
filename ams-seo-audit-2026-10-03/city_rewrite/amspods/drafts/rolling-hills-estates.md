# Long-Ride Harley Bagger Audio for Rolling Hills Estates

Sound that is fine around town falls apart three hours into a ride. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, sets up Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King audio for Rolling Hills Estates riders who spend whole days in the saddle. ## What Rolling Hills Estates riders bring us

**Audio that holds up on a long ride.** Fatigue comes from distortion, not volume. The amplifier is tuned so the system stays clean for hours at highway speed, and the speakers are placed so the rider is not turning it up every few miles.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Built in the Carson shop, chrome or gloss, sitting on the crash bar with bracket and hardware included. Pods fill in the midrange that fairing speakers lose once the windshield is doing its job.

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** Power comes from the battery with a fuse at the source, a tank pull on the Glides, a saddlebag mount with quick disconnect on the Road King.

**Speaker replacement.** Worn stock speakers are swapped for weather-resistant units that match the amp.

## Rolling Hills Estates riders ask

### What makes a bagger stereo comfortable for all-day riding?

Headroom and placement. An amplifier running well inside its limits keeps the sound clean at any speed, and lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider mean you are not fighting wind with the volume knob. Clean sound at moderate volume is what lets you ride all day without ear fatigue.

### Should a rider from Rolling Hills Estates choose pods or new fairing speakers first?

If the fairing speakers are still healthy, pods make the biggest difference because they add sound where the wind is not. If the fairing speakers are blown, replace those first and add pods when they are built. Nick will tell you which order makes sense for your bike.

### How does the Road King get decent sound with no fairing?

The pods carry the music, and the amplifier goes in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still lifts off. Add bag-lid speakers and the Road King stops being the quiet bike on the ride.

### How long are the pods on back order?

They are not back ordered, they are built to order in Carson and take about three weeks. Book the install date when you order so the pods are ready when you ride down. Hardware, bracket and the recommended Hertz Neo speakers are handled at the same time.

### Can I wait at the shop while the amp goes in?

Yes for most amplifier and pod installs. A full system with a tank pull, head unit and saddlebag work may need the bike for longer, and Nick will say so when you book. Call (310) 513-8800 to set it up.
