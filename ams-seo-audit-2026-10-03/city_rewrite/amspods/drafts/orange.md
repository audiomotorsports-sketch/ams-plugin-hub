# Harley Bagger Audio for Orange Riders Who Ride in Groups

In a line of baggers, the stereo that wins is the one the rider can hear without touching the volume. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds group-ride audio for Orange riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King: AMS lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider, an amplifier with headroom, and a tune done with the engine running. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, about 23.8 miles from Orange.

## What Orange riders bring us

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation for riding together.** Set up so the music reaches you over the bikes beside you at cruising speed, which comes from speaker placement and clean amplifier power rather than from turning everything up.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Made in Carson, chrome or gloss, bracket and hardware included. Mounted on the crash bar under the wind, they carry the midrange that fairing speakers lose when the group is moving.

**Amplifier installation.** In the fairing on the Glides, in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect on the Road King, fused at the battery and tuned on the bike so it stays clean at group-ride volume.

**Speaker replacement.** Stock fairing speakers that have been overdriven are swapped for weatherproof units matched to the amp.

Audio MotorSports also does car stereo installation, subwoofers, window tint, alarms and remote start, in case the group includes a truck.

## Group-ride questions from Orange riders

### Can a riding group from Orange book the shop for a day?

Call with the number of bikes and the models and Nick will stagger the appointments across a day or more, within what one bay can do. Pods are built to order, so send the models ahead and every pair is waiting when the group rides up.

### What keeps my music audible next to loud pipes?

Placement first, power second. Pods on the crash bar sit in calmer air and point at you, so the midrange arrives without the volume being maxed. The amplifier supplies clean headroom so what you do turn up stays clear instead of turning to noise.

### Does a Road King in the group get the same setup as the Glides?

The same pods on the same crash-bar mount. The Road King has no fairing, so its amplifier goes in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect and the pods do most of the work. Each bike is tuned on its own, so the systems match on the road.

### How long does each bike take?

Pods alone is a same-day visit, and many riders wait at the shop for it. Pods with an amplifier and tune is most of a day for one bike. For a group, the work is spread so nobody from Orange is sitting around waiting on another rider's install.

### Why ride to Carson for pods instead of ordering them?

The people who build the pods are the ones who install and tune them, on your bike, before you leave. A shipped box gives you housings and a weekend with a wiring diagram. Riders already come from Temecula and farther for this, and Orange is well inside that circle.
