## Questions Hermosa Beach riders ask

### Why do stock bagger speakers blow so often near the beach?

Two reasons stack up. Factory cones are not sealed against moisture, and a factory radio pushed to full volume sends a clipped signal that overheats the voice coil. Replace the speakers with weatherproof units and add an amplifier with clean headroom, and both causes are gone.

### Can I ride from Hermosa Beach and wait while the speakers are replaced?

A fairing speaker replacement on its own is usually a same-day job, so waiting at the shop is realistic. If an amplifier or AMS pods go on at the same time, plan on most of the day and consider dropping the bike in the morning.

### Should I replace the speakers or just add pods?

Replace the speakers first. Pods add a second pair down low, but a distorted fairing stage still sounds bad above them. The most common Hermosa Beach order is new fairing speakers, an amp, then pods once the three-week build is done.

### Which replacement speakers hold up to salt air?

The shop uses weatherproof motorcycle speakers, with Hertz Neo as the standard choice in AMS pods. Compact neodymium magnets, moisture-resistant construction and clear output when loud are the reasons. Fairing replacements are matched to those so the system sounds like one set.
