## Pods questions from Newport Beach riders

### Why have the pods installed in Carson instead of fitting them at home?

Because the install is where the sound comes from. The wiring is run inside the lower, the pods are angled for the rider, the speakers are matched to the amp, and the system is tuned with the engine running. The shop that designs and builds the pods also knows exactly how to fit them.

### Do AMS pods hold up to Newport Beach salt air?

The housings are finished in chrome or gloss and the speakers we recommend, Hertz Neo, are built for weather. Wiring connections are made inside the pod and the fairing, not out in the open. Riding near the coast is normal use for these pods.

### Do the pods fit a Road King the same way as a Street Glide?

Yes, the crash-bar mount is the same across the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King. On the Road King there is no fairing, so the pods carry the sound and the amplifier lives in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect. On the Glides the amp sits in the fairing.

### How long does a Newport Beach rider wait for pods to be built?

Each pair is built to order, so text the year, model and finish and the pods get started. Book the install for when they are ready and ride up once. Install day itself is a single appointment, and many riders wait at the shop for it.

### Can I add an amplifier later without redoing the pods?

Yes. The pod wiring is run so an amp drops in afterward. Plenty of riders start on the stock radio and add amplifier installation when they want full volume at freeway speed. Done in order, nothing gets pulled apart twice.
