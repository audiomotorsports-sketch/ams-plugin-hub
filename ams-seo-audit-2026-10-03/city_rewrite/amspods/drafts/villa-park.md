Villa Park riders who log 300-mile days need a bagger stereo that is still pleasant at the end of them. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds Harley-Davidson bagger audio for the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King around its own lower-fairing pods. ## What Villa Park riders bring us

**Sound tuned for distance.** Fatigue comes from distortion. An amplifier with real headroom keeps the music clean at freeway speed for hours, and pods aimed at the rider mean you are not chasing the wind with the volume.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Chrome or gloss, built to order in Carson, crash-bar bracket and screws included. They supply the midrange the fairing speakers lose once the windshield is doing its job.

**Amplifier installation and tuning on the bike.** Power from the battery with a fuse at the source; fairing amp and tank pull on the Glides, saddlebag amp with an AMS quick disconnect on the Road King.

**Speaker replacement.** Worn stock speakers are replaced with weatherproof Hertz Neo units that stay clear when they are loud.

**Bag-lid bass.** Cut lids, carpeted spacers, inverted speakers and soldered quick disconnects turn the saddlebags into a subwoofer that still closes.

For the car, Audio MotorSports also does car stereo and CarPlay installation, ceramic window tint, Viper alarms, remote start and GPS trackers.

## Villa Park riders ask

### Is an hour from Villa Park a sensible ride for bagger audio?

For riders who do long days, yes. A system built for distance is set up once and then simply works. The pods are built in the building, the amplifier is tuned on your bike, and the whole thing is tested at speed before you ride back to Orange County.

### What stops a bagger stereo from being tiring after a few hours?

Clean power and smart placement. An amplifier that never clips does not produce the harshness that wears on your ears, and lower-fairing pods let you ride at a moderate volume that still beats the wind. That combination is the difference between a stereo you use and one you turn off.

### Will new fairing speakers alone fix a Street Glide that sounds thin at speed?

They help, but thin sound at speed is mostly a placement problem. Fairing speakers sit up in the wind; lower-fairing pods fire at the rider from below it. Most long-distance riders end up with both, plus an amplifier to drive them.

### How does the Road King do on a long ride with no fairing?

Better than people expect. The pods carry the midrange, cut bag lids carry the bass, and the amplifier rides in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with a quick disconnect. The sound is aimed at you rather than the windshield.

### How long is the wait for AMS pods?

Each pair is made to order in the Carson shop, which takes about three weeks, so order them when you book and the install lands on the day they are ready. Text (213) 429-1092 with your model year and which bagger you ride to get it started.
