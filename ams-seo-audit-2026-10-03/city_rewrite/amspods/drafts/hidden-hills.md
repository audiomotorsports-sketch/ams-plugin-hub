## Questions Hidden Hills riders ask

### Why solder the connections instead of using crimp connectors?

A crimp loosens with heat cycles and vibration, and a bagger supplies both for hours at a time. A soldered joint sealed and tied down does not. It takes longer in the bay, which is the point of riding to a shop instead of having it done in a driveway.

### Is the ride from Hidden Hills worth it just for wiring?

For a bike you plan to keep, yes. Riders cover far longer distances to get this bay, and wiring is the job that is hardest to fix later because everything else sits on top of it. One careful trip beats a second trip to redo it.

### Why does the tank have to come up during the install?

The power cable for a fairing amp runs from the battery forward, and the clean route is under the tank. Lifting the tank lets the cable be secured away from heat and moving parts instead of being forced along the outside of the frame.

### Can you rewire a fairing that another installer did?

Yes. The installer checks the fuse, ground, routing and every connection first, then quotes what needs to be redone before touching the bike. If the amp and speakers are sound they stay; only the wiring that will fail gets replaced.
