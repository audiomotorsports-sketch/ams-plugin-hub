## First-upgrade questions from Northridge

### What is the one upgrade a new bagger owner should do first?

Pods. The thing most riders notice first is the sound disappearing above 50 mph, and lower-fairing pods fix exactly that by putting speakers below the wind and aiming them at you. If the fairing speakers are blown, those get replaced in the same visit.

### Should a Northridge rider order pods before riding down?

Yes, if you want everything done in one trip. Each pair is built to order, so text your year and model, get the pods started, and book the install for when they are ready. One ride down, one appointment, home with the system tuned.

### Will the stock radio on my Road Glide run the pods?

It will drive them, and plenty of riders start that way. The factory deck just runs short of clean power at freeway speed. When you are ready for real volume, an amplifier installation is the next stage and the pod wiring is already in place for it.

### How do I avoid paying twice as I upgrade in stages?

By having the first stage wired for the second. Pod wiring is run so an amp drops in later; the amp is chosen so a head unit or bag-lid speakers can follow. Done once and planned, the stages add up instead of being redone.

### How long does a pods install take once they are built?

A same-day appointment. Ride in from Northridge in the morning, and most riders either wait at the shop or take a walk while the pods go on and the system is checked. Adding an amplifier the same day makes it a longer stay, so say so when you book.
