# Harley Bagger Audio for West Carson Group Rides

Ride with a pack and the bike with the weak stereo is the one everybody notices. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds and installs Harley-Davidson bagger audio for West Carson riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, only 1.8 miles from West Carson, and the pods are made in that building.

## What West Carson riders bring us

**Bagger audio installation for riding in a group.** When ten bikes roll out together your music has to carry over everyone else's pipes, not just your own. The amplifier is sized and tuned so the system stays clear at pack speed instead of turning to noise when you chase the volume.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Chrome or gloss, built to order in the Carson shop, mounted on the crash bar at an angle with the bracket and screws in the box. They put sound down low where the fairing speakers cannot reach.

**Amplifier installation and tuning on the bike.** Street Glide and Road Glide get a fairing amp with a tank pull for the power run; a Road King carries the amp in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect.

**Speaker replacement.** Tired stock speakers come out and weather-resistant Hertz Neo units go in, matched to the amp so nothing distorts at speed.

The same bay also handles car stereo and CarPlay installation, ceramic window tint, Viper alarms, remote start and GPS trackers for the truck you tow with.

## West Carson riders ask

### Why do West Carson riders bother with pods when the shop is this close anyway?

Because being close makes the install easy to stage. A rider from West Carson can drop the bike for the pod install one week and come back for the amplifier and tuning another, without a long haul each time. The pods are built in the same building, so there is no shipping step.

### How loud does a bagger system need to be for group riding?

Loud enough to hear clearly at highway speed next to other bikes, which is a question of clean power more than raw volume. A properly tuned amplifier and lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider give you that without clipping. Turning a weak system all the way up only adds distortion.

### Does a Road King work for group riding with no fairing?

Yes. On a Road King the lower-fairing pods do the talking and the amplifier rides in a bag with a quick disconnect so the bag still comes off. Add cut bag lids with inverted speakers and the bike holds its own in any pack.

### How long does a full bagger audio installation take in Carson?

Pods are built to order and take about three weeks. Once they are in, a pods-and-amp install is usually a day. A complete build with head unit, amplifier, speakers and bag lids can run up to about ten business days, and Nick confirms the timeline when he sees the bike.

### What should a West Carson rider send before the first visit?

Text (213) 429-1092 with the model year, which of the three baggers you ride, and a photo of the fairing and saddlebags if the lids are aftermarket. Nick writes a plan from that and books your slot, then the final quote is set on the bike at the counter.
