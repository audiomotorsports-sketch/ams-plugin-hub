## What La Crescenta-Montrose riders bring us

**Amplifier installation.** The amp is matched to the speakers and the deck, mounted inside the fairing on a Glide or in a saddlebag on a Road King with a quick disconnect, and fed by a fused lead from the battery with the remote turn-on from the head unit.

**Tuning on the bike.** Gains set to the deck's actual output so nothing clips, crossovers set so the pods take the mids and highs, left and right balanced with the engine running, then a ride to confirm it.

**Head unit installation.** A deck with clean preamp outputs when the factory radio is the weak link.

## Amplifier questions from La Crescenta-Montrose riders

### My Street Glide has an amp already and still sounds harsh; why?

Nine times out of ten the gains are turned up as if they were a volume knob, which sends a clipped signal to the speakers. Sometimes a channel is out of phase or the crossover is letting bass into the pods. A proper tune on the bike fixes all three, and we check the wiring while the fairing is open.

### What size amplifier does a bagger with pods need?

Enough headroom that it loafs at the volume you ride, which is more than most riders expect. Fairing speakers plus pods is a four-channel job; bag-lid speakers add channels. We spec the amp from your bike and how you ride, and quote it on the bike.

### Will a La Crescenta-Montrose rider need two trips for an amp and pods?

No. Text the year, model and finish, and the pods are built before your appointment. Amplifier installation, pods and the tune happen in one long day. Ride down in the morning and ride home in the afternoon with the system set.

### Does the amplifier go in the fairing or the saddlebag?

On the Street Glide and Road Glide, inside the fairing behind the inner panel, with the power lead run under the tank. On the Road King, in a saddlebag with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still comes off. Either way it is fused at the battery and grounded to bare metal.

### Can I bring an amplifier I already bought?

Yes. We will say plainly whether it suits the pods and your deck before it goes in, and quote the labor on the bike. If it is the wrong channel count or short on power, you hear that first, not after the fairing is apart.
