# Bagger Amp and Radio Installation for Costa Mesa Riders

When a Costa Mesa rider says the stereo is loud but muddy, the cause is almost always the signal chain: a factory radio with weak outputs feeding an amp that was never tuned. AMS Pods, the Harley bagger audio shop within Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, is about 23.7 miles away and fixes it in order, head unit first, then amplifier installation and tuning on the bike, then AMS lower-fairing pods if the rider wants more.

## What Costa Mesa riders bring us

**Head unit installation.** Diamond Audio, Sony and Soundstream Harley radios are in stock. The right one depends on the year of the Street Glide or Road Glide and whether the factory unit has usable preamp outputs.

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** Mounted in the fairing on the Glides or in a saddlebag on a Road King, fed from the battery through a fuse, then tuned with the engine running: gains set below clipping, crossovers chosen for the speakers, levels balanced at road speed.

**AMS pods.** Lower-fairing speaker pods built in the shop in chrome or gloss, bracket and screws included, driven by their own amp channels so they are not sharing power with the fairing.

**Saddlebag subwoofer installation.** Cut lids, inverted speakers, carpeted spacers and soldered quick disconnects turn a bag into a bass enclosure.

Audio MotorSports also installs car stereo and CarPlay systems, window tint, car alarms, remote start, GPS trackers and dash cameras.

## Questions Costa Mesa riders ask

### How do I know whether my factory radio is the bottleneck?

If the system distorts before the volume knob is near the top, or an added amp still sounds flat, the radio is usually the limit. Nick checks the year and the outputs at the shop and tells you whether a head unit is needed before any amp is sold.

### Is Costa Mesa close enough for a one-day amp and radio install?

Yes, with an appointment. A head unit and amplifier on a Glide, including the tank pull for the power run and the tuning ride afterward, is normally done in a day. Drop the bike in the morning and it is usually ready before closing.

### Does an amplifier need its own tuning after a new radio goes in?

Always. A new radio changes the output level, so the amp gains and crossover points are reset with the engine running. Skipping that step is how a brand-new system ends up clipping and taking the speakers with it.

### Where is the amp mounted on a Road King from Costa Mesa?

In a saddlebag, on a carpeted board, with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still lifts off. The pods carry the front stage on a Road King because there is no fairing to put speakers in.
