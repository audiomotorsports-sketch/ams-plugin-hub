## Speaker questions from Placentia riders

### How can I tell whether my bagger speakers are blown or just wired badly?

A speaker that buzzes on bass notes or has gone dead is usually blown; one that cuts in and out over bumps is usually a loose connection. Ride it in and we check both before anything is ordered. Sometimes a rider from Placentia needs a wiring fix, not a pair of speakers.

### Will new speakers blow again on my factory radio?

They can, if you ride at the same volume. The factory deck distorts when pushed and that distortion is what cooks speakers. Pods bring the sound to you at a lower setting, and an amplifier delivers clean power at the volume you actually ride. Together they are the long-term fix.

### What speakers do you put in a Road Glide fairing?

Weatherproof units matched to the amplifier and to the Hertz Neo speakers we recommend in the pods, so the fairing and the pods play at the same level. The exact choice depends on the amp and the deck, and it is quoted on the bike.

### Can the speaker replacement be done in one visit from Placentia?

Yes. A speaker swap alone is a short appointment and riders often wait at the shop for it. Speakers plus pods and an amp is most of a day, so take a morning slot. Text your year and model ahead so pods can be built before you ride down.

### Is it cheaper to just keep replacing stock speakers?

Not over time. Each set that blows is money and a ride down. Fix the cause with pods and clean amplifier power and the speakers last. Replacing them once, properly, beats replacing them every season.
