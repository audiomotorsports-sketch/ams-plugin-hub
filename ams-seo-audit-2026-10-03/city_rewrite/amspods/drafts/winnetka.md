## Winnetka riders ask

### What should a Winnetka rider do first on a stock Street Glide?

Speakers and an amplifier on the first visit, with the pods ordered the same day so they are built by the time you return. The bike sounds better on the ride home and nothing you bought gets replaced later.

### Will the stock Harley radio drive an aftermarket amp?

Depends on the model year. Some have enough clean output; others are better replaced with a Harley-fit head unit from Diamond Audio, Sony or Soundstream, which the shop keeps in stock. Nick tells you straight before anything is ordered.

### Is an hour from Winnetka worth it for a first bagger upgrade?

Yes, because the first upgrade done right is the only one you pay for. The pods are made in the building, the installer sees baggers every week, and the plan is written for your bike. Done once, done right costs less than a quick job that comes back apart.

### How is a first upgrade handled on a Road King?

Lower-fairing pods and an amplifier in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect, since there is no fairing to work in. Bag-lid bass can follow later. The bag still lifts off in seconds.

### Can I spread the cost across the stages?

Yes. Staging a first upgrade is the normal way to do it, and a payment plan is available if you ask Nick. Each stage is quoted on the bike before the work starts, so the number is known before you hand over the key.
