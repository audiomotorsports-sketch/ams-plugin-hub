## Tarzana riders ask

### Why do stock Harley fairing speakers keep blowing?

Because the factory radio is asked for more than it can give. Pushed past its limit it clips, and a clipped signal overheats a speaker quickly. New speakers on the same radio will go the same way; new speakers on a tuned amplifier will not.

### Is it worth an hour from Tarzana for a speaker replacement?

Yes, because the job is the labor, not the speakers. Getting the fairing apart cleanly, mounting the speakers so they do not buzz, soldering the wiring and testing at speed are what you ride for. A shop that opens baggers every week does it without drama.

### Should I add the pods when the speakers are replaced?

If you ride on the freeway, pods are the next step after speakers and an amplifier. They fire at the rider from below the wind, so the fairing speakers no longer have to be cranked. Order them at the first visit; they take about three weeks to build.

### What about a Road King with no fairing speakers?

The Road King gets its sound from lower-fairing pods and, for bass, from cut bag lids with inverted speakers. The amplifier mounts in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still comes off.

### How quickly can the Carson shop turn the bike around?

Speaker replacement with an amplifier is usually a one-day job. A complete build with pods, head unit and bag lids can take up to about ten business days. Text (213) 429-1092 with the model year and Nick gives you a timeline before you ride down.
