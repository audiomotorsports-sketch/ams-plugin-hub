# Group-Ride Bagger Audio for Paramount Harley Riders

Riding in a group means your music is competing with every exhaust in the line. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, sets up bagger audio for Paramount riders who ride together: AMS lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider, an amplifier with headroom to spare, and a tune done on the Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King with the engine running. The shop sits at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, 7.4 miles from Paramount.

## What Paramount riders bring us

**Bagger audio installation for the pack.** The system is tuned to reach you at cruising speed with bikes on both sides, which is a matter of clean amplifier power and low speaker placement rather than peak loudness.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Built in Carson, chrome or gloss, bracket and hardware supplied. On the crash bar they sit under the wind blast and deliver the midrange that fairing speakers lose when the group gets rolling.

**Amplifier installation.** A fairing amp on the Glides, a saddlebag amp with a quick disconnect on the Road King, fused at the battery and set up so it never clips at the volumes a group ride takes.

**Speaker installation.** Fairing and bag-lid speakers matched to the amp so each bike in the club sounds as good as the next.

Audio MotorSports also installs car stereos, subwoofers, window tint, alarms and remote start for the chase truck.

## Questions from Paramount riders who ride together

### Can our club from Paramount get bikes done on the same day?

Within the limits of one bay, yes. Give us the number of bikes and the models, and the appointments are staggered through the day or across a couple of days. Pods are built to order, so the models need to be in before the group rides down.

### Why do pods matter more in a group than solo?

Because the noise around you is higher. Fairing speakers up top fight wind and exhaust; pods on the crash bar sit in calmer air and aim at your ears, so you hear the music without the volume being maxed. The amplifier keeps that volume clean.

### What if some of us have Road Kings and some have Glides?

Same pods, same crash-bar mount. The difference is where the amplifier lives: in the fairing on a Street Glide or Road Glide, in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect on a Road King. The tune is done per bike so the systems match on the road.

### How long will each bike be at the shop?

Pods alone is a same-day visit, and riders often wait at the shop. Pods with an amp and tune is most of a day per bike. For a group, Nick spreads the work so nobody is stuck waiting on someone else's install.

### Is it worth riding from Paramount rather than ordering pods online?

The pods are built here and installed here by the same people, and tuned on your bike before you leave. A box in the mail gives you housings and a weekend of wiring. Riders come from over 100 miles away for the install; Paramount is a quarter of an hour.
