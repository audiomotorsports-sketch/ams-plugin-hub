## Yorba Linda riders ask

### Why did my stock Harley speakers blow in the first place?

Usually the factory radio was asked for more than it had. Push a small amplifier past its limit and it clips, and clipped signal cooks a speaker fast. The fix is a real amplifier with headroom, not another set of stock speakers wired to the same radio.

### Is it worth riding from Yorba Linda just for speaker replacement?

Yes, because the job is more than swapping cones. The fairing has to come apart cleanly, the new speakers have to be mounted and wired so nothing buzzes, and the system has to be tested at speed. A shop that does baggers every week makes that routine.

### Should the amplifier go in at the same time as the new speakers?

It is the efficient way to do it, since the fairing is already open. New speakers on a tuned amplifier stay clean at highway speed; new speakers on the stock radio will eventually be pushed just as hard as the old ones.

### What happens to the Road King with no fairing speakers to replace?

The Road King gets its sound from the lower-fairing pods and, if you want bass, from cut bag lids with inverted speakers. The amplifier rides in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still lifts off.

### How long will the bike be at the Carson shop?

Speaker replacement with an amplifier is normally a day. Pods are built to order and take about three weeks, so order them at the first visit. A complete build can take up to about ten business days, and Nick gives you a straight timeline when he books it.
