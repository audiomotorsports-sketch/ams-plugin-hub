# Harley Amplifier Installation and Tuning for Sylmar Riders

The amplifier is the part of a bagger system that decides whether it is clear at freeway speed or just loud at idle. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, installs and tunes amplifiers on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King for Sylmar riders, and builds the lower-fairing pods that go with them. ## What Sylmar riders bring us

**Amplifier installation.** A fused power lead straight from the battery, a solid ground, signal cables routed away from the ignition. On the Glides the amp mounts in the fairing with a tank pull; on the Road King it sits in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect.

**Tuning with the engine running.** Gains, crossovers and levels are set on the bike and then checked on the road, because wind noise is part of the equation.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Carson-built, chrome or gloss, bracket and screws included. A tuned amplifier is what lets them carry the music at seventy.

**Speaker replacement.** Stock speakers that cannot handle amplifier power are swapped for Hertz Neo units that can.

## Sylmar riders ask

### Is a bagger amplifier necessary if I already have decent speakers?

If you ride on the freeway, yes. Good speakers on the factory radio run out of clean power around the speed where you most want music. A tuned amplifier gives those same speakers headroom so they stay clear instead of clipping.

### What does tuning the amplifier on the bike actually mean?

Setting the gain so the amp never clips, setting the crossover so the pods and fairing speakers each handle what they are good at, and balancing the levels between them. Then the bike goes out at speed and the settings are adjusted for real wind.

### Is Sylmar to Carson too far for amplifier installation?

No. It is a little over an hour, and the value is in the wiring: a fused lead from the battery, a clean ground, soldered splices and a harness that is tied down. That is what makes the amp reliable for years, and it is the part you cannot order online.

### Where does the amplifier go on a Sylmar rider's Road King?

In a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect, so the bag lifts off in seconds. The lower-fairing pods carry the midrange, and cut bag lids with inverted speakers can carry the bass if you want it.
