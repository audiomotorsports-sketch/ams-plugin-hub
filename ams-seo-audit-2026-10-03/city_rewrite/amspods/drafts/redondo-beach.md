# Your First Harley Bagger Audio Upgrade, Redondo Beach

New to the bagger world and tired of a stereo you cannot hear past 40 mph? AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, walks Redondo Beach riders through a first audio upgrade on a Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King, from lower-fairing pods to a full amplified system. ## What Redondo Beach riders bring us

**A first upgrade that makes sense.** Most riders start with AMS lower-fairing speaker pods because they add the most sound for the money without opening up the whole fairing. Chrome or gloss, built in Carson, bracket and hardware included.

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation.** When you are ready for the next step, the shop installs a head unit with real preamp outputs, an amplifier wired from the battery, and matched speakers.

**Amplifier installation.** The amp on a Glide goes in the fairing with a tank pull for the power lead; on a Road King it rides in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect.

**Speaker replacement.** Tired stock speakers are swapped for weatherproof units; the shop recommends Hertz Neo in the pods.

Audio MotorSports installs car stereos, subwoofers, Apple CarPlay, window tint, alarms and remote start for the vehicles that are not Harleys.

## First-timer questions from Redondo Beach

### Where should a first bagger audio upgrade start?

Start with what you hear least: usually the sound disappearing at speed. For most riders that means AMS lower-fairing pods, which put speakers below the wind. If your fairing speakers are already blown, those get replaced first, and an amplifier comes when you want real volume.

### Can I ride from Redondo Beach for a consultation before buying anything?

Yes. Call or text with your model year and bike, then ride over so Nick can look at the fairing, radio and bags. He will lay out a first step and a path for later, and nothing is ordered until you say so. The ride is 7.2 miles.

### Will a stock Street Glide radio run AMS pods?

It can drive them, but the factory radio runs out of clean power quickly. Many riders start with pods on the stock radio and add an amplifier later. If you want full volume at freeway speed from day one, plan on the amplifier installation at the same visit.

### What is the difference between pods and bag-lid speakers?

Pods mount on the crash bar and aim at the rider, filling in midrange and vocals at speed. Bag-lid speakers turn the saddlebags into a bass enclosure with cut lids, carpeted spacers and inverted drivers. Pods are the usual first upgrade; lids come later for low end.

### How long is my bike at the shop for pods?

Once your pods are built, which takes around three weeks, the install is a same-day appointment and many riders wait at the shop. Order the pods, book the date, and ride down from Redondo Beach when they are ready.
