## Temple City riders ask

### What goes wrong with bagger wiring that was done in a hurry?

Power tapped from a nearby wire instead of the battery, no fuse at the source, splices twisted and taped, and a harness left loose to rub on the inner fairing. Vibration finishes the job within a few thousand miles. Clean wiring is why a good install still works years later.

### Is riding from Temple City to Carson worth it for fairing wiring?

Yes. Wiring is pure labor, and it is the part you cannot order online. A shop that opens baggers every week knows where the harness goes, how the fairing comes apart without broken tabs, and how to test it before you ride home. That is what the hour buys.

### Will new wiring in the fairing bother the factory electronics?

Not when it is routed properly. Signal cables are kept away from ignition wiring so there is no alternator whine, and the amplifier draws from its own fused lead so the factory circuits are untouched. The bike's original functions stay exactly as they were.

### How does the Road King handle audio wiring with no fairing?

The amplifier lives in a saddlebag on a carpeted board with an AMS quick disconnect, and the pod wiring runs inside the frame to the crash bar. Everything is loomed and tied so the bag still lifts off and nothing rubs on a hot case.
