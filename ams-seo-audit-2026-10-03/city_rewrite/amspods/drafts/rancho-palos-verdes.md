## Pods questions from Rancho Palos Verdes

### What exactly is an AMS lower-fairing pod?

A speaker enclosure that mounts on the crash bar, below the fairing and out of the wind, with the speaker aimed up at the rider. It fills in the midrange and vocals that the upper fairing speakers lose at speed. Each pair is built in this shop, in chrome or gloss, with bracket and hardware.

### Does the pods install leave any marks or drilling on my Road Glide?

No drilling into the fairing. The bracket clamps to the crash bar and the wiring runs inside the lower and along the factory harness. Take the pods off later and the bike looks the way it did, which matters on a bike you keep nice.

### Can I ride down from Rancho Palos Verdes and have pods installed the same day?

Yes, once your pair is built. Text the year and model, the pods get made, and you book the install for when they are ready. Install day is a single visit, and most riders wait at the shop or take a short walk while it is done.

### Do the pods come with speakers or do I buy those separately?

Ask when you order. The shop recommends Hertz Neo speakers in the pods and will quote them with the install, so you ride away with a working pair rather than empty housings. The amplifier, if you add one, is quoted on the bike.

### Is 9 miles from Rancho Palos Verdes worth the ride for pods?

Riders come from well over 100 miles for pods, and the ride down the hill is one of the shorter trips we see. You get the people who build the pods installing and tuning them on your bike, and a shop to come back to if anything ever needs a look.
