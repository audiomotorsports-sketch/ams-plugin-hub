## Questions Duarte riders ask

### Why do pods make such a difference on a group ride?

Fairing speakers fire forward into the wind. Pods on the crash bar fire at the rider from below, so they are heard before the airflow and the surrounding bikes swallow the sound. On a group ride that difference is the gap between music and noise.

### Can a Duarte rider book pods and a full install for one visit?

Yes, with a little planning. The pods are built to order over about three weeks, so the usual sequence is to text the year and model, let the pods get built, then ride in once for pods, amplifier installation and tuning in a single appointment.

### Will a louder system drain the battery on long rides?

Not when it is wired correctly. The amp draws from the battery through a fused lead and the charging system covers it at riding speed. The shop checks charging output during tuning, and a Road King with a bag amp gets the same check as a Glide.

### Does a Road King work for this kind of setup?

It does, and it is a common bike for riders who ride in groups. With no fairing, the AMS pods handle the front and the amplifier sits in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect. The bag still lifts off without tools.
