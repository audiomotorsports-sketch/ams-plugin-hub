## What Woodland Hills riders bring us

**Pod installation.** Pods mount to the crash bar on an angle using the bracket and screws that ship with them. Speaker wire runs inside the frame, loomed and tied so it stays away from heat and never rubs through.

**Pods built to order.** Chrome or gloss, made in Carson, about three weeks from order to ready. Book the install for the day they are done.

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** Pods without power are just chrome. The amp is fused at the battery; fairing amp with a tank pull on the Glides, saddlebag amp with an AMS quick disconnect on the Road King.

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation.** If the rest of the system is stock, the head unit, fairing speakers and pods are planned together so the result sounds like one system.

## Woodland Hills riders ask

### What do lower-fairing pods change on a bagger?

They add midrange and presence from below the wind. Fairing speakers sit up high and get blown back at speed; pods on the crash bar fire at the rider from down low. Together they let you hear the music clearly at a volume that does not wear you out.

### Is Woodland Hills to Carson a reasonable ride for a pod install?

Yes. It is about an hour, and the pods are built in the building you ride to. You get the bracket, the hardware, the recommended Hertz Neo speakers and the install from the people who made them, in one visit.

### Can I buy the pods and fit them myself?

You can; the bracket and screws come with them. Most riders let the shop do it because the wiring run through the frame, the amplifier tuning and the test ride at speed are what make pods sound right rather than merely loud.

### Which speakers does the shop put in the pods?

Hertz Neo: small neodymium magnet, weatherproof, clear when it is loud. They are matched to the amplifier so the pods blend with the fairing speakers instead of competing with them.

### Do the pods mount the same on a Road King as on the Glides?

The crash-bar mount is the same on all three bikes. What changes is the amplifier location: in the fairing on a Street Glide or Road Glide, in a saddlebag on a carpeted board on a Road King. Text (213) 429-1092 with your model year to confirm.
