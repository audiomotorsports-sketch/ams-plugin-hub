# AMS Pods Installation for Compton Harley Riders

Compton riders are about 5.4 miles from AMS Pods, the Harley-Davidson bagger audio side of Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson. The lead job here is the pods themselves: AMS lower-fairing speaker pods fitted to a Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King, mounted on the crash bar and wired so the music holds up once the bike is moving.

## What Compton riders bring us

**AMS pods installation.** The pods are built in-house in chrome or gloss and ship with the crash-bar bracket and screws. On the bike they sit angled toward the rider, the speaker leads run under the tank with soldered quick disconnects, and the bracket is torqued so nothing walks loose from vibration.

**Amplifier installation.** A pair of pods wants real power, so a fairing amp goes in on the Glides and a bag-mounted amp with a quick disconnect goes in on a Road King. Power comes straight off the battery with a fuse at the source.

**Speaker replacement in the fairing.** Factory cones that have gone soft get swapped for weatherproof speakers that match the pods, so the front stage sounds like one system instead of two.

**Head unit installation.** When the stock radio is the weak link, a Harley-specific radio with proper preamp outputs is fitted before anything is tuned.

If the truck needs work the same week, Audio MotorSports also does window tint, car stereo installation, CarPlay and Android Auto, car alarms, remote start, GPS trackers and dash cameras in the same building.

## Questions Compton riders ask

### How are AMS pods mounted on a Street Glide or Road Glide?

They bolt to the crash bar with the included bracket, angled so the speaker faces the rider rather than the road. The wiring runs along the frame and under the tank to the amplifier in the fairing, with quick disconnects at the pods so the bar can still come off for service without cutting anything.

### Do I have to order the pods before I ride in from Compton?

Yes, and that is the normal order of things. AMS pods are built to order and take about three weeks, so call or text with your year and model, let the pair get built, then book the install day. Riding in from Compton for the final fitting is one trip, not two.

### Will pods alone fix stock speakers that sound thin at speed?

Pods add a second pair of speakers down low, which makes a big difference in the wind, but they do not repair a tired front stage. Most Compton riders pair the pods with a fairing speaker replacement and an amplifier so every speaker on the bike is pulling its weight.

### What do AMS pods cost installed on a Road King?

The pods start from $649 a pair as of October 2026, chrome or gloss, bracket and hardware included. A Road King has no fairing, so the amplifier is mounted in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect, and that labor plus the amp is quoted at the shop after Nick sees the bike.

### Which speakers go inside the pods?

The shop fits Hertz Neo motorcycle speakers as the standard choice. They use a compact neodymium magnet, they are built to take weather, and they stay clear when the volume is up. Other speakers can be quoted, but that is the pairing the shop trusts for riders who ride year-round.
