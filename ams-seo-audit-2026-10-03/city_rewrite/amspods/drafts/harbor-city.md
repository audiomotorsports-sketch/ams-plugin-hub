# Harley Bagger Amplifier Installation for Harbor City Riders

AMS Pods is the Harley-Davidson bagger audio side of Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, about 2.8 miles from Harbor City. Riders bring in a Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King for amplifier installation and tuning, AMS lower-fairing speaker pods and a stereo that stays clear at freeway speed.

## What Harbor City riders bring us

**Amplifier installation and tuning on the bike.** The amp is mounted where it is protected from weather and vibration, wired with proper gauge power straight from the battery, and then tuned with the engine running so gain and crossover settings match how the bike is actually ridden. An amp set on a bench never sounds the same on the road.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Built in-house, chrome or gloss, with the crash-bar bracket and hardware included. The pods put a speaker down near your knees so the music does not vanish the moment you leave a stoplight.

**Speaker replacement in the fairing.** Stock paper cones give out; the shop fits weather-resistant replacements and seals the fairing back up cleanly.

**Head unit installation.** Factory radios are often the bottleneck, so a Harley-specific head unit with a proper preamp output is installed before the amp is tuned.

Audio MotorSports also handles car audio, window tint, car alarms and remote start installation, dash cameras and GPS trackers if the truck in the driveway needs attention too.

## Questions Harbor City riders ask

### Why have the amplifier tuned on the bike instead of just installed?

A bagger amplifier needs its gains, crossovers and levels set with the engine running and the fairing closed. That is the only way to hear what the rider hears. Tuning on the bike at Carson means the system is loud without clipping and the speakers are protected from a bad setting.

### Can a Harbor City rider get pods and an amp done in one visit?

Usually yes, once the pods are built. AMS pods are made to order and take roughly three weeks, so the normal path is to book, let the pods get built, then ride in from Harbor City for pods, amplifier installation and tuning in a single appointment.

### Does the amp install touch the tank or the fairing wiring?

On a Street Glide or Road Glide the amp lives in the fairing and power runs from the battery, which means a tank pull to route the cable correctly. On a Road King the amp sits in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect so the bag still comes off.

### Which speakers do you put in AMS pods?

The shop recommends Hertz Neo motorcycle speakers for the pods: a compact neodymium magnet, weatherproof build, and clear output when the volume is up. Other speakers can be quoted, but that pairing has been the standard choice for riders who want the pods to keep pace with the fairing.

### Is Harbor City close enough to just ride over without an appointment?

It is close, but an appointment keeps the one bay moving. Call (310) 513-8800 or text (213) 429-1092 with the year and which of the three baggers you ride, and Nick will set a time. Walk-in questions are welcome; actual installs are scheduled.
