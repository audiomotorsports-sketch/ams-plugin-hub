## Questions Palos Verdes Estates riders ask

### What does clean fairing wiring actually involve on a Street Glide?

The fairing comes off, the power lead is pulled under the tank from a fuse at the battery, signal cables are routed away from ignition and lighting wiring, connections are soldered and heat-shrunk, and every run is tied off so vibration cannot chafe it. Then the fairing goes back on with nothing pinched.

### Does salt air near Palos Verdes Estates affect a bagger audio install?

Coastal moisture is why the shop uses weatherproof Hertz Neo speakers in the pods and fairing, soldered joints instead of bare crimps, and proper quick disconnects. The hardware is chosen to be rained on. Rinse the bike the way you normally would and the system will not mind.

### Why solder the pod connections instead of using plug-in crimps?

A crimp that loosens on a crash bar at freeway speed cuts a channel out, usually mid-ride. Soldered leads with a real quick disconnect at the pod give you a joint that holds and a plug you can still pull for service. It is slower, and it is the right way.

### Can riders from Palos Verdes Estates see the wiring before the fairing is closed?

Yes, if you wait at the shop. Nick will walk you through where the amp sits, how the power run is fused, and where the quick disconnects are. Riders who drop the bike off get the same walk-through at pickup, with the fairing opened again if you want to look.

### How long does a wiring-first bagger build take?

An amplifier and speaker install with clean fairing wiring is normally a single day. Pods need about three weeks to build, so order them ahead and the fitting is one more visit. A complete build including head unit and cut bag lids can take up to about ten business days.
