## Wilmington riders ask

### What does marine air do to a bagger stereo in Wilmington?

It corrodes cheap speaker baskets, exposed terminals and unsealed splices. The answer is weatherproof Hertz Neo speakers, soldered and heat-shrunk connections, and an amplifier mounted up on a carpeted board where moisture cannot pool. Done that way, the system shrugs off the harbor.

### Why does fairing wiring matter more than the brand of amplifier?

Because the wiring is what fails. An amp fed by an unfused tap off a nearby wire, with twisted splices and a loose harness, will quit no matter who made it. A fused lead from the battery and a tied-down harness keep a modest amp working for years.

### Is a three-mile trip from Wilmington worth it for fairing wiring?

Yes, and it makes staging easy. Wilmington riders can have the wiring and amplifier done one visit and come back for pods when they are built, without a long haul either time. The pods are made in the same building.

### How is the Road King wired with no fairing?

On a Road King the amp mounts on a carpeted board inside a saddlebag, connected through an AMS quick disconnect, while the pod wiring is routed inside the frame out to the crash bar. The harness is loomed and tied so the bag still lifts off and nothing touches a hot case.
