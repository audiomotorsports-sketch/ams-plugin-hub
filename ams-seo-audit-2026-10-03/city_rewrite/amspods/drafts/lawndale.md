What is behind the inner fairing decides whether a bagger stereo works for years or fails on the first hot afternoon. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, wires Lawndale riders' Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King systems the proper way, crimped, fused and routed with the factory harness, then fits AMS lower-fairing pods built in the same shop. ## What Lawndale riders bring us

**Fairing wiring.** Power, ground, remote and speaker leads run along the factory loom and secured so a full lock-to-lock turn of the bars pulls on nothing. Every joint is crimped and covered rather than twisted and taped.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Mounted on the crash bar in chrome or gloss with bracket and hardware supplied, the pod leads routed inside the lower and up to the amp. Built in Carson by the people who install them.

## Wiring questions from Lawndale riders

### What does bad fairing wiring look like from the seat?

A stereo that drops a channel over bumps, hums when the engine revs, or fades after an hour in traffic. Behind the panel it is loose grounds, taped splices and wire pinched by the fairing bolts. Doing the harness once, properly, takes those problems off the bike.

### Does the ground really matter that much on a Street Glide?

It matters more than almost anything. A poor ground causes alternator whine, weak output and amps that go into protect. We ground to clean, bare metal with a proper lug and check it with the engine running, which is the difference between a system that is loud and one that is clean.

### Can a Lawndale rider bring a bike that was wired somewhere else?

Yes. Ride it in, we open the fairing and show you what is there, and quote the rewire on the bike. Often the parts are fine and only the install needs redoing. If pods or an amp are on your list, we wire for them at the same time so nothing gets done twice.

### How is the Road King wired without a fairing?

The amplifier goes in a saddlebag with an AMS quick disconnect so the bag still comes off, the power lead runs fused from the battery, and the pod wiring runs inside the lowers up to the amp. With no fairing, the pods carry the sound, so the wiring to them gets the same care as the amp.

### How long is a wiring job with pods at the shop?

Rewire, pods and an amp together is most of a day; book the morning slot and pick the bike up tuned in the afternoon. Pods on a bike that is already wired right is a shorter visit, and riders from Lawndale usually just wait at the shop for that.
