# Clean Fairing Wiring and AMS Pods for Malibu Harley Riders

Fog in the morning, salt in the air all day: a bagger ridden along the coast needs wiring that is sealed, crimped and routed to last. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, wires bagger audio properly for Malibu riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King, and fits AMS lower-fairing pods built in the same shop. The bay is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, 32.6 miles from Malibu.

## What Malibu riders bring us

**Wiring inside the fairing.** Power, ground, remote and speaker leads follow the factory harness and are secured away from the fork stops, with every joint crimped and sealed so moisture has nowhere to get in.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Chrome or gloss, clamped to the crash bar with the supplied bracket and hardware, wired inside the lower. Loaded with Hertz Neo speakers that are made for weather.

**Amplifier installation.** Mounted inside the fairing on the Glides or in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect on the Road King, on a fused lead from the battery, grounded to bare metal and tuned on the bike.

**Speaker installation.** Fairing speakers reseated with their gaskets so a wet ride does not end up inside the fairing, and matched to the pods and the amp.

Audio MotorSports also installs car stereos, Apple CarPlay, window tint, Tesla tint and alarms for the vehicles that stay in the garage.

## Wiring questions from Malibu riders

### Is corrosion the reason my bagger stereo keeps cutting out?

Near the coast it often is. Unsealed splices and bare grounds pick up salt and moisture, resistance builds and the system drops out or hums. The repair is to redo the connections with sealed crimps, ground to clean metal and route the harness where it stays dry.

### How should the wiring be run on a Road Glide for pods and an amp?

Power from the battery through a fuse to the amp in the fairing, remote turn-on from the head unit, speaker leads along the factory loom to the fairing speakers and down inside the lowers to the pods. Secured so turning the bars pulls on nothing, and nothing shows with the panels on.

### Can a Malibu rider get the wiring fixed without replacing parts?

Yes, when the parts are sound. We open the fairing, show you what is there, and quote the rewire on the bike. Riders bring us plenty of systems with good gear and poor installs. If pods or an amp are in the plan, we wire for them now.

### How long is the bike at the shop for a rewire with pods?

A rewire, pods and an amplifier together is most of a day. Ride down for a morning slot and the bike is ready, tuned, by late afternoon. Pods on a bike that is already wired right is shorter, and riders often wait at the shop.

### Is a 32-mile ride from Malibu worth it just for wiring?

Riders come to this bay from San Diego, about 120 miles, for the same work. A system wired once, properly, by the shop that builds the pods is the reason. Thirty-two miles down the coast is a good ride, and you leave with a stereo you stop thinking about.
