# Long-Ride Harley Bagger Audio for San Pedro Riders

A stereo that sounds fine at a stoplight can wear you out by the second tank of gas. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds Harley-Davidson bagger audio for San Pedro riders who put in real miles on a Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, about 6.5 miles from San Pedro, and makes its own lower-fairing pods.

## What San Pedro riders bring us

**Audio tuned for all-day riding.** Ear fatigue on a long ride comes from distortion. The amplifier is set with headroom to spare so the music stays clean for hours at highway speed, and the pods are aimed so you are not fighting the wind with the volume knob.

**AMS lower-fairing pods.** Made in Carson, chrome or gloss, with the crash-bar bracket and screws included. They fill in the low midrange that fairing speakers lose once you pass fifty.

**Amplifier installation on the bike.** Power comes straight from the battery with a fuse at the source. The Glides take a fairing amp and a tank pull; the Road King carries its amp in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect.

**Bag-lid bass.** Cut lids with carpeted spacers, inverted speakers and soldered quick disconnects turn the saddlebags into a subwoofer enclosure that still closes and still comes off.

**Speaker replacement.** Harbor air is hard on stock speakers; Hertz Neo units are weatherproof and stay clear when they are loud.

Need something for the car as well? Audio MotorSports does car stereo installation, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, ceramic window tint, car alarms, remote start and GPS trackers in the same building.

## San Pedro riders ask

### Is it worth the ride from San Pedro for AMS lower-fairing pods?

Yes, because the pods are built and installed by the same people, 6.5 miles up the road. You get the bracket, the hardware, the Hertz Neo speakers the shop recommends and the install from one bay. There is no reseller and no shipping damage to argue about.

### What keeps a bagger stereo from wearing me out on a long ride?

Clean power and sensible placement. An amplifier running inside its limits does not clip, and lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider deliver the music without being cranked. You end up riding all day at a moderate volume that still beats the wind.

### Can the salt air off the harbor ruin a bagger speaker?

Marine air corrodes cheap speaker baskets and exposed terminals. The shop uses weatherproof Hertz Neo speakers, solders and heat-shrinks the splices, and keeps the amplifier up off the bag floor on a carpeted board so moisture has nowhere to collect.

### Will a Road King from San Pedro get the same sound as a Street Glide?

Different layout, same result at speed. With no fairing, the Road King leans on the pods and the bag-lid speakers for the full sound, and the amplifier lives in a saddlebag with an AMS quick disconnect. Many riders are surprised how close it gets.
