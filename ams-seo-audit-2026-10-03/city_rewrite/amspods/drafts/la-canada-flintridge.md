## Amplifier questions from La Canada Flintridge

### How many channels does a bagger amplifier need for pods?

It depends on what is playing. Fairing speakers plus pods is a four-channel job; add bag-lid speakers and you are looking at more channels or a second amp. We spec it from your bike and what you want to hear, not from a one-size kit.

### Why tune the amplifier on the bike instead of on the bench?

Because the bike is the room. Engine noise, wind and where the pods sit all change what you hear, so the gains and crossovers are set with the motor running and checked on a ride. A bench tune is a guess; an on-bike tune is the real thing.

### Will a La Canada Flintridge rider lose the bike for more than a day?

No. Amplifier installation with pods and a tune is a long single day, not an overnight. Ride down for a morning appointment and the bike is ready by late afternoon. Pods alone is a shorter visit.

### Does the amp drain my battery when the bike sits?

Not when it is wired right. The amp turns on with the head unit through a remote lead and goes to sleep when the bike is off. A fused power lead straight from the battery and a clean ground keep the draw where it should be, which is nowhere while parked.

### I already have an amp that sounds bad; can it be retuned?

Usually, yes. Many systems that sound harsh or thin are simply set wrong: gains maxed, crossovers off, a channel out of phase. We check the install, fix the wiring if it needs it, and tune the amp on the bike. If the amp itself is the limit, we say so before you spend anything.
