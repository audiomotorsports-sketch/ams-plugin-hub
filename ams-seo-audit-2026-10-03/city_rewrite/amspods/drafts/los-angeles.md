A bagger that sounds right at 30 mph on Sunset and still sounds right six hours later on the 5 is a different build. ## What Los Angeles riders bring us

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation for distance.** Everything is chosen for hours at a steady speed: an amp with headroom, speakers that are not working at their limit, wiring that does not loosen with vibration.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Pods on the crash bar restore the midrange that wind strips away above 50 mph. Made in Carson, chrome or gloss, hardware included.

## Long-ride questions from Los Angeles riders

### What makes a bagger system hold up over a full day of riding?

Headroom and heat. An amplifier running at a comfortable fraction of its output stays clean and cool for hours; one that is maxed gets hot and goes into protect on the first long climb. Speakers placed low, like the pods, mean the volume never has to be pushed that far to begin with.

### Does a Los Angeles rider need a new head unit for long-ride sound?

Not necessarily. Many riders keep the factory deck and feed the amp through a signal converter, with pods and fairing speakers doing the work. A head unit installation comes in when you want cleaner output or more sources, and it is quoted on the bike.

### How do pods change the sound at freeway speed on a Road Glide?

The frame-mounted fairing on a Road Glide helps the upper speakers, but the wind still takes the midrange above about 50 mph. Pods on the crash bar sit in calmer air and aim at you, so vocals come back and the amp is not asked to shout.

### Can the bike be done in one trip from Los Angeles?

Yes. Order the pods ahead by text with your year and model, book the install for when they are ready, and ride down once. Pods, amplifier and tune together is a full-day appointment; pods alone is shorter and riders often wait at the shop.

### What is the shop's record for how far riders travel for this?

Riders have come from San Diego and Temecula, and the shop has taken calls from San Jose. Fifteen miles across Los Angeles is an ordinary appointment, and you ride home with a system that was tuned on your bike by the people who built the pods.
