## Questions Anaheim Hills riders ask

### What usually blows stock speakers on a Harley bagger?

A factory radio run at full volume sends a clipped signal, and clipping overheats the voice coil. Moisture in a paper cone finishes the job. New weatherproof speakers fix the second cause; an amplifier with clean headroom fixes the first.

### Should an Anaheim Hills rider replace just the dead speaker?

Replace them as a pair at minimum. A new speaker beside a tired one sounds uneven, and the tired one is usually close behind. Most riders replace the fairing set and add an amp in the same visit so the failure does not come back.

### Can speaker replacement be done the same day I ride in?

Fairing speakers alone are normally a same-day job, so waiting at the shop works. If an amplifier or pods are added, plan on most of the day. The pods are built to order over about three weeks, so order those before the ride.

### Will new speakers sound better without an amplifier?

They will sound cleaner, but a factory radio still runs out of power at highway speed and will eventually do the same damage. Pairing the replacement with amplifier installation and tuning is the shop's recommendation for anyone who rides at freeway speed.
