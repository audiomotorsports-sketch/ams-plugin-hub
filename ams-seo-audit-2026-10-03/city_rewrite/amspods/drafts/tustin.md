## Tustin riders ask

### Where should a Tustin rider start with a stock Street Glide?

Start with what you hear every day. If the fairing speakers are the weak spot, replace them and add an amplifier so they have real power. Order the pods at the same visit so they are built by the time you come back. That sequence avoids paying for anything twice.

### Do I need a new radio for a first bagger audio upgrade?

Not always. Some factory radios have enough output to feed an amplifier; others do not. The shop stocks Harley-fit head units from Diamond Audio, Sony and Soundstream, and Nick will say whether yours is worth keeping before anything is ordered.

### Is riding from Tustin to Carson worth it for a first upgrade?

Yes, because a first upgrade done right is the one you never redo. The pods are made in the building, the installer does baggers every week, and the plan is written for your bike. Done once, done right beats a bargain install that comes back apart.

### How is a Road Glide different from a Street Glide for audio?

The shark-nose fairing on a Road Glide is frame-mounted, so the speaker and amp layout differs a little from the fork-mounted batwing on a Street Glide. Both take a fairing amplifier and a tank pull for power. The pods mount on the crash bar the same way on either bike.

### Can I pay for a bagger audio build in stages?

Yes. A staged plan is the normal way to do a first upgrade, and a payment plan is available if you ask Nick. Each stage is quoted on the bike so you know the number before the work begins.
