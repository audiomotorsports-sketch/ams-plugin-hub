## What Torrance riders bring us

**Wiring inside the fairing.** The batwing is tight, hot and shakes constantly. Every connection is soldered or crimped properly, loomed, and tied down so nothing chafes against the inner fairing or works loose after a few thousand miles of vibration.

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation.** Head unit, amplifier and speakers installed as one system with power pulled from the battery under the tank, not tapped from a convenient wire.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** Chrome or gloss, mounted to the crash bar on an angle, bracket and screws in the box. Pods are built to order in Carson, roughly three weeks.

**Saddlebag bass.** On bikes with cut lids the bags become a subwoofer enclosure: carpeted spacers, inverted speakers, soldered quick disconnects and a lid that still closes.

The same shop installs car stereos, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, ceramic window tint, Viper alarms and remote start for whatever else is in the garage.

## Torrance bagger questions

### What does clean wiring inside a Harley fairing actually involve?

It means a dedicated power lead from the battery with a fuse at the source, a solid ground, signal cables routed away from ignition wiring, and every splice soldered and heat-shrunk. The harness is loomed and tied so the inner fairing goes back on without pinching a single wire.

### Do Torrance riders need to leave the bike overnight?

A pods-only install is a same-day job once your pods are built. A full fairing system with head unit, amplifier and speakers can take longer, and Nick will give you an honest timeline when you book. Many riders simply wait at the shop for the shorter work.

### Will the stock Harley radio work with new speakers and an amp?

Sometimes, but the factory unit is often the weakest link. The shop stocks Harley-fit head units with real preamp outputs, and Nick will tell you whether yours is worth keeping before anything is ordered. The answer depends on the model year and what you expect at speed.

### Is it worth riding from Torrance for AMS lower-fairing pods?

Yes, because the pods are built here and installed here by the people who make them. There is no middle step. You get the bracket, the hardware, the Hertz Neo speakers the shop recommends and the install from one bay, 4.4 miles from Torrance.

### How do I get a quote before I ride down?

Text (213) 429-1092 with the model year, which bagger it is, and a photo of the fairing and bags if you have aftermarket lids. Nick replies with a plan and a time slot, and the final number is confirmed when he sees the bike in person.
