# AMS Lower-Fairing Pod Installation for Reseda Riders

The pods are the reason most Reseda riders make the trip. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds its own lower-fairing speaker pods and installs them on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King in the same bay. ## What Reseda riders bring us

**The pod install itself.** Pods bolt to the crash bar on an angle with the bracket and screws that come in the box. The speaker wire is run inside the frame, loomed, and tied so it never chafes on a hot engine case.

**Pods built to order.** Chrome or gloss, made in Carson, about three weeks. Order them when you book so they are ready the day you ride down.

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** Pods need real power. The amp is fused at the battery; Street Glide and Road Glide take a fairing amp and a tank pull, the Road King carries it in a saddlebag with an AMS quick disconnect.

**Harley-Davidson bagger audio installation.** If the rest of the system is tired, the head unit, fairing speakers and pods are planned together so the bike sounds like one system, not three.

For the car in the driveway, Audio MotorSports also does car stereo and CarPlay installation, ceramic window tint, alarms, remote start and GPS trackers.

## Reseda riders ask

### What do AMS lower-fairing pods actually add to a bagger?

Midrange and presence down low, right where the wind is not. Fairing speakers sit up high and get blown back at highway speed; pods on the crash bar fire at the rider from below. The combination is what lets you hear the music clearly without cranking it.

### Is an hour from Reseda a reasonable ride for a pod install?

Yes. Reseda riders are a fraction of the distance others cover for this shop, and the pods are made in the building you ride to. You get the bracket, the hardware, the recommended Hertz Neo speakers and the install from the people who built them.

### Can I install the pods myself if I buy them?

You can, and the bracket and screws come with them. Most riders have the shop do it because the wiring run inside the frame, the amplifier tuning and the final test at speed are the parts that make pods sound right instead of just loud.

### Which speakers go inside the pods?

The shop recommends Hertz Neo: small neodymium magnet, weatherproof, and clear when it is loud. They are matched to the amplifier so the pods and the fairing speakers blend rather than compete.

### Do the pods fit a Road King the same way they fit a Street Glide?

The crash-bar mount is the same across the three bikes. The difference is where the amplifier lives: in the fairing on the Glides, in a saddlebag on a carpeted board on the Road King. Text (213) 429-1092 with your model year and Nick confirms the fit.
