# Long-Ride Bagger Audio for Diamond Bar Harley Riders

A stereo that sounds fine at a stoplight and vanishes an hour into the ride is the complaint that brings Diamond Bar riders to AMS Pods, the Harley bagger audio shop inside Audio MotorSports at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, about 28.1 miles away. The fix is a system built for distance on a Street Glide, Road Glide or Road King: AMS lower-fairing pods, an amplifier tuned on the bike and speakers that do not fatigue.

## What Diamond Bar riders bring us

**Bagger audio built for long days.** Hours of freeway wind punish a system that is set too hot. The amp is tuned for headroom rather than peak loudness, so the music is still clean at mile two hundred and your ears are not worn out by it.

**AMS pods.** Lower-fairing speaker pods built in the shop, chrome or gloss, with the crash-bar bracket and screws. On a long ride the pods are what let you turn the fairing down and still hear everything.

**Speaker replacement.** Weatherproof Hertz Neo speakers in the pods and matching replacements in the fairing, chosen because they stay clear when they are loud for a long time.

**Amplifier installation.** Fairing-mounted on the Glides, bag-mounted with a quick disconnect on a Road King, powered from the battery through a fuse and wired with soldered connections that hold up to vibration.

Audio MotorSports also does window tint, car stereo installation, CarPlay and Android Auto, car alarm and remote start installation, GPS trackers and dash cameras for the vehicle you leave at home.

## Questions Diamond Bar riders ask

### Why does my bagger stereo seem to get quieter the longer I ride?

It usually is not getting quieter; the wind is getting louder and your ears are tiring. A system tuned for clean headroom instead of peak volume, plus AMS pods close to the rider, keeps the music intelligible without pushing the fairing speakers into distortion for hours at a stretch.

### Is an hour-long ride from Diamond Bar reasonable for a pods install?

It is, and it is shorter than many of the rides the shop sees. The pods are built to order over about three weeks, so the trip is one planned visit for pods, amp and tuning rather than a series of short ones.

### Do long rides wear out the speakers faster?

Only when the amp is clipping. A distorted signal heats voice coils and that is what kills speakers on a touring bike. Tuning the amplifier on the bike at road speed is the shop's way of making sure a Diamond Bar rider never finds out what a blown speaker sounds like.

### Can the saddlebags carry a subwoofer for more bass on the highway?

Yes. The lids are cut for inverted speakers on carpeted spacers, the connections are soldered with quick disconnects and the bag stays usable. On a long ride that low end fills in what the wind strips out of the fairing.

### How much are AMS pods for a Road Glide ridden from Diamond Bar?

AMS lower-fairing pods are from $649 a pair as of October 2026, in chrome or gloss with the bracket and screws. The amplifier, fairing speakers and any bag subwoofer are quoted at the shop once Nick has the bike in front of him. Payment plan available, ask Nick.
