## What Gardena riders bring us

**Pods for the rider, not the crowd.** AMS pods sit on the crash bar angled up at you, so your music reaches you before the group's pipes do. Built in the shop, chrome or gloss, bracket and screws included.

**Amplifier installation and tuning.** In a group the volume stays up for a long time, so the amp is tuned for headroom with the engine running. That protects the speakers and keeps the sound clean at the back of the pack.

**Speaker replacement.** Fairing speakers that have been overdriven on rides get replaced with weatherproof units matched to the pods.

**Bag subwoofers.** Cut lids with inverted speakers on carpeted spacers for the low end that carries at speed.

## Questions Gardena riders ask

### Can a group of Gardena riders book the shop for several bikes?

Yes, but one bay means the bikes go in one at a time. Call with the year and model for each bike and Nick will stagger the appointments so nobody sits around. Pods for each bike are built to order over about three weeks, so order those first.

### Why do pods help more than a louder fairing when riding in a group?

Turning the fairing up just pushes sound into the wind and toward the bike ahead of you. Pods on the crash bar point at the rider from below, so you hear your own music at a lower level while the pack noise stays where it is.

### Will the system still work with the bags off?

Yes. A Road King bag amp and any saddlebag subwoofer use AMS quick disconnects, so the bags come off without tools and the fairing and pods keep playing on their own.

### Does the shop tune the amp with the engine running?

Always. The gains, crossovers and levels are set with the engine on and the fairing closed, then checked on a ride. A tune done on a bench in silence does not survive the first freeway mile.
