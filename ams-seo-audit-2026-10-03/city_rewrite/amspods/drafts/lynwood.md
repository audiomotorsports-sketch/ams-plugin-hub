# Blown Stock Speakers Replaced on Harley Baggers for Lynwood Riders

A crackle on the left side, a buzz on bass notes, then nothing at all: stock bagger speakers do not last forever, especially when a rider has been turning them up to beat the wind. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, replaces blown fairing speakers for Lynwood riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King, and fixes the reason they blew with AMS lower-fairing pods and a proper amplifier. ## Speaker questions from Lynwood riders

### Why did my stock Street Glide speakers blow in the first place?

Usually because the factory radio was driven into distortion trying to beat the wind. A clipping signal cooks voice coils. Replacing the speakers fixes the symptom; pods and an amplifier fix the cause, because the sound reaches you without being pushed that hard.

### Can a Lynwood rider get just the speakers swapped and nothing else?

Yes. Speaker replacement on its own is a same-day job and plenty of riders wait at the shop for it. We will tell you honestly whether the new speakers will survive on the stock radio at the volume you ride, and what the next step would be if not.

### Do replacement speakers need an amplifier?

Not always, but they last longer with one. An amp supplies clean power at the volume the speakers are built for, while a factory deck turned all the way up delivers a distorted signal. If freeway volume is the goal, plan the amplifier installation with the speakers.

### What speakers go in the AMS pods?

The shop recommends Hertz Neo for the pods, chosen because they handle weather and the power a bagger amp puts out. Fairing speakers are matched to them so the whole system plays evenly, with the tune done on the bike.

### How soon can I ride from Lynwood and get the bike fixed?

Call or text with the year and model and we find you a slot. A speaker swap is a short visit; speakers plus pods and an amp is most of a day, so take the morning appointment. Lynwood is a short ride, so a drop-off and pick-up works well.
