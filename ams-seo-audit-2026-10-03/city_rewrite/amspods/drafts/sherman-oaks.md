## Sherman Oaks riders ask

### Can a Sherman Oaks rider get the whole system done in one trip?

Often, if the pods are ordered ahead. Pods take about three weeks to build, so text Nick first, book the install for when they are ready, and the amplifier, speakers and pods go in on the same visit. A complete build with a head unit may take longer.

### What makes bagger audio hold up when riding with a group?

Headroom and placement. An amplifier running well inside its limits stays clean, and lower-fairing pods aimed at the rider deliver music where the fairing speakers cannot at speed. Clean sound at moderate volume beats a distorted system turned all the way up.

### Does the shop recommend a particular speaker for the pods?

Hertz Neo. Small neodymium magnet, weatherproof, and clear when it is loud. They are matched to the amplifier so the pods and the fairing speakers blend rather than compete for the same frequencies.

### Why ride from Sherman Oaks instead of using a shop closer to the Valley?

Because the pods are made here and installed here by the people who build them. There is no middle step, and the installer sees baggers every week. For a system you want to last, that is worth an hour on the 405.
