# Harley Bagger Audio for Long Beach Riders

When you ride with a pack, your music has to carry over a dozen exhausts. AMS Pods by Audio MotorSports, Carson, builds and installs lower-fairing speaker pods and full bagger audio systems for Long Beach riders on the Street Glide, Road Glide and Road King. The shop is at 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A, 5.7 miles from Long Beach.

## What Long Beach riders bring us

**Bagger audio built for riding with a group.** The system is tuned so the rider hears it clearly at cruising speed with other bikes alongside, which takes real amplifier power and speakers placed low as well as in the fairing.

**AMS lower-fairing speaker pods.** The pods angle off the crash bar and fill in the sound that the fairing speakers lose to wind. Built in-house, chrome or gloss, with bracket and hardware supplied.

**Amplifier installation.** A fairing amp on the Glides, a saddlebag amp with an AMS quick disconnect on the Road King, each wired from the battery with a fused lead.

**Speaker installation.** Fairing speakers and bag-lid speakers matched to the amp so nothing is starved and nothing is overdriven.

Need the car done too? Audio MotorSports installs car stereo systems, subwoofers, window tint, remote start and dash cameras in the same building.

## What Long Beach riders want to know

### How loud does a bagger system need to be for group rides?

Loud enough to hear over wind and the bikes around you without distorting. That is less about peak volume and more about clean amplifier headroom and speaker placement. Lower-fairing pods near your legs do a lot of that work because they sit out of the main wind blast.

### Can several Long Beach riders book the same day?

Yes, within the limits of one bay. Call ahead with the number of bikes and what each one needs, and Nick will stagger the appointments. Pods for every bike are built in advance, since each pair is made to order over roughly three weeks.

### What goes into a Road Glide fairing versus a Road King?

A Road Glide has a frame-mounted fairing with room for an amp and a proper head unit, so the system lives up front. A Road King has no fairing, so the pods carry the sound and the amplifier goes in a saddlebag with a quick disconnect.

### Are AMS pods weatherproof enough for riding near the coast?

The pods are finished in chrome or gloss and the shop pairs them with Hertz Neo speakers, which are built to shrug off weather. Mounting hardware is included. Salt air and morning fog in Long Beach are part of normal riding and the setup is chosen with that in mind.

### Do I need to bring anything besides the bike?

Bring the keys and any aftermarket parts you want used, such as cut saddlebag lids. Text a photo of the fairing and bags beforehand if the bike is not stock. Everything else, from wiring to brackets, is handled in the shop.
