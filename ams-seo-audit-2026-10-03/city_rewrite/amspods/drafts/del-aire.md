## What Del Aire riders bring us

**Amplifier installation and tuning on the bike.** The amp is mounted inside the fairing on the Glides or in a saddlebag on a Road King, fed with properly sized power cable from the battery, and then set up with the engine running. Gains, crossover points and levels are dialed in by ear and meter at idle and again at road speed so nothing clips when you roll on the throttle.

**AMS pods.** Lower-fairing speaker pods built in the shop, chrome or gloss, with the crash-bar bracket included. They give the amp a second pair of speakers to drive and fill in the sound that disappears at 60 mph.

**Head unit installation.** A factory radio with weak outputs holds back any amp. Diamond Audio, Sony and Soundstream Harley radios are stocked, so the signal chain can be fixed in the same visit.

**Saddlebag subwoofer installation.** Bag lids can be cut for inverted speakers on carpeted spacers, which turns the bag into a bass enclosure without giving up the bag.

## Questions Del Aire riders ask

### What does tuning an amp on the bike actually involve?

With the fairing closed and the engine running, the installer sets input gain so the amp is not clipping at the radio's top volume, picks crossover points that keep bass out of the small fairing speakers, and balances pods against the fairing. Then the bike goes for a ride and the levels get checked again at speed.

### Is Del Aire close enough to wait at the shop for an amp install?

It is close, but an amplifier install on a Glide includes a tank pull and careful wiring, so plan on most of a day. Many Del Aire riders drop the bike in the morning and pick it up before closing. Waiting at the shop is fine if you would rather see it done.

### Where does the amplifier go on a Road King?

A Road King has no batwing fairing, so the amp is mounted on a carpeted board inside a saddlebag with an AMS quick disconnect. The bag still comes off in seconds and the amp stays protected from weather and vibration.

### Can you tune an amp that another shop installed?

Yes, provided the wiring is safe. The installer checks power and ground, the fuse at the battery and the signal path first, then tunes the amp on the bike. If the wiring needs to be redone the work is quoted before anything is touched.
