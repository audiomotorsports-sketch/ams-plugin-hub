### What is the difference between Solar and Solar Ceramic tint?

Solar film darkens the glass and cuts glare and UV at a lower price. Solar Ceramic uses a ceramic layer that also rejects infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler at the same shade. If the car lives outside in Palos Verdes Estates, ceramic is the one to get.

### How soon after tinting can I roll the windows down?

Give it three to five days. The film is held by a water-based adhesive that needs time to cure, and rolling a window early can catch the top edge and peel it. Light haze or small water pockets in the first week are normal and clear as the film dries out.

### Do you tint windshields?

### Can I get window tinting and a dash camera installation in one appointment?
