### My La Palma car has a factory screen; can an Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation keep it?

Often yes. Many vehicles from the mid-2010s onward accept a module that feeds CarPlay or Android Auto through the original screen while keeping the factory radio, camera and settings menus. Where the screen is too old to support it, a replacement head unit with full integration is the answer.

### What is the difference between a wired and a wireless CarPlay install?

Wired CarPlay connects through a USB cable and charges the phone while you drive. Wireless pairs over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi so the phone stays in your pocket. Most new head units do both, and wired factory systems can be made wireless with an adapter. We set up whichever you prefer.

### Will I lose my backup camera or steering-wheel buttons with a new deck?

No. The camera comes through an interface harness and the wheel buttons through a control module, both matched to your year, make and model. We test the camera, the buttons and the Bluetooth before you leave the shop so nothing is discovered missing on the drive home.
