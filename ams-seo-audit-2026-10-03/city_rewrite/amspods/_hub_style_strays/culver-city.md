### What does a proper car stereo installation include?

The deck, the dash kit, the wiring harness, the steering-wheel interface and the camera retention, all matched to your year, make and model before the dash is opened. Then every function is tested before you leave. Done once, done right, instead of an install that has to be redone later.

### Does speaker replacement make a real difference without an amplifier?

Yes, a noticeable one. A good speaker on factory power is clearer and tighter than the paper cone it replaces, especially in the midrange. Add an amplifier installation later and the same speakers open up further. Many Culver City cars start with speakers and come back for the amp.

### How is a subwoofer installation done in a car with a factory stereo?

A line-output converter takes the signal from the factory speaker wires, an amplifier drives the sub, and the enclosure is secured in the trunk so it does not move. A Dual Kicker Comp box is $249 box only as of October 2026, with the amp and wiring quoted on the car.
