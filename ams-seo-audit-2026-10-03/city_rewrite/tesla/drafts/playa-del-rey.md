## Playa del Rey Tesla tint questions

### What makes Model 3 tint different from an ordinary sedan?

The rear window. On most sedans it is a modest, gently curved pane; on a Model 3 it is one wide, steep piece that wraps toward the roof. The film has to be shrunk to that curve and laid as a single sheet. The glass roof is the other difference, and it gets its own film for heat.

### Is Playa del Rey too far to bother for Tesla tint?

Not close. It is half an hour, and the shop sees Tesla owners from more than 100 miles out for the same ceramic install. You book, you drive in, the film goes on in a few hours, and you drive home with glass that will not need redoing.

### Will the tint show from the outside on a white Model 3?

The side and rear glass will look darker, which most owners want. What ceramic film does not do is turn the glass mirrored or give it a purple cast over time. The roof already reads black, so film there changes almost nothing visually. Nick can show samples on the car.

### Does the Model 3 roof tint go on from inside or outside?

Inside. The film is shaped on a separate surface, then carried in and applied to the underside of the roof glass with the headliner left in place. No trim comes off and no exterior work is involved. It is the same approach used for the one-piece rear window.

### Can I combine a dash camera install with my Model 3 tint?

Yes, and it is a common pairing. Audio MotorSports does dash camera installation with the wiring hidden along the headliner and pillar so nothing dangles from the mirror. Let us know when you book and the appointment will cover both jobs in one visit.
