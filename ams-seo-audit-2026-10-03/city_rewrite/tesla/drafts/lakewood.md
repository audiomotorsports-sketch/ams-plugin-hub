## Lakewood Tesla tint questions

### Which Tesla cameras sit on tinted glass, and how do you work around them?

Only the windshield housing is on glass that gets any film, and it is left fully uncovered; the visor strip stops above it. The pillar, fender and rear cameras are not on the door or rear glass. The door film is cut to the seal and never near a camera. Nothing is covered and cut out afterward.

### Will tint change how the phone key or the Tesla app connects in Lakewood traffic?

No. Metallic films can weaken radio signals; the ceramic film we install has no metal, so the phone key, Bluetooth and cellular data pass through the glass as before. Owners do not notice any change in how the car unlocks or how the app connects.

### Is a Lakewood owner able to get tint and a dash camera in one appointment?

Yes. The dash camera is wired to a fused source with the cable hidden behind the headliner and pillar trim, so there is no loose cord near the mirror. It adds time, so say you want both when you book and we will set aside enough of the day.

### Does the rear window film block the backup camera on a Tesla?

It cannot, because the backup camera is in the trunk or hatch lid, not on the glass. The rear window is tinted as one sheet without any effect on the camera. The rear defroster keeps working too; just let the film cure a few days before using it.

### What front shade keeps a Tesla legal in California?

The front door glass has to pass 70% of visible light. Ceramic film at that shade is light to look at but still rejects most infrared heat, so the fronts get cooler without drawing attention. The rear doors and rear window can be as dark as you choose, and the factory roof glass is already dark.
