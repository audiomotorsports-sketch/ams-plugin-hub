## El Segundo Tesla tint questions

### How do you make sure the tint never covers a Tesla camera?

By marking the camera zones before any film is cut. The windshield housing stays uncovered and the visor strip ends above it. The pillar and fender cameras are not on the door glass, and the rear camera is in the lid. The door film is cut to the seal, nowhere near a lens.

### Does ceramic film weaken the phone key or the cell signal in a Tesla?

No. Films with a metal layer can, which is why we do not use them on Teslas. Ceramic film has no metal, so the phone key, Bluetooth and cellular data pass through the glass the same as before. Owners do not notice any change in connection or unlocking.

### Can an El Segundo owner combine Tesla tint with a dash camera install?

Yes. The dash camera is wired to a fused source with the cable hidden behind the headliner and pillar trim, so nothing hangs near the mirror. It adds time to the visit, so say you want both when you book and we will reserve enough of the day.

### Is the rear camera affected by one-piece rear window tint?

It is not. The rear camera lives in the trunk or hatch lid, not on the glass, so the rear window film never covers it. The rear defroster keeps working under the film; just let the film cure for a few days before switching the defroster on.

### What shade keeps the fronts legal on a Tesla in California?

Front door glass must pass 70% of visible light. Ceramic film at that shade still rejects most infrared heat, so the fronts get cooler without a dark look. The rear doors and rear window have no shade limit and can go as dark as you prefer.
