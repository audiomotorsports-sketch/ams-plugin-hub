## Tint for a Tesla that just arrived in Studio City

**New-car ceramic tint.** Factory glass with no residue is the ideal surface. Ceramic film on the doors and rear glass goes on clean and starts rejecting infrared heat the day you pick the car up, which is the main complaint with a glass-roofed Model 3 or Model Y.

**Nothing cut on the car.** Every panel is patterned and heat-shaped on a separate glass board. A new Tesla's paint, door trim and headliner are never touched by a blade.

**Rear window and frameless doors.** The rear glass is one formed sheet; the door glass is cut to the glass line so the edge is clean when the door swings open.

**Model S, Model X and Cybertruck.** Larger glass on the S and X and flat panels on the Cybertruck, each with its own pattern and its own figure.

**Cameras and connectivity.** Camera zones are masked and the ceramic film is non-metallic, so a new Tesla keeps every driver-assist and phone-key function it was delivered with.

A dash camera or GPS tracker can be installed on the same visit, and Audio MotorSports also handles car stereo, CarPlay and Android Auto, car alarm, remote start and Harley bagger audio work.

### Is the drive from Studio City too long for Tesla tint?

### Can tinting a brand-new Tesla damage the paint or interior?

Not when the film is cut and shaped on a glass board away from the car. No blade touches the paint, the door panels or the headliner, and the electronics behind the rear window are covered before the glass is wet.

### How much is tint for a new Model Y or Model 3?

### Will tint change the cameras or phone key on a new Tesla?

No. The camera cluster and the side repeaters are masked and the film pattern stops short of them. Ceramic film carries no metal, so the phone key, GPS and cellular data are unaffected and the car keeps every feature it left the delivery center with.
