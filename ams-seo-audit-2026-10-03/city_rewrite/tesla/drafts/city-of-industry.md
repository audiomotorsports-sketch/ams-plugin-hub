### How far is City of Industry from the Tesla tint shop?

### Can a Cybertruck be tinted legally on the front doors?

Yes. The front glass stays at California 70% VLT, and ceramic film at that shade still blocks a large share of infrared heat. The rear doors and back glass can be darker. The result is a cooler cab with no inspection concern.

### Does the film interfere with Cybertruck cameras or the phone key?

No. Camera zones are masked and left clear, and the ceramic film contains no metal, so nothing is blocked. Cameras, sensors and the phone key keep working as they did before the tint went on.

### Why drive to Carson instead of a closer tint shop?

Because the film is hand-cut to the glass rather than a plotter pattern trimmed short, the cameras are protected every time, and the quote is by model with nothing hidden. Done once, done right, and it stays on the truck for years.
