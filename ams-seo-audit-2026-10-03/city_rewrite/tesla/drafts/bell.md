## Bell drivers ask

### Is a Model S harder to tint than other Teslas?

The side glass is longer and curves more, so the film needs more heat-shaping before it will lie flat without fingers at the edge. That is extra labor, which is why the S is quoted on its own. The result on a well-shaped piece is a clean edge and no distortion.

### How dark can I go on a Tesla in California?

Front door windows must stay at 70% VLT. Behind the front doors there is no limit, so the rear doors and back glass can go dark. Ceramic film at the legal front shade still rejects most of the infrared heat, which is the part you feel.

### Does ceramic film block the Tesla phone key or cameras?

It does not. The film has no metallic layer, so Bluetooth, cell and camera signals pass through it. The camera windows on the glass are masked off and left uncovered as well, so the car sees exactly what it saw before.
