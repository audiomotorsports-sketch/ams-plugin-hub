### What makes Model 3 tint different from a regular sedan?

No door frames, a large curved one-piece rear window and cameras in the glass areas. The door film is cut to the exact glass line, the rear window is done as one formed sheet, and the camera zones stay clear.

### Can I keep the Model 3 front windows legal and still cut heat?

Yes. The front door glass stays at 70% light transmission, and ceramic film at that shade still rejects a large share of infrared heat. The rear doors and back window can go darker, which is where most owners put the shade.

### How are the other Tesla models priced compared with a Model 3?

### Will tint affect the Model 3 cameras or phone key?

No. The windshield camera cluster and the side repeaters are masked and the film pattern stops short of them. Ceramic film carries no metal, so the phone key, cellular and GPS signals are unchanged and Autopilot sees the same view as before.
