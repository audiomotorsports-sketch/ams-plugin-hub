### Is it worth leaving Harbor City for Tesla window tint when the shop is only a few minutes away?

### Which film do you recommend for a Model Y with the glass roof?

Ceramic. A Model Y carries a lot of glass, and ceramic film rejects heat without going darker than you want. The front doors stay at the California-legal 70% VLT and the rear glass can go darker. We walk you through the shades at the counter before cutting anything.

### Will tint on my Tesla interfere with the cameras or the key signal?

No. Ceramic film is metal-free, so there is no signal interference with the phone key, cameras or antennas. The camera areas on the glass are left clear and the rear-window electronics are protected during prep. Your Tesla drives away working exactly as it did when it arrived.

### How long does Tesla window tint take if I drive in from Harbor City?

Plan on the better part of a morning or afternoon depending on how much glass you want covered. Most Harbor City owners drop the car off and pick it up later the same day, though you are welcome to wait at the shop. Call ahead so the slot is yours.

### Do you cut the film on the Tesla glass itself?

Never. Every piece is cut on a glass board first and then applied to the car. Cutting on the vehicle is how windows get scratched and rear defroster lines get sliced. The film edge sits right up to the gasket so there is no hairline of light later.
