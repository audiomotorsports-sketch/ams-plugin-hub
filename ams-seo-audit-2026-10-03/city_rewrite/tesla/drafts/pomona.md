## Pomona Tesla roof tint questions

### How much cooler does a Tesla get with the roof tinted?

Enough that you notice it the first hot afternoon. The roof is the largest glass panel on the car and it takes the sun from directly overhead. Ceramic film cuts a large share of that heat, so the cabin is cooler when you open the door and the climate system reaches a comfortable temperature sooner.

### Why drive from Pomona instead of tinting closer to home?

Because the roof and the one-piece rear glass are the two panels most often done poorly, and Tesla Tint LA shapes both as single sheets, week in and week out. The shop regularly sees owners from Temecula and San Diego, so a Pomona trip is a normal one for film that is installed once and correctly.

### Does roof tint change the outside look of my Tesla?

Very little. Tesla roof glass already reads nearly black from outside, and ceramic film adds only a slight extra depth. It never goes mirrored and it does not turn purple over time. The difference is felt inside, not seen from the driveway.

### Is anything removed from the car to tint the roof?

No. The film is shaped on a separate surface, carried inside and applied to the underside of the roof glass with the headliner, trim and lighting all left in place. No blade touches the glass and nothing is drilled or glued. The one-piece rear window is done the same way.

### Will ceramic film block the phone key or the cameras?

No. Ceramic film has no metal layer, so the phone key, card reader and the car's cellular and GPS signals pass through as before. The windshield camera window and the pillar cameras are left clear. Driver-assist features and the app connection are unchanged.
