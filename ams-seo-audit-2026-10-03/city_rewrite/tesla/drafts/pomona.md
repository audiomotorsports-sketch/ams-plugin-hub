### Why drive from Pomona instead of tinting closer to home?

Because the one-piece rear glass is the panel most often done poorly, and Tesla Tint LA shapes it as a single sheet, week in and week out. The shop regularly sees owners from Temecula and San Diego, so a Pomona trip is a normal one for film that is installed once and correctly.

### Does the film change the outside look of my Tesla?

Only on the glass that gets filmed. The side and rear windows look a little darker, which most owners want. Ceramic never goes mirrored and does not turn purple over time. The factory roof glass already reads nearly black, so the car keeps its original look from the driveway.

### Is anything removed from the car to film the rear glass?

No. The film is shaped on a separate surface, carried inside and applied to the rear glass with the trim and lighting left in place. No blade touches the glass and nothing is drilled or glued. The door windows are done the same way.

### Will ceramic film block the phone key or the cameras?

No. Ceramic film has no metal layer, so the phone key, card reader and the car's cellular and GPS signals pass through as before. The windshield camera window and the pillar cameras are left clear. Driver-assist features and the app connection are unchanged.
