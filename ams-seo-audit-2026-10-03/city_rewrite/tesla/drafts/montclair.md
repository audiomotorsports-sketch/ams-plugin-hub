### Can tint interfere with the ultrasonic or radar sensors?

No. Those sensors live in the bumpers and behind body panels, not in the glass, so window film never touches them. The only glass-mounted items are cameras, and those are left clear. Ceramic film also carries no metal, so nothing in the car's signal path is blocked.
