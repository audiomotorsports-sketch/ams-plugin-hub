## El Monte Tesla roof and tint questions

### How much cooler does a Tesla feel with film on the glass roof?

Noticeably, especially on the front seats where the sun lands. The roof is the largest piece of glass on the car and it faces up all day. Ceramic film rejects most of the infrared heat before it enters the cabin, so after a parking lot afternoon the headliner and seats are not radiating at you.

### Is an hour's drive from El Monte worth it just for roof film?

Most owners do the roof and the windows together, which makes the trip count. Done in one visit you get a one-piece rear window, legal fronts, dark rear and a cooler roof. Owners come from farther than El Monte for this, and the car is usually ready the same day.

### Can the roof film be seen from outside the car?

Not really. The factory roof glass is already dark, and the film goes on the inside, so from the street the car looks the same. From inside you notice the glare is softer and the heat is down. There is no change to the roof's exterior finish.

### Does the roof film go on without removing the headliner?

Yes. The film is applied to the inside surface of the roof glass with the car fully assembled. Nothing is unbolted, drilled or taken apart. The shaping is done on the outside of the glass with heat, then the sheet is moved inside and installed.

### What front shade is legal on a Tesla in California, and does it still help?

The front doors must pass 70% of visible light. Ceramic film at that shade looks light but rejects most of the infrared heat, so the fronts still get cooler. The rear doors, the back window and the roof have no shade limit, so those can go darker.
