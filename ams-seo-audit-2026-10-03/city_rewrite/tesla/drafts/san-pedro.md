### How much does ceramic tint actually help with Tesla cabin heat?

A lot, but be clear about what it covers. The glass roof itself is already tinted from the factory; the side and rear glass are not. Ceramic film on those panels rejects a large share of infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler and the air conditioning works less.

### Can you tint the one-piece rear glass on a Model Y without a seam?

Yes. The rear glass is tinted as one panel. The film is heat-shrunk to the curve on a glass board, then applied in a single piece with the edge set to the gasket. No seams, no overlapping strips, no bright line at the bottom.

### Will the tint cause any issue with my Tesla's phone key or cameras?

No. Ceramic film is non-metallic, so there is no signal interference with the key, cameras or antennas. Camera windows on the glass stay uncovered, and the rear-window electronics are protected before the film goes near them.
