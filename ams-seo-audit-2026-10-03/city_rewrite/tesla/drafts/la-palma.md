### What goes wrong when a Model 3 rear window is tinted badly?

Fingers at the edges, seams where strips meet, and haze where the film was not shrunk to the curve. All of it sits in your mirror. Avoiding that means shrinking one sheet on the outside of the glass until it fits exactly, which takes time and practice.

### Is La Palma close enough to wait at the shop for Model 3 tint?

### Will the film affect the Model 3 cameras, the phone key or Autopilot?

No. The ceramic film is non-metallic, so it does not block the phone key, Bluetooth or cellular data. The windshield camera housing stays uncovered and the pillar cameras are not on tinted glass. The car drives and connects the same after the install.

### What front shade is legal on a Model 3 in California?

The front doors have to pass 70% of visible light, so they stay light. Ceramic film at that shade still rejects most of the infrared heat, so the front seats get cooler. The rear doors and back glass can go as dark as you want.

### How long before the windows can come down after the tint?

A few days. The film is curing against the glass, so keep the windows up and the rear defroster off during that time. After that, use the car normally and clean the film with an ammonia-free cleaner and a soft cloth. It will not fade or bubble.
