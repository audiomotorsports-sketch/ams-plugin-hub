## Los Alamitos Model 3 questions

### Why is the Model 3 rear window such a test for a tint shop?

The curve. It bends front to back and side to side at the same time, and a flat sheet of film has to be shrunk with heat until it follows every bit of it. A shop that rushes will split it into strips. Done as one piece, it looks like factory glass; that takes practice.

### Should a Los Alamitos owner wait at the shop or drop the Model 3 off?

Either works. The trip is short enough that many owners wait, and a Model 3 is usually done the same day. If you are adding roof film or a dash camera, drop it off in the morning and pick it up later. We will tell you which makes more sense when you book.

### Does ceramic film affect the Model 3 cameras or the phone key?

No. The film is non-metallic, so signals for the phone key, Bluetooth and cellular pass through the glass. The windshield camera housing is left uncovered, and the pillar cameras are not on tinted glass. Autopilot sees what it always saw.

### What is the legal front window shade on a Model 3 in California?

The front doors must pass 70% of visible light, so they stay light. Ceramic film at that shade still cuts most of the infrared heat, so the front seats get cooler without a dark look. The rear doors and back glass have no limit and can go dark.

### How long before I can use the rear defroster and roll the windows down?

Give it a few days. The film cures against the glass during that time, so keep the windows up and the rear defroster off. After that, everything is normal. Clean the film with an ammonia-free cleaner and a soft cloth, and it will stay clear for years.
