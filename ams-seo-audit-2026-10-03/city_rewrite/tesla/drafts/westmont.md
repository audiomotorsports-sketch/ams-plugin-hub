### Why does a Model Y get so hot inside?

The panoramic roof and the large side and hatch glass let in sun from every direction. The roof is factory-treated; the doors and hatch are not. Ceramic film on those windows rejects infrared heat and brings the cabin temperature down noticeably.

### Is the Model Y hatch glass tinted as one piece?

Yes. The hatch window is heat-formed on a glass board so one sheet of film follows the full curve, then applied in a single pass. That avoids a seam across the rear camera view and keeps the defroster lines intact.

### How is a Model Y priced compared with a Model 3 or Model X?

### Will the Model Y cameras or phone key be affected?

No. The camera cluster at the top of the windshield and the fender repeaters are masked and the film stops before them. Ceramic film has no metal, so there is no interference with the phone key, GPS or cellular data.
