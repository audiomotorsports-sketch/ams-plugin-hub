## What Carson Teslas come in for

**Every Tesla model.** The Model 3 and Y with their frameless doors, the S with its long curved glass, the X with the tallest windows in the lineup and the Cybertruck with the flattest. Each gets film cut on a board to the actual glass.

**Ceramic film.** Chosen for heat rejection without darkness, and free of metal, so the phone key, cell signal and cameras are untouched.

**One-piece rear glass.** Shaped with heat on a board until it matches the curve, then laid as a single piece. No seam, no haze.

**Electronics prep.** Defroster lines and wiring near the rear window are shielded before any water goes on the glass.

### Can a Carson owner get same-day Tesla tint?

### What is the legal front window tint for a Tesla?

California requires 70% VLT on the front doors. Ceramic film at that shade still blocks a large share of infrared heat, so the cabin stays cooler without a dark look. Rear doors and the back glass can be darker.

### Does ceramic tint interfere with Tesla cameras or the key?

No. The camera zones on the glass are masked and left clear, and ceramic film has no metal layer, so nothing blocks the cameras, sensors or phone key. We check each camera view before the car leaves the bay.
