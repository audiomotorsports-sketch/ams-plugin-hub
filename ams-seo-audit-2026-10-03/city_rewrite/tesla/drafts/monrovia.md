## Questions Monrovia owners ask

### Why does the one-piece rear glass need a different technique on a Tesla?

A Model Y rear window is one large curved panel with defroster lines across it. The film has to be heat-shaped before application so it follows the curve exactly. Rushing that step leaves fingers or creases, which is why an experienced installer matters on this panel.

### Is a drive from Monrovia worth it just for Tesla window tint?

For many owners it is. The ride is under an hour and a half each way, and the result is a clean install done once. Customers regularly travel over 100 miles to this bay, so the Monrovia trip is a normal errand compared with that.

### Does ceramic Tesla window tint affect Autopilot cameras or Wi-Fi?

No. Ceramic film has no metal layer, so cell, GPS, Wi-Fi and phone key signals pass through normally. Camera lenses and sensor spots are masked off and left clear, so driver assistance features keep seeing the road the way Tesla designed them.

### Can a Monrovia driver wait at the shop during a Tesla tint job?

Yes. Book a slot, arrive at your time, and stay in the seating area with Wi-Fi and Netflix while the film goes on. Many people prefer to drop the car and return later, and either choice works as long as you schedule ahead.

### What does ceramic tint do for a Tesla compared with standard film?

Ceramic film rejects more heat than a plain dyed film at the same darkness and holds its color over time. Inside a Tesla's large glass cabin that means a noticeably cooler seat and steering wheel after the car has been parked in the sun.
