## Ladera Heights Model X questions

### How do the falcon-wing doors change the tint job on a Model X?

The rear glass on those doors is curved and hinged at the roof, so the film has to be shaped to the curve and cut so nothing catches when the door swings up and seals down. It takes longer than a normal rear door, and it is the main reason the Model X carries its own quote.

### Is Ladera Heights close enough to wait at the shop for a Model X tint?

Close enough, but a full Model X with the roof done can take most of a day, so many owners drop it off in the morning and come back later. If you prefer to wait, you are welcome to. Either way, book ahead so the bay is reserved for your car.

### Does ceramic tint affect the Model X cameras, the sensors or the phone key?

No. The film is non-metallic, so the phone key and the cellular and Bluetooth signals pass through. The camera areas on the glass are left uncovered and the film stops clear of the sensor housings. Autopilot sees exactly what it saw before the tint.

### Can the third-row windows on a Model X be tinted dark?

Yes. California only restricts the front door glass, which has to pass 70% of visible light. The rear doors, the quarter glass by the third row and the rear window can all go dark for privacy and heat. We match the shade across the rear so the car looks even.

### How soon can the falcon-wing doors be used after tint?

Immediately for opening and closing; the film is on the inside of the glass and does not touch the hinge or seal. Keep the windows up for a few days so the film cures, and avoid the rear defroster during that time. After that, normal use.
