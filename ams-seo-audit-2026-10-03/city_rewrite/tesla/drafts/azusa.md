### Will ceramic film block the cameras on a new Model Y?

No. Each camera zone on the glass is masked and left uncovered, and the ceramic film has no metal layer, so there is nothing to interfere with the cameras or the phone key. The car works the same as it did on delivery day.

### What is included in a Tesla tint quote?

The film, the hand-cut install on each piece of glass you choose, and the prep around the rear-window electronics. Teslas are quoted by model at the shop because glass area varies so much. Text the year and model and Nick will send the number.

### Does new Tesla glass need any special prep before tint?

It needs a thorough clean and a careful mask of the camera and sensor areas, but no stripping. The rear glass is shaped on a board rather than on the car, and the electronics near the back window are shielded before any water goes on.
