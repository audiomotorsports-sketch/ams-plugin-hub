### Is it worth driving from West Los Angeles to tint a Model Y?

### Why does a Model Y need tint more than most cars?

The panoramic roof and the large side and hatch glass let in heat from every direction. Ceramic film on the doors and hatch rejects infrared heat rather than absorbing it, which lowers the cabin temperature and the load on the climate system.

### Is the Model Y rear hatch tinted in one piece?

Yes. The hatch glass is tall and curved, so the film is heat-formed on a glass board to match before it is applied in one sheet. That avoids a seam in your rear camera view and keeps the defroster lines intact.

### How do you quote a Model Y compared with a Model X?

By model, at the shop. The Model X has far more glass and tall doors that take longer to shape, so it is a different price from a Model Y. Text (213) 429-1092 with the year and model and you will have a figure before you leave West Los Angeles.

### Will the tint affect the Model Y's cameras or phone key?

No. The camera zones on the windshield and fenders are masked and kept clear, and ceramic film contains no metal, so there is no signal interference with the phone key, cellular data or GPS. Everything works the way it did before the tint.
