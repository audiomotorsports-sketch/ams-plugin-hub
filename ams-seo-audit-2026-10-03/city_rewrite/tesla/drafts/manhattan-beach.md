### Can the huge Model X windshield be tinted?

### How do you handle the falcon-wing door windows?

Each piece is patterned and shrunk away from the car, then laid in with the door open and supported. The glass is cleaned to the gasket line so the edge sits tight and nothing lifts later. It is slow work, which is why Model X is quoted separately from a Model 3 or Model Y.

### Will the tint affect my Tesla's cameras, Autopilot or phone key?

No. The forward cameras sit behind a window in the windshield that is left clear, the side repeaters and pillar cameras are never covered, and ceramic film has no metal to interfere with the phone key or the car's own radios. Everything that worked before the tint works after it.

Yes, and it is the common setup. The front doors go at the California legal level, the rear doors and rear glass can go darker for privacy, and the factory roof glass is already dark. Nick matches the shades so the car reads as one piece from outside.
