### What makes the Model X a different tint job from the sedans?

The falcon-wing doors. Their glass is curved, hinged at the roof and sealed differently from a normal door, so the film is shaped to the curve and cut so the door opens and closes without catching an edge. The front doors are taller than a Model S too. It is more labor, which is why it is quoted by model.

### Is a 10-mile drive from Florence-Firestone worth it for Tesla tint?

Tesla owners drive here from much farther than that, some from well over 100 miles. Ten miles is a short run for film that is cut to the glass, shrunk flat, and kept clear of the cameras. A redo elsewhere costs more than the extra miles would.

### Does ceramic window tint change how the Model X cameras or sensors work?

No. The film is non-metallic, so it does not interfere with the cameras, the phone key or the cellular signal. We leave the camera zones on the glass uncovered and keep the film away from the sensor housings, so Autopilot features see exactly what they saw before.

### Can I bring a Tesla and a second car from Florence-Firestone on the same day?

You can, but book them as two appointments so each gets its own time in the bay. A Tesla with the roof and all windows done can fill most of a day. Call and we will line both cars up on a schedule that works, and you can drop them off together.

### How long will the Model X tint last in the heat around here?

Ceramic film is made for exactly this kind of sun. It does not turn purple or bubble the way cheap dyed film does, and the heat rejection stays steady over the years. Keep the windows up for a few days after the install so the film bonds fully.
