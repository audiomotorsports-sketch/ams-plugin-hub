### Will ceramic film affect the Model Y cameras or the phone key?

No. The film has no metallic layer, so the phone key, the app and cellular data work as before. The camera housing at the top of the windshield stays uncovered, and the pillar and fender cameras do not sit behind film. Autopilot is unaffected after the install.

### How dark can a La Mirada owner go on a Model Y?

California limits the front door glass to a shade that passes 70% of visible light; the rear doors and hatch glass have no limit. Ceramic film at the legal front shade still cuts most of the heat, so light fronts with a dark rear is the usual choice.

### Can I add a dash camera during the Model Y tint appointment?

Yes. The dash camera is wired to a fused source with the cable hidden behind the headliner and pillar trim, so nothing hangs from the mirror. It adds time to the visit, so tell us when you book and plan to drop the car off rather than wait.
