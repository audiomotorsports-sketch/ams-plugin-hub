### Why do Model Y owners tint the rear half of the car first?

The back windows and tailgate glass are the largest area where passengers sit in direct sun. Film there lowers cabin heat and adds privacy, and you can always add the front side windows at the legal shade later.

### Does tint on a Model Y affect the rear camera or parking sensors?

No. The rear camera and sensor spots are masked and left clear of film, so backing up looks exactly as it did. The ceramic film itself carries no metal, so it does not touch any radio signal.

### How do you handle the Model Y rear glass in one piece?

The installer shrinks the film to the curve with heat before application, then squeegees it into place around the defroster lines. That prep is what keeps the large rear window smooth, with no fingers or lifted edges.
