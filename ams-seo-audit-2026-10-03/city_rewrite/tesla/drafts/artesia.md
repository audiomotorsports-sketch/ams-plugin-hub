**Camera zones masked.** Tesla mounts cameras behind the glass, and the car relies on them. We mask every camera window before film goes on, leave it bare, and look through each one before the car leaves.

**Metal-free ceramic film.** No metallic layer means nothing blocks the phone key, cell signal or sensors, and ceramic film blocks more infrared heat per shade than dyed film.

**Model 3, Model Y, Model S, Model X.** Frameless doors cut to the gasket, one-piece rear glass shaped with heat, and the S and X given extra board time for their longer and taller glass.

## Artesia Tesla tint questions

### How do you make sure the Tesla cameras are not covered?

Every camera window on the glass is masked before the film is laid, so the film never crosses the lens area. When the install is finished we check the view through each camera zone. Because the ceramic film has no metal, the surrounding film causes no interference either.

### How far is Artesia from the Tesla tint shop?

### Does tint affect the Tesla phone key or Bluetooth?

No. Ceramic film has no metallic layer, so Bluetooth, cell and key signals pass straight through it. The only thing that changes is the amount of heat and glare coming through the glass.

### Will the sensors behind the glass still work after tint?

Yes. The sensor and camera areas are masked and left uncovered, and the film around them is non-metallic. Autopilot, parking cameras and the rest see exactly what they saw before the car came in.
