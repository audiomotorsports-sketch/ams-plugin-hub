## Rolling Hills Tesla tint questions

### What goes wrong when a Tesla rear window is tinted in strips?

You get a horizontal line across the glass that is visible in the mirror and from outside, and a seam that can collect dust or lift at the edge. The strips also age at slightly different rates. A single shaped sheet avoids all of that, which is why it is the only method used at Tesla Tint LA.

### How do you keep the defroster lines safe during a rear glass tint?

The defroster is printed on the inside of the glass, which is the surface the film bonds to. The film is pressed down with soft tools in long strokes and never scraped, so the lines stay intact. Ceramic film contains no metal, so the defroster and antenna work exactly as before.

### Will tint on a Tesla cause any trouble with the cameras?

No. The forward cameras sit behind a clear window in the windshield that film never covers, and the pillar cameras and fender repeaters are not in the glass at all. The rear camera is above the license plate. Ceramic film has no metal, so the phone key and radios are unaffected too.

### Can I bring a Model X or Cybertruck for the same treatment?

Yes. The X hatch and the Cybertruck's tall rear window are also done as single sheets, each from its own pattern. The X's falcon-door glass and the truck's flat panels need different handling than a Model 3, which is why Nick quotes by model at the shop after seeing the car.
