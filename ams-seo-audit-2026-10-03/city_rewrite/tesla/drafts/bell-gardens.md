### How is the curved rear window of a Tesla filmed without a seam?

The sheet is cut oversize, laid on the outside of the glass and shrunk with heat until it follows the curve, then moved inside and set. The result is one clean piece edge to edge. Nick explains the process when you book, and the work is done the same day in most cases.

### Is ceramic film worth it over dyed film on a Tesla?

For heat, yes. Dyed film mostly darkens the glass, while ceramic rejects infrared without needing to be dark, which is what makes a legal 70% front window feel cooler. On a car that is already mostly glass, that is the difference you notice.

### Will the film affect the Tesla cameras or the phone key?

No. The camera zones on the glass are masked and left clear, and the ceramic film contains no metal, so nothing interferes with the cameras, sensors or key. The car works the same as it did when you drove in, only cooler.
