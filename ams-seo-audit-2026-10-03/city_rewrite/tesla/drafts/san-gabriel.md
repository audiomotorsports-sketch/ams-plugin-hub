## Questions San Gabriel owners ask

### Why does the Tesla rear window need special handling?

Because it is one big, deeply curved piece of glass. Film that is not shrunk enough will show fingers along the edges; film that is overworked will distort. The trick is shaping it off the car until it lies flat on the curve, then installing it as one sheet with the defroster lines protected.

### Is it realistic to drive from San Gabriel for Tesla window tint?

Yes. It is about 45 minutes each way, and the result is a rear window with no seams and side glass that fits to the edge. Audio MotorSports regularly sees customers from more than 100 miles out, so a San Gabriel trip is well within what people do for a tint they will not redo.

### Do you use a single sheet or two strips on the rear glass?

A single sheet, always. Splitting the rear window into strips is faster but leaves a visible line through the glass and a seam that can lift. Tesla Tint LA shapes one continuous piece of ceramic film for the whole pane so the view through the mirror is uninterrupted.

### Will the film harm the rear defroster or the antenna?

No. The defroster grid is on the inside surface of the glass, and the film is squeegeed over it with soft tools so the lines are never scraped. Ceramic film has no metal content, so the antenna elements and the car's radios keep working exactly as before.
