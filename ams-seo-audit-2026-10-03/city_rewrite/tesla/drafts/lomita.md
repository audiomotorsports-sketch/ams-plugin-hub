## Questions Lomita owners ask

### Does tinting the glass roof on a Tesla actually make the cabin cooler?

Yes. The roof is the biggest single pane of glass on a Model 3 or Model Y, and a ceramic film cut for it rejects a large share of the heat that comes straight down at midday. The look from outside barely changes, and the cabin stops feeling like a greenhouse while parked.

### Why is Tesla window tint quoted by model instead of one flat price?

Every Tesla has a different glass shape. The Model X has those big curved front panes, the Model Y has a one-piece rear glass, and the Cybertruck is its own pattern. Nick looks at the actual car and quotes by model at the shop so the number matches the glass in front of him.

### Is it worth the short drive from Lomita for Tesla tint?

It is a 7 to 9 minute hop, and the work is done by a shop that tints Teslas every week. People drive from well over 100 miles away for the same job, so a Lomita owner gets the same careful install with almost no time spent on the road.

### Will ceramic tint interfere with the cameras or the key card reader?

No. Ceramic film is not metallic, so it does not block radio signals, the phone key, or the key card reader on the B-pillar. Camera windows and sensor areas are left clear, and the electronics around the rear glass get prep before any film goes down on that panel.
