## Hacienda Heights Model X questions

### What is different about tinting falcon-wing door glass?

The glass is curved and the door swings up from the roof, so the film has to follow the curve and stop exactly where the seal meets the body, or an edge will catch every time the door closes. It is slower than a normal rear door, which is why the Model X has its own quote.

### Should I plan a full day in Carson for a Model X tint from Hacienda Heights?

Plan on most of a day if you are doing all the glass. Most Hacienda Heights owners drop the car off in the morning and pick it up later rather than wait. If you only want the sides and rear, it can be quicker; we will tell you when you book.

### Do the Model X cameras and sensors still work after ceramic tint?

Yes. The film is non-metallic, so the phone key, Bluetooth and cellular signals pass through, and the camera areas on the glass are left uncovered. The film is trimmed clear of every sensor housing. Autopilot sees the same view it did before.

### How dark can the rear of a Model X go in California?

As dark as you want. The law limits only the front door glass, which must pass 70% of visible light. Rear doors, the third-row quarter glass and the rear window have no limit, and most owners go dark back there.

### Can I use the falcon-wing doors normally right after the tint?

Yes, immediately. The film sits on the inside of the glass and does not touch the hinge or the seal. Just keep the windows up for a few days while the film cures, and leave the rear defroster off during that period. After that, use everything as normal.
