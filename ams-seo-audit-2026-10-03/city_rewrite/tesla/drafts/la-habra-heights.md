## Questions from La Habra Heights Tesla owners

### How do you keep the tint from covering the Tesla cameras?

Each camera zone on the glass is marked before cutting. The windshield housing stays clear, the pillar cameras are outside the door glass entirely, and the film is trimmed to leave them open. We do not cover a camera and cut it out later, because that leaves a ragged edge and a scratch risk.

### Will window film interfere with the phone key, Bluetooth or the cell signal in my Tesla?

Not with the film we use. Metallic films can weaken signals; ceramic film has no metal in it, so the phone key, the app connection and the cellular data pass through the glass the same as before. That is the main reason we install ceramic on Teslas.
