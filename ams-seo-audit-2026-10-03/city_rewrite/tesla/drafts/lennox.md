## Lennox Model 3 tint questions

### What is the hardest part of tinting a Model 3?

The rear window. It curves in two directions, and a single sheet of film has to be heat-shrunk until it lies flat against every inch of it before it is installed. Get that right and the back glass looks like factory dark glass. Get it wrong and you see fingers and seams.

### Does a short drive from Lennox get me the same work as the long-distance customers?

The exact same work. Every Tesla in this bay gets ceramic film cut on the glass, a one-piece rear window and camera zones left clear, whether the owner drove nine miles from Lennox or well over a hundred. The difference is you can wait at the shop and take it home that afternoon.

### Will the tint hurt the Model 3 cameras, the phone key or Autopilot?

No. Ceramic film is non-metallic and does not block signals, and the camera areas on the glass are kept uncovered. The pillar cameras sit outside the door glass altogether. After the install the car connects, unlocks and drives the way it did before.

### Can I stay legal on the front doors and still cut the heat in a Model 3?

Yes. California requires the front door glass to pass 70% of visible light. Ceramic film at that shade looks light but still rejects most of the infrared heat, so the cabin is cooler. The rear doors and rear glass can be as dark as you like.

### How long is a Model 3 tint appointment and can I drive it home right after?

Most Model 3 jobs are done the same day, and you can drive it home as soon as it is finished. Keep the windows up for a few days afterward while the film cures, and hold off on the rear defroster during that time. We explain the care at pickup.
