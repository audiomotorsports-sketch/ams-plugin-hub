## What Buena Park Teslas come in for

**Model S ceramic tint.** Long doors and compound-curved glass. The film is heat-formed on a board until it lies flat, then moved to the car and set to the gasket. Rushing this step is what leaves fingers and bubbles on an S.

**Model 3 and Model Y.** The frameless-door Teslas. Each piece is cut to the glass, not trimmed short from a pattern, so there is no light line at the top edge.

**Model X.** Tall glass and a windshield that runs into the roof. It uses the most film of any Tesla and is quoted by model.

**Non-metallic film.** Ceramic film has no metal layer, so the phone key, cell signal and cameras are unaffected, and it blocks more heat per shade than dyed film.

## Buena Park Tesla tint questions

### What is different about tinting a Model S door?

The glass is long and curves two ways, so the film will not lie flat until it has been shaped with heat on a board. Skip that and the edge lifts or the film shows fingers. We shape every S piece before it goes near the car.

### Can I go darker on the rear of a Tesla than on the front?

Yes. California holds the front doors at 70% VLT, but the rear doors and back glass have no limit. Ceramic film at the legal front shade still blocks a large share of infrared heat, so the whole cabin stays cooler.

### Does a Buena Park Model S get the same quote as a Model 3?

No. The S uses more film and more shaping time, so it is priced on its own. Every Tesla is quoted by model at the shop; text the year and model and Nick will give you the figure for your car.

### Are the Tesla cameras protected during the tint?

Always. Camera zones on the glass are masked and left clear, and the ceramic film has no metal, so nothing interferes with what the car sees. We look through each camera area before the car leaves the bay.
