## Tesla tint Watts drivers come in for

**Cybertruck window tint.** Flat glass sounds easy, but hard corners and straight edges show every fraction of a millimeter. Each Cybertruck panel is cut on a glass board to follow the trim lines exactly, and the large rear glass is covered in one piece.

**Model 3 and Model Y ceramic tint.** Curved door glass and a tall one-piece rear window. The film is heat-shaped on the board, then applied so the edge runs to the glass line and the defroster lines stay intact.

**Model S and Model X.** The S has the longest doors and the X the tallest glass; both need more shaping time and are priced per model.

**Sensor and camera clearance.** The windshield camera cluster and the side cameras are masked before prep, and the film has no metal in it, so nothing about Autopilot, the phone key or the cellular signal changes.

Dash camera installation and GPS tracker installation are available on the same visit, and Audio MotorSports also does car stereo, CarPlay and Android Auto, car alarm, remote start and Harley bagger audio installs.

## Watts Tesla and Cybertruck tint questions

### Can a Watts Cybertruck owner get the flat panels tinted without visible edges?

Yes. The Cybertruck's flat glass is patterned on a glass board so the film edge tracks the trim line on every side, and the rear glass goes on as a single piece. Straight edges show mistakes more than curves do, which is why the panels are cut away from the truck.

### Is Tesla tint from Watts a same-day trip?

Normally, yes. At 7.6 miles you can book a slot, drive down, and either wait at the shop or leave the car and collect it later the same day. Doors and rear glass on a Model 3 or Model Y are usually complete within the afternoon.

### What film goes on a Cybertruck versus a Model 3?

The same ceramic film; only the pattern and the shaping differ. The Cybertruck panels are flat and are cut to straight trim lines, while the Model 3 glass is curved and heat-formed. Both are priced by model at the shop.

### Does tint block the Cybertruck's cameras or the phone key?

No. The camera zones are masked and the film stops short of them, and ceramic film has no metal, so the phone key, GPS and cellular data all pass through as before. Sentry Mode and the backup camera see the same picture.

### How does the shop quote a Tesla from Watts?

By model. A Cybertruck, a Model Y and a Model X do not have the same glass or the same labor, so each is quoted by model at the shop. Text (213) 429-1092 with the year and model and a figure comes back before you drive over.
