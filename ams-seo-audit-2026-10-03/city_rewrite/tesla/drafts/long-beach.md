## Long Beach Tesla tint questions

### How do you tint the one-piece rear glass on a Model Y without seams?

The film is heat-shrunk to the curve of the glass on the outside first, then moved inside and laid as a single sheet. That is slower than splitting it into pieces, but it leaves no visible seam and nothing for the sun to peel back in a year.

### Will window tint affect my Tesla rear camera or the glass antenna?

Ceramic film contains no metal, so the antenna lines in the glass keep working and the phone key is unaffected. The rear camera sits outside the glass, and the small windows the cameras look through are left clear during the install.
