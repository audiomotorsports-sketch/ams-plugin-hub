### Is the Model S harder to tint than a Model 3?

It takes more time, mostly because of the window length and the hatch glass. The film for the long side windows has to be heat-shaped so it hugs the curve edge to edge, and the rear piece is large. Done patiently it looks factory; rushed, it shows. That is why Model S work is quoted on its own.

### Will ceramic tint get in the way of my Tesla's phone key or cameras?

No. Ceramic film has no metal in it, so Bluetooth from your phone key, the card reader on the pillar and the cellular antenna all keep working. The camera lenses in the windshield and the side repeaters are never covered; the film stops short of every sensor window.
