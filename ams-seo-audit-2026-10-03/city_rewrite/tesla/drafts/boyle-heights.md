## Boyle Heights Tesla tint questions

### Why is the Tesla rear window tinted as one piece?

Because a seam would show, and because a single piece shaped to the curve looks like factory glass. The film is heat-formed on a board to match the compound curve, then transferred and squeegeed down. It takes longer than a flat window and it is worth it.

### Can the defroster lines be damaged during tinting?

Not when the area is prepped properly. We shield the lines and the electronics near the rear glass before any water touches the window, and the film is applied over the lines without scraping. The defroster works the same after the tint as before.

### What is the legal front tint for a Tesla in California?

Front door glass must remain at 70% VLT. Ceramic film at that shade still blocks a large share of infrared heat, so the front seats are comfortable. Rear doors and the one-piece back glass can go darker if you want more privacy.

### Do you cut the film on my Tesla or off the car?

Off the car, on a glass board. Cutting on the vehicle risks the glass and the trim, and a plotter pattern trimmed short leaves a visible light gap. Hand-cutting to the actual glass means the edge sits at the gasket where it belongs.
