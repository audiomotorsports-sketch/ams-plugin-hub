### Does a brand-new Tesla need any prep before tint?

Only a careful cleaning, which is part of the job. New glass has no adhesive from a previous film and no scratches, so the ceramic bonds evenly. The sooner it goes on, the sooner the seats and dash are protected from direct sun. Many Pico Rivera owners book within the first week of delivery.

### How soon can I roll the windows down after the tint?

Wait a few days. The film needs time to cure to the glass, and moving a window too early can catch the edge. Nick gives you the exact timing at pickup. A light haze or a few small water pockets in the first days are normal and clear on their own as the moisture evaporates.

### Is the glass roof on a new Model 3 or Model Y worth filming?

Yes. It looks dark from outside but it passes a lot of heat onto the front seats. A ceramic film applied from inside, with the headliner left in place, cuts that heat noticeably. It is one of the most requested parts of a new-delivery tint because the difference is immediate on a hot day.

### Can I add a dash camera while the car is in for tint?

Yes. Dash camera installation fits easily into the same appointment, with the power lead routed along the headliner and down the pillar so nothing hangs from the mirror. Audio MotorSports does it in the same bay. Mention it when you book and the time will be reserved.
