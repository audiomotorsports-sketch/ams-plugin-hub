## Inglewood Model Y questions

### How is the Model Y hatch window tinted without a seam?

One sheet of ceramic film is heat-shrunk on the outside of the hatch glass until it matches the curve, then moved inside and installed. It takes longer than laying strips, but the result is a rear window that looks like factory dark glass instead of film with lines across it.

### Is it worth coming from Inglewood for Model Y tint instead of the nearest shop?

The Model Y has frameless doors, a curved hatch window and cameras in the glass. A shop that handles those every day gets them right the first time. Inglewood is about twenty minutes away, which is far less than many of our Tesla customers drive.

### Will ceramic tint affect the cameras or the phone key on my Model Y?

No. The film has no metallic layer, so it does not block the phone key, the cellular signal or Bluetooth. Camera zones on the glass are left uncovered, and the pillar and fender cameras are not on tinted glass at all. Autopilot works the same after the install.

### How dark can the Model Y go and still be legal in California?

Front door glass must pass 70% of visible light, so the fronts stay light. The rear doors and hatch glass have no limit. Ceramic film at the legal front shade still rejects most of the heat, so a light front with a dark rear is the usual choice.

### Can an Inglewood owner add a dash camera on the same visit?

Yes. A dash camera on a Model Y is wired to a fused source with the cable hidden behind the headliner and trim, so nothing dangles. Mention it when you book so the full day is set aside, and plan to drop the car off rather than wait.
