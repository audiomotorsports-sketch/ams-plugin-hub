## Hawaiian Gardens new-Tesla questions

### Is there any reason to wait before tinting a new Tesla?

No. The glass is ready the day you take delivery, and the sooner the film is on, the sooner the cabin stops heating up and the interior is protected from UV. Most Hawaiian Gardens owners book within the first week or two of picking the car up.

### How do you protect a brand-new Tesla interior during the install?

Covers go over the seats and door panels before any water is used, and the film is cut and shaped on the outside of the glass, so the blade work happens away from the interior. Nothing is drilled, no trim is cut and nothing is unplugged to install tint on a Tesla.

### Does tint on a new Tesla create any problem with the factory warranty?

Window film is applied to the inside of the glass and can be removed without harm, so it does not alter the car. For a specific warranty question, ask Tesla directly. We can describe the install precisely: film on the glass, nothing else touched.

### Which shades do new Tesla owners from Hawaiian Gardens usually choose?

A legal shade on the front doors, which in California means glass that passes 70% of visible light, and a darker shade on the rear doors and back glass. We put the samples on your glass outside so you can judge the look in real light.

### What do I need to do after I drive the new car home?

Keep the windows up for a few days while the film cures, and leave the rear defroster off during that time. After that the car is back to normal, and the film is cleaned with an ammonia-free cleaner and a soft cloth. Ceramic film does not fade or bubble.
