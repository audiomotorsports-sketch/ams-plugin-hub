## Tesla work Lawndale drivers book with us

**Model Y window tint.** The Y has a tall rear hatch window and frameless door glass that drops when the door opens, so the film edge has to be cut to sit just below the seal line. **Ceramic window tint for the Model 3, S and X.** Same ceramic film, different glass shapes. The Model 3 rear window is a one-piece job, the Model S side glass is long and curved, and the Model X has tall doors, so each model gets its own quote at the shop.

### Does ceramic film on a Model Y block the phone key or the cameras?

No. The ceramic film we install has no metal layer, so the phone key, the cellular connection and the glass-mounted cameras all behave exactly as they did before. The camera windows on the glass are left clear on purpose, not covered and trimmed afterward.
