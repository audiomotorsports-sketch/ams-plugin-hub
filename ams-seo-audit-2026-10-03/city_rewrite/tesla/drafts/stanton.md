## Tint for a Tesla new to Stanton

**First-week ceramic tint.** Factory glass with no old film is the simplest surface to tint. Ceramic film on the doors and rear glass goes on clean and starts rejecting infrared heat immediately, which matters most on a glass-roofed Model 3 or Model Y.

**Film cut away from the car.** Every panel is patterned and heat-shaped on a glass board before it goes to the window. A new car's paint, trim and headliner never meet a blade.

**One-piece rear glass and clean door edges.** The rear window gets a single formed sheet; the frameless door glass gets an edge cut to the glass line so nothing shows when the door swings open.

**Model S, Model X and Cybertruck.** Larger glass on the S and X and flat panels on the Cybertruck. Each model has its own pattern and quote.

**Cameras and connectivity preserved.** Camera zones are masked and the film is non-metallic, so a new Tesla keeps its full Autopilot and phone-key behavior.

### How soon after delivery should a Stanton owner tint a Tesla?

As soon as you can book it. There is no waiting period for new factory glass, and the earlier the film goes on, the sooner the cabin stops baking through the doors and back glass. Call (310) 513-8800 with the delivery date and a slot can be set for the week the car arrives.

### Is a new Tesla from Stanton safe to tint without harming the paint?

Yes. All cutting and heat-shaping happen on a glass board away from the car, so the paint, the door trim and the headliner are never touched by a blade. The electronics behind the rear window are covered before any water is applied.

### Does tint affect the cameras on a brand-new Tesla?

No. The camera cluster and the side repeaters are masked and the film stops short of them, and ceramic film has no metal, so there is no interference with the phone key, GPS or cellular data. The car keeps every feature it was delivered with.
