## Fullerton Tesla tint questions

### Why does one-piece rear glass matter on a Model 3 or Model Y?

Because the rear window is the glass you look through in the mirror all day. Film laid in strips leaves lines that catch the light. A single sheet, heat-shrunk to the curve before it is installed, looks like dark factory glass with nothing to see from inside or out.

### Is Fullerton too far to drive for Tesla window tint?

Not at all. Owners come to this shop from more than 100 miles away for Tesla work, and Fullerton is under an hour outside rush hour. Book a morning slot, drop the car off, and in most cases it is ready the same day.

### Does a one-piece rear window install affect the defroster lines?

No. The film goes on over the defroster lines after the glass is cleaned carefully, and the lines keep working. We give the film a few days to cure before you run the defroster, then it is normal use from there. Nothing is scraped or scored on the glass.

### Can ceramic film block the Tesla rear camera or the antenna in the rear glass?

It cannot. The rear camera sits in the trunk lid, not on the glass, and the ceramic film has no metal to disturb antennas. The side glass camera areas on the pillars are left clear, so every camera and the phone key work exactly as before.

### How dark can a Fullerton Tesla owner go in the back?

As dark as you like. California limits only the front doors, which must pass 70% of visible light. Rear doors, the rear glass and the roof have no restriction. Most owners pick a dark rear with a legal front, and we show samples on your glass before cutting.
