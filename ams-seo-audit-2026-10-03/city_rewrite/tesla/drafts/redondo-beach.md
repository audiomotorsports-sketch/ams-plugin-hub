## Redondo Beach Cybertruck and Tesla questions

### Is Cybertruck glass easier or harder to tint than a Model 3?

Different rather than easier. There is almost no curve to shrink, but the corners are sharp and the panes are large, so the pattern has to be exact and the edges clean. The rear window in particular is tall and takes a big single sheet. It is quoted by model at the shop like every Tesla.

### How long is the drive from Redondo Beach for Tesla tint?

Around a quarter of an hour. Redondo Beach is one of the closer cities the shop serves, and Tesla owners drive in from more than 100 miles away for the same ceramic install. Book a slot, drive over, and the truck is done in a few hours.

### Will tint on a Cybertruck interfere with its cameras?

No. The forward cameras look through a window in the windshield that stays clear, the pillar and fender cameras are not covered, and the rear camera is in the tailgate area rather than the glass. Ceramic film has no metal, so none of the truck's radios or the phone key are affected.

### What does ceramic film do that a cheaper dyed film does not?

Ceramic rejects heat, not just light. A dyed film darkens the glass but lets most of the heat through and tends to fade or turn purple over time. Ceramic film holds its color, keeps the cabin cooler, and has no metal layer to interfere with electronics. It is the only film Tesla Tint LA puts on a Tesla.

### Can I get a dash camera installed on the Cybertruck during the tint?

Yes. Dash camera installation is a frequent add-on, with the wiring hidden along the headliner and pillar so nothing hangs loose. Audio MotorSports does it in the same bay as the tint. Mention it when you book so enough time is set aside for both.
