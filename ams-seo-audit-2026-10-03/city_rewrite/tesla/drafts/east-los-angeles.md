## What East Los Angeles Tesla owners want

**Model 3 window tint.** The 3 is the Tesla we tint most. Its frameless door glass needs an edge that sits at the gasket, and its rear window is one curved piece that gets shaped with heat on a board before it meets the car.

**Model Y.** More rear glass and a bigger roof than the 3, same ceramic film and the same careful edge. The roof panel is quoted on its own if you want it.

**Model S and Model X.** The S has the longest door glass, the X the tallest. Both take more shaping and more film, and the quote reflects that.

**Cameras and the phone key.** Camera windows are masked and left bare, and the ceramic film has no metal in it, so signals pass through unchanged.

Usually an edge cut short from a plotter pattern, which shows as a line of light at the top of the frameless glass, or a rear window that was stretched instead of heat-shaped. We cut on a board to the actual glass and form the rear piece before it goes on.

### Can I tint the front doors of a Model 3 in California?

Yes, at 70% VLT. Ceramic film at that shade looks close to clear and still blocks a large share of the infrared heat, so the front seats stay comfortable. Rear doors and the back glass can be as dark as you like.
