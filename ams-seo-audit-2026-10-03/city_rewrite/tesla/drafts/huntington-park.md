## Huntington Park Model S questions

### Why is Model S door glass treated differently from a Model 3?

Length and curve. A Model S front door pane is long and bends in two directions, so a flat sheet of film must be shaped with heat until it lies on the glass without a wrinkle. A Model 3 door is shorter and nearly flat by comparison. More shaping means more labor, which is why the quote is by model.

### Can a Huntington Park owner get the Model S done and drive it home the same day?

In most cases, yes. Book a morning appointment, drop the car off, and it is usually ready by late afternoon. If you would rather wait at the shop, the trip from Huntington Park is short enough to make that easy. We confirm timing when you book.

### Does ceramic film interfere with the Model S windshield sensors?

No. The windshield camera and rain sensor housing is left uncovered, and the film we use has no metal in it, so nothing is blocked. Cellular, Bluetooth, the phone key and the cameras all behave the same after the tint as before it.

### What is the legal shade for the front doors on a Model S in California?

The front doors must let 70% of visible light through, so they stay light. Ceramic film at that shade still rejects most infrared heat, which is what you feel. The rear doors and the back glass can go darker, and the factory roof glass is already dark.

### Does the rear window defroster still work after one-piece film?

Yes. The film lies over the defroster lines without cutting or scoring them. Let the film cure for a few days with the windows up before using the defroster, and after that it works normally. The lines are visible through the film, as they are through factory glass.
