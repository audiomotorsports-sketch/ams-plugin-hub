## La Puente Tesla tint questions

### What does a rear window tinted in strips look like compared to one piece?

Strips leave horizontal lines across the glass that catch the sun and show in the mirror. A single sheet, shrunk to the curve before it goes on, looks like dark factory glass. On a Tesla you look through that rear window constantly, so the difference is there every drive.

### Is it worth the drive from La Puente for a one-piece rear window?

If you plan to keep the car, yes. The rear glass is the hardest piece on the car and the one that shows poor work most. About an hour each way gets you a shop that tints Teslas every day. Drop the car off in the morning and it is usually ready the same day.

### Does one-piece film cover the Tesla rear camera?

No. The rear camera on a Tesla sits in the trunk or hatch lid, not on the glass, so the rear window film never touches it. The pillar cameras are outside the door glass. The windshield camera zone is left uncovered. Nothing about the cameras changes.

### How dark can the back glass go on a Tesla in California?

As dark as you want. California restricts only the front door glass, which must pass 70% of visible light. The rear doors and rear window have no limit, and most owners go dark in the back for privacy and heat with a legal shade in front.

### How long does the back window need to cure after the install?

A few days with the windows up and the rear defroster off. The film is bonding to the glass during that time. After that, use the defroster normally and clean the film with an ammonia-free cleaner. Ceramic film does not fade or bubble, so it is low maintenance from then on.
