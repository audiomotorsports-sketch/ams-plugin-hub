### Does the Model S need a different film than a Model 3?

Same ceramic film, different cut. The Model S has longer, more curved door glass, so the panels are shaped with heat on a glass board before installation. A Model 3 piece is shorter and flatter. The quote is by model because the glass area and the labor are not the same.

### Can I keep my Tesla fronts legal and still cut the heat?

Yes. Front door glass stays at California's 70% VLT, and ceramic film at that shade still rejects a large share of infrared heat. The rear doors and back glass can go darker. You end up with a cooler cabin and no trouble at an inspection.

### What happens with the cameras and the rear-window electronics?

They are protected first and left uncovered. The camera zones on the glass stay clear, the electronics near the rear window are shielded during prep, and the ceramic film carries no metal, so your phone key and cameras work exactly as before.
