## Lynwood Tesla tint questions

### Why not just tint the Tesla rear window in two or three strips?

Because every strip leaves a line across the glass that shows in daylight and in the mirror. Shrinking a single sheet to that curve takes longer and more skill, but the back window ends up looking like factory dark glass. On a car you look through all day, that is the difference.

### Can a Lynwood owner get the Tesla tinted while they wait?

Yes. The trip is short, and a Model 3 or Model Y with sides and rear done is usually finished the same day. If you are adding a dash camera, it may make more sense to drop the car off in the morning. We will tell you when you book.

### Does the one-piece rear film affect the backup camera or the defroster?

Neither. The backup camera is in the trunk or hatch lid, not on the glass, so the film never touches it. The defroster lines are under the film and keep working; just give the film a few days to cure with the defroster off before using it.

### How dark can the back glass be on a Tesla in California?

There is no limit on the rear doors or rear window; the only California rule is for the front door glass, which has to pass 70% of visible light. Most Lynwood owners go dark in the back and keep a legal shade in front; ceramic film at that front shade still cuts most heat.

### Will ceramic film block the phone key or the Tesla cameras?

No. The film contains no metal, so the phone key, Bluetooth and cellular signals pass through the glass. The windshield camera housing is left uncovered and the pillar cameras are outside the door glass. Autopilot and the app work the same after the tint.
