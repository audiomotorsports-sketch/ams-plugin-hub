## Questions Monterey Park owners ask

### How do you keep Tesla cameras clear when tinting windows?

Before any film goes on, the installer marks the camera, sensor and radar areas around the glass and trims the film away from them. Those spots stay open, so Autopilot, Sentry and the cabin camera see through bare glass, never through film.

### Should a Monterey Park owner choose ceramic or regular film for a Tesla?

Ceramic is the better fit. It stops more heat at the same shade and has no metal, so the phone key and cell signal behave normally. Regular dyed film costs less but fades sooner and does less against sun on a glass-heavy car.
