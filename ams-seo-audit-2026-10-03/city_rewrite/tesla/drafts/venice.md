## Venice Model S tint questions

### Is it worth bringing a Model S from Venice to Carson for tint?

Yes. The Model S has the most curved door glass in the Tesla range, and film that is not properly heat-formed to it will show fingers and lift at the edges. At 15.9 miles the drive is about half an hour outside rush hour, and owners come from far beyond Venice for this work.

### Why does Model S tint take longer than a Model 3?

The panels are longer and the curve is deeper, so each piece needs more time on the glass board to shrink to shape before it is applied. That extra labor is why the Model S is quoted separately; text (213) 429-1092 with the year and model for a figure.

### Is the Model S rear window done in one piece?

Yes. One sheet of ceramic film is heat-formed to the curve of the rear glass on a glass board, then applied in a single pass over the defroster lines. There is no seam and the lines are not scratched or lifted.

### Will tint on a Model S affect the cameras, sensors or phone key?

No. The camera positions on the windshield and fenders are masked and the film pattern stops short of them. The ceramic film contains no metal, so the phone key, cellular and GPS signals pass through the glass unchanged.

### What should a Venice owner expect right after the tint goes on?

Leave the windows up for several days while the film cures. A little haze or a few small water pockets in the first days are normal and clear on their own. Book an appointment before driving over so the bay is ready for the car.
