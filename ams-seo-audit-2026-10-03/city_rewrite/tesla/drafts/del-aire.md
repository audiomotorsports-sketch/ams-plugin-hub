## What Del Aire Tesla owners come in for

**Model 3 ceramic window tint.** The 3 has frameless door glass that drops a hair when the door opens, so the film edge has to be cut to the gasket or it lifts. We cut each piece on a glass board, never on your car, and heat-shape the rear window before it goes on.

**Model Y and Model S tint.** The Y shares most of its glass logic with the 3 but carries more rear area; the S has longer, more curved doors. Both get the same ceramic film, shaped to the actual glass rather than a shortened plotter pattern.

**Model X side glass.** Tall doors, a tall windshield and a lot of light. The X is quoted by model because the glass area and the labor are simply larger.

**Camera and sensor clearance.** Tesla cameras live on the glass. We mask those zones, leave them clear, and the ceramic film has no metal in it, so nothing interferes with the car.

Audio MotorSports also handles dash camera installation, car alarm installation, GPS tracker installation and car stereo installation in the same bay, so ask when you book if you want anything else done on the Tesla.

### Why does frameless Model 3 glass matter for tint?

Because the window drops slightly every time the door opens and seats again when it closes. A film edge cut short shows a line of light; an edge cut long peels. We cut to the gasket on a board so the edge sits where it should and stays there.

### Can you keep the front windows legal for a Del Aire Tesla?

We can. Front door glass stays at California 70% VLT, and ceramic film at that shade still blocks a large share of infrared heat. Rear doors and the back glass can go darker. You keep the cooler cabin without a problem at an inspection.

### Will ceramic tint affect my Model 3 cameras or phone key?

No. The camera zones on the glass are masked and left uncovered, and the ceramic film carries no metal layer, so there is nothing to block a signal. Your phone key, cameras and any other sensors behave exactly as they did before the tint.
