## What Los Angeles Tesla owners want to know

### Can you tint a Cybertruck, and what is different about it?

Yes. The Cybertruck's glass is flat, so there is no shrinking, but the panels are big and the window edges are dead straight, which leaves no room to hide a wavy cut. We template each pane, cut on a board away from the truck, and install the film in one piece per window.

### Is it worth driving in from Los Angeles for Tesla window tint?

It is for owners who want the job done once. Ceramic film cut to the glass, cameras kept clear, one-piece rear window, and a shop that tints Teslas all week. The drive is about half an hour each way and you get the car back the same day in most cases.

### Does the film interfere with Tesla cameras, Autopilot or the phone key?

It does not. Our ceramic film carries no metal, so cellular and Bluetooth signals pass straight through. The camera areas on the windshield and the side glass are left uncovered, and the film is trimmed clear of every sensor. Autopilot sees the same view it always did.

### What is the legal front window tint on a Tesla in California?

The front doors must pass 70% of visible light, so the fronts stay fairly light. Rear doors and the rear window have no limit. Ceramic film at the legal front shade still blocks most of the infrared heat, which is what makes the cabin comfortable.

### How do I keep the Cybertruck film looking right after the install?

Leave the windows up for several days while the film bonds to the glass. After that, clean it with a soft cloth and a cleaner without ammonia. Ceramic film does not fade or bubble in the sun, so the maintenance is just normal window cleaning.
