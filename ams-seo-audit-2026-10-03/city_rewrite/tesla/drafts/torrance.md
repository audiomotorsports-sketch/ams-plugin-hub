### What does one-piece rear glass tint actually involve?

A single sheet of ceramic film is laid over the outside of the rear window and shrunk with heat until it follows the curve of the glass. It is then moved inside and applied in one pass over the defroster lines. No seam, no overlap, and the lines are not scratched.

### Does tint interfere with Tesla cameras, sensors or the phone key?

It does not. Ceramic film carries no metal, so there is no signal interference with the phone key, the cellular connection or the cameras. The camera window at the top of the windshield and the side repeater cameras are masked and stay clear.
