### How are the falcon-wing door windows tinted?

Each piece is patterned and heat-shaped away from the car, then installed with the door raised and supported so the hinge carries no extra load. The glass is cleaned to the gasket and the film is laid edge to edge. It is slower than a normal door, which is reflected in the model-by-model quote.

No. The windshield camera window and the pillar cameras are left clear, the ultrasonic sensors are in the bumpers, and ceramic film carries no metal to interfere with the phone key, the card reader or the car's radios. Every feature works exactly as it did before the tint.

Yes. The front door windows go at the legal shade; the rear doors and the hatch can be darker for privacy and heat. The factory roof glass is already dark, so Nick matches the door and hatch shades to it. Nick lines up samples on the car so the shades flow together from the outside.
