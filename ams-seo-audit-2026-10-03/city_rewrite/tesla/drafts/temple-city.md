### Does the rear glass on a Model S get tinted as one piece?

Yes. The rear window is covered with a single sheet of ceramic film that is shrunk to the curve on a glass board, then applied over the defroster lines. There is no seam and the lines are not damaged.

### Will the film block my Tesla's phone key or cameras?

No. Ceramic film has no metal, so the phone key, cellular and GPS signals pass through. The camera zones on the windshield and the side repeaters are masked and stay clear, so Autopilot and Sentry Mode behave the same.
