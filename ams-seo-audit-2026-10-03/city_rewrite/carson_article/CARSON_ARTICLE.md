## Car stereo and audio installation

Car stereo installation is where most Carson customers start. We install head units with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, replace worn factory speakers, and add subwoofers and amplifiers for people who want more punch without losing trunk space. Wires are routed and hidden, the dash is not drilled, and panels go back without rattles. Our audio work is also sold under the LA Car Beats name, and we install popular brands that car audio fans already know.

If you ride a Harley bagger, our AMS pods bring the same care to motorcycle audio. We keep the focus on car and bike sound, because that is what we do all day.

## Window tint

Our window tint is cut by hand on a glass board and then applied, so the film fits the edges cleanly. Ceramic film cuts heat and keeps GPS and phone signals working, which Tesla owners especially appreciate. As of October 2026, Solar tint starts at $249 and Solar Ceramic at $329 installed. Side and rear glass are what we film, and factory roof glass is already dark. Tesla owners can also see our Tesla Tint LA desk, quoted by model at the shop.

## Car alarms and remote start

We install Viper car alarms and Compustar remote start, wired so the system is hard to bypass and the car still feels like your own. As of October 2026, a Viper 3108V alarm is $269 installed and Compustar 9910 remote start starts at $499. ## Dash cameras and GPS

A dash camera installation done properly hides the cable inside the trim, so nothing dangles across the windshield. A GPS tracker installation gives you a way to find your car if it is ever taken. Both fit neatly alongside an alarm, and we can do them in the same visit.
