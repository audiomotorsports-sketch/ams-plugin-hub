### Can everything be installed in a single day for a Chino Hills car?

Yes. Drop the car when we open and a head unit, four speakers, an amplifier and a subwoofer are finished by late afternoon on most vehicles. We confirm the dash kit, harness and integration parts by year and trim before you leave Chino Hills so nothing waits on parts.

### Will the new head unit keep my camera, chimes and wheel buttons?

Yes. The factory camera is wired to the new unit with the reverse trigger connected, and a control module programmed for the vehicle keeps the steering-wheel buttons and reproduces chimes on cars that route them through the radio. Each is tested before the car leaves the bay.

### Does Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation need a new radio?

Not always. Plenty of newer cars accept an interface that puts CarPlay and Android Auto on the factory screen while climate, camera and wheel controls stay as they were. If the radio is a basic unit, a CarPlay head unit is the smarter spend. Send the model and we will confirm.

### What if my Chino Hills car has a premium factory sound system?

It can still be done; the integration harness for that system wakes the factory amplifier and keeps every channel playing. Speaker replacement uses drivers matched to that amplifier's output. We identify the system from the trim before you arrive so the quote and parts are right.
