### Is it worth the drive from Hawaiian Gardens for a subwoofer installation?

Yes. The box is only part of it; the amplifier wiring, the fuse at the battery, the ground sanded to bare metal and the gain set properly are what keep the bass clean and the car safe. Hawaiian Gardens is about 11 miles, and the install is done the same day.

### My current sub rattles the trunk. Can a subwoofer upgrade fix that?

Usually. Rattles come from a loose box, an underpowered amp clipping, or trunk panels that need a little deadening. A subwoofer upgrade here starts with securing the enclosure and matching the amplifier to it, then treating the panel that buzzes. We listen before and after.

### How much bass can I get from the $249 Dual Kicker Comp box?

Plenty for a daily driver. As of October 2026 the $249 covers the loaded Dual Kicker Comp box only, two subs in a sealed enclosure, and with a properly matched mono amp it gives tight, musical bass. The amplifier installation and wiring are quoted separately for the car.

### Can you add a subwoofer to my factory radio?

Yes. A line-output converter pulls a clean signal from the factory speaker wires and turns the amp on automatically, so the dash stays original. If you later want a screen with CarPlay, car stereo installation can be added without redoing the sub wiring.

### Do I need new door speakers with a subwoofer installation?

Not required, but often worth it. Once the sub handles the low end, worn factory speakers in the doors are the next thing you hear. Speaker replacement on adapters lets us set a crossover so the doors play mids and highs cleanly while the box does the bass.
