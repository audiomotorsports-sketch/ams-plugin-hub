## Lakewood speaker FAQ

### My factory speakers crackle at volume. Is speaker replacement the fix or is it the radio?

Usually the speakers. Foam surrounds on factory drivers dry out and tear, and the crackle is the cone hitting the frame. We test each speaker before quoting so a Lakewood driver pays for speaker replacement only where it is needed, and we check the radio output so the new speakers are not starved.

### Does a Lakewood car need an amplifier installation with new speakers?

Not always. Aftermarket speakers are more efficient than most factory units, so a head unit alone sounds noticeably better. An amplifier installation is the next step when you want clean volume on the freeway without distortion; we will say honestly whether your car benefits.

### How far is Lakewood from the shop and can I wait while you work?

About 7.7 miles, well under half an hour outside rush hour. For a pair of front speakers, waiting works fine. For four doors, tweeters and an amp, drop the car in the morning and pick it up later the same day; we call or text when it is done.

### Can you replace speakers in a car with a premium factory sound system?

Yes, with the right parts. Premium systems often run odd impedances and have the amplifier built into the radio, so speaker replacement uses drivers matched to that output or adds an integration piece. We confirm the system by year, trim and model before the Lakewood car arrives.

### What makes speaker installation at a shop different from doing it in the driveway?

Adapters that actually fit the door, sealed mounting so the midbass does not leak, correct polarity on every speaker, and the door panel back on with every clip intact. Driveway jobs often skip one of those and the result is a rattle or thin sound. We do this every day.
