## Exposition Park subwoofer questions

### Why drive from Exposition Park for an amplifier installation?

Because the amp is only as good as the power run, ground and tune behind it. A fused cable through a firewall grommet, a short ground to bare metal and a gain set with a meter are what keep the amp and speakers alive. Doing that right once beats redoing a cheap install.

### What signal does a subwoofer installation use if I keep the factory radio?

A line-output converter takes the speaker-level signal from the factory stereo, turns it into a clean low-level signal and feeds the amp, with a remote turn-on so the amp powers up with the car. The factory radio stays, and the sub plays like it was built in.

### Is a subwoofer upgrade cheaper than a full new install?

Usually, if the existing power wire is the right gauge and properly fused and the amp matches the new sub. Then a subwoofer upgrade is a swap and a re-tune. If the old wiring is undersized or unfused, we will show you and quote the safe fix first.
