## Santa Ana amplifier questions

### What size fuse and wire does an amplifier installation use?

It depends on the amplifier. The main power wire is sized to the current the amp draws and fused near the battery at the rating that wire can carry, so a short cannot overheat it. The ground is the same gauge and kept short. We size both to the amplifier rather than using whatever is in the kit.

### Can I add an amplifier to a Santa Ana car that still has the factory radio?

Yes. Using a line-output converter, the speaker-level output of the factory radio becomes a clean low-level signal for the amplifier, and a remote turn-on is derived so the amp wakes with the ignition. The factory head unit, screen and controls stay as they are.

### How do you keep an amplifier from overheating under a seat?

By mounting it where air can move around it, not buried in carpet, and by setting the gain correctly so it is not driven into clipping. A properly sized power and ground also keeps the amp from working harder than it should. We check the mounting spot before committing to it.
