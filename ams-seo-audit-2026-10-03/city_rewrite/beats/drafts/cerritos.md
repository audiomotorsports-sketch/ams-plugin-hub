### Can I bring a head unit I bought online?

Yes. We install customer-supplied head units regularly. We check the unit, source the correct harness and dash kit for your car, and do the installation the same way as a unit bought from us. Bring the box and any cables that came with it.

### Does the new stereo keep my factory Bluetooth microphone?

Usually the new head unit comes with its own microphone, which we mount near the factory location for clear calls. In some cars the factory microphone can be reused during car stereo installation; we will check your vehicle and tell you which applies.
