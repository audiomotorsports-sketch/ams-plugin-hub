## Fountain Valley head unit questions

### Will a head unit installation void anything or leave marks on my Fountain Valley car?

No wiring is cut and no dash is drilled. The new unit sits in a vehicle-specific kit and plugs into a harness, so the car can go back to the factory radio at any time. The trim panels are removed with the proper tools and go back on without marks.

### What makes a factory-look install different from a basic radio swap?

The kit, the harness and the modules. A basic swap leaves gaps, loses the steering-wheel buttons and sometimes the camera. A factory-look head unit installation matches the dash trim, keeps every original function and routes cables so nothing shows. Done once, done right.
