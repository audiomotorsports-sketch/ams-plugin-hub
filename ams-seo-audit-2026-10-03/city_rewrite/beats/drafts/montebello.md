## Montebello car stereo questions

### Can a Montebello driver build the stereo in stages instead of all at once?

Yes, and it is a smart way to do it. Start with a head unit installation and speaker replacement, and we plan the wiring so an amplifier and subwoofer can be added later without redoing anything. Each stage is a complete, working system on its own.

### What does the shop need from me before the car stereo installation?

Year, make, model and trim, whether the car has a factory amplifier or premium sound, and what you want the result to be. With that we confirm the dash kit, harness, modules and speaker sizes, give you a quote and have the parts ready when you arrive from Montebello.

### Will the new head unit keep Apple CarPlay, the backup camera and the steering-wheel buttons?

Yes. The head units we install carry Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, the backup camera is connected to the new screen and a programmed module keeps the steering-wheel buttons working. Chimes that route through the radio are carried over on vehicles that need it.

### How is the wiring hidden in a full system?

Power runs from the battery through a fuse and under the carpet to the amplifier, speaker wire follows the factory paths into the doors, signal cables run away from power, and the box is anchored. Trim and panels go back on with the clips intact, so the cabin looks stock.
