## Services Compton customers ask for

**Speaker replacement.** Speaker installation means more than swapping cones. We mount new speakers on the right adapter, seal the door opening, and wire them so the front stage sounds clear at every volume.

**Car stereo installation.** A new receiver goes in with a harness and dash kit built for your car, and we test every function before you leave: radio, Bluetooth, camera and steering-wheel buttons.

**Subwoofer upgrade.** If you already have a sub that sounds loose, a subwoofer upgrade fixes the enclosure, the amplifier gain and the wiring, not just the driver.

**Amplifier installation.** Amplifier installation includes a fused power run from the battery, a solid chassis ground and signal cables routed away from noise sources, so there is no alternator whine.

**Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation.** For Compton commuters who want maps and calls on a real screen, we install CarPlay and Android Auto head units that keep the factory look of the dash.

## Questions from Compton drivers

### How long does speaker replacement take for a Compton customer?

Most front and rear speaker replacement jobs are finished the same day you drop off. Door panels come off, new speakers go on adapters, the openings are sealed and everything is tested. Compton is close enough that you can drop the car in the morning and pick it up after work.

### Why do my new speakers still sound bad on the factory stereo?

Usually it is power or installation, not the speakers. Factory head units deliver very little clean power, and speakers mounted without sealing or proper brackets lose their bass. Amplifier installation and correct speaker installation fix both problems, which is why we do them together.

### Is Carson far from Compton for a subwoofer upgrade?

No. The shop is about 5.4 miles from Compton, close to a quarter-hour drive. A subwoofer upgrade is worth that trip because the enclosure, amplifier settings and wiring are what make a sub sound tight, and those are the parts most often done wrong elsewhere.

### Will car stereo installation damage my dash?

Not when it is done with the right parts. We use a vehicle-specific dash kit and harness so the dash is not cut and no factory wires are spliced. The new unit sits flush and the trim snaps back the way it was.

### Do you install Apple CarPlay in older cars?

Yes. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation works in most cars from the last twenty years that have a standard radio opening. We pick a head unit that fits your dash, add the harness and interface, and keep your existing speakers if they are still healthy.
