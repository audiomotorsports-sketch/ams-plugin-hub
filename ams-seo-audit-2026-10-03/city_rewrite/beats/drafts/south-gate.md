### Which cars from South Gate can get Apple CarPlay installed?

### Does the CarPlay head unit work with my steering-wheel buttons?

Yes. During Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation we add a steering-wheel control interface matched to your vehicle, so volume, track and voice buttons keep working. Factory chimes and the backup camera are retained through the same module when your car has them.

### Is it worth driving from South Gate for head unit installation?

Yes. South Gate is about 9.3 miles out, a quick trip, and head unit installation is the kind of work where a clean harness, correct dash kit and a full function test save you from a loose screen or dead buttons later. Done once, done right.

### Can I get wireless CarPlay?

Many current head units support wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and we install those. Wireless units still get a USB connection for charging and wired fallback. We will recommend a wired or wireless unit based on your phone and your budget.

### What else should I do while the dash is apart?

Speaker replacement is the usual add-on, because the door panels and dash are already open during car stereo installation and the labor overlaps. A dash camera installation can also be wired into the same harness while we are in there.
