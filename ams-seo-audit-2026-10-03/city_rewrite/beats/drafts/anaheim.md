Usually under the rear seat, where there is room and airflow. The amplifier installation runs a fused power cable from the battery through a firewall grommet, a short ground to bare metal and signal from the radio, with the wiring loomed and kept clear of seat tracks and pedals.

### Is driving from Anaheim for a subwoofer installation worth it?

For about 20 miles, yes. The sub is the easy part; a fused power run, a proper ground, the right signal and a gain set with a meter are what make it last. Having it done once by a shop that does it daily beats redoing a loose install later.

Yes. A line-output converter takes signal from the factory radio and feeds an amplifier and sub, so the screen and controls stay as they are. Many Anaheim SUV owners choose this over a head unit installation, then add speaker replacement for the front doors.
