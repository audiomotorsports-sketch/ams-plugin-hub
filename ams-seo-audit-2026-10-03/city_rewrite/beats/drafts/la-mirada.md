## La Mirada speaker FAQ

### Is a speaker replacement worth a 15-mile drive from La Mirada?

Yes. The speakers are the cheap part; the install is what you are driving for. Adapters that fit the door, sealed mounts, correct polarity on every driver and a check of the factory amplifier output are what keep the new speakers from sounding thin or rattling. The drive is about half an hour.

### Which speakers go first if I cannot do all four doors at once?

Front doors. That is where the sound stage lives, and the rear speakers mostly fill in. Many La Mirada drivers do the fronts and tweeters first, then come back for the rears and an amplifier installation. We set the first pair up so the second visit is simple.

### Does my car need an amplifier with new speakers?

Not necessarily. Aftermarket speakers are more efficient than most factory ones and sound better on the same power. Amplifier installation is the next step when you want clean volume on the freeway without distortion. We will say honestly whether your La Mirada car benefits.

### Can you replace speakers in a car with a premium factory system?

Yes, with matching. Premium systems often run unusual impedances and have the amplifier built into the radio, so speaker replacement uses drivers suited to that output or adds an integration piece. We identify the system by trim before the car arrives.

### Why do my new speakers rattle after a driveway install?

Usually the adapter does not fit the door, the speaker is not sealed to it, or a clip was missed on the panel. Speaker installation at the shop fixes all three: the right adapter, foam sealing behind the basket, and the panel back on with every clip. Bring it in from La Mirada and we will sort it.
