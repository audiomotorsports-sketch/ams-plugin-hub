## Questions from Gardena

### Will Android Auto installation on my factory screen keep the backup camera?

Yes. The interface modules we use pass the factory camera through, and on a new head unit the camera is wired to the unit's input with the reverse trigger connected. Either way you shift into reverse and see the same picture you had before the Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation.

### Is a 4.9-mile drive from Gardena worth it for a CarPlay install?

At that distance you spend more time choosing a radio than driving to the shop. What you get for the trip is wiring that is loomed and tucked, a USB port that does not hang out of the dash, and a test of phone projection, calls and the camera before you pull out.

### Which is better for a Gardena commuter, a new head unit or a CarPlay interface?

If the factory radio controls climate or vehicle settings, an interface keeps all of that and adds CarPlay to the existing screen. If the radio is a basic unit in a standard opening, head unit installation gives a bigger screen and a faster processor for less money. We confirm by year and model.

### Can you add wireless CarPlay to a car that only has wired?

Yes, in most cases. Some new head units have wireless CarPlay and Android Auto built in, and there are adapters that make a wired system wireless. The right answer depends on the radio already in the car, so send the model and we will quote the install that fits.

### Does speaker replacement change how CarPlay sounds?

It changes everything downstream of the radio. CarPlay only feeds the signal; worn paper-cone factory speakers are what make it sound flat. Speaker replacement with properly mounted and sealed drivers, and an amplifier when the budget allows, is what makes a Gardena car sound like the phone promised.
