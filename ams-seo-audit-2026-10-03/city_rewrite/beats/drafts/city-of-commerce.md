## Commerce amp and sub questions

### What amplifier should a Commerce driver pair with the Dual Kicker Comp box?

A mono amplifier in the box's rated power range at its impedance. Too small and the amp clips and damages the subs; too large with the gain cranked does the same. We match the amp to the box at the shop and set the gain with a meter during the amplifier installation.

### Can you run an amplifier and subwoofer from a factory radio?

Yes. A line-output converter takes the factory speaker signal, turns the amplifier on automatically and feeds it a clean low-level signal. The dash stays untouched. Subwoofer installation this way is common on newer cars where the factory screen controls other functions.

### Is Commerce close enough to wait while the sub goes in?

At about 13.1 miles, yes. A loaded box with a mono amp is a few hours in the bay, and most Commerce customers wait. If you are adding speaker replacement or car stereo installation to the same ticket, drop the car and we will text when it is ready.

### Why does a shop amplifier installation last longer than a quick one?

Because of the parts nobody sees. A fuse at the battery protects the car, a grommet protects the wire at the firewall, a bare-metal ground stops hum and shutdown, and loomed wiring stays out of seat tracks. Skip those and the amp works for a season, then does not.

### Will a subwoofer installation rattle my trunk lid?

It can on any car if the box is loose or the lid is thin. We secure the enclosure so it cannot move and, where needed, treat the panel that resonates. Setting the crossover and gain correctly also removes the boomy frequencies that excite the lid in the first place.
