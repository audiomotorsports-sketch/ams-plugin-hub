## Irwindale amplifier FAQ

### Why drive over an hour from Irwindale for an amplifier installation?

Because the amp is cheap compared to the wiring that makes it safe and quiet. A fuse within inches of the battery, a grommet at the firewall, a bare-metal ground and a gain set by meter are what keep an amp from humming, cutting out or starting a fire. One trip, done once.

### Can an amplifier run from my factory radio?

Yes. A line-output converter takes the speaker-level signal from the factory radio and gives the amplifier a clean low-level one, with remote turn-on built in. The dash stays original. Amplifier installation this way is common on newer Irwindale cars where the factory screen has to stay.

### What causes engine whine through the speakers after an amp install?

A ground on painted metal, or power and signal wires run side by side. We sand the ground point, use a tight ring terminal and route the RCA cables on the opposite side of the car from the power wire. Nearly every case of whine comes down to one of those two.

### What amp size does a subwoofer installation need?

The amp is matched to the box, not guessed. A loaded enclosure like the Dual Kicker Comp box wants a mono amp in its rated range at the correct impedance. We choose the pairing at the shop so the subs are not underpowered or overdriven, and set the gain with a meter.

### Can an Irwindale driver add speaker replacement to the same amplifier visit?

Yes, and it is the right order. New speakers on sealed adapters can use the amplifier's power without distortion, and we set the crossover so the doors handle mids and highs while the sub handles bass. Booking both together saves a second long drive from Irwindale.
