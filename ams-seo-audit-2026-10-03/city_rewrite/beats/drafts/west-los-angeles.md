### What is the difference between coaxial and component speaker replacement?

Coaxial speakers put the tweeter in the center of the woofer, a simple one-piece upgrade for each door. Component sets separate the tweeter and mount it higher, which gives clearer detail at ear level. We recommend based on your car, your budget and whether an amplifier is part of the plan.

### Will new speakers work with the factory radio in my West Los Angeles car?

Yes. Replacement speakers are more efficient than tired originals and sound cleaner on factory power. If the car has a factory amplifier, we choose speakers that match its output. When you want more volume without strain, an amplifier installation is the next step, not a requirement.

No. The panels come off with the right tools and go back on with the clips intact, the speakers mount on adapters in the factory position and plug-in harnesses connect to the stock wiring. Nothing is cut or glued, and the car could be returned to original if needed.
