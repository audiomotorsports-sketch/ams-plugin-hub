### How do I know my speakers are blown and not the radio?

Buzzing or crackling at normal volume, one side much quieter than the other, or silence from one corner usually means a speaker. Speaker replacement fixes that directly. If the whole system is simply weak, amplifier installation or car stereo installation may be the better first step.

### Will new speakers work with my factory amplified system?

They can, with matching. A car with a factory amplifier needs speakers picked to suit that amplifier output, and sometimes an integration harness as well. We check this before speaker installation so the new speakers are neither underpowered nor overdriven.

### Should I add an amplifier with new speakers?
