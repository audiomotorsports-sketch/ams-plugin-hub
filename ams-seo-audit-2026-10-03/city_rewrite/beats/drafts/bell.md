## Questions from Bell

### Should a Bell driver replace speakers before adding an amplifier?

Do them together if the budget allows. Factory speakers cannot use the power an amplifier provides and will distort first. Speaker replacement with drivers rated for the amp, then amplifier installation with gains set to those speakers, gives clean volume without the cycle of blowing cheap cones.

### Why does my amp cut out when the bass hits?

Almost always a bad ground or an undersized power wire. When the amp draws current, voltage sags across a poor connection and the protection circuit trips. Amplifier installation here uses the correct gauge, a fuse at the battery and a ground on bare metal, which is why ours do not cut out.

### How far is Bell from the shop and can the work be done in a day?

About 11.3 miles, under half an hour outside rush hour. Speaker replacement in four doors with a four-channel amplifier is a one-day job when the car is dropped in the morning. We call or text when it is finished so you can plan the return trip from Bell.

### Can you install speakers in a car with a factory amplified system?

Yes, with matching. Factory amplified cars often run low-impedance speakers and have the amp built into the radio, so we choose drivers that suit that output or add an integration piece. We identify the system by trim before the Bell car arrives so the quote is right the first time.

### Does car stereo installation make sense if I already have a good amp?

If the amp is fed by a line-output converter from a factory radio, a head unit with real pre-amp outputs gives it a cleaner, stronger signal and adds CarPlay. Car stereo installation reuses your existing amp wiring, so the cost is the head unit, kit and the labor to connect it.
