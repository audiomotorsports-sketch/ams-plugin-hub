## Installed to look original

**Head unit installation.** The new receiver goes into a model-specific dash kit with matching texture and color, wired through a harness that plugs into the factory connector. The dash is never cut.

**Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation.** A CarPlay or Android Auto screen that fits the opening cleanly, with the factory camera, chimes and steering-wheel buttons retained.

**Speaker replacement.** Speakers go behind the original grilles on sealed adapters so the doors look untouched and sound far better.

**Subwoofer installation.** Compact enclosures under seats or in trunk corners, with the amplifier mounted out of sight and wiring run under carpet.

**Amplifier installation.** Power wire fused at the battery, ground sanded to bare metal, signal cable routed away from power to prevent whine.
