## What we install for Rolling Hills trucks and SUVs

**Car stereo installation for trucks and SUVs.** Head unit installation in a pickup or SUV usually involves a larger dash opening and a factory amplifier. We use the correct integration harness so chimes, OnStar-style modules and cameras keep working.

Yes. Full-size trucks are a large part of our work. Car stereo installation on a truck means an integration harness for the factory amplifier, a dash kit sized for the wide opening and retention of the backup camera. Rolling Hills customers usually drop the truck for the day.

### Where does a subwoofer go in an SUV without losing cargo space?

Subwoofer installation in an SUV often uses a shallow enclosure under the rear seat, along the cargo side wall or in the spare-tire well. The amplifier mounts to the enclosure or behind a panel. You keep the floor clear and the bass is still there.

### Is the drive from Rolling Hills to Carson reasonable for amplifier installation?

### Will new speakers fix road noise on the freeway?

Speaker replacement helps a lot. Factory paper-cone speakers lose detail at speed, so a sealed, properly powered set keeps vocals clear over tire noise. Pairing speaker installation with amplifier installation gives the biggest improvement in a large truck or SUV cabin.

### Can I keep my factory screen and still add Apple CarPlay?

In many trucks and SUVs, yes. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation can use an interface module that adds CarPlay to the factory display, or we replace the radio with a larger CarPlay head unit. We will tell you which option your vehicle supports.
