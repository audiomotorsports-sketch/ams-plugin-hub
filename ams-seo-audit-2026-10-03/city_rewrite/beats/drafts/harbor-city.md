## Harbor City stereo questions

### How long does head unit installation take for a Harbor City car?

Most single-DIN or double-DIN swaps are done in a couple of hours, and Harbor City is close enough that waiting at the shop makes sense. Cars with a factory amplifier or an integrated climate screen take longer because an interface module has to be wired and programmed before the dash goes back on.

### Do you keep the steering-wheel controls during car stereo installation?

Yes. A steering-wheel control interface is part of every car stereo installation on a vehicle that has buttons on the wheel. Volume, track, mute and voice commands are mapped to the new radio and tested before you leave, so the wheel works the way it did from the factory.

### Can I get Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation without changing my dash?

Often, yes. On many vehicles an interface adds Apple CarPlay and Android Auto to the factory screen and keeps the original look and controls. Where that option does not exist, a head unit installation with CarPlay built in is the route. Text the year and model and we will say which applies.

### Is it worth leaving Harbor City for a speaker replacement when parts are sold online?

The speakers are the easy part; the install is why you drive. Speaker replacement done here means the right adapters, sealed mounts, correct polarity and a check of the factory amplifier output, so the new speakers do not sound thin or rattle. Harbor City is close enough to wait while it happens.

### What size subwoofer box fits a daily driver from Harbor City?

It depends on the trunk or cab, which is why we look at the car first. A compact loaded enclosure suits most sedans and crossovers, while trucks usually take a shallow box behind the rear seat. Subwoofer installation includes securing the box so it never becomes a projectile under braking.
