### Why does my current amp cut out on bass notes?

Nearly always a poor ground or an undersized power wire. When the amp draws current, voltage sags across the weak connection and protection trips. Amplifier installation at the shop uses the right gauge, a fuse at the battery and a ground on bare metal, which is why ours stay on.

### Can you replace speakers in a car with a factory amplified system?

Yes, with matching. Factory amplified cars often run low-impedance speakers and build the amp into the radio, so we select drivers that suit that output or add an integration piece. We identify the system by trim before the Hacienda Heights car arrives so the quote is right.

### Does car stereo installation make sense if I already have an amp?

If the amp is fed by a line-output converter from a factory radio, a head unit with real pre-amp outputs gives it a cleaner signal and adds CarPlay. Because the existing amp wiring stays, car stereo installation costs only the head unit, the kit and the labor to tie it in.
