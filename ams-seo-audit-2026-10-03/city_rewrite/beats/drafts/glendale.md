### What does amplifier installation protect against?

Blown speakers from clipping, blown fuses from undersized wire and the risk that comes with an unfused power cable. The installation uses the correct gauge wire with a fuse at the battery, a short ground to bare metal, and a gain set with a meter so the amp never clips.

### Can a subwoofer work with my Glendale car factory stereo?

Yes. A line-output converter pulls signal from the factory speaker wires, feeds the amplifier and triggers it on with the car. The factory radio and screen stay untouched, and the sub plays like it was built in. This is the most common setup for newer cars.
