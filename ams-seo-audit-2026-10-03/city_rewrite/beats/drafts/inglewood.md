## Inglewood speaker questions

### How do I know my speakers need replacing rather than the radio?

If the sound buzzes or crackles at normal volume, or one side is quieter than the other, the speakers are usually the problem. Speaker replacement fixes it directly. If everything is simply flat and quiet, car stereo installation with more power may be the better first step, and we will tell you which.

### Is Carson a long way from Inglewood for speaker installation?

About 10.6 miles, so less than half an hour in normal traffic. Speaker installation is worth that drive because sealed mounting, correct wiring and a check of the factory amplifier are what make new speakers sound right. Inglewood customers typically drop off in the morning.

### Can you replace just the front speakers?

Yes. Front speaker replacement alone makes a noticeable difference because that is where most of the sound comes from. Many Inglewood drivers do fronts first and come back for rears or a subwoofer installation later when the budget allows, which spreads the cost.

### Will aftermarket speakers work with my factory amplified system?

They can, with the right matching. Factory amplified cars need speakers chosen for the amplifier output and sometimes an integration module. We check this before any speaker replacement so the new speakers are not underpowered or overdriven by the factory amplifier.

### What is the difference between coaxial and component speaker installation?

Coaxial speakers put the tweeter in the center of the woofer and drop into the factory location, which keeps speaker installation simple. Component sets separate the tweeter for better imaging but need a crossover and a tweeter mount. We will recommend one based on your car and your listening.
