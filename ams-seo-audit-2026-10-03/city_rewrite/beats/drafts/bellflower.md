## Bellflower truck audio FAQ

### Can a Bellflower crew cab get a subwoofer without losing the rear seat?

Yes. Shallow-mount subwoofers in a low-profile box fit under or behind the rear bench on most crew cabs, and the seat folds back down. Subwoofer installation includes bolting or strapping the box so it stays put when the truck is loaded. We confirm fit by year and cab style.

### Will my truck's backup camera still work after car stereo installation?

It will. Car stereo installation on a truck includes wiring the factory camera to the new head unit or carrying it through an interface, with the reverse trigger connected so it switches automatically. Parking sensors and the steering-wheel buttons are retained the same way and tested before pickup.

### Why drive from Bellflower instead of using a mobile installer?

A bay with a lift, the right dash kits in stock and a technician who does trucks daily beats a parking-lot install. Grounds are sanded, power is fused at the battery and nothing is spliced with tape. Bellflower is close enough that the drive costs you less than a redo would.

### Is amplifier installation needed if I only add a subwoofer?

Yes, a subwoofer always needs its own amplifier; the head unit cannot drive it. A mono amp sized to the box, a fused power wire, remote turn-on and a line-output converter if the factory radio stays are all part of the job. We quote it as one package.

### How long does a full truck system take at the shop?

A head unit alone is a few hours. Add a sub box, mono amp and door speakers and it is a full day in the bay, so plan to drop the truck in the morning. Bellflower drivers usually get a ride home or wait for the shorter jobs, and we text when it is ready.
