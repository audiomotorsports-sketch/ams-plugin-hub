### What goes into a subwoofer installation that a kit from a store leaves out?

A fuse sized to the wire and mounted near the battery, a ground bonded to sanded metal, signal cables routed away from power, a remote turn-on that follows the radio, a box anchored so it cannot slide and a gain set with a meter. Those details are why self-installs come in for a redo.

### Can the sub be added without replacing the factory radio in my Pomona car?

Yes. A line-output converter takes the signal from the factory speaker wires and feeds the amplifier, and the remote turn-on is derived so the amp follows the key. The factory head unit and controls stay. A head unit installation later can feed the same amplifier directly.

### How do you keep the bass from overpowering the rest of the music?

The crossover on the amplifier limits the sub to the low notes, the gain is matched to the head unit output instead of maxed, and the box is matched to the sub. If the door speakers are weak, speaker replacement brings them up so the mix is balanced rather than all bass.
