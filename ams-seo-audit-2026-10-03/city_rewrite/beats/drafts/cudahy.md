### Can you do amplifier installation on my factory radio in a Cudahy car?

### How do you keep an amplifier from causing engine whine or hum?

By doing the ground right. The ground point is sanded to bare metal, the ring terminal is tight and sealed, and the power and signal wires run on opposite sides of the car. Most hum we fix on cars that came from elsewhere traces back to a ground on painted metal.
