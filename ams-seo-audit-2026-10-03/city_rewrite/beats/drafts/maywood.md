## Maywood head unit questions

### My factory radio in the Maywood car is dead; can a new head unit go straight in?

Almost always. We confirm the radio is the fault rather than a fuse or a harness issue, then fit a new head unit on a dash kit with a plug-in harness. The steering-wheel controls are retained with a module, and the backup camera, if the car has one, is connected to the new screen.

### Does head unit installation keep the backup camera and the factory chimes?

Yes. The camera feeds the new screen and switches on in reverse. On vehicles that play chimes and warning tones through the radio, the integration module carries them over. Send the year, make and model and we will confirm what your specific vehicle needs before you arrive.

### Will the new head unit fit my dash without a gap?

A vehicle-specific dash kit is part of every head unit installation. It fills the factory opening, matches the surrounding trim and holds the unit flush and tight, so there is no visible gap or bracket and nothing rattles on rough pavement.

### Can Maywood drivers get speaker replacement added to the same appointment?

Yes, and it is efficient to do both at once. While the dash is apart for the head unit, the doors come off for speaker replacement, and the whole system is tested together. It saves a second drive from Maywood and the car comes back finished in one visit.
