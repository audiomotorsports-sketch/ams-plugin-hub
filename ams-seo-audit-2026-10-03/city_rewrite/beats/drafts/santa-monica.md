## Santa Monica speaker questions

### Which speakers are worth replacing first in a Santa Monica car?

The front doors and tweeters, since that is where you hear detail. Rear speakers matter less for the driver and can follow later. We test each factory speaker before recommending anything, and if only one side has failed we say so rather than selling a full set.

### Will replacement speakers sound right on a factory premium system?

Yes, if they are chosen for it. Factory premium systems have their own amplifier and sometimes their own crossover settings, so the new speakers need the right impedance and sensitivity. We match them to the system, and when the vehicle needs an integration piece we include it.

### Does speaker replacement mean cutting the doors or splicing wires?

No. Adapters hold the speaker in the factory position, plug-in harnesses connect to the stock wiring and the door panels go back on with their clips intact. Nothing is cut or glued, so the car can return to original if it is ever sold or leased.

### Is driving from Santa Monica for speaker replacement sensible?

Yes. The mounting and wiring are the service, and they decide whether the speakers sound right and stay rattle-free. That is daily work for this shop, which is why drivers come from well beyond Santa Monica for it. A job done once, done right costs less than redoing a quick one.

### Can I pair an amplifier installation with the speaker job in one visit?

Yes, and it is the most efficient time to do it. With the doors open, speaker wire runs to an amplifier under a seat and the system is tuned together. One drive from Santa Monica, one finished system, tested at volume before pickup.
