## Speaker questions from Santa Fe Springs

### Which speakers should a Santa Fe Springs driver replace first?

The front doors. They carry most of the sound and are usually the first to wear out. Speaker replacement in the front doors on sealed adapters makes the biggest difference per dollar, and the rears can be done later or at the same time if you want the full cabin to match.

### Do you cut or splice factory wiring during speaker replacement?

Not when a plug-in speaker harness exists for the vehicle, which is most cases. The new speaker plugs into the factory connector, so the car can go back to stock if needed. Where no adapter exists, the connection is crimped and insulated, never twisted and taped.
