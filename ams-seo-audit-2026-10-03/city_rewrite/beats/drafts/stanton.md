### Is it worth driving from Stanton to Carson for head unit installation?

Yes. The drive is a bit over half an hour outside rush hour. Head unit installation done with the right harness and kit, and tested before the dash goes back together, is what keeps a stereo working for years instead of coming loose or losing buttons. Done once, done right.

### Can you install a head unit I already bought?

Yes. Customer-supplied head unit installation is common. We check the unit works, find the correct harness and dash kit for your car and install it with the same care as a unit bought from us. Bring the box and the cables it came with.

### Will my steering-wheel buttons still work after the install?

Yes. During car stereo installation we add a steering-wheel control interface programmed for your vehicle, so volume, track and voice buttons keep working with the new head unit. On cars that play their warning chimes through the radio, the chimes are retained as well.

### Should I replace my speakers at the same time?

If the factory speakers are old or sound thin, yes. Speaker replacement during head unit installation saves labor because the door panels are already off. If the speakers are healthy, we will say so and leave them until you are ready for amplifier installation or a subwoofer.
