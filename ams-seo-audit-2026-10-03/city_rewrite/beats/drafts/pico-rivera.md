## Pico Rivera subwoofer questions

### Where does a subwoofer go in a Pico Rivera pickup with no trunk?

Behind the rear seat or under it, in a box shaped for that space. The amplifier mounts nearby, power runs from the battery through a fuse and the box is strapped so it stays put. The cab keeps its seating and you get bass that a truck with no trunk is not supposed to have.

### Will the subwoofer drown out my door speakers?

Not when it is tuned correctly. The amplifier crossover is set so the sub only plays the low end and the doors handle the rest, and the gain is matched to the head unit output. If the doors are weak, speaker replacement brings them up to the level of the sub.

### Can I add a sub to a factory stereo without changing the radio?

Yes. A line-output converter takes signal from the factory speaker wires and feeds the amplifier, with a remote turn-on so the amp follows the radio. The stock head unit and controls stay. A head unit installation later can feed the same amp directly if you decide you want CarPlay.

### What makes a shop subwoofer installation better than a self-install?
