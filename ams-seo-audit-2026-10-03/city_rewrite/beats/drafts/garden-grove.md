## Garden Grove subwoofer FAQ

### Is Garden Grove too far to drive for a subwoofer installation?

No. At about 18.9 miles it is under an hour outside rush hour, and the install is finished the same day. What you drive for is the wiring: fused at the battery, grommeted through the firewall, grounded to bare metal, with the gain set by meter so the subs are never clipped.

### What amplifier goes with the Dual Kicker Comp box?

A mono amplifier in the box's rated range at its impedance. Undersized amps clip and damage subs; oversized amps with the gain cranked do the same. We pair the amp to the box during the amplifier installation and set the gain properly so a Garden Grove driver gets clean bass that lasts.

### Can a subwoofer be added to my factory radio?

Yes. A line-output converter takes the factory speaker signal, turns the amp on automatically and delivers a clean low-level feed. The dash stays original. If you later want a bigger screen, car stereo installation can be added without redoing the sub wiring.

### Will the box take up my whole trunk?

No. A sealed loaded enclosure fits a trunk corner or against the rear seat, and in an SUV it sits in a cargo corner and leaves the floor usable. We measure the car before choosing the box, and subwoofer installation includes securing it so it cannot move under braking.

### Why does my current amp shut off when the bass hits?

Nearly always a weak ground or an undersized power wire; voltage drops under load and the protection circuit trips. Amplifier installation here uses the correct gauge, a fuse at the battery and a ground sanded to bare metal, which is why ours stay on. Bring the car in from Garden Grove and we will check it.
