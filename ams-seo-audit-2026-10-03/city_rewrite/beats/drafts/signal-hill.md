## Signal Hill subwoofer questions

### What does a complete subwoofer installation include for a Signal Hill car?

The loaded box, an amplifier sized for it, a fused power wire from the battery, a solid ground, a remote turn-on lead, signal from the head unit or a line-output converter on a factory radio, and a tune. The box is secured so it stays put when you brake hard.

### Will a subwoofer rattle my trunk lid and license plate?

A well-matched sub in a solid box, with the gain set correctly, should not. Rattles usually come from an overdriven amp or loose trim. During the install we check the trunk, rear deck and plate and address anything that buzzes before handing the car back.

### Can I keep my factory radio and still add a subwoofer?

Yes. A line-output converter takes signal from the factory speaker wires and feeds the amplifier, so the stock head unit stays. If you later want Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation, a new head unit can feed the same amp directly.

### How far is Signal Hill from the shop, and can I wait?

About 5.8 miles, roughly a quarter hour on a normal day. A box-only hookup to an existing amplifier can be waited on. A full subwoofer installation with a new amplifier and wiring is better as a drop-off so the wiring can be routed and hidden without rushing.

### Why not just buy a powered sub online and install it myself?

You can, and the part is the easy half. The reason Signal Hill drivers come to us is the labor: a fused power run, a ground that does not hum, a crossover and gain set properly, and a box that is anchored. We fix plenty of self-installs; done once, done right is cheaper.
