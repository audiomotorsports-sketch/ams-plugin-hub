### Will the new head unit look like it belongs in the dash?

That is the point of using the right dash kit. The kit fills the opening, matches the surrounding trim and holds the unit flush, so there is no gap, no visible bracket and no rattle over bumps. We also keep the hazard switch and any climate controls that share the panel working.

### Can West Carson drivers get Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation without replacing the factory radio?

On many late-model vehicles, yes. An interface module adds CarPlay and Android Auto to the factory screen while leaving the original radio, camera and buttons in place. When the vehicle does not support that route, a head unit installation with both built in gets you there.

### Does speaker replacement require an amplifier installation too?

Not always. A good set of speakers on factory power is a clear step up for everyday listening. If you want real volume without distortion, or you are adding a subwoofer, an amplifier installation makes the speakers do what they were built to do. We will tell you honestly which you need.
