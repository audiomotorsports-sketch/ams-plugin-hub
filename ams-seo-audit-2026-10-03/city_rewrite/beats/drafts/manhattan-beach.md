## Manhattan Beach speaker questions

### Will speaker replacement change how the interior of my Manhattan Beach car looks?

No. Speakers mount in the factory positions on vehicle-specific adapters and the original grilles go back on. Tweeters sit where the factory ones were. Unless you tell someone, the only sign of the upgrade is the sound, which is the way most Manhattan Beach drivers want it.

### Can new speakers run on a factory premium system without an amplifier?

Yes, as long as they are chosen to match the factory amplifier output. We select speakers with the right sensitivity and impedance for the system so they play correctly on stock power. If you want more volume later, an amplifier installation can be added without redoing the speakers.

### How are the speakers mounted so the doors do not rattle?

Each speaker sits on a rigid adapter that matches the factory hole, with a foam gasket sealing it to the door so sound goes into the cabin instead of the panel. Connections use plug-in harnesses, not bare splices. The door panel is refitted with its clips intact.

### Is a 9.3-mile drive from Manhattan Beach reasonable for a speaker job?

Yes, and it is the labor that justifies it. Adapters, seals, correct wiring and tweeter placement decide whether the speakers sound right and keep sounding right. That is a shop skill, not a parts purchase, and it is done here every day. Done once, done right.

### Can an amplifier installation and subwoofer be added in the same visit?

Yes. With the doors open for speaker replacement, speaker wire can be run to an amplifier under a seat and a loaded box added in the trunk, then the whole system is tuned together. One appointment from Manhattan Beach, one finished system.
