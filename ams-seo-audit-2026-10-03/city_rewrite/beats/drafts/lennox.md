# Subwoofer and Amplifier Installation for Lennox

Lennox drivers who want real bass come to LA Car Beats, the car audio bay at Audio MotorSports, 22025 S Avalon Blvd Ste A in Carson, about 9 miles away. Subwoofer installation and amplifier installation are the lead services for Lennox: a loaded enclosure, a properly fused power run and an amplifier set up so it hits hard without distortion. Car stereo installation and speaker replacement are done alongside.

## Bass and power for Lennox cars

**Subwoofer installation.** We mount the enclosure so it cannot slide, run the power wire through a grommet with a fuse near the battery, and tune the crossover so the sub blends with the doors.

**Amplifier installation.** Amplifier installation covers power, ground, remote turn-on and signal. The ground is sanded to bare metal and the gain is set with a meter, not by ear.

**Subwoofer upgrade.** Already have a sub that sounds boomy? A subwoofer upgrade may mean a better enclosure, a correctly matched amplifier or simply fixing the wiring someone else left loose.

**Car stereo installation.** A new head unit with pre-amp outputs gives the amplifier a clean signal. Head unit installation uses a dash kit and harness so the factory dash stays intact.

**Speaker replacement.** Once the bass is handled, speaker replacement in the front doors brings the rest of the music up to match.

## Lennox bass questions

### What does subwoofer installation involve for a Lennox customer?

Subwoofer installation starts with choosing the enclosure and amplifier for your car, then running fused power wire from the battery, grounding to bare metal, adding a signal feed and securing the box. We set the gain and crossover before handing the keys back. Most Lennox jobs are finished the same day.

### How far is the shop from Lennox?

About 9 miles, under half an hour in normal traffic. Customers travel to us from as far as San Diego for amplifier and subwoofer work, so the trip from Lennox is short by comparison. Book a slot and we will have the bay ready when you arrive.

### Do I need a new head unit for amplifier installation?

Not always. Amplifier installation on a factory stereo uses a line-output converter to create a clean signal. If your factory unit is old or you want CarPlay anyway, car stereo installation with pre-amp outputs gives the amplifier a better signal and more control.

### Why does my current sub rattle the trunk?

Rattles usually come from a loose enclosure, an unbraced license plate or trunk lid, or a box that is too small for the driver. A subwoofer upgrade addresses the enclosure and mounting first, then the amplifier settings, so the bass sounds tight instead of loose.

### Can you install a Kicker loaded box with an amp in one visit?

Yes. A loaded Kicker box plus amplifier installation is one of the most common jobs in the bay. We supply the box, wire the amplifier correctly and tune the system in a single appointment. Bring the car from Lennox in the morning and it is ready in the afternoon.
