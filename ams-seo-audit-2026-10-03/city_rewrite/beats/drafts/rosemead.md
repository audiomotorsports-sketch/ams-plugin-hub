### Where does a subwoofer fit in a crew cab or SUV?

Subwoofer installation in a crew cab uses a shallow sealed enclosure under the rear seat, bolted down. In an SUV it goes against the cargo side wall, under the rear seat or in the spare-tire well. The amplifier mounts to the box or behind a panel and the floor stays clear.

### Will amplifier installation cause my lights to dim?

Not when it is done correctly. Amplifier installation uses wire sized for the amplifier draw, a fuse at the battery and a clean chassis ground, and we check the charging system on larger builds. Dimming lights point to undersized wire or a poor ground.

### Can new speakers fit my truck doors without drilling?

Usually yes. Speaker replacement in most trucks and SUVs uses adapter brackets that bolt to the factory holes, so no drilling is needed. The adapters also seal the speaker to the door, improving bass and stopping panel rattles.
