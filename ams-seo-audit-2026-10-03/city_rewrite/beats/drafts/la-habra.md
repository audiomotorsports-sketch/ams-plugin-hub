### How do I know whether my speakers are blown?

Buzzing or crackling at normal volume, a speaker that is much quieter than its partner, or no sound from one corner are the usual signs. Speaker replacement fixes it directly. If the whole system is simply weak, car stereo installation or amplifier installation may be the better fix.

### Can a La Habra driver replace only the front speakers?

Yes. Front speaker replacement alone makes the biggest difference since most of the sound comes from the front. Many La Habra drivers do the fronts first and return for rears, a subwoofer installation or an amplifier later, spreading the cost over two visits.

### Do new speakers work with a factory amplified system?

They can, with matching. Factory amplified cars need speakers chosen for the amplifier output and sometimes an integration harness. We check that before speaker installation so the new speakers are neither starved for power nor overdriven by the factory amplifier.

### Coaxial or component speaker installation, which is right for me?

Coaxials drop into the factory location and keep speaker installation simple. Components separate the tweeter for better imaging but need a crossover and tweeter mount. We will recommend one based on your car, your music and how loud you listen.
