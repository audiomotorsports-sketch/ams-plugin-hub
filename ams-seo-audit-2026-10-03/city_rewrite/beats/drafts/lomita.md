## Lomita car audio questions

### Why would a Lomita driver bring factory speakers to Carson instead of ordering a set online?

Because the speaker is the easy part. Speaker replacement is about fitting the driver to the door, sealing it, matching impedance to the radio or amp and wiring it so the panel never rattles. Doing that every day is what you drive 3.7 miles for; a mismatched online kit often has to be redone.

### Does speaker replacement require a new car stereo?

No. Most factory head units will drive a better set of door and dash speakers without any other change, and you will hear the difference right away. If you want more volume later, an amplifier installation can be added behind the same speakers without starting over.

### How long does car stereo installation take for a Lomita daily driver?

A straightforward head unit installation with a dash kit, harness and steering-wheel control module is usually a same-day job when you arrive at opening. Vehicles that need a factory-amp bypass or a camera retention module take longer, and we will tell you before you drop off.

### Can a subwoofer go in a hatchback or small sedan from Lomita?

Yes. A compact loaded enclosure like the Dual Kicker Comp box fits a trunk or hatch with the seats still usable. Subwoofer installation includes a fused power run, a clean ground and signal from the radio or a line-level converter, so it works with a factory stereo too.

### Do you do more than car audio for Lomita vehicles?
