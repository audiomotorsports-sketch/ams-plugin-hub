### What does a La Puente driver get in an amplifier installation?

Amplifier installation includes fused power wire from the battery, a ground sanded to bare metal, remote turn-on, signal cables routed away from power to prevent whine, secure mounting with airflow and a gain setting done with a meter. We test the system before you leave.

### Can I add an amplifier and keep my factory radio?

Yes. When the factory head unit stays, amplifier installation takes its signal from the speaker wires through a line-output converter so the amp gets a clean feed. That lets you add a subwoofer or power new speakers without replacing the radio, which many La Puente drivers choose first.

### My subwoofer sounds loose. Can it be fixed?

Usually. A subwoofer upgrade checks enclosure size, gain, crossover and wiring before replacing anything. Loose or boomy bass is most often a tuning or enclosure issue, and fixing it tightens the sound without the cost of a new driver.

### Will a loaded box and amplifier be too much for my electrical system?

Not with amplifier installation done to spec. Wire sized for the draw, a fuse at the battery and a clean ground keep the system efficient, and we check charging capacity on larger builds. A single loaded box is well within what a healthy alternator supplies.
