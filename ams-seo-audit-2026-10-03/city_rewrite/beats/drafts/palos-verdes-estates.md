## Questions from Palos Verdes Estates drivers

### Can the car stereo be upgraded without replacing the factory screen in a newer vehicle?

In most cases, yes. The factory screen stays and the upgrade happens behind it: an amplifier fed by a signal processor or line-output converter, new speakers in the stock locations and a sub if you want one. The navigation, camera and vehicle settings all remain on the original display.

### How do you keep an amplifier installation out of sight?

Amplifiers go under a seat, against a trunk wall or in a spare-tire well, with wiring run under the carpet and sill trim. The power lead is fused near the battery and the ground is bonded to clean metal. Nothing is visible in the cabin and no cargo space is lost that you did not agree to.

### Is it worth driving from Palos Verdes Estates for speaker replacement alone?

Yes, because the mounting is what you are buying. Speakers fitted on proper adapters with sealed baffles, tweeters in the factory positions and plug-in connections sound cleaner and last longer than a quick swap. The drive is short and the result is done once, done right.

### Will Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation work with my factory premium sound system?

Yes. On supported vehicles an interface adds CarPlay and Android Auto to the factory screen and routes audio through the existing premium amplifier. Where a new head unit is needed instead, the factory amp is integrated with the proper module so every speaker, including the sub, keeps playing.

### What should a Palos Verdes Estates driver send before booking?

Year, make, model and trim, whether the car has a premium sound package or factory amplifier, and what you want the end result to be. With that we can tell you whether the factory screen can stay, which parts are needed and what the installation will cost before you leave the hill.
