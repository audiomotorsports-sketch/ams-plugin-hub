### Can I add an amplifier without replacing my factory radio?

Yes. Amplifier installation on a factory stereo uses a line-output converter to create a clean signal from the speaker wires. That lets you add a subwoofer or power new speakers while keeping the factory head unit, which many Whittier drivers prefer.

### My sub sounds boomy. Can you fix it instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. A subwoofer upgrade looks at enclosure volume, gain, crossover and wiring before anything is replaced. Boomy bass is usually a tuning or enclosure issue, and correcting it tightens the sound without buying a new driver, which saves you money.

### Will a subwoofer and amplifier drain my battery?

Not when amplifier installation is done to spec. Wire sized for the amplifier draw, a fuse at the battery and a clean ground keep the system efficient, and we check charging capacity on larger builds. A typical loaded-box setup is well within a healthy alternator.
