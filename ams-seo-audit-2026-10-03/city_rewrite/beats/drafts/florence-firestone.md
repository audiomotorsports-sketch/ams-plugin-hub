### Will the new head unit look factory or stuck on?

Factory. Head unit installation uses a kit molded for the exact dash, with the texture and color matched and the screen sitting flush. The harness plugs into the car's connector, so no factory wires are cut, and the trim goes back on with every clip in place.

It can. Most head units we install have CarPlay and Android Auto built in, so the install includes running the USB to a sensible spot and pairing your phone. If your factory screen has to stay, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation through an interface is quoted instead.
