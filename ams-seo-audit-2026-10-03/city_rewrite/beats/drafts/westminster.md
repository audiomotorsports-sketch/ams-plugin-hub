## Westminster subwoofer questions

### What size subwoofer should a Westminster driver choose?

It depends on the vehicle, the amplifier and how much bass you want. A single loaded box is enough for most daily drivers; a larger enclosure suits a truck or SUV with room behind the seat. We match the sub, box and amp so they work as a set rather than selling the biggest one available.

### Will adding a subwoofer require a new head unit?

No. A line-output converter takes signal from the factory speaker wires and feeds the amplifier, with a remote turn-on derived so the amp follows the radio. The factory head unit stays. If you want Apple CarPlay and Android Auto later, a head unit installation can feed the same amp directly.

### How do you stop the sub from rattling the trunk?

The box is anchored so it cannot move, the gain is set to the head unit output instead of maxed, and the crossover keeps the sub to the low notes. After the install we play it at volume and check the trunk lid, rear deck and license plate, fixing any buzz before the car goes back.
