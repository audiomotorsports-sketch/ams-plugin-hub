## South Pasadena amplifier questions

### How is an amplifier wired so it does not hum or whine?

The power lead runs from the battery through a fuse near the terminal, the ground is bolted to sanded bare metal on a short lead, and the signal cables run away from the power wire. The gain is set with a meter rather than by ear alone. That combination keeps alternator whine and hum out.

### Can an amplifier be added to the factory stereo in a South Pasadena car?

Yes. A line-output converter takes the speaker-level signal from the factory radio and gives the amplifier a clean low-level feed, with a remote turn-on so the amp powers up with the car. The stock head unit, screen and controls stay exactly as they were.

### Do I need new speakers with an amplifier installation?

Not necessarily, but factory speakers were built for factory power. With an amp behind them they may distort before they get loud. Speaker replacement at the same time lets the amplifier do its job, and we will tell you honestly whether your existing speakers can handle it.

### Is driving from South Pasadena worth it for an amplifier job?

Yes. The amplifier is a part; the fusing, grounding, routing and tuning are the service, and they decide whether the system is quiet and reliable. Drivers come here from much farther than South Pasadena for that reason. Done once, done right beats redoing a noisy install.

### How long does an amplifier installation take and can I wait?

A replacement amp on existing wiring is a lobby wait. A fresh installation with a power run, ground, signal cables, remote lead and tuning takes most of a day, so dropping the car in the morning and picking it up in the afternoon is the better plan for South Pasadena drivers.
