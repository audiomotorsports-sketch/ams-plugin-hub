### Will CarPlay installation keep my factory backup camera working?

Yes. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation includes camera retention, either wired directly to the new unit or through an interface module. Reverse still shows the image on the new screen, and steering-wheel buttons and chimes keep working through the same module.
