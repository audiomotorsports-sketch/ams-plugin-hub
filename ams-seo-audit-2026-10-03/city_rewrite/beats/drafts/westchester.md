## Westchester bass and power installs

**Subwoofer installation.** The enclosure is secured so it cannot shift, the power wire is fused within inches of the battery, and the crossover is set so the sub blends with the door speakers.

**Amplifier installation.** Four-channel or mono amplifiers mounted where they can breathe, with signal cables routed away from power wire so there is no alternator whine.

**Subwoofer upgrade.** If your current sub rattles or distorts, a subwoofer upgrade looks at enclosure volume, amplifier matching and the wiring before anything is replaced.

**Car stereo installation.** A head unit with pre-amp outputs gives the amplifier a clean, controllable signal. Installed with a dash kit and harness.

**Speaker replacement.** Front door speaker replacement on sealed adapters so the midrange keeps up with the new bass.

Subwoofer installation means choosing an enclosure and amplifier that match, running fused power wire from the battery, grounding to bare metal, adding a signal feed from the head unit or a line-output converter, securing the box and setting gain and crossover. ### How far is Westchester from the shop and should I drop off?

About 12.1 miles, under half an hour outside rush hour. For a subwoofer and amplifier installation we suggest dropping the car in the morning, since the wiring runs under carpet and panels. A quick subwoofer upgrade on an existing system can sometimes be done while you wait.

### Can I add an amplifier to a factory stereo?

Yes. With a factory head unit, amplifier installation adds a line-output converter that turns the speaker-level wires into a clean signal for the amp. It works well for adding a sub or powering new door speakers without replacing the radio, and many Westchester drivers go this route first.

### Why does my bass sound muddy?

Muddy bass usually comes from an enclosure that is the wrong size, a gain set too high, or a crossover that lets the sub play too high. A subwoofer upgrade corrects those settings and, if needed, the enclosure, which tightens the sound without changing the driver.

### Will a subwoofer drain my battery?

Not when amplifier installation is sized correctly. We use wire gauge matched to the amplifier, fuse it at the battery and check the charging system on larger builds. A normal loaded-box system draws well within what a healthy alternator can supply.
