## City of Industry stereo FAQ

### Can a City of Industry work van get a head unit with a backup camera?

Yes. Car stereo installation on a van includes a head unit with a camera input, the factory camera retained or a camera added at the rear door, and the reverse trigger wired so the picture appears automatically. It is tested before the van goes back to work.

### Is it worth taking a work truck out of service for a day to drive to Carson?

For a stereo that will not fail on a job site, yes. City of Industry is about 27.2 miles, and the install is done the same day you drop off. A plug-in harness, a kit that holds the radio solid and a fused, grounded amplifier mean the truck does not come back for rework.

### Will Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation handle calls over road noise?

That depends on microphone placement, which we do deliberately: near the driver, away from vents and glass. Combined with speaker replacement so the cab is clear, hands-free calls on a work truck become usable at highway speed. We test a call before you leave.

### Can you install a stereo in a fleet vehicle with a plain radio opening?

Yes. Fleet trucks and vans usually have a standard opening, which makes head unit installation straightforward: dash kit, harness and a steering-wheel module if the vehicle has wheel buttons. Several vehicles can be booked on consecutive days so one is never out of service long.

### Does amplifier installation hold up in a truck that carries tools?

When it is mounted and wired properly, yes. The amp goes under a seat or behind a panel, the power wire is fused at the battery and run inside the body, and the ground is sanded to bare metal. Nothing is left loose where a ladder or tool bag can catch it.
