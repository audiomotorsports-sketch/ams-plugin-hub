## Willowbrook frequently asked questions

### Can a Willowbrook driver get a stereo installed that still looks stock?

Yes, that is the specialty. Factory-look car stereo installation uses the dash kit designed for your exact model, so the new screen fills the opening with no gaps or filler. Trim pieces are reused, the harness plugs in and the dash never gets cut.

### Does Android Auto installation keep my backup camera?

It does. During Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation we add a camera retention module or wire the factory camera directly to the new unit, so reverse still shows the image on screen. Steering-wheel controls are retained the same way.

### How far is the shop from Willowbrook and should I wait or drop off?

About 6 miles, a short drive from Willowbrook. For a head unit or speaker job you can wait in the shop; for a full system with subwoofer installation and amplifier installation, dropping the car off for the day is easier. Either way, book first so the bay is ready.

### Will my new speakers rattle the door panels?

Not if the speaker installation is done correctly. We fit speakers on solid adapter rings, seal around the opening and secure any loose clips in the door card. That is the difference between a speaker swap and proper speaker replacement.

### Can a subwoofer be hidden so the trunk stays usable?

Yes. Subwoofer installation can use a compact enclosure under a seat, against a trunk wall or in a spare-tire well, with the amplifier mounted on the enclosure. You keep your cargo space, the box stays hidden and you still get real bass.
