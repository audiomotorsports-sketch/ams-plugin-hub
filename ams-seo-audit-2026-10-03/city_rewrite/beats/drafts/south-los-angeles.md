### Can you add bass to an SUV without taking the cargo area?

Yes. Subwoofer installation in an SUV can use a shallow enclosure along the side wall, under the rear seat or in the spare-tire well. The amplifier mounts to the box or behind a panel. The floor stays flat and usable.

### Does amplifier installation dim my headlights?

Not when it is wired correctly. Amplifier installation uses wire sized for the amplifier draw, a fuse at the battery and a clean ground, and we check charging capacity on bigger systems. Dimming lights are a sign of an undersized or poorly grounded install.
