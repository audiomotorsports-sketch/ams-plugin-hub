## Questions from Lawndale truck owners

### Where does a subwoofer go in a crew-cab truck from Lawndale?

Under or behind the rear seat is the usual answer, using a shallow enclosure built for that space. Subwoofer installation then covers a fused power wire, a proper ground, signal from the factory or new radio and tuning the amp so the sub blends instead of booming.

### Can you install a stereo in an SUV with a big factory screen?

Often, yes. Many SUVs have a dash kit that replaces the factory screen with an aftermarket head unit and a module that keeps climate controls, backup camera and vehicle settings. Some newer models are better served by a CarPlay interface behind the factory screen; we will tell you which applies.

### Is Lawndale close enough to wait while the work is done?

At about 6.5 miles, yes. Speaker replacement or a basic head unit installation is a wait-and-drive job for most trucks. A full subwoofer and amplifier installation takes longer, so Lawndale owners usually drop it off in the morning and come back in the afternoon.

### Do I need an amplifier installation for truck door speakers?

Not always. New door speakers on factory power already sound cleaner. An amplifier installation is what gets you volume on the freeway without distortion, and it is the right next step when the truck is loud inside. We size the amp to the speakers you actually have.

### Can a Lawndale truck get tint or an alarm during the same appointment?

Yes. Window tint and ceramic window tint, car alarm installation, remote start installation, GPS tracker installation and dash camera installation all happen under the same roof as the stereo work.
