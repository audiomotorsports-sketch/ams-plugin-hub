## Beverly Hills CarPlay questions

### Can I add Apple CarPlay to my factory screen in a Beverly Hills luxury car?

In many late-model luxury cars, yes. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation can use an interface module that routes CarPlay through the factory display and factory controls, so the interior stays original. Text the year, make and model and we will confirm what your car supports before you drive from Beverly Hills.

### Is it worth the trip from Beverly Hills for a CarPlay install?

Beverly Hills is about 18.5 miles from the bay, under three quarters of an hour outside rush hour. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation on a luxury car depends on the correct interface and careful trim work, and that is what makes the trip worthwhile. Done once, done right.

### Does the install keep my parking sensors and camera?

Yes. Camera retention and factory parking-sensor display are part of every Apple CarPlay and Android Auto installation we do on cars that have them. The interface passes those signals through so the screen still shows the camera and sensor graphics in reverse.

### Will adding CarPlay void anything or leave marks on the interior?

The install uses plug-in harnesses and the factory trim is removed and refitted with the proper tools, so the interior shows no marks. We do not cut factory wiring, and the car can be returned to stock. Warranty questions should be checked with your dealer.

### Can I improve the sound at the same time?

Yes. Speaker replacement and amplifier installation can be done in the same visit as CarPlay, and on factory-amplified cars we match the new speakers to the amplifier output. A hidden subwoofer installation adds bass without changing how the trunk looks.
