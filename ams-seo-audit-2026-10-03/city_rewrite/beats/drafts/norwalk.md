## Norwalk bass questions

### Is it worth driving from Norwalk for subwoofer installation?

Yes, because the labor is what you are paying for. A sub that is fused, grounded and tuned correctly plays for years; one that is wired loose blows fuses or amps. Roughly 23 to 28 minutes from Norwalk to have it done once, done right, is the better deal.

### What size amplifier installation does a single subwoofer need?

A monoblock matched to the sub power rating and impedance is the usual choice. We size the amp to the sub actually in the car, set the gain with a meter so it never clips, and set the low-pass crossover so the sub handles only bass. Bigger is not always better.

### Can a subwoofer upgrade keep my existing amp and wiring?

Often, yes. If the existing power wire is the correct gauge and properly fused, and the amp suits the new sub, a subwoofer upgrade is a swap and a re-tune. If the old wiring is undersized or unfused, we will show you and quote the fix before doing anything.

### Will the bass rattle my Norwalk car trunk and license plate?

Rattles come from loose panels and a loose enclosure, not from the sub itself. Strapping the enclosure down, checking the trunk lid and plate, and setting the crossover properly keeps the bass tight. If a panel still buzzes we will show you where and what fixes it.
