### Can I keep the factory look with a new head unit?

Yes. Dash kits are molded for the specific dash with matching texture and color, and the screen sits flush rather than proud. Car stereo installation done this way does not look aftermarket from the driver's seat, which matters on a leased or newer car.

On many newer cars, an interface module adds CarPlay and Android Auto to the factory display while keeping climate menus, the camera and the wheel buttons. Where no interface exists, a head unit with CarPlay built in is the route. Send the model and we will confirm.
