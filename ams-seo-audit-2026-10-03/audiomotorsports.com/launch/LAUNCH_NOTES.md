MIME type text/plain". But when it hits, the page renders unstyled/with a missing hero and LCP jumps to 10.5 to 11.7s (see (d)). Classification: UNVERIFIED, treat as MUST-FIX to investigate: check Vercel runtime logs / edge for 502s on /sites/*, /assets/* and the mount rewrites around 2026-10-03 22:00-04:00 PDT.
