# SERP + competitor research: audiomotorsports.com (hub)

Date: 2026-10-07. Read-only research. Nothing was changed, posted, submitted or contacted. Every recommendation improves an existing page; nothing is removed, merged, redirected or noindexed. Nothing goes live before Zack and Tom review it together.

## 0. Read this first: what this evidence is and is not

**Method:** about 30 WebSearch calls (US-only) covering the 15 target queries from `AIO_REPORT.md` section 5 and `AI_VISIBILITY_QUERY_SET.md`, plus cheap-wording variants, competitor name lookups, sister-domain brand checks. Then WebFetch on the hub and on competitors.

**Limits (important, they change how much you can trust the table):**
- **WebSearch is not a SERP and not a rank tracker.** It returns a short list of links plus an AI-written summary. Order is not a ranking. There is no Maps pack, no People Also Ask, no AI Overview, no personalization or location. Everything labeled "(snippet)" below comes from that list/summary, not from Google.
- **The index it searches is noisy.** Many results are scraper or spam pages ("Near Me" posts on unrelated .edu, .gov and corporate domains, for example `cms.ncwc.edu`, `sherritt.com`, `vis.copcap.com`). I ignored them as competitors. Real local businesses are scarcer in these results than in real Google.
- **Competitor pages could not be fetched.** The egress proxy blocked `rapidwindowtinting.com`, `tint-spot.com`, `tintworld.com`, `carmeleonskin.com`, `windowfilmdepot.com`, `loc8nearme.com`, `yelp.com`, `local.yahoo.com`, `caraudioshops.com` and `teslamotorsclub.com`. I did not bypass this. So competitor "titles, H1, schema, review counts" are **mostly unavailable**. What I have for competitors is the link title and the search summary text only. Where I cannot see something, it is marked **[not visible]**, never judged.
- **Fetchable and fetched:** `www.audiomotorsports.com` pages (`/tint/`, `/torrance/`, `/amspods/`, `/services/remote-start/`, `/tesla/tesla-tint-carson/`, `/contact-us`) and the `teslatintla.com` apex homepage.
- Prices, review counts and hours shown for competitors are snippet text and are **[CONFIRM]** before anyone uses them.
- Whether any query would show an AI Overview in a real browser is **unknown**. I only note signals that make an answer-style result likely.

## 4. (b) Content and format patterns worth copying (with evidence)

1. **Prices as quotable lines and tables, dated.** Search summaries quoted Rapid Window Tinting's price guides and the Tesla Tint LA page text for dollar figures, and ignored pages without numbers. Copy: one dated table per money page.
2. **"Price guide" blog posts with the year in the title** (Rapid: "2026 price guide", Tesla/PPF guides). Copy the format into the existing AMS blog posts, not into new thin pages.
3. **Named people and a named installer** (Carmeleon: "ask for Jay", Master Installer named). AMS already has "ask for Nick" and named staff; add bylines to blog and service pages.
4. **Opening hours and phone as the first static text** on local pages (listing-style results for "open now").
5. **Neighbor-city naming on a single local page** (Window Film Depot names Long Beach and South Bay). AMS city pages already have drive time; keep it.
6. **Per-model landing pages with install videos** for Harley audio (Sonic Electronix, ABT, Rockford). AMS Pods already has a bike-by-bike FAQ; add a short "how we wire bags" numbered list and link the shop's own videos (the page has a "Watch the work" block).
7. **Tesla roof as its own price line** (Tint-Spot: roof +$249). Add a roof line to the `/tesla/` table if the shop prices it **[CONFIRM]**.
